## N01 — `g^*Δ` in 9.10a (c) is a pullback the text has not defined

In 9.10a (c) (line 48), `g^*Δ` is the pullback of a `ℚ`-Weil divisor that need not be `ℚ`-Cartier, along an étale `g`; the `f^*` defined in the first paragraph of 9.10a is for `ℚ`-Cartier Weil divisors only. A clause saying that `g^*Δ` is the flat pullback of Weil divisors (the divisor of `g^{−1}` of each component, with multiplicity one since `g` is étale) would fix the object; the same for "the pullback `Δ′` of `Δ`" in the field-extension clause.
