## H01 (CBirkbeck) — applied

README 3C used to say that `rayFundamentalDomain 𝔪` "meets each orbit of that subgroup once" and that it restricts to Mathlib's `fundamentalCone` at the trivial modulus. It now has the modulo-torsion contract of the pinned `TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.rayFundamentalDomain` (`TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Counting/RayFundamentalDomain/Basic.lean`). The finite union is indexed by cosets of `E_𝔪 μ_K`. Existence holds on `posRegion 𝔪 ∩ {N ≠ 0}`, and the signs at `𝔪∞` are preserved. Each `E_𝔪`-orbit has exactly `w_𝔪 = #(E_𝔪 ∩ μ_K)` representatives, and the index identity is `[E:E_𝔪]·w_𝔪 = [E:E_𝔪 μ_K]·w_K`. The volume is `[E:E_𝔪 μ_K]·2^r₁ π^r₂ R_K / 2^s`. 3D now divides the coset count by `w_𝔪`, 3G.4 carries `[E:E_𝔪 μ_K]`, `w_𝔪` and `2^s`, and three Checks were added: ℚ at the trivial modulus (2 representatives per orbit), ℚ at `(3)` (`w_𝔪 = 1 ≠ w_K = 2`), and ℚ at `∞`. In `Suggested.lean`, the sorry'd local `rayFundamentalDomain` (old lines 280–284) and its boundary theorem (old lines 286–297) are deleted, not re-docstringed, because both exist at the pin.
