## Source issues

- **Error** in CS24 at Theorem 7.1.2, printed p. 87: the regular clause admits local rings that are fields when `d = 1`, and then the injectivity conclusion fails for `X = Spec k ⊔ Spec k` with `Z` one point; it should be regular of dimension `≥ max(d − 1, 1)`, as Theorem 6.2.7 excludes fields; affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in CS24 at proof of Theorem 3.3.1(b), p. 35: the source says `vdim(R_d) ≥ 3`; it should be `vdim(R) ≥ 3`; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in CS24 at proof of Theorem 6.2.7, p. 85: the source says `f ∈ (p, x_1, …, x_n)²`; it should be `(p, x_1, …, x_d)²`; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in CS24 at proofs of Theorems 7.2.1 (p. 89) and 7.2.5 (p. 90): the source says `n ≥ 0`; it should be `n ≥ 1`; affects nothing.
- **Gap** in CS24 at Lemma 4.1.12, p. 40: the source assumes `n < dim R`, which its proof never uses, and the proof of Theorem 6.2.7 (p. 85) applies the lemma in degree `n = dim R`; it should be stated for every integer `n`; affects the proof.
