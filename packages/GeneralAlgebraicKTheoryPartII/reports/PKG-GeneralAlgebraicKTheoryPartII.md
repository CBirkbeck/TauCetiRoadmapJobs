# Package report: general algebraic K-theory, part II

The package contains the complete reader in packages/GeneralAlgebraicKTheoryPartII/README.md, its carrier-honest acceptance file in packages/GeneralAlgebraicKTheoryPartII/Suggested.lean, metadata.toml and sources.toml. The reader has eleven layers, 103 local targets, prose interfaces, discriminating Checks, per-layer examples and dependencies, four worked examples, and exact supplier contracts. All 73 accepted target objects remain accounted for: 67 local targets and six imported constructions; three local proof inputs and 33 chromatic targets make the total 103. The original targets' API facts and Checks are retained either at their local target or in the supplying interface. The global truncating assertions are supplied facts; the retained variant quantifies the acyclicity of every auxiliary ring.

The separately named design/review.json was absent, and reports/ initially contained no report. I read and applied the accepted review embedded in design/plan.json and design/README.md, then checked its mathematical claims independently. The plan's top-level gaps list is the only changed part of the design. No content-checkout source, merge order, tool configuration or library pin was edited.

## Corrections and support limits

- Retained the design's correction to global height-zero redshift: T(0)=S[1/p] does not imply nonzero rationalization. For odd p the characteristic-two one-periodic Tate ring has height 0, rational part 0 and 2-torsion K-theory, contradicting unrestricted global height-zero redshift. The reader requires a nonzero rational part, automatically present for p-local nonnegative-height input. The p-local scope of BSY §9 is visible at bsy line 3777; its example is bsy line 3929, and the published unrestricted wording is lmmt line 175.
- Retained all accepted review safeguards: right modules/opposites; unrestricted extension but perfect restriction; uncountable regular flasque enlargement; connected positive plus space; relative acyclic plus square rather than plus of an arbitrary fiber; finitely dominated A-theory; full versus connective mapping spectra; objectwise boundedness; noetherian heart for nonconnective heart comparison; regular/noetherian/coherent support in spectral dévissage; separate bottom and positive telescope conventions; full semilinear supersingular groups; and uniform descent towers.
- Corrected the moved Thom coefficient conventions. The y family uses S_p^∧. At p=2 the z family used here is the integral sign Thom family followed by 2-localization, with π₀=ℤ_(2) and endpoint Hℤ_(2). Giving z the completed coefficient ℤ₂ while keeping the endpoint Hℤ_(2) would supply no such natural coefficient map. The corrected integral model is read at akq line 186 and Construction 2.1 at akq line 218; the K-theory endpoint is lmmt line 971.
- Corrected the transferred rank witness: under R→R, [R²] maps to 2[R]; under a free rank-d extension B/R, [B] maps to d[R]. The unqualified rank-two B-module sentence would omit the extension's rank.
- Corrected the small Morava splitting Check: rational ranks of K(𝔽_p)[1/p] and its suspension in degrees (0,1) are (1,0) and (0,1), respectively; only their sum has ranks (1,1).
- Added the missing QWittVectors characteristic-series bridge as an exact supplier gap. The coordinate model in its §1.3 does not state that chart. The specified chart is χ(a)=∏(1−a_dt^d), with ghost law −tχ′/χ; its zero is 1 and its unit is 1−t, matching weibel line 38. At p=2 the t² coefficients of the unit chart and its inverse are 0 and 1, so this also distinguishes the two sign conventions where their linear terms agree.
- Defined the Morava weight-p map by its actual extended-power composite and added its basepoint normalization x↦x^p. At scalar 2 the restrictions are 4 and 8 at p=2 and 3; an identity-constant map would give 2 and is excluded. The raw additivity errors are the transfer values 2 and 6, not zero.
- Kept the polynomial variable in the Witt characteristic-series action: a(νx), not a(ν). For ν²=0 and a(t)=1−rt the value is 1−rνx, with evaluation 1 at x=0. Negative-degree continuity is a target via natural Bass contraction and finitely many representatives, not a connective-only action.
- Restated finite fiberwise homology comparison through coherent fiber local systems. An infinite CW base and periodic homology theory do not invalidate strong convergence of the exhaustive homological filtration; its cohomological analogue has separate inverse-limit conditions. The KU/ℂP∞ Check respects that distinction.
- Kept the BP truncation Bousfield class as a target: the supplied inverted BP/E(n) class does not contain the residual HF_p summand by itself.

The original G1 is a proof audit of this package's own Witt-action construction, not a missing supplier theorem. It is recorded here as a local construction obligation and is not left as a supplier-free record in the plan's gaps list. Weibel Theorem 3.1 and its consequences were read, including the negative Bass-contraction paragraph at weibel line 211, including the fact that the original construction is attributed there to a subsequent Stienstra text. This package does not claim to have independently read that original construction proof. The reader states the action, finite-annihilator and negative Bass-extension targets completely; there is no claimed implementation or sorry'd data.
