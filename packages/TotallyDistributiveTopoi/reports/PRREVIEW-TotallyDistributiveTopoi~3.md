## R02 (P3) — The 2.2 decomposition route asserts that `π₀ η` is the identity

- **Location.** `README.md` lines 305–306: "`π₀` preserves coproducts and `π₀ η` is the identity, so each `π₀ F_x` is a point".
- **Claim.** `π₀ η_F : π₀ F ⟶ π₀ Δ π₀ F` is the identity.
- **Witness.** In `Sh X` for `X` the two-point discrete space, `π₀ F = F_a ⊔ F_b`, and `π₀ Δ S = S ⊔ S` (since `Hom(π₀ Δ S, T) = Hom(S, Γ Δ T) = Hom(S, T × T)`), so `π₀ η_F : F_a ⊔ F_b ⟶ (F_a ⊔ F_b) ⊔ (F_a ⊔ F_b)` is not even an endomorphism. What is the identity is the adjoint transpose of `η_F` under `π₀ ⊣ Δ`. The conclusion holds: with `p : F_x ⟶ F` and `q : F_x ⟶ ⊤` the pullback projections and `c_x : ⊤ ⟶ Δ π₀ F` the coprojection at `x`, the transposes of `η ∘ p = c_x ∘ q` are `π₀ p` and the constant map at `x` (`c_x` transposes to the constant map `π₀ ⊤ → π₀ F` at `x`), so `π₀ p` is constant at `x`; since `∐_x π₀ F_x ⟶ π₀ F` is a bijection through the `π₀ p`, each `π₀ F_x` is a point. Checked on the two-point space: `F_x = ({x}, ∅)` for `x ∈ F_a`, `π₀ F_x = {x}`.
- **Repair.** Replace "`π₀ η` is the identity, so each `π₀ F_x` is a point" by "the transpose of `η` is the identity, so `π₀` of each pullback projection `F_x ⟶ F` is constant at `x`, and `π₀ F ≅ ∐ₓ π₀ F_x` makes each `π₀ F_x` a point".
