## R01 (P3) — The cited GS lines do not contain the Hermitian-isometry construction of Aut(H₃(B))

**Claim:** GS §§4.1–4.2 construct Aut(H₃(B)) as the μ₂-quotient of the unitary group of the standard rank-three B-Hermitian form, acting by conjugation on Hermitian matrices.

**Repair:** Keep GS lines 542–543 as the locator for the H₃(B) model and its split specialization only. State the isometry-group isomorphism as this roadmap’s own construction in the proof route, without a source claim, or cite the primary source for the automorphism group of a quaternionic Hermitian Jordan algebra of degree three (GS’s own reference [KMRT]) with a recorded locator and a `sources.toml` entry.
