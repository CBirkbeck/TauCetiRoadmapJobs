   Location: README.md:1329, "In the contains-1 case I₃(τ) is irreducible by the applicable non-discrete-series criterion".

   Claim: for selfdual tempered τ of PGL₃ whose parameter contains 1, θ_H(θ_{M₃}(τ⁺)) = I₃(τ), an irreducible representation.

   Witness: the only reducibility targets for I₃ are §1.12 (README.md:502), which covers discrete-series τ and Borel series only, and §1.11 (README.md:490), which covers I₁₃(s,τ⊗1) for supercuspidal τ only; GS Proposition 10.2 (GS line 1206) and 10.3 (GS line 1220) have the same hypotheses. Instance: τ = st × 1 (also st_χ × 1 with χ² = 1) is selfdual with parameter Sp₂ ⊕ 1 ∋ 1, and I₃(st × 1) = I₁₃(0, st ⊗ 1) is in neither list. The statement is true (the 7-dimensional parameter is 2·Sp₂ ⊕ 3·1, whose centralizer in SO₇ is Sp(W) × SO₃ with W the 2-dimensional multiplicity space, connected, so the tempered packet is a singleton; equivalently L(s, st_χ, ∧²) = L(s, 1) has a pole at s = 0 and the Harish-Chandra–Silberger measure vanishes), but no target or gap owns it: `gap:rank-one-plancherel` (README.md:85) asks only for maximal parabolics with supercuspidal data, and GS Theorem 15.2(ii) (GS lines 2021–2025) asserts θ(π) ≅ I₃(τ) without a reducibility statement for this τ (see Source issues).    Repair: in §1.12 add the target sentence "For σ a unitary selfdual discrete series of GL₂ with ω_σ = 1 (σ supercuspidal, or σ = st_χ with χ² = 1), the Siegel induction I₃(σ × 1) = I₁₃(0, σ⊗1) is irreducible; the supercuspidal case is §1.11 at s = 0, the Steinberg case follows by induction in stages from the Borel series and the segment resolution of st_χ, as in §§1.2 and 1.5, since the Borel exponents are then those of Proposition 3.3 with no |·|^{±1} ratio." In §8.2 replace "by the applicable non-discrete-series criterion" with "by §1.12". In §1.11, either add "the I₁₃ assertions are proved by induction in stages through the GSp₄ Siegel series and §1.13's P₁ series" to the proof route, or widen the needs sentence of `gap:rank-one-plancherel` to "unitary discrete-series inducing data on a maximal parabolic of G or of a Levi subgroup of G" (and the same words in `design/plan.json`), so that the gap covers its consumers.

3. **F03** (P3) §0.9 leaves the GSp₄ coordinate of M₁ unpinned, and the sign of its modulus depends on it

   Location: README.md:314, "M₁≅GSp₄. The three maximal parabolics P₃, P₂, P₁ have moduli |det|², |det α det β|⁵, |ν|³".

The coordinate acting on ⟨x₁₂,…,x₁₅⟩ ⊂ J is h″ = (t/ν)h with ν(h″) = t²/ν, giving |ν(h″)|³ = δ_{P₁}; the coordinate acting on ⟨x₂₆,…,x₅₆⟩ is h′ = t⁻¹h with ν(h′) = ν/t², giving |ν(h′)|⁻³ = δ_{P₁}. The Check at README.md:330 ("at ν(m₁)=ϖ the P₁ modulus is q⁻³") is correct for the x₁ⱼ coordinate and false for the x_{j6} coordinate.

   Repair: after "M₁≅GSp₄" insert "via its action on ⟨x₁₂,x₁₃,x₁₄,x₁₅⟩ (the coordinate in which the x₁ⱼ block carries the similitude t²/ν)", and extend the Check with "in the coordinate on ⟨x₂₆,…,x₅₆⟩ the same element has modulus q³, so the coordinate is part of the convention".
