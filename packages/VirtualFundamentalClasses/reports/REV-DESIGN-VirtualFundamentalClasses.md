   **Claim.** Both directions of the two equivalences, for 𝔐 an algebraic `k`-stack of pure dimension (Convention 8, which allows non-reduced 𝔐).

   **Witness.** 𝔐 = Spec k[ε]/(ε²) (pure dimension 0), M = Spec k, f the closed point: of Deligne–Mumford type, M separated. The relative local embedding (U, W) = (Spec k, Spec k[ε]) has I = (ε), I/I² ≅ k, Ω_{W/𝔐} = 0, so K = [k → 0], N_{M/𝔐} = C(k) = 𝔸¹, a vector bundle; and τ^{≥−1}L_{M/𝔐} = k[1] is perfect of amplitude [−1, 0]. 3. **F03** (P2) The dominating global resolution of 10.1 uses undefined terms and an unjustified step

   **Location.** `design/README.md` lines 1569–1575 (10.1: "`K^0 = H^0 ⊕ F^0` and `K^{−1}` the fibre product of `E^{−1} → E^0 ← K^0`"); plan target 10.1.

   **Claim.** For two global resolutions F^•, H^• of E ∈ D(O_M) there is a third, K^•, receiving both by degreewise monomorphisms with locally free cokernels.

   **Witness.** E is an object of D(O_M) (7.3); `E^{−1}`, `E^0` are terms of no chosen representative. The source (behrend-fantechi1997 lines 1303–1305) first assumes "without loss of generality" that both resolutions map to E by morphisms of complexes, which is where the content lies: on a DM stack a morphism in D(O_M) between two-term complexes of vector bundles need not be realised by a morphism of complexes (the difference lives in H¹(M, Hom(H^0, F^{−1}))), and vector bundles are not projective in QCoh. With E^• := F^• and an honest comparison H^• → F^•, the fibre product is K^{−1} ≅ H^0 ⊕ F^{−1}, which is locally free, so the step that must be stated and proved is the existence of the comparison as a morphism of complexes (or a roof H^• ← H'^• → F^• with H'^• a two-term complex of vector bundles).

   **Repair.** Rewrite 10.1 as: "Prove that for global resolutions F^•, H^• of E there is a two-term complex of vector bundles H'^• with a quasi-isomorphism H'^• → H^• and a morphism of complexes H'^• → F^• inducing the comparison in D(O_M) (route: …), and then that K^• with K^0 = H'^0 ⊕ F^0, K^{−1} = H'^0 ⊕ F^{−1} is a global resolution receiving both by degreewise monomorphisms with locally free cokernels." Give a route (for instance through the resolution property when M is a quotient stack, or through the Čech model of 8.2) or declare the needed input; 10.3 then cites the repaired 10.1. The list in `## Source issues` records the gap in the source.
