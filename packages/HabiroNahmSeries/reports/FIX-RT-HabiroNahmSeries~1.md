# Residue normalization calculation

Write β=diag(A)−A1 and c_A=∑ᵢ<ⱼAᵢⱼ. The corrected form multiplies J_A(T,w,q)dw/w by `(−1)^(N+tr A)q^(−c_A)T⁻¹w^β`. At coefficient Tᵏ, its summation index is n=k+1 and the wᵢ-exponent is `(Ak)_i+A_ii`. The sum of the ordinary finite residues of `w^(r−1)dw/∏_{s=1}^n(1−q^s w)` is `(−1)^nq^(−nr+n(n−1)/2)[r−1 choose n−1]_q`. Combining these factors gives exactly GW (47), including the signs and negative upper indices; the extra q^c_A cancels. Root coalescence is coefficientwise in x, so the same rational identity applies at every order m.

At x=0 the logarithmic pole Jacobian is mΛ_A, and its inverse determinant is `(−1)^(N+tr A)∏t_i z_i^(−β_i)/(m^Nδ)`. Multiplying the residue form gives the finite prefactor and weight in §7.1. These are corrections to GSWZ (218)/(220), not quotations of those formulas.
