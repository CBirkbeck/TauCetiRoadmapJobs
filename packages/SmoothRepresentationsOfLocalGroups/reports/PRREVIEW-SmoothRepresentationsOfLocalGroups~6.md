## Verified sound

### The changed passages

**The ReductiveGroups contract and the Needs line.** ReductiveGroups on main (a0ad405) has Layer 2 at `TauCetiRoadmap/ReductiveGroups/README.md:227–231` (Lie(G), the differential of a homomorphism, Ad), Layer 4 at 432–434 (“Jordan decomposition of elements into semisimple and unipotent parts … functorial under representations”) and Layer 5P at 460–464 (characteristic-zero polynomial exp/log; “log(g) is tangent to the polynomial one-parameter subgroup g^t, whose integer values are powers of g and whose equations vanish identically in characteristic zero”). Each of the three uses in the new route (dρ and `ρ(g) dρ(X) ρ(g)⁻¹ = dρ(Ad(g) X)` from Layer 2; unipotence of ρ(exp N) and the semisimple part of ρ(g) from Layer 4; the one-parameter subgroup u^t from Layer 5P) cites the layer that states it. Layer 4's decomposition is geometric, over k̄; over the characteristic-zero raw fields it is defined over E, so the statement about (r_1(Φ),…,r_k(Φ)) ∈ GL(V_1)×⋯×GL(V_k) is in scope. ReductiveGroups precedes this roadmap in the merge order; the dependency check below shows no UPWARD or CYCLE line.

**The nilpotency route (4576–4586).** Each step was checked against its supplier or target: exp N_i − 1 = N_i(1 + N_i/2 + ⋯) is nilpotent, so exp N_i is unipotent; u = ρ(exp N_1,…,exp N_k) is unipotent by Layer 4; Y = log u is nilpotent and exp(log u) = u by the API of *nilpotent-exp-log* (4495–4517: “log u is nilpotent”, “exp(log u) = u”); t ↦ ρ(exp(tN_1),…,exp(tN_k)) and t ↦ exp(tY) have polynomial entries since every N_i and Y is nilpotent and ρ is a morphism of varieties; at t ∈ ℤ both equal u^t (exp(tN) = (exp N)^t for integers t by `IsNilpotent.exp_add_of_commute`, which the Needs line of *nilpotent-exp-log* cites, and exp(tY) = exp(Y)^t = u^t); two polynomial maps over a characteristic-zero field agreeing on ℤ agree identically; the derivative at t = 0 of the first is dρ applied to the tangent vector (N_1,…,N_k) by the definition of the differential, and of the second is Y. The conclusion dρ(N) = Y is both the nilpotency and the identity `ρ(exp(tN)) = exp(t·dρ(N))`.

**The carrier identification (4600–4604).** At the Mathlib pin `SymmetricPower R ι M` is `(addConGen (SymmetricPower.Rel R ι M)).Quotient` with `Rel` identifying `⨂ₜ i, f i` with `⨂ₜ i, f (e i)` for every permutation e (`Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorPower/Symmetric.lean:46–53`), over a commutative semiring. At the Tau Ceti pin `TauCeti.SymmetricAlgebra.homogeneousSubmodule R M n` is `LinearMap.range (SymmetricAlgebra.ι R M) ^ n` (`TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SymmetricAlgebra/Homogeneous.lean:54`), the image of the n-th tensor power in `SymmetricAlgebra R M`, which at the Mathlib pin is the quotient of the tensor algebra by the ring congruence generated by `ι x * ι y = ι y * ι x` (`Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/SymmetricAlgebra/Basic.lean:40–50`). That ideal is homogeneous and its degree-n part is spanned by a·(xy − yx)·b with a, b pure tensors, i.e. by the differences of a pure tensor and an adjacent transposition of it; adjacent transpositions generate every permutation, and a pure tensor minus a permutation of it telescopes into such differences, so the two quotients coincide over every commutative ring. Neither is the submodule of symmetric tensors. The sentence as written is correct and the characteristic hypothesis is correctly not attached to it.

**The Ber87 citation (710–712).** ber87 lines 100–109 (§1.1, printed p. 3) state that the category of nondegenerate modules over an idempotented ring is abelian with direct limits, that filtered direct limits are exact, and that products are the nondegenerate part of the set-theoretic product. That is exactly what the scoped sentence attributes to it; the arbitrary-coefficient smooth-representation statements are now proved in the passage and not attributed to Ber87.

**Ownership of the new Checks' objects.** The ℤ_2 unipotent extension, the product of the permutation modules A[ℤ/p^n] and the augmentation map A[ℤ/p] → A are worked examples of this roadmap's own smooth category; no roadmap on main or at a readable open head builds SmoothRep A G over an arbitrary commutative ring (the r4 ownership search stands, since no object changed). The composite-parameter construction remains owned here as r5 recorded.
