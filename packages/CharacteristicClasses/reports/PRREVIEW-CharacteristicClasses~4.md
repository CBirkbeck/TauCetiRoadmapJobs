## R01 (P3) — a Check's justifying clause is false in general

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/CharacteristicClasses/README.md` line 1931–1932, "and the product of the three roots is 0, so e(E)=0 is forced by the roots too."

Claim: the vanishing of the product of the Chern roots 0, x−y, y−x of End₀(F) forces e(su(F))=0.

Witness: for an oriented real rank-3 bundle E, the roots of E⊗ℂ are always 0, x, −x, so their product is 0 for every such E, while c₃(E⊗ℂ)=±e(E)², not e(E); the universal rank-3 bundle on BSO(3) has e(E)=W₃≠0 (the README's own C7.3, line 1875: "e mod2=w₃≠0 on BSO(3)"). The roots force e(E)²=0, never e(E)=0.

Repair: delete the clause after "p₁(E)=+4u on HP¹"; the body already proves e(E)=0 correctly from H³(S⁴;ℤ)=0 (line 1909). Optionally replace it by the true statement "c₃(End₀(F))=0, consistent with e(E)²=0".
