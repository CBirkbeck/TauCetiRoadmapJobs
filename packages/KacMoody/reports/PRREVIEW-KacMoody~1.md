## R02 (P2) — The invariant form of KM1 is not nondegenerate, and not complement-independent, on the non-minimal realizations KM0 admits

Location: `README.md` line 34 ("Choose a complement `H''` … put `(h_i,h)=α_i(h)/d_i` for all `h∈H` and `(h',h'')=0` … it is nondegenerate on the whole `H` for every realization of KM0, including singular `A`" and "a different `H''` gives an isometric realization through an explicit map fixing every `h_i` and `α_i`").

Claim: Kac's recipe (Kac, §2.1, Lemma 2.1) gives a nondegenerate form on every realization of KM0, unique up to an isometry fixing the simple roots and coroots.

Witness: KM0 (line 24) admits "specified finite-dimensional central enlargements" and `RationalRealization A m` (Suggested.lean line 31) admits every `m`. Take `A = (2)` (type A1), `d = 1`, `H = ℚ²`, `h = (1,0)`, `α = 2x₁`, `z = (0,1)`. With `H'' = span(z)`: Gram matrix of `(h, z)` is `[[2,0],[0,0]]`, `z` is in the radical. With `H'' = span(z+h)`: Gram matrix of `(h, z+h)` is `[[2,2],[2,0]]`, determinant `−4`, nondegenerate. The two complements give non-isometric forms, so both sentences fail. With `H = ℚ³` and the same datum, every complement `H''` has dimension 2, the kernel of `α` on it is nonzero, and that kernel is in the radical of the form for every choice of complement: no complement makes the recipe nondegenerate once `dim H > 2|I|`. Kac's Lemma 2.1 is stated for the minimal realization (`dim H = 2|I| − rank A`), where the affine A1 computation of line 34 is right: Gram matrix of `h_0, h_1, d` is `[[2,−2,1],[−2,2,0],[1,0,0]]` with determinant `−2`, and I checked that a second complement `d' = d + a h_0 + b h_1` is carried to the first by `d ↦ d − (a + (a−b)²)c`, which fixes `h_0, h_1, α_0, α_1`. The defect propagates: `ν : H → H*` (line 34), the transported form `(α_i,λ) = d_i λ(h_i)` on `H*`, and the toral factor `v^((μ,ν))` and `v^(−2(ρ,μ))` of the KM5 Casimir (line 78) all need `ν` to be an isomorphism.

Repair: state Theorem 2.2, nondegeneracy, `ν` and the change-of-complement isometry for minimal realizations, which is Kac's hypothesis, and add the two-dimensional A1 example above as a negative-control Check. For a specified central enlargement, either require as data a symmetric form on the enlargement that is nondegenerate on `H`, satisfies `(h_i,h) = α_i(h)/d_i` and is no longer zero on `H''`, with Theorem 2.2's extension stated under that hypothesis, or keep the form target on the minimal realization only and make KM5's Casimir consume the minimal one.
