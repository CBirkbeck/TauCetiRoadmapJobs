## R01 (P3) — The contract states a sign convention for `IsExactLagrangianMap` that the pinned predicate does not carry and that the new Liouville statements contradict

**Location.** `README.md` line 58 ("`IsExactLagrangianMap` (a Lagrangian map with a specified primitive, in the convention `σ=−dθ`)"), against lines 648 (FK4.2: "compact branes exact for its primitive in the sense of `IsExactLagrangianMap`", the primitive being the completion's `α` with `dα=ω`), 898 (FK9.0: "exact Lagrangian for the completion's primitive in the sense of `IsExactLagrangianMap`") and 58 itself ("in the convention `dα=ω`, `ι_Zω=α`, so the Liouville primitive of `(T*L,ω_can)` is `−λ_can`").

**Claim.** The pinned exact-Lagrangian predicate is tied to the convention `σ=−dθ`, and the Liouville exact data use it for the primitive `α` of the completion, `dα=ω`.

**Witness.** At the pin, `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.IsExactLagrangianMap` (`TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Exact.lean` line 89) has three fields: `IsLagrangianMap`, smoothness of `h`, and `f^*θ=dh`; no field relates `θ` to `σ`, and its docstring only says it is "meant for" `σ=−dθ`. So `hW.isNondegenerate.IsExactLagrangianMap J α f h` with `dα=ω` in `liouvilleExact_intersection_bound` is the right, satisfiable hypothesis (the zero section of `T*S¹` with `α=−p dq`, `h=0`, satisfies it; the fibre circle `p=1` fails it, `∫(−dq)=−2π≠0`), and the Lean docstring (lines 140–141) says exactly this: the predicate is indifferent to the sign of the primitive. The README's contract says the opposite, and a reader of the README alone sees FK4.2 and FK9.0 apply a predicate "in the convention `σ=−dθ`" to a primitive with `dα=ω`.

**Repair.** At line 58 replace "(a Lagrangian map with a specified primitive, in the convention `σ=−dθ`)" by "(a Lagrangian map with a smooth function `h` and `f^*θ=dh`; the predicate imposes no equation between `θ` and `σ`, so it serves both the cotangent convention `σ=−dθ` and the Liouville convention `dα=ω` of FK4.2 and FK9.0)". Tested: the three fields of the pinned structure are as quoted; the two instances above compute as stated.
