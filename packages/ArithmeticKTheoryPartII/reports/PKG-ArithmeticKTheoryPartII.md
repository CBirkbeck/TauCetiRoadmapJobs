## Mathematical corrections and unsupported assertions

1. The design misread the CM lift equation in FGV line 1617. It equates the subscripts τc and cτ and the conjugate embedding c∘w_τ. It does not assert w_τ∘c=c∘w_τ on the Hilbert field. Section 5.1 now uses conjugation-paired lifts only. For a minus class a of order 3, a c a⁻¹=a²c differs from c, so a simultaneous centralizer requirement would exclude genuine lifts.
2. The accepted universal-relative-extension Check asserted that any nonzero universal kernel prevents a global splitting. This is false for the category with a chosen subgroup section. In the sign module modulo 5 with G=C₂,H=1, the universal relative kernel is ℤ/5 and c(m,t)=(−m,t+m), but (m,2m) is a global G-section. Section 5.4 restricts the argument to an initial object in the unsectioned category, or to a case where forgetting the chosen section is an equivalence. The arithmetic applications satisfy that condition.
3. The transfer-descent proof route conflated two spectral sequences. Section 0.4 now uses the compact-support cohomological Hochschild–Serre edge after Poitou–Tate; its outside terms vanish because H⁰_c is the kernel of an injective global/local root restriction, as in FGV lines 685–704. The homological relative edge is used separately for Artin–Bott and local-pair comparisons.
4. Finite v₁ self-maps may be replaced by common iterates for tower compatibility. The formula 2pⁿ⁻¹(p−1) is not imposed as the exact degree of every compatible choice.
5. The section-forgetting Hom/Ext argument is stated for Λ-projective coefficients, and its applications use finite-free coefficients. No unrecorded projectivity requirement is inferred from a bar presentation alone. The Hodge pairing in §8.4 explicitly distinguishes odd m (a normalized Bott lift pairs to 1) from even m (the negative ku summand and the pairing on KSp vanish). The partition bound is written as n_j<p for every part, so an empty partition needs no undefined maximum.
6. All 19 accepted source corrections are carried through the relevant sections. In particular the hyperbolic composite is 2, not 1; the kernel inclusion is (1/2)H; the positive native compatible structure is −J_H; raw odd dual-Hodge characters pair with −c_H; inverse actions in the native bar differential are explicitly compared; fixing H_q kills a p-primary ideal class, not necessarily its full ideal class; the c-fixed prime is chosen in H∞ before arbitrary extension to ℚ(p).
7. The README does not claim the source’s Hurewicz endpoint 2p−2 or the characteristic-number boundary p=max n_j. Original gaps G2 and G3 remain in the unchanged design data. Its theorem uses 1≤i≤2p−3, integral finite type and p>max n_j. G1 is represented concretely by the graded Moore realization supplier gap. These are mathematical scope limits, not missing Lean substitutes.

No notion was imported from a later roadmap. The search found a change of ownership at the earlier BorelRegulators design: its Scope and ownership explicitly excludes the symplectic R17 request. Consequently symplectic integral stability and rational polynomial cohomology are targets of §§8.4–8.5 here, as their accepted target statements already require. ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces supplies finite arithmetic quotient/Borel–Serre models, not the symplectic stabilization theorem. No contract is assigned to the later CM Part II.
