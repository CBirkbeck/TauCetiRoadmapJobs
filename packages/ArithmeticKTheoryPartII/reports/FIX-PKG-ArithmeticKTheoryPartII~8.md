**Why the repair differs from the suggested text.**

1. **"Compatible with the Pontryagin product of an H-space and with cap products" is dropped.** It states no law, and nothing in Layer 8 needs it.
   - Naturality of β_r in maps is enough for "H-maps preserve D". That fact also uses naturality of the cross product and `singularHomologyMap_eq_of_homotopic`.
   - The image of β_r is the kernel of p^r on H_(j−1)(X;ℤ_p), by the coefficient long exact sequence. So it is p^r-torsion, and every ℤ_p-valued pairing kills it automatically.
   - In §8.6 the cap products have integral coefficients only.
2. **Finite generation now restricts only the Tor statement.** The Bockstein itself exists for every space.
3. **"β_d(xy)" is made precise.** When d<r, u is not p^d-torsion, so no mod-p^d class has Bockstein u. The lift must satisfy β_d(x)=p^(r−d)u.
   - Proof on Moore complexes ℤ_p --p^r→ ℤ_p (e↦p^r f) and ℤ_p --p^s→ ℤ_p (e′↦p^s f′), with r≤s: H_(a+b+1) is ℤ/p^r, generated by z=p^(s−r)e⊗f′−(−1)^a f⊗e′.
   - β_r(e⊗e′) = ∂(e⊗e′)/p^r = −(−1)^a z.
   - Changing x by a reduced integral class ρ(w) changes the result by ±w×β_d(y). That lies in the image of the Künneth map, which the Tor map kills.
4. **The names go where they are used.** Cap product and fundamental class go into the §8.6 *Needs* line, not §§8.1–8.2, because only §8.6 uses them. The H-space product is stated as the cross product followed by k⊗k≅k and μ_*. `TopCat.pontryaginMul_map_map` identifies this with `pontryaginMul` on monoid objects.

**Same pattern elsewhere, also repaired.**

- Scope paragraph (README line 17): the pinned products are now listed among what is already in the library.
- §8.1 proof route: the H-space product is now formed from the cross product.
- New §8.2 Check, K(ℤ/p²,1)×K(ℤ/p,1): d=1, β₁(x)=pu. Taking d=max(r,s) would predict a group of order p².
- Two docstring bullets of `Suggested.lean` said that a "Pontryagin-homology" carrier and a "cap-product realization" were missing. They now name what is actually missing: the homology Bockstein (and so Qᶦⁿᵗ), spectra, ppav schemes, and Hodge Chern characters.
