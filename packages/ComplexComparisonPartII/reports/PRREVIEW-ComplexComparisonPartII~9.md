## R02 (P3) — "Pullback and restriction of the charts" is attributed to B1.5, which states pullback and restriction of the connection

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ComplexComparisonPartII/README.md:706–707` ("fibrewise diffeomorphisms between the fibres of a component, and pullback and restriction of the charts"), `:3006–3007` ("with pullback and restriction of the charts"), `:3029–3030` (*Needs:* "B1.5 (the proper-submersion trivialization, its componentwise model fibre, product charts, pullback and restriction)"), `:143` ("with product charts, pullback and restriction").

**Claim:** SmoothBundlesConnections B1.5 supplies the pullback and restriction of the bundle charts of a proper submersion.

**Witness:** `TauCetiRoadmap/SmoothBundlesConnections/README.md:498–523` on main: B1.5 defines Ehresmann connections, "develop[s] pullback, restriction and local horizontal lifts" of the connection, and its API bullets are that the horizontal and vertical projections sum to the identity and "pullback and restriction preserve that splitting and agree on overlaps" (:518–519), that horizontal lifts are unique (:520–521), and that "local transport gives smooth product charts and diffeomorphisms between fibres on each connected component" (:522–523). No bullet pulls back or restricts a chart, and transport along the radial paths of a ball in a pulled-back base is not the pullback of transport along radial paths in the original base, so the chart pullback is not a consequence of the connection pullback either. The pulled-back charts are what C5.2's own base-change clause constructs (README:3022–3028: "Pull back each chart … the pulled-back charts are the pullbacks of B1.5's charts, with the identity and composition comparisons"), on Mathlib's `Bundle.Trivialization.pullback` (`Mathlib/Topology/FiberBundle/Constructions.lean:305`) and `FiberBundle.pullback` (`:345`).

**Repair:** At 706–707 and 3006–3007 write "pullback and restriction of the Ehresmann connection and its horizontal–vertical splitting" for what B1.5 supplies; at 3029–3030 and 143 write "product charts; pullback and restriction of the connection"; leave the base-change clause at 3022–3028 as C5.2's own, adding `Bundle.Trivialization.pullback` to its *Needs:* line. No statement changes.
