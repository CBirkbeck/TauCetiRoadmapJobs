## R01 (P3) — The Riesz special case of `schwartzFiniteness` holds only for the complex-scalar case of the Lean statement

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ComplexComparisonPartII/README.md:333–335` (“the two Riesz theorems are its Banach case `g = id`, `h = −K`”) and `README.md:535–536` (“This is the case `g = id`, `h = −K` of `schwartzFiniteness`”).

**Claim:** `IsCompactOperator.isClosed_range_one_sub` and `IsCompactOperator.finiteDimensional_quotient_range_one_sub`, stated for a Banach space over `ℝ` or `ℂ`, are a special case of `schwartzFiniteness`.

**Repair:** Write “its complex Banach case `g = id`, `h = −K`” at both places (or “the `𝕜 = ℂ` instance of the Riesz theorems”). No statement changes.
