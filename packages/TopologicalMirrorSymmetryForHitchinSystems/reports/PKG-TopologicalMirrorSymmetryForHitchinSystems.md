## Corrections and ownership

- Corrected the pin inventory: line-bundle classes have inverses and a commutative-group instance, not merely a monoid. `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/LineBundle/Class.lean:50` defines `LineBundleClass X := Skeleton (InvertibleSheaf X)`, line 87 constructs dual inverse, line 104 gives `inv_mk`, line 142 constructs the commutative group. This removes the design’s supposed missing Picard inverse.
- Imported the actual pointed-family Picard functor and its distinguished point from `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/PicardFunctor/Rigidified.lean:66` and `Point.lean:40`. Its inputs are a scheme map, a section and the section equation; it is a functor of rigidified line-bundle classes, not represented Picτ or an abelian dual. The splitting targets specialize this input and do not reconstruct it.
- Corrected the general nonabelian sector sum to use the stabilizer of each connected component, or the full centralizer on the invariant union. Individual components need not be preserved by the full centralizer. The S₃ test has three conjugacy sectors, not six.
- Distinguished formal Lefschetz roots from positive real roots in finite-field evaluation, reserved ℒ for the Lefschetz symbol, made N positive and stated localization at ℒ whenever negative exponents occur. The counts use q≠0, so the inverse maps to q⁻¹.
- Added explicit nonsplit-Jacobian-torsion descent to the generic orbifold supplier gap. - Replaced design-era symbolic supplier locators by the actual sections: abelian duality A2.2–A2.3 and A3.2–A3.7; moduli gerbes §§4.1–4.3, 4.9, 4.20 and rigidification §5.1; Galois recognition §§1.7–1.8 and density §§5.1–5.3; Hodge–Tate degeneration §2.15; local abelian duality/isogeny index D7.6; proper-smooth purity §7.15; Hitchin geometry §§3.6–3.14 and anisotropic finite-stabilizer weighted traces §3.16.
