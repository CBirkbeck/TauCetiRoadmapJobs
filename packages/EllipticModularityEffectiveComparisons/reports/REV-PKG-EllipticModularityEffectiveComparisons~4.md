1. **F01** (P3) §4.2 names a "rational r-kernel point" that the hypotheses do not supply

   **Location.** `packages/EllipticModularityEffectiveComparisons/README.md` line 871, §4.2 proof route: "The rational r-kernel point prevents additive reduction there, since additive component-group order is at most four and r>4."

   **Claim.** The kernel of the rational r-isogeny is a Galois-stable subgroup of order r; its nonzero points are in general not rational (the roadmap's own §4.0 says so: "its generator need not be rational", README line 831). As written the sentence invokes an input the statement does not have.

   **Witness.** The curve y²=x³+2 of README line 831 has a Galois-stable subgroup of order 3 with no nonzero rational point; §4.2's hypotheses are the same shape (a stable cyclic subgroup, not a rational point). The argument is nevertheless valid in its intended form: after the totally ramified extension L that kills the ramification of the kernel character, the kernel becomes pointwise rational over the maximal unramified extension of L, and E(L^nr)[r] is trivial for additive reduction because the geometric component group has order at most 4, 𝔾_a(k̄) is q-torsion and the formal group is pro-q, while r>4 and r≠q. So additive reduction over L is excluded and potential good reduction gives good reduction over L. This is precisely the step mazur-1978 (6.1)–Proposition 6.3 (mazur-1978 lines 966–985) needs.

   **Repair.** Replace the quoted sentence by: "Over the maximal unramified extension of that totally ramified extension the kernel is pointwise rational, and its r points cannot lie on a curve with additive reduction: the geometric component group has order at most four, the kernel of reduction on the identity component is q-primary, and r>4, r≠q. Potential good reduction therefore gives good reduction over the extension." Tested on y²=x³+2 at r=3 (the counterexample to the literal wording): the repaired sentence makes no rationality claim about the generator and the stated group-order argument applies verbatim.
