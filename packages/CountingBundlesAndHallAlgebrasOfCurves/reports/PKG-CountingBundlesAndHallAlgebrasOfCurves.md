Additional scope and proof corrections are these:

- Eventual-period Fourier extraction explicitly assumes a field K, r>0 and (r:K)≠0. In characteristic2, r=2 gives 1−z²=(1−z)², so the claimed simple poles and two-root Fourier average would fail. This is more general than imposing characteristic zero globally; the universal application is characteristic zero.
- Ambient submersivity is asserted at the stable central-line points; the restricted map is smooth over the line. A zero covector over a decomposable O⊕O(1) has a nontrivial effective projector stabilizer; the whole cotangent chart cannot be the asserted submersion domain. The central deformation explicitly carries p>C. The BB theorem used by purity is its general all-characteristic affine-stratum theorem, not the large-characteristic family specialization.
- The fixed-source generation estimate is extended to every U⊆V₁ needed by the tight-pair proof. If W is its image and rank W=r′, then deg W≤d+(r−r′)d₁ and μ_min(K_U)≥−deg W−(r′+1)d₁≥−d−(r+1)d₁. The same d₂ works. This does not assert a bound for arbitrary source dimensions. ACK07 Lemma 5.5 really assumes regularity (C:4), so its adapted image/kernel argument is stated as work to prove rather than imported without hypotheses.
- The full constructible Hall direct-sum pullback first has a product-function target. Its image need not lie in an algebraic tensor product. The algebraic Hopf target is proved only for the spherical generated subalgebra. BTL12 §4 explicitly works with modules over a finite-dimensional complex algebra; its finite-length proof is not claimed already to apply to Coh(X).
- The tuple (−1,−2), q=2, is a formal reciprocal tuple, not a Weil tuple: its absolute values are 1 and2, not √2. Its rank-two rational evaluation is 6, but no curve count is asserted at it. A genuine normalization witness is y²+y=x³ over 𝔽₄: its two eigenvalues are −2,−2, its point count is9 and its cubic-extension count81.
- The parabolic Euler form is written explicitly for increasing flags: ordinary χ minus ∑ᵢ∑_{j<k}mᵢⱼnᵢₖ. On P¹ with one two-step mark, source/target line jumps1,2 give χ_par=0 and reverse jumps give1, matching flag-preserving Hom. The locator is LIN15 §1.6, Corollary 1.5, (1.11), LIN15 line 517.
- The rank-one worked example now explicitly divides the Higgs count by q^g before origin evaluation. For g=1, qP_X(1) has origin0, while the regular normalized polynomial P_X(1) has origin1. Similarly t²(1−t)² has constant coefficient0, while its quotient by t² has constant coefficient1.
- Correct the introduction's source section labels: in the published S16, the partition and residue conventions are §1.4, not §1.3. The HN-membership theorem is HN10 Theorem 3.1 and Corollary 3.5; S16's Corollary 4.3 concerns rational Eisenstein values, not spherical HN membership. Supplier section labels are resolved to their actual prose headings.
