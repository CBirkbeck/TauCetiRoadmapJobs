## Source issues

- **Error** in ECD at Lemma 7.2(iii), printed page 29: the source says global sections of set-valued sheaves commute with all finite colimits when every open cover splits; it should say global sections send epimorphisms to surjections, since on the two-point discrete space `Γ(1 ⊔ 1)` has four elements and `Γ(1) ⊔ Γ(1)` two; affects a stated result.
- **Gap** in ECD at Proposition 13.12, printed page 80: the source proves representability for a quasicompact separated diamond by applying Proposition 13.11, which needs quasiseparatedness, but separated does not imply quasiseparated (its Remark 10.8), and the quotient of `ℤ_p̲ × Spa(K₀, K₀°)` by the Frobenius-twisted translation action of `ℤ` is quasicompact and separated with non-Hausdorff `|Y|_B`; it should assume quasiseparatedness; affects the proof.
- **Error** in GLX at Lemma 3.2, printed page 16: the source says `π₀(F/K) = π₀(F)/K` for a locally profinite `K` acting on a small v-sheaf; for `ℤ` acting by translation on `ℤ_p̲ × Spa(C, O_C)` the left side is a point and the right side `ℤ_p/ℤ` is uncountable; it should assume the component-orbit space is totally disconnected; affects a stated result.
- **Error** in KL16 at Remark 8.1.4, printed page 159: the source says the structure sheaf of a seminormal rigid space equals the direct image of the structure sheaf of its resolution; for the node `K⟨u,v⟩/(uv)` the direct image is `K⟨u⟩ × K⟨v⟩`, strictly larger; it should be the equalizer of the two pullbacks to the reduced overlap, the h-descent form of its Proposition 1.4.22; affects the proof of Theorem 8.2.3.
