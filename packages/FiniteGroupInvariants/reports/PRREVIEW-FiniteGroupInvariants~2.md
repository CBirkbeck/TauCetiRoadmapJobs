## R01 (P2) — 7.5 and 7.6 use "equal zeta functions force equal Galois closures", which no layer or supplier states

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/FiniteGroupInvariants/README.md:981` ("together with the number of roots of unity, which the zeta function also determines") and `README.md:995–997` ("by 7.4 and the Galois correspondence").

**Claim:** 7.5 reads `h · R` off the residue using the number of roots of unity; 7.6 counts the fields arithmetically equivalent to `F` as Gassmann classes inside the Galois closure of `F`, both "by 7.4".

**Witness:** 7.4 is stated for two subgroups of one Galois group `Gal(L/ℚ)`. A number field `F'` with `ζ_{F'} = ζ_F` is not, by anything in Layer 7, a subfield of the Galois closure of `F`; without that, neither the roots-of-unity step (which is Perlis' theorem through Gassmann equivalence, desmit-perlis line 15 citing Perlis) nor the count in 7.6 follows from 7.4. The needed input is Bauer's theorem (equal sets of totally split primes force equal Galois closures). `roadmaps grep "Bauer's theorem"` matches nothing on main or in any open roadmap; Completed/Chebotarev Layer 10 (its lines 416–422) derives the density of split-completely primes and the non-Galois statement through a Galois closure but not Bauer's theorem; Completed/NumberFieldArithmetic 1.5 proves only that total splitting passes to the Galois closure.

**Repair:** add a target to 7.4 (before Perlis' theorem): for number fields `F`, `F'` with `ζ_F = ζ_{F'}`, the Galois closures coincide. Route: the coefficient of `p^{−s}` equals `[F : ℚ]` exactly when `p` is totally split (`LSeries_eq_iff_of_abscissaOfAbsConv_lt_top` gives equal coefficients); NumberFieldArithmetic 1.5 moves "totally split in `F`" to the Galois closure `F̃`; Chebotarev Layer 10 gives the totally split primes of a Galois `M` density `1/[M : ℚ]`, so `Spl(F̃) = Spl(F̃')` forces `[F̃ F̃' : ℚ] = [F̃ : ℚ] = [F̃' : ℚ]` and `F̃ = F̃'`. Then list it in the `*Needs:*` lines of 7.5 (for the roots-of-unity step) and 7.6, and let 7.6 say that every field arithmetically equivalent to `F` is a subfield of `F̃`. Test on the instance that refuted the route: `ℚ(⁸√−15)` and `ℚ(⁸√−240)` have one Galois closure of degree 32 (desmit-perlis lines 31–32), so the count of 7.6 is taken inside it; `ℚ(√2)` and `ℚ(√3)` have different coefficient sequences (`a_2 = 1` and `a_2 = 1`, but `a_3 = 0` and `a_3 = 1`), so no Galois-closure statement is asserted for them.
