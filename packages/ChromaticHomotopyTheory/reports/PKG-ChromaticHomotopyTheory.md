## Corrections, source limits and supply direction

1. The connected-cover notation for the finite James stage was inconsistent with the standing convention X⟨r⟩=fib(X→τ_≤(r−1)X). The fiber of J_rS²→K(ℤ,2) kills π₂ and is X⟨3⟩ here. AKQ’s notation ⟨2⟩ counts connectedness instead; the actual fiber is unchanged. Likewise the completed S³ fiber killing π₃ is the cover indexed4 under this convention. The explicit kernel/cokernel calculation is in the README and audit table.
2. The design’s strongest routed truncation claim is correctly rejected: BP⟨n⟩→E(n) is a T(n)-equivalence, not generally an L_n^f-equivalence. At n=1 its negative rational coefficient groups are0 versusℚ. This accepted correction is retained.
3. The formal-group point adapter and elementary p-series calculations do not name this roadmap’s deep spectral objects. They remain README Checks, with no weak substitute in Lean.
4. A proposed API in the imported coefficient-theory target quantified over a bare BP-module with invertible v_n and then compared module mapping spaces over different quotient algebras. Invertibility alone does not impose the killed-coefficient actions. That API is not reproduced as a target. The supplier’s actual quotient and localization models and their own module universal properties are consumed instead.
5. Tate vanishing remains localized, finite-local blueshift lowers an upper bound and need not give exact height, p=2 and3 are retained, and the height-zero bottom uses S[1/p] on all spectra. All of the accepted review’s locality, completion, marking, discriminant and residue-field corrections are retained.
6. The direct fiberwise argument uses the pointwise Kan-extension formula and composition theorem actually stated in BordismFormalGroups F6.5. Its space-indexed specialization therefore needs no additional supplier gap. It avoids inferring convergence of an unbounded periodic Serre spectral sequence.
7. The design suggested new boundary-moduli and spectral-sheaf Parts II. Boundary geometry and the Otop sheaf now have the readable owner EllipticCohomology E1–E3. Only the generic quasicoherent spectral-module contract remains a supplier gap with EnhancedDerivedSheaves. No later boundary roadmap is cited as an input.
8. The generic spectral perfect-module K-theory needed for the BHLS witnesses occurs in the board design of the chromatic K-theory consumer. To keep a single direction of supply, it is contracted to the earlier generic K-theory owner GeneralAlgebraicKTheory, independently of purity. This is the foundational notion moved out of the consumer’s direction; no theorem of chromatic purity is used to prove its input theory. The generic cyclotomic input is contracted to RefinedTraceMethods, while the non-hyperdescent and separating witness remain in §8.3.

The accepted design’s original proof-expansion gaps remain in `plan.json`; its old process descriptions are not mathematical assumptions or README qualifications. The package writes their retained proof obligations into the corresponding route, and imports the portions with an existing owner. Only the top-level gaps list was edited. A parsed equality check confirms that every other field, including all nodes and the embedded accepted review, is unchanged.

The missing standalone RefinedTraceMethods specification is not claimed to have been verified: the supplied board directory has reports only, and `roadmaps show` finds no roadmap of that name on main or at the indexed roadmap heads. Its required trace input is explicitly a gap, not an assertion that its named RT.2–RT.3 already proves it. No files of an unspecified board branch were fetched. No later roadmap is an input; consumer names occur only in the downstream paragraph.

The exact KU field calculation is not attributed to Clausen–Mathew Remark2.8: that remark gives nonzero lower-height Tate, not the full oriented computation. The added greenlees-sadofsky line343 states the cyclic inverse Thom-tower comparison; its Corollary1.4 at line180, proved at line413, gives rationality for integral Morava theory. The added hesselholt-ku line1658 is Jonas McCandless’s explicit uncompleted KU calculation on p.844 of the 2018 Oberwolfach report. §9.5 proves the completed comparison through the same oriented tower, and does not infer it from a claim that Tate commutes with completion. The p=2 and3 algebra is computed explicitly. No coefficient quotient alone is claimed to prove the spectral comparison.
