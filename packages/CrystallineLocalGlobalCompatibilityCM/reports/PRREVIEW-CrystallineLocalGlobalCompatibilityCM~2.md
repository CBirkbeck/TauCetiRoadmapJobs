## R01 (P2) — Specify the compact integral preimages in the PGL₂ comparison

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/CrystallineLocalGlobalCompatibilityCM/README.md:1566`, “the inverse images K_G and K′_G in GL₂ of K and K′”.

**Claim:** These inverse images are used as GL₂ levels admitting neat finite-index refinements, so the ambient group must be specified as GL₂(Ô_F). The full inverse images under GL₂(𝔸_F,f) → PGL₂(𝔸_F,f) are not compact. The compactness assumption of ALS.6.1 and of the cited AKT argument therefore does not follow from the construction as written. This is a new defect in the repair, not the previous review's missing arithmetic ownership.

**Witness:** At K_v=PGL₂(Z₃), every 3^r I₂ lies in the full preimage and v₃(det)=2r. This infinite valuation image precludes compactness. CN line 4209 takes preimages inside GL₂(Ô_F); ALS.6.1 requires compact open levels.

**Exact repair:** Define K_G and K′_G as the preimages under GL₂(Ô_F) → PGL₂(Ô_F), with V pulled back along this map. State compact openness, normality of K′_G in K_G and K_G/K′_G ≅ K/K′ before applying the finite-cover argument. On the test K_v=PGL₂(Z₃), this gives GL₂(Z₃), whose determinant valuation is identically zero; the extraneous scalars 3^r I₂ for r≠0 disappear. The central units remain, as required by AKT Corollary 5.5. Retain the full PGL₂ endpoint and the uniform bound.
