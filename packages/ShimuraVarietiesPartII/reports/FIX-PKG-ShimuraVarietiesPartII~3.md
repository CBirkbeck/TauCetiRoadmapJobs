**Where the repair departs from the review, and why.**
- *Centralizer-dimension gap deleted rather than kept.* ReductiveGroupsPartIII §0.7 already proves that two semisimple F̄-points have the same image in Conj′(H) exactly when some element of Aut′(H)(F̄) carries one to the other. Suppose φ∈Aut′(G)(Ω) satisfies φ(x)=y. Then φ maps Z_G(x) isomorphically onto Z_G(y), so the two centralizers have the same dimension. Take Ω algebraically closed and containing both coefficient fields; §0.7 says that formation of Conj′ commutes with field extension. This one-line argument is now written into the proof sketch of §4.13, which cites §0.7 directly. Keeping a gap for this corollary would invent a supplier target. The step being replaced is HT line 1103–1104, the dimension step of the proof of Corollary 2.1.9.
- *The stable-centralizer gap is restated, not only renumbered.* The old wording was "for a semisimple γ∈G(Q)… compatibly with base change to Q_ℓ and Q_p". §4.9 does not apply it to elements of G(Q). It applies it to the realizations γ_(x,ℓ)∈G(Q_ℓ) and to the crystalline norm over W(F_(q^m))[1/p]. So the obligation is now stated over any characteristic-zero field F. Let S be the closure of the group generated by γ and e the index [S:S°]. For every n divisible by e, the closure of the group generated by γ^n is the torus S°, and Z_G(γ^n)=Z_G(S°) is connected reductive and does not depend on n. §3.5 itself is stated over a number field and only gives connectedness in the neat case.
- *Stabilizer-immersion gap placed beside §5.3a, not in Layer 2.* §5.3a already proves Fix_H(x)=H(K)∩Fix_H′(x′) for a closed immersion with identical derived groups. A closed immersion of the stabilizer schemes gives exactly this equality on integral points when K̃=K. The R-smooth-tori, R-smooth-centralizer and compact-quotient obligations are described as new Layer 2 targets, as the review asks.
