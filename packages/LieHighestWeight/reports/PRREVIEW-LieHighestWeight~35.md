## R02 (P3) — §7.5: the cited Weyl-orbit theorem does not carry simple-reflection invariance to every root

- **Location:** `README.md:1045–1049`: "the dimensions of the graded pieces are invariant under the simple reflections … since every root is a Weyl translate of a simple root (native `TauCeti.exists_mem_support_weylGroupToPerm_eq`) … every root piece is at most a line".
- **Claim:**
  - The route proves that the dimensions of the graded pieces are invariant only under the simple reflections `sᵢ`.
  - The cited theorem gives some `w : P.weylGroup`. That group is generated by all root reflections, so `w` need not be a word in the `sᵢ`.
  - The missing input is that every root is reached from a simple root through simple reflections. This input is pinned, but it is not named. The same gap is in the Layer 7 entry of *From Tau Ceti* (`README.md:389–393`).
  - This gap came in with the repair of R04.
- **Witness:**

  - Etingof's Theorem 24.2(iv) uses "a composition of simple reflections" (EtingofLieGroupsLieAlgebras line 4896).
- **Repair:**
  - Write instead: "since every positive root is reached from a simple root by simple reflections of decreasing height (Mathlib `RootPairing.Base.IsPos.induction_on_reflect`, `LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Base.lean:782`; equivalently, the native theorem with `TauCeti.weylGroup_eq_closure_simple`, `LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimpleReflections.lean:121`)".
  - Add the declaration you use to §7.5's *Needs*, to Layer 7's Dependencies and to the contract entry at `README.md:389–393`.
  - Tested on `G₂` (`α₁` short):
    - `θ = 3α₁+2α₂` goes by `s₂` to `3α₁+α₂` (pairing `1`), then by `s₁` to `α₂` (pairing `3`), so `dim g_θ = dim g_{α₂} = 1`.
