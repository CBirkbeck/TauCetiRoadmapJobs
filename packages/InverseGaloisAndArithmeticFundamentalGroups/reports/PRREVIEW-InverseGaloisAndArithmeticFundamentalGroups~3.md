## R02 (P2) — The supersolvable Grunwald theorem is statable at the pins but absent from `Suggested.lean`

Location: `README.md` line 4600 (*Supersolvable Grunwald prescriptions*), `Suggested.lean` lines 28–31 ("Brauer–Manin density, local prescriptions and Property E require ... the system of local Galois embeddings ...; these structures are not available at the pins"). Claim: the local-prescription headline cannot be stated. Witness: the README's own statement is a field statement, "a Galois extension `L/k` with group `G` such that for every `v ∈ S`, `L ⊗_k k_v` is a product of copies of `L_v`", which needs no system of embeddings. At the pins the carriers exist: `IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum.adicCompletion` (`Mathlib/RingTheory/DedekindDomain/AdicValuation.lean`) for the completions at finite places, `NumberField.InfinitePlace.Completion` (`Mathlib/NumberTheory/NumberField/InfiniteAdeleRing.lean` and the `Completion/` directory) for archimedean places, `IsGalois`, `AlgEquiv`, `TensorProduct`, and the residue-characteristic condition through the residue field of `v`. This is the deepest realization theorem of Layer IG.4 (Harpaz–Wittenberg, Théorème B and its corollary, harpaz-wittenberg lines 137–149) and the roadmap already states its weaker relative `exists_realizations_coprime_degrees`. Repair: state it, for a finite supersolvable `G`, a number field `K`, a finite set `S` of places none of whose finite members has residue characteristic dividing `Nat.card G`, and for each `v ∈ S` a field `L_v` Galois over the completion with an injective homomorphism `(L_v ≃ₐ L_v) →* G`, the conclusion being a finite Galois `L/K` with `Nonempty ((L ≃ₐ[K] L) ≃* G)` and, for each `v`, a completion-algebra isomorphism of `L ⊗[K] K_v` with a finite power of `L_v`. If the author judges the archimedean completions too awkward, the docstring must name that carrier as the missing one; the present reason (local embeddings) does not apply to the field form.

## Source issues

- **Error** in hops at Theorem 3.6 sketch, PDF p. 11 (line 457): the source says that pulling back by the `m`-th power map multiplies `d = dim H¹(π₁(𝔸¹_k), H)` by `m`; it should be `d′ = m·d + (m−1)·dim H`, since `d = Sw_∞(H) − dim H` and Swan conductors scale by `m` (for `S₃` in characteristic 2 one gets `d = 0`, `d′ = 2`); affects the proof (the strictness argument still goes through with the corrected formula).
- **Misprint** in hops at §3.2.1, PDF p. 9 (line 380): the source says the étale `ℤ/p`-covers of `𝔸¹_k` are given by `y^p − y = x^{−n}`; it should be `x^{n}` (`x^{−n}` has a pole at `x = 0`, so the cover is not étale over `𝔸¹`); affects nothing.
