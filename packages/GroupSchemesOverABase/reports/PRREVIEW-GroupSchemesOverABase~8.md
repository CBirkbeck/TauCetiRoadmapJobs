## R02 (P3) — 5.3, non-affine route for `ρ_n` and `ρ_u`: the generic-fibre comparison is not justified

**Location.** README lines 946–949: "prolong the prime-to-`p` torsion subgroups of a split maximal torus of the special fibre to finite étale subgroups … (3.1 …), take their centralizers (3.6 …), and compare them with the centralizer of the torus by the density of torsion (3.3)".

**Claim.** This proves `ρ_n(s) ≥ ρ_n(s′)` for every `G` of Layer 5, affine or not.

**Witness.** With `H_n ≅ μ_n^r` the lift of `T_s[n]` over the complete base, 3.6 gives `Z_G(H_n)` smooth over `S`, so `dim Z_{G_s}(H_{n,s}) = dim Z_{G_η}(H_{n,η})`, and 3.3 gives `Z_{G_s}(H_{n,s}) = Z_{G_s}(T_s)` for `n` large, of dimension `ρ_n(s)`. The inequality then needs `dim Z_{G_η}(H_{n,η}) ≥ ρ_n(η) = dim Z_{G_η}(T_max)`, which holds when `H_{n,η}` lies in a maximal torus of `G_η` and is not automatic for a finite multiplicative-type subgroup: in `PGL₂` over `ℚ̄` the Klein four-group `μ₂ × μ₂` (images of `diag(1, −1)` and `[[0, 1], [1, 0]]`) has centralizer equal to itself, of dimension `0 < 1 = ρ_n`. The route does not say why the lifted `H_{n,η}` lie in a torus (the affine proof, SGA 3 XII 1.7, lifts the torus itself through `Hom(𝔾_m^r, G)`, which is 3.4 and needs `G` affine; the source for the non-affine statement, sga3-10 lines 1478–1489, states it without proof, and the editors' note (69) asks for a reference).

**Repair.** Either name the input: choose the lifts compatibly, `H_n ⊆ H_{nm}`, by SGA 3 XI 2.2 (sga3-11 lines 290–294, formal smoothness of `Hom(H₂, G) → Hom(H₁, G)` for `G` smooth, no affineness), and add the statement that the closure of `⋃ H_{n,η}` in `G_η` is a commutative subgroup whose prime-to-`p` torsion lies in a maximal torus of `G_η`, with a locator or a proof sketch; or state the `ρ_n`, `ρ_u` inequalities of 5.3 for `G` affine only (where they are 5.4, SGA 3 XII 1.7 a)) and move the non-affine case to the Research context next to `d_s`. The `ρ_ab` and `ρ_r + ρ_ab` inequalities, through the Chevalley quotient and A4.1, are not affected.

Line 654: "form a closed subgroup scheme of `G` (SGA 3, Exp. VIII, 6.4 and 6.5 e)), of finite presentation since `H` is finite locally free and `G` of finite presentation".

## R04 (P3) — 3.6 and 5.2 are narrower than their sources
