## Source issues

- **Error** in EVW (Annals 183) at proof of Lemma 8.4, (8.4.1), printed p. 772: the source says a fixed `f ∈ Sur(X,A)` has at least `ℓ−1` surjective lifts through a fixed `π : A′ ↠ A`; it should be the aggregate bound `|Sur(X,A′)| ≥ (ℓ−1)|Sur(X,A)|` for a suitable `A′`, since for `X=A′=ℤ/9×ℤ/3`, `A=(ℤ/3)²`, 36 of the 48 surjections have no lift; affects the proof.
- **Gap** in EVW (Annals 183) at §8.5, printed p. 774: the source says the distribution statement is equivalent to the moment statement (8.5.1); it should say the moment statement implies the distribution statement, the converse needing uniform integrability (mixtures `(1−ℓ^{−n})μ + ℓ^{−n}δ_{(ℤ/ℓ)^n}` converge in total variation with `ℤ/ℓ`-moment `→ 2`); affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in Majumder (arXiv 1407.3066v1) at the definition of `M_i`, p. 6: the source says the rows of the `i`-th strip satisfy `μ₁+⋯+μ_{i−1} < x < μ₁+⋯+μ_i`; it should be `≤` on the right; affects nothing.
- **Error** in Koymans–Pagano, *Higher Rédei reciprocity* (arXiv 2005.14157v2) at Appendix A.1, (A.7), p. 48: the source says `P(x∈ker T) = Σ_i P(x∈ker T | A_{i,x})P(A_{i,x})` with `P(x∈ker T | A_{i,x}) = 1/(2^{i+1}−1)`; it should note that the `A_{i,x}` cover only `{U(x)=0}` and that `T, U` are independent in the stated space (at `n=1` the two sides are `1/3` and `1/6`), so (A.7) does not yield (A.2), which is nevertheless true (checked exactly for `m ≤ 11`); affects the proof.
