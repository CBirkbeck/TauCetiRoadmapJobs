## R01 (P2) — The Verdier-quotient `K₀` sequence is stated without thickness, where it fails

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/HopfologicalAlgebra/README.md:145–147`, “the right-exact sequence `K₀(S) → K₀(T) → K₀(T/S) → 0` for the raw Verdier quotient `T/S` (no idempotent completion) by a triangulated subcategory `S`, its first image the subgroup generated by the classes of `S`”.

**Claim:** for every triangulated subcategory `S` of a triangulated category `T`, `K₀(T/S) ≅ K₀(T)/⟨[S]⟩`.

**Witness:** `T = Dᵇ(k-mod)`, `S` the full subcategory of objects with even Euler characteristic. `S` contains `0`, is stable under shifts and cones (the Euler characteristic is additive on triangles and changes sign under shift), so it is triangulated at the pin (`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Triangulated/Subcategory.lean:177`, `IsTriangulated` has no retract-closure field, and `trW` at line 458 needs none). It is not thick: `k ⊕ k ∈ S`, `k ∉ S`. Every object of `T` is a retract of an object of `S`, so the kernel of `T → T/S` is all of `T` and `K₀(T/S) = 0`, while `K₀(T)/⟨[S]⟩ = ℤ/2ℤ`. The sequence is not exact at `K₀(T)`. The correct kernel is the subgroup generated by the classes of the thick closure of `S`; for thick `S` it is `⟨[S]⟩`. The consumer in §8.2 (`README.md:736–738`) applies the sequence to `I`, which Laugwitz–Qi prove thick (Theorem 5.5; the containment is “idempotent complete” at `laugwitz-qi` line 1340), so the `K₀(O_n)` computation stands.

**Repair:** write “by a thick subcategory `S`” in the DGAInfinity contract (`README.md:146`) and in the §8.2 route say that the sequence is applied to the thick ideal `I` of Laugwitz–Qi, Theorem 5.5. On the instance above the repaired statement no longer applies, and on `n = 4`, `t = 1`, `I_1` the projectives, it gives `K₀(O₄) = ℤ[ν,ν⁻¹]/(1 + ν²)` as the Check at `README.md:757` states. (DGAInfinity's own bullet, `TauCetiRoadmap/DGAInfinity/README.md:505–508` on main, also states the sequence for a full DG subcategory `B` without thickness; that is the supplier's defect to repair and is noted here for its owner, but this roadmap's contract must carry the hypothesis it needs.)

## R02 (P3) — Qi's Lemma 2.3 is cited for all four projectivity statements but states three

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/HopfologicalAlgebra/README.md:285–287`, “`M ⊗ P`, `P ⊗ M`, `Hom_k(M, P)` and `Hom_k(P, M)` are projective when `P` is projective (Khovanov, Proposition 2, for the tensor products; Qi, Lemma 2.3, for all four)”.

**Claim:** Qi, Lemma 2.3 covers all four modules and Khovanov, Proposition 2 covers both tensor products.

**Witness:** Qi's Lemma 2.3 (`qi` lines 263–264) lists `M ⊗ N`, `Hom_k(M,N)` and `Hom_k(N,M)` for `N` projective: three of the four, with the projective on the right of the tensor. Khovanov's Proposition 2 (`khovanov` lines 78–80) states `H ⊗ M` free and `P ⊗ M` projective: only the projective on the left. Together the two sources cover all four; neither covers all four alone.

**Repair:** “(Khovanov, Proposition 2, for `P ⊗ M`; Qi, Lemma 2.3, for `M ⊗ P` and the two Hom spaces)”.

## R03 (P3) — The DGAInfinity contract is consumed at a generality its Layer 7 does not state

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/HopfologicalAlgebra/README.md:144–147` and `736–738`, the sequence consumed for “the raw Verdier quotient `T/S`” of a triangulated category, applied to `H_n-gmod`.

**Claim:** DGAInfinity, Layer 7 states the sequence for an arbitrary triangulated category and triangulated subcategory.

**Witness:** DGAInfinity, Layer 7 on main (`TauCetiRoadmap/DGAInfinity/README.md:490–508`) is written for a small DG category `C` and a full DG subcategory `B`; its Verdier quotient is `H⁰` of the DG quotient (`README.md:502–504`), and the `K₀` bullet (`505–508`) is stated for that `C/B`. `H_n-gmod` is the stable category of a Frobenius category and is not presented as `H⁰` of a DG category anywhere in this roadmap. The proof of the sequence is purely triangulated (objects of `T/S` are objects of `T`; every distinguished triangle of `T/S` is isomorphic to the image of one of `T`; a morphism becomes invertible exactly when its cone lies in `S`, for `S` thick), so the statement does hold at the generality used, but the contract should say so.

**Repair:** either state in the contract that DGAInfinity's sequence is consumed for the abstract raw Verdier quotient of a triangulated category by a thick subcategory, its proof being the same, or state that sequence as a target of §6.1 with its route (as above) and a locator (for instance Schlichting, *Negative K-theory of derived categories*, Math. Z. 253 (2006), §3.1, which Laugwitz–Qi cite at `laugwitz-qi` line 1346), keeping DGAInfinity, Layer 7 for the DG comparison. Combine with R01.
