## R01 (P2) — The Verdier-quotient `K₀` sequence is contracted without thickness, where it fails

Unrepaired since the previous review (its R01); same witness, re-verified at the pin.

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/HopfologicalAlgebra/README.md:145–147`, “the right-exact sequence `K₀(S) → K₀(T) → K₀(T/S) → 0` for the raw Verdier quotient `T/S` (no idempotent completion) by a triangulated subcategory `S`, its first image the subgroup generated by the classes of `S`”.

**Claim:** for every triangulated subcategory `S` of a triangulated `T`, `K₀(T/S) ≅ K₀(T)/⟨[S]⟩`.

**Witness:** `T = Dᵇ(k-mod)`, `S` the objects of even Euler characteristic. At the pin `ObjectProperty.IsTriangulated` (`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Triangulated/Subcategory.lean:177`) extends only `ContainsZero`, `IsStableUnderShift ℤ` and `IsTriangulatedClosed₂`, which `S` satisfies (`χ` is additive on triangles and changes sign under shift); `trW` (same file, line 458) is defined for any object property, so the localization exists. `S` is not thick: `k ⊕ k ∈ S`, `k ∉ S`. Every `X` is a retract of `X ⊕ X ∈ S`, so `T/S = 0` and `K₀(T/S) = 0`, while `K₀(T)/⟨[S]⟩ = ℤ/2ℤ`. The sequence is not exact at `K₀(T)`. The only consumer, §8.2 (`README.md:736–738`), applies it to `I`, which Laugwitz–Qi prove thick (Theorem 5.5, `laugwitz-qi` lines 1139–1140; the containment is idempotent complete at line 1340), so `K₀(O_n)` is unaffected.

**Repair:** write “by a thick subcategory `S`” at `README.md:146`, and in the §8.2 route say the sequence is applied to the thick ideal `I` of Laugwitz–Qi, Theorem 5.5. On the witness the repaired statement no longer applies; on `n = 4`, `t = 1`, `I₁` the projectives, it gives `K₀(O₄) = ℤ[ν,ν⁻¹]/(1 + ν²)` as the Check at `README.md:757` states.
