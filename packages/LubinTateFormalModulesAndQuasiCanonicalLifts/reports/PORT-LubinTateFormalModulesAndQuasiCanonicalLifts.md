## Draft pull request body

This roadmap builds the theory of one-dimensional formal modules over the integers of `p`-adic
local fields and its two classical arithmetic applications, Lubin–Tate theory and the canonical
and quasi-canonical lifts of Gross and Yu. Starting from Mathlib's `FormalGroup` and Tau Ceti's
formal-group homomorphisms, logarithms, `[n]`-series and evaluated points, it adds the
coefficient-ring action (formal `A`-modules), constructs the Lubin–Tate formal module of a
uniformizer by interpolation over every discrete valuation ring with finite residue field,
computes torsion, `π`-adic Tate modules and division fields, and proves the explicit reciprocity
law `Art_K(π^r u) = [u⁻¹]_f` and the norm groups `π^ℤ · U(K,m)` against Tau Ceti's arithmetic
local Artin map, whose cyclotomic case Tau Ceti already has. It then develops the deformation
theory of formal `O_K`-modules of finite height over arbitrary specified coefficient rings
(representability by `B[[t₁,…,t_(h−1)]]`), Honda's functional-equation theory with the comparison
showing that the formal group of a supersingular elliptic curve with scalar Frobenius is
Lubin–Tate, integral lattice quotients of formal modules, the classification and fields of moduli
of quasi-canonical lifts (ring class fields of degree `q₀^(s−1)(q₀+1)` and `q₀^s`, generated by a
uniformizing modulus), and the explicit tame and anticyclotomic towers of Burungale–Kobayashi–Ota
with their formal traces, which AnticyclotomicLocalUnits consumes. `Suggested.lean` states the
Lubin–Tate theorem with its formal-module structure, the interpolation lemma, the degree of the
division fields, the explicit reciprocity law and the norm groups, and the Lubin–Tate structure of
the formal group of a supersingular elliptic curve over `W(𝔽_(p²))`; its docstring names the
headlines whose carriers are absent at the pins.

## Suggested.lean: what changed and why

Nothing was moved out of the Lean file; it had no padding, no sorry'd data and no stand-ins. The
changes are supplier repairs and headlines that are statable at the pins but were missing:

1. **`FormalModule` (new structure, §0.1).** The formal `A`-module structure: `isComm`, an action
   `act : A → TauCeti.FormalGroupHom F F`, `coeff_one_act`, `act_one`, `act_mul`, and `act_add`
   (`[a + b] = F([a], [b])`, written with `MvPowerSeries.subst` as Tau Ceti writes
   `nsmulSeries_add`). It bundles data that exists at the pins (Tau Ceti's endomorphisms), and is
   needed to state the Lubin–Tate theorem with its module structure and the elliptic headline. It
   matches the contract FunctionFieldArithmetic §4.1 reads from §0.1 ("a commutative `FormalGroup R`
   with a unital ring map `a ↦ [a]_F` into its endomorphisms … the pinned `TauCeti.FormalGroupHom`").
2. **`exists_formalGroup_of_isLubinTateSeries` → `exists_formalModule_of_isLubinTateSeries`.** It
   now gives a `FormalModule O F` whose action of `π` is `f` (LT65 Theorem 1's module structure,
   previously not stated), and states "`f` is an endomorphism of `G`" with the native
   `TauCeti.FormalGroupHom G G` instead of the inline substitution equation (a supplier repair).
3. **`artinMap_apply_lubinTate_root` (new, §2.5).** The explicit reciprocity law, previously listed as
   "not statable". It is statable without evaluating power series on `K̄`: for `φ = [u⁻¹]_f`
   (characterized by the interpolation hypotheses) and any polynomial `P` with
   `P − φ ∈ (f^{∘m}) ⊂ O_K[[X]]`, every lift of `Art_K(ϖ^r u)` sends each root `t` of `f^{∘m}` to
   `P(t)`. This is exactly `[u⁻¹]_f(t)`, because evaluation at `t ∈ 𝔪_(K(t))` is a continuous ring
   map killing `f^{∘m}`; such `P` exist by Weierstrass division. It uses the same `Units.map
   (algebraMap _ _).toMonoidHom u` form as Tau Ceti's `localCyclotomicCharacter_artinMap_padic`.
4. **`exists_lubinTate_endomorphism_formalGroup` → `isLubinTateSeries_zsmulSeries_formalGroup`.** It
   now names the endomorphism, Tau Ceti's `FormalGroup.zsmulSeries` at `sp`, instead of an
   existential series, and adds the formal `O`-module structure whose action of `sp` is that series.
   This is a supplier repair and a sharper statement. It also removes a docstring line the
   signature tool misparsed as a declaration `theorem it`.
5. The module docstring was rewritten: the stated headlines, and for each non-statable headline the
   missing carrier (Tate module and `Gal(K_π/K) ≅ O_K^×`: the `G(𝔪_(K̄))` construction of §0.2;
   §3.6: the marked deformation functor of §3.1; §§4.2–4.3: `O_σ[[T]]`; §§6.3–7.2: lattice quotients
   and fields of moduli; §§8.4–9.3: quasi-canonical fields and formal traces).
