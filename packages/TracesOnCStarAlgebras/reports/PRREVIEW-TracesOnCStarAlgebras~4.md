# Review of TracesOnCStarAlgebras (pull request #2464 at 1d9b51d)

**Verdict:** needs changes

**Summary.** Changes are needed, for one reason: the opening Summary states the K-theory isomorphism of Layer Tr4 without the density hypothesis, and without it the statement is false on the roadmap's own example `C[0,1]` with `∫ f(t) t⁻¹ dt`. The layers themselves, the Lean file and every source locator sampled were recomputed and hold; the head differs from the one last reviewed only by moving two sections to the end of the README. There are two small corrections and one format note, none blocking, and one licence question for a human.

## R01 (P2) — The Summary says that the inclusions `J_p ⊆ B` induce isomorphisms on `K₀` and `K₁` with no hypothesis on `τ`; this fails for a semifinite lower semicontinuous trace that is not densely defined

A reader who stops at the overview takes away a headline theorem that is false as written; the other headlines of the same paragraph carry their hypotheses ("a semifinite lower semicontinuous trace is `τ̄ ∘ π_τ`", "every densely defined trace"), so the omission reads as a claim for every trace Layer Tr3 treats. README lines 16–17: "Layer Tr4 proves that the inclusions `J_p ⊆ B` induce isomorphisms on `K₀` and `K₁`".

Take `B = C[0,1]` and `τ(f) = ∫₀¹ f(t) t⁻¹ dt`, the Conventions' witness of a semifinite lower semicontinuous trace that is not densely defined (lines 61–62). Every `f ∈ J_p` has `f(0) = 0`, and `J_p` contains the functions vanishing near `0`, so its closure is `C₀((0,1])`. By Tr4.1 applied to that ideal, `K₀(J_p) ≅ K₀(C₀((0,1])) = 0`, while `K₀(C[0,1]) = ℤ` is generated by `[1]`; the inclusion induces `0 → ℤ`. Tr4.1 itself (line 488) has the right hypothesis, "For a densely defined lower semicontinuous trace".

The smallest change: in the Summary write "proves that, for a densely defined lower semicontinuous trace, the inclusions `J_p ⊆ B` induce isomorphisms on `K₀` and `K₁`". The preceding sentence on Layer Tr3 would also be exact with "of a semifinite lower semicontinuous trace" after "the trace ideals", which is the standing hypothesis of line 366.

## C01 — The Dixmier-trace Check of Tr4.2 names `TauCeti.dixmierTrace` for an unspecified `ω`, but additivity, hence the trace property, is supplied only for dilation-invariant `ω`

In the second Check of Tr4.2 (lines 538–542) the map built from `Tr_ω` is called "a densely defined trace". The pinned definition takes any generalized limit `ω`, and its file says that additivity on positive operators needs in addition `TauCeti.GeneralizedLimit.IsDilationInvariant` (`TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/DixmierTrace.lean`, lines 41–43, definition at line 91); NoncommutativeGeometry N4 likewise proves additivity "for a dilation-invariant ω" and only subadditivity in general. Adding "for a dilation-invariant `ω`" after "of NoncommutativeGeometry N4" makes the Check say which `ω` it is about.

## C02 — The description's Tr4 bullet has the same omission as the Summary

"`K_*(J_p) ≅ K_*(B)` through OperatorKTheory K3.5 (constant `C = 1`)" carries no hypothesis on the trace. "for a densely defined lower semicontinuous trace, `K_*(J_p) ≅ K_*(B)` …" would be right. The rest of the description agrees with the head: a new roadmap, four layers, 25 `sorry`s, the pins Lean v4.36.0-rc2, TauCeti `2fd255d`, Mathlib `1b1ccf4`, eight supplier roadmaps and seven sources.

## N01 — Thomsen's Theorem 1.1.1 is stated for semi-weights

In Tr1.2 (lines 148–149), "both for lower semicontinuous weights" fits Kustermans–Vaes, Theorem 1.6; Thomsen's Theorem 1.1.1 is for his semi-weights (subadditive, monotone, positively homogeneous and lower semicontinuous: thomsen-kms lines 156–162, 207–212), which include them. "for lower semicontinuous weights (Thomsen: semi-weights)" would be exact.
