## Draft pull request body

Build normalized p-adic regulators from algebraic K-theory and cyclotomic Iwasawa cohomology to de Rham periods. The roadmap proves the completed local K₃ lattice isomorphism for unramified étale ℚₚ-algebras at p>3, compares curve and family regulators with Bloch–Kato logarithms in good and semistable reduction, and develops Perrin–Riou interpolation, Coleman images and their integral indices. Explicit signs, Tate bases, Frobenius conventions and residue computations make the regulator interfaces usable by arithmetic consumers.
