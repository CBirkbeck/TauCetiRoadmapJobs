## Corrections and limits of support

- Corrected the native cup-product namespace to TopCat.singularCup and verified its actual coefficient, monoidal and degree binders at the pin. The design’s statement that singular cochains postdate its old pin does not describe the supplied newer pin; they are imported at that pin.
- Replaced the older source-packet revisions with the supplied manifest pins. Replaced SF.2 as a finite/adic/Artin all-purpose supplier with the verified narrower owners and exact sections.
- Retained the accepted correction that the barycentric comparison before stabilization is an injection with bounded-degree image, not a graded isomorphism. On an edge x₁ is missing at t-degree zero; on a point each fixed degree is an isomorphism. These are compatible assertions.
- Retained principal-quotient coefficient change. The infinite discrete-set counterexample to arbitrary quotient ideals is reproduced in authored mathematical form; its kernel contains (xᵢ), outside I∏R. At p=0 the preceding exterior degree is −1 with value zero; it is not natural-number 0−1=0.
- Kept the pro-object on both sides of the inverse-limit formula; the countable field and model are k₁ and X₁; cover indices retain that distinction; the comparison construction is the object deduced from finite Artin comparison.
- Retained the coefficient algebraic closure as a field for tensor products and its nonzero elements only as eigenvalue indices. The model’s filtration bound agrees on both sides of the rational splitting equation.
- Kept geometric Frobenius, q>1, the degree i+1 Tor term, generalized rather than ordinary eigenspaces, the direction Σrⱼ≥n, proper smooth without projectivity, and the distinction between connected and simply connected.
- Made the Lie-bracket sign explicit: dφ(u,v)=−φ([u,v]), so dc=ab gives [A,B]=−C. This fixes an unspecified transpose convention in the design without changing its Lie algebra up to the conventional sign transport or any weight bound.
- Corrected the two-arc circle negative control: its ordinary nerve has one edge and H¹=0; its connected-component Čech diagram has two edges and H¹ of rank 1. A genuine good cover is required for the ordinary nerve equivalence.
- Scoped the survival of γ₂ in a one-vertex example to ℤ, rather than asserting a nonzero element over the zero ring. Added a dual-number pair of algebra maps showing why γ₁ alone cannot characterize integral divided-power maps.
- Separated the genus-zero curve case: generator and relation spaces both vanish, while the nonzero single symplectic relation belongs to genus at least 1.
- Retained Morgan’s published correction: associated graded indecomposables are rigid; a general indecomposable space is not. Nothing asserts a functorial weight splitting or a conjugator homotopic to identity.
- Removed an accidental forward use of the common-filtration theorem in the eigenvalue-product proof route. The eigenvalue induction uses §6.2’s own blocks; §6.6 may then use the grading theory without an internal cycle.
