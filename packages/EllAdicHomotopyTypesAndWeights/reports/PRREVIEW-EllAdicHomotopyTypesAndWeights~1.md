# Review of EllAdicHomotopyTypesAndWeights (pull request #1644 at ce640e6)

**Verdict:** needs changes

## R01 (P1) — An arbitrary lift does not give a homogeneous relation ideal

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/EllAdicHomotopyTypesAndWeights/README.md:872`, §8.3a: “Choose a linear map V→lim_n u_n” followed by “Then the tower, V, F̂(V) and I are graded with negative Lie weights”.

**Claim:** Positivity of the model grading makes the kernel of the chosen presentation homogeneous. The chosen lift is only linear; it need not respect weights.

**Witness:** Give A,B,D weight −1, C weight −2, P,Q weight −3, with [A,B]=C, [A,C]=P, [B,C]=Q. Lift D̄ to D+C. Then [A,D]−[A,[A,B]] maps to zero; its two weight components map to P and −P.

Here all other basis brackets are zero apart from skew symmetry. This is the free class-three nilpotent Lie algebra on A,B, direct summed with the central line KD. It satisfies Jacobi; evaluating all 216 ordered basis triples gives zero. Its Chevalley–Eilenberg algebra is the finite, ordered, positive-weight 1-minimal algebra Λ(a,b,z,c,p,q), all generators of cochain degree one, with dc=−ab, dp=−ac, dq=−bc and da=db=dz=0. Its weights are 1,1,1,2,3,3. Take this algebra itself for the connected target A. H¹ has dimension three, and every weight piece is finite. Thus it satisfies §8.3a's hypotheses. Its canonical Lie tower has dimensions 3,4,6 and is then constant. The allowed lift is Ā↦A, B̄↦B, D̄↦D+C. The kernel contains the displayed sum but neither homogeneous component, so it is not graded.

**Exact repair:** Keep arbitrary linear lifts for the ungraded presentation theorem. In the graded clause choose a compatible weight-preserving lift of V and weight-stable Hirsch stages before asserting that the presentation map and its kernel respect weights. State the completed grading through its finite weight quotients. Add the displayed nonhomogeneous lift as a negative-control Check. With the corrected lift D̄↦D, [A,D] maps to 0 while [A,[A,B]] maps to P; the kernel is homogeneous, and the spurious mixed relation disappears. Morgan's proof makes precisely the missing graded choice: `morgan line 2252`, Theorem 9.4, p. 198. Its relation-duality argument at `morgan line 2270`, p. 199, does not remove that requirement.

## R02 (P1) — Own the Lie-cohomology inputs used by presentation duality

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/EllAdicHomotopyTypesAndWeights/README.md:881`, §8.3a: “At each finite stage apply the five-term inflation–restriction sequence”; the supplier list is at line 883.

**Claim:** The ungraded continuous presentation duality is proved using Chevalley–Eilenberg cohomology, a five-term transgression sequence, and vanishing of the free-Lie H² term after passage through nilpotent quotients. The listed inputs supply neither the latter two results nor the all-degree cohomology carrier.

**Witness:** `Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Cochain.lean:101` and line 156 give d₁₂ and two-cocycles, not a five-term theorem. For the two-generator free Lie algebra F, H²(F/Γ₂,K)=K, whereas H²(F,K)=0; the finite-quotient term cannot simply be discarded.

The README itself correctly acknowledges that the pinned file has no cohomology quotient. An existing earlier owner of the full complex is `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces/README.md:948`, §ALS.F.5: it constructs C^q(g,V)=Hom(Λ^q g,V) and H^q for characteristic-zero fields, agreeing with the pinned low-degree differential. It is absent from this target's contracts and Needs line. That section does not supply the five-term sequence or the continuous free-pronilpotent vanishing needed here. Searches by these objects in the pinned libraries, main roadmaps, board packages and available open-roadmap texts did not locate such a supplier. Morgan's cited argument at `morgan line 2270` treats negatively graded Lie towers; it does not state this general ungraded continuous result.

**Exact repair:** Import the full Chevalley–Eilenberg complex from ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces §ALS.F.5. Before §8.3a, add explicit owned targets for the trivial-coefficient five-term sequence of a Lie extension, with transition naturality, and for the vanishing of the relevant H² colimit of free nilpotent quotients. Equivalently, give an explicit low-degree continuous cocycle/transgression target proving the same bridge. Include these in §8.3a's Needs. Do not reconstruct the supplier's generic complex or replace the colimit claim by finite-stage vanishing. Test the bridge on F/Γ₂→F/Γ₃ contravariantly: the abelian quotient has H²=K·[ab], and inflation to the Heisenberg quotient kills it because dc=−ab. Thus the required image is zero although the first finite-stage group is one-dimensional. This repair also keeps the ungraded theorem's stated generality, instead of silently restricting it to negative weights.
