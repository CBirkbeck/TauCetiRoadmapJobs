1. **F01** (P2) §7.3's route uses a base-change law for the stable filtration of §6.2 that no target or contract states

   **Claim.** The Frobenius lift on M_Q⊗ℚ_ℓ can be chosen to preserve (M_n)⊗ℚ_ℓ for the admissible filtration (M_n) of the rational model M_Q that defines the ℚ-group G₂, so that it is a ℚ_ℓ-point of a section of G₂⊗ℚ_ℓ→𝔾_m.

   **Witness.** §6.2 only gives, for a model M over a fixed field K, *some* admissible filtration with the stable-representative property. Applied to K=ℚ_ℓ and M=M_Q⊗ℚ_ℓ it yields a filtration F′ of M_Q⊗ℚ_ℓ with no relation to (M_n)⊗ℚ_ℓ; applied to K=ℚ it yields (M_n) on M_Q, but nothing in §6.1, §6.2, §7.1 or the five AT2.4 contracts says that (M_n)⊗ℚ_ℓ keeps the stable-representative property for M_Q⊗ℚ_ℓ (gap `sullivan-scalar-extension`, `:69`, covers only full and 1-minimal *model maps*; §7.1's law `:751` covers only the equations of G₁, G₂). Instance: M_Q=Λ_ℚ(a₁)⊗Λ_ℚ(b₁), d=0, with the two-step filtration M₀=Λ(a), M₁=M. §6.2 over ℚ₇ may return the constant filtration M′₀=M, which is admissible and stable, while a section of G₂⊗ℚ₇ must preserve Λ(a)⊗ℚ₇; the Frobenius lift supplied by §6.2 over ℚ₇ need not. The source takes the compatibility for granted (weil-II lines 3909 and 3919: the filtration is chosen on the rational model, the lift "respecte une filtration comme en (5.3.1)"), so the step is a genuine proof obligation of this roadmap, and the base-change compatibility of the finite-block construction is what discharges it.

   **Repair.** (1) Add to §6.2, after its statement: "Base change: for every extension L/K of characteristic-zero fields, the filtration (M_n⊗_K L) of M⊗_K L has the same property: every automorphism of M⊗_K L is homotopic to one carrying each M_n⊗_K L onto itself. The finite Hirsch blocks and the lifting obstructions of the construction commute with scalar extension." Add to §6.2's Needs: "AlgebraicTopologyPartII §AT2.4 through gap:sullivan-scalar-extension". (2) In §7.3's proof paragraph replace "a filtration-preserving lift on M_Q⊗ℚ_ℓ" by "a lift on M_Q⊗ℚ_ℓ preserving (M_n)⊗ℚ_ℓ, which exists by the base-change law of §6.2 applied to the filtration (M_n) of M_Q chosen for §7.1".
