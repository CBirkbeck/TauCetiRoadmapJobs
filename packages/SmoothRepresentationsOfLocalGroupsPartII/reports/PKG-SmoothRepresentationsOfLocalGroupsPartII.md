## Corrections and ownership

- Gan–Savin arXiv v2 does not share the published Appendix B numbering. The published legal PDF supplies all GS23 citations (GS23 line 1306 and GS23 line 1398); the manifest retains one key with both version identifiers. The published BHV text also differs in pagination from the design’s author copy, so source lines and PDF pages resolve the numbering.
- Kato’s irreducibility criterion is Theorem 2.2, p. 935 (Kato81 line 229); Theorem 2.4 is cyclicity (Kato81 line 253). The institutional scan was read after OCR. This corrects the source locator, not the criterion.
- Aubert’s full original and 1996 erratum were legally obtained. The corrected resolution route now refers to the erratum’s exact complex and Bruhat filtration (Aub96 line 27), rather than reporting the text inaccessible. Ban02 remains independent endpoint corroboration. The all-characteristic extension is an algebraic target with exact complex Jacquet and Bruhat inputs, not an inferred analytic extension.
- The normalized principal-series sign, finite-type projector degree, zero-summand norm bound, positive-kernel radical, noncentral G₂ exception, hermitian locus, actual nonsplit companion isogeny and complete E₈ endpoint formulas from the review are retained.
- Rodier exponents require the positivity set of wα when the exponent is written wχ. In A₂ the weights s₁χ and s₂s₁χ lie in one block; the inverse convention gives different sets. The README fixes the direction and computes both values.
- Casselman asymptotics for normalized Jacquet exponents include δ_B^(1/2). The A₂ intermediate constituent has actual asymptotic factor 1 on a boundary ray, while St has q^(−2m); the invariant-form contradiction now uses those actual factors.
- Tadić extraction allows a zero limiting representation. Escaping conductor gives zero character and has no cuspidal-support point; support convergence is stated only for nonzero limits. The fixed-level extraction and Bernstein-center argument remain distinct.
- The E₈ quarter-lattice argument uses strict ν₁+ν₄<1 and ν₁≥1/4, hence ν₄<3/4. It does not assert that the quarter lattice itself is empty. The Dₙ simple-type range is n≥4, with D₃=A₃ and D₂=A₁×A₁ handled separately.
- Dominant alcoves retain chamber faces through zero, so the zero tempered point remains included. The explicit E-type tables include every boundary root vector; the B/D parity condition includes the rank-one endpoint convention.
- The purported reason that a split companion cannot embed in the original group did not prove nonexistence of an embedding. That Check now tests the defined full root datum and makes no unsupported nonembedding theorem.
