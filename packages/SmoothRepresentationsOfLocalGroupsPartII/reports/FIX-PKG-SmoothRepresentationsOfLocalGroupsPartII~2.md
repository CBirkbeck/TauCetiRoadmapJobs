## F01 (P2) — disputed

The README's claim is right. The reviewer's witness uses a character that is not unramified in the sense the supplier defines.

Evidence. SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.3.1 *unramified-characters* defines G° as the subgroup generated by all compact subgroups and Ψ(G) = Hom(G/G°, ℂ^×). Ber92 line 1281–1284 (Ch. II §2.1, Proposition 22(2)–(4), p. 39) says that G/G° is a lattice and Z(G)G° has finite index. If Z(G) is compact then Z(G) ⊆ G°, so the lattice G/G° is finite and therefore trivial. Hence Ψ(G) = 1, and the Bernstein component of a supercuspidal σ is {σ}.

Witness check on PGL₂(ℚ_p): w = [[0,1],[p,0]] has w² = p·1, which is central and so trivial in PGL₂. So ⟨w⟩ is a compact subgroup of order 2, and χ(w) = (−1)^{v(−p)} = −1. Therefore χ = (−1)^{v det} is not trivial on G° = G, and χ ∉ Ψ(G). Being trivial on PGL₂(ℤ_p) alone does not make a character unramified. When σ⊗χ ≇ σ, the twist σ⊗χ has a different inertial class, so the Bernstein-centre idempotent e kills it and (σ⊗χ)(e∗1_K) = 0. The route stands.

The reviewer's alternative ("X_nr(G) finite") would not arise under SR's definition.
