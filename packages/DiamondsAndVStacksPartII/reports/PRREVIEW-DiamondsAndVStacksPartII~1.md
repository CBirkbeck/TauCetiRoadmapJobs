## R01 (P2) — The noetherian-ring-of-definition flatness gap asks for what AdicSpacesPartII already provides

**Location.** README line 96 (`What AdicSpacesPartII must provide (the noetherian-ring-of-definition flatness contract)`), line 134 (Contract Check: "The non-Tate formal source O[[t]] is covered by the noetherian-ring-of-definition extension"), line 1161 (D10.7 proof sketch) and line 1165 (D10.7 `*Needs:*`), plus `gaps[noetherian-definition-rational-flatness]` in the board's `design/plan.json`.

**Claim.** D10.7 needs flatness of the rational localization `B[[t]] → O(U)` for `U = {|π| ≤ |t| ≠ 0} ⊂ Spa(B[[t]])` with `B` topologically of finite type over `O_E` and the `(π,t)`-adic topology; the README declares this missing from every supplier and asks AdicSpacesPartII R0.18 for a new theorem over all complete separated Huber rings with a noetherian ring of definition.

**Witness.** AdicSpacesPartII (main) R0.17, target *etale-ring-flatness* (README line 7104): for an étale morphism `s : X → Y` of affinoid adic spaces with `Y = Spa(A, A⁺)` complete Hausdorff and in Huber's standing class (clause (a): finite type over a noetherian ring of definition), `O_Y(Y) → O_X(X)` is flat (Huber96 Lemma 1.7.6). `B[[t]]` is noetherian, so it is in clause (a) with ring of definition itself. R0.16's conventions (line 6220) record that a rational embedding is étale, and line 8271 that open immersions into locally Huber-standard spaces are locally of finite presentation; so the rational embedding `U → Spa(B[[t]])` is étale and *etale-ring-flatness* applies. This is exactly AGLR's step ("rational localizations are flat for Huber pairs admitting a noetherian ring of definition", AGLR line 770). The wider class the contract asks for (noetherian type, not finitely generated over the ring of definition) is used nowhere in the README.

**Repair (tested on the instance above).** Delete the contract paragraph at line 96 and the clause of line 134 that invokes it. In D10.7 replace "(the noetherian-ring-of-definition flatness contract for the complete noetherian formal source, followed by the pinned flatness theorem on strongly noetherian Tate charts)" by "(AdicSpacesPartII R0.17 *etale-ring-flatness*, applied to the rational embedding `U → Spa(B[[t]])`, which is étale by R0.16 since `B[[t]]` is noetherian and in Huber's standing class; the pinned `flat_toCompletionLoc` covers the further rational localizations of the resulting strongly noetherian Tate charts)". In D10.7's `*Needs:*` replace the contract by `AdicSpacesPartII R0.16, AdicSpacesPartII R0.17`, and in the D10 Dependencies line (1328) drop "and R0.18 (the noetherian-ring-of-definition flatness contract)". The author retires `gap:noetherian-definition-rational-flatness` from the plan's `gaps` (the README is the only file this review can ask a repair to edit; say so in the repair report if the plan is not editable there).
