# PotentialAutomorphyDworkMotivesPartII package report

The package expands the accepted ten-stage design into `packages/PotentialAutomorphyDworkMotivesPartII/README.md` (Layers 0–9, 88 numbered targets), `Suggested.lean` (6 headline theorems, 5 finite-data definitions), `metadata.toml` (`topic = "math.NT"`, as on main) and `sources.toml` (19 works). In `design/plan.json` only the top-level `gaps` value changed: a parsed comparison against the original shows `gaps` as the sole differing key. Every design target is present. Its design numbering maps DM.k to Layer k. Seven targets were added or split: §§5.1–5.4, the group-theoretic cores of the residual monodromy theorems; §6.5, finite-field-valued roots; §7.5, recognition of the comparison character, split from the design's DM.3 Hecke-character node; and §7.6, the Hodge–Tate weights of `W`, split from the design's Hodge–Tate node. Every design Check and unit test is present as a prose Check. The 19 design constructions keep their API lists, written as prose bullets with no invented Lean names. Each has at least three Checks, and every target has at least three. All ten layers end with `### Examples` and `### Dependencies`.

The design's `review.json` and its `reports/` folder did not exist in the package. The acceptance record is inside `design/plan.json` (`review`), and its per-node correction notes were applied. Every node is either reproduced in its corrected form or further corrected below.

## Corrections to the design, and what the README could not support

1. **The pins.** The manifest pins are Mathlib 6b7abb3c and Tau Ceti a91d3aaf. The design was checked at Mathlib 082e2d37 and Tau Ceti f7904748. Every library declaration cited in the README was re-read at the new pins.
2. **Transvections already exist in Mathlib.** Mathlib now has `LinearMap.transvection`, `LinearEquiv.transvections`, `LinearEquiv.dilatransvections`, their determinants, base change, `mem_dilatransvections_iff_finrank` and Dieudonné's generation theorem (Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Transvection/Basic.lean and Generation.lean). The design's API (`IsPseudoReflection`, `IsTransvection`, `.det`, `.conj`, `.baseChange`, `exists_eq_one_add`) is deleted. §0.1 defines pseudo-reflections as non-identity dilatransvections, because Mathlib's sets contain the identity. It keeps only the characteristic-polynomial formula, the dichotomy and the closure `SL_n = ⟨transvections⟩`, none of which is at the pin. `Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.exists_list_transvec_mul_diagonal_mul_list_transvec` is cited.
3. **Suppliers later than this roadmap.**
   - CohomologyComparisons (#210 in the merge order) is replaced by PadicHodgeTheory §8.1, which supplies the de Rham, crystalline and semistable comparisons.
   - PotentialAutomorphyInfrastructurePartII (PL.0) is a consumer of this roadmap, so citing it would create a cycle. "Ordinary of weight" is now LocalGaloisDeformationRings §1.7 `IsOrdinaryOfWeight`, with the criteria of PadicHodgeTheory §3.6. Auxiliary Hecke characters are now PotentialModularityAndCompatibleSystems §§2.15 and 3.1 with GlobalNumberFields Layers 9–10.
   - AutomorphicGaloisRepresentationsPartII AG2.0 is replaced by PotentialModularityAndCompatibleSystems §1.9, which states both directions.
   - CohomologicalPointCounting, ComplexComparison, EllAdicRealization and TraceFormula are not in the merge order. They are replaced by ComplexComparisonPartII §§C5.2, C5.3, C5.5, C6.5, C6.6 and InverseGaloisAndArithmeticFundamentalGroups §§IG.0.T2, IG.0.T3, IG.1.3.
   - FiniteFieldsAndCharacterSums, which exists nowhere, is replaced by Mathlib's Gauss and Jacobi sums.
   - The graph adapter "SF.2" is dropped. EtaleCohomologyFoundations, which is now on main, is cited by its own sections.
4. **PotentialModularityAndCompatibleSystems is #198, after this roadmap (#137).** It owns Moret-Bailly (§§2.17, 2.18, 2.20), disjointness through towers (§2.14), weakly compatible families (§1.1), induction (§1.4), character families (§1.9), induced purity (§1.11), density-one irreducibility (§1.21) and CM characters (§§2.15, 3.1). Restating Moret-Bailly's integral-point theorem here would duplicate a roadmap that already plans it. The README therefore cites it, and the movable dependency check moves this roadmap to just after it (output below). PotentialModularityAndCompatibleSystems does not name this roadmap, and every consumer of this roadmap sits after #198, so there is no cycle. No notion was moved down from a later roadmap.
5. **Design gaps closed internally.** Each of these targets is now proved inside the roadmap rather than requested.
   - G5, the equivariant correspondence comparison: for `h ∈ H₀`, `h·Frob` is the Frobenius of a twisted form (SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.16). The trace formula and purity give `λ`-independence (§6.8), and Katz–Messing (§RD.7.7) applied to the twisted forms gives the crystalline comparison (§7.7). No change to RD.7 is needed.
   - G7, recognising the comparison character: `Ψ_λ^n = det V_λ ⊗ ε^{n(n−1)/2}` (§3.5(2)) is de Rham with weight `nM(a_σ)`. A character whose `n`-th power is de Rham of weight divisible by `n` is potentially crystalline by PadicHodgeTheory §3.6, hence de Rham. PotentialModularityAndCompatibleSystems §1.9 then recognises it (§7.5), and §6.8(3) supplies `λ`-independence. Neither step uses §7.6 or later.
   - G8, finite-field roots: take the Teichmüller lift, take a root by ArithmeticGaloisDuality §R02.4.6, and reduce. With `μ_m ⊂ k` and `χ̄` valued in `(k^×)^m`, every `m`-th root of `χ̄(g)` lies in `k` (§6.5).
   - G10, the local inverse: this is LefschetzPencilsAndVanishingCycles §2.4's henselian implicit function theorem over `O_{F_v}`, together with Mathlib `IsAdicComplete.henselianRing` (§9.5).
   - G1 (Katz's ESDE Chapter 8), G2 (Zalesskii–Serezhkin and Artin) and G4 (general-character ordinarity) are not supplier gaps. The first two are source-access matters for targets owned here, now recorded as uncertified sources. The third is a theorem with explicit hypotheses (§8.6).
6. **Four supplier gaps remain.** They are written into `plan.json` and stated verbatim under their suppliers.
   - `tame-point-specialization`, from LefschetzPencilsAndVanishingCycles.
   - `curve-crystalline-companions`, from PadicDifferentialEquationsAndRigidCohomology. The search for Abe companions and Drinfeld–Kedlaya slope bounds returned NOT FOUND there.
   - `equivariant-toroidal-semistable-reduction`, from SchemeAndStackFoundations. There is no Kempf–Knudsen–Mumford–Saint-Donat statement anywhere.
   - `twisted-modular-curve-over-number-fields`, from ModularCurvesPartII. ModularCurves §5C and ModularCurvesPartII §5.14 work over `ℚ` only.
7. **Rank two in the symplectic core.** The design applied Zalesskii–Serezhkin only for `n > 2` and left Dickson's rank-two input open. §0.11(2) states Dickson's classification for transvection-generated subgroups of `SL₂`. §5.6 excludes the binary icosahedral case: in `2.A₅`, a product of two elements of order three has order 4, 6 or 10, never the odd order `N` (I computed this with quaternions, as a check on the published argument).
8. **Two-prime product.** §0.10 is split. The symplectic non-isomorphism needs no `> 10` bound, because `#PSp_{2m}(𝔽_q)` is strictly increasing in odd `q`, as Barnet-Lamb–Geraghty–Harris–Taylor, Corollary 4.8, uses. §5.10 therefore drops the bound when both factors are symplectic. A new Check notes that `Sp₂(𝔽₃)` never arises for odd `N ≥ 3`, since that would need `3 ≡ ±1 (mod N)`.
9. **Comparison characters.** The design's DM.3 node used §6.8-type `λ`-independence and §5.9 descent inside Layer 3. Its parts were moved (§3.5 keeps the duality relation and the determinant identity on `U`) so that every layer depends only on earlier ones.
10. **Page locators.** Several Barnet-Lamb–Geraghty–Harris–Taylor page numbers in the design were off by one, because the PDF page and the printed author page differ. All were recomputed from the printed page numbers in the author copy; for example Lemma 4.6 is on pp. 32–33, Lemma 5.3 on p. 37, and Corollary 4.8 on p. 34. Boxer–Calegari–Gee–Newton–Thorne Lemma 5.2.3 is on p. 51.
11. **Wrong Checks repaired.**
    - The design's §2.7 test treated the Hesse case as "the self-dual datum". It is the zero-shaped `(0, 0, 0)`, which `N > n + 1` excludes from the self-dual family.
    - The design's non-zero-shaped Hodge-grade test, at rank two with `N = 5`, cannot fail, because two lines always have grades differing by at most one. It is replaced by the computed rank-three counterexample `N = 7`, `a = (0, 0, 0, 1, 3, 5, 5)`, `B = {1, 3, 5}`, with grades `2, 2, 3`.
    - A Layer 1 example claimed an order for `3` modulo `401`. The computed order is `400`, with `3^{200} ≡ −1`, and the example now uses it.
12. **Process language removed.** Errata identifiers (E1–E50, PAPER-…), "planet" labels, pull-request numbers, "graph adapter", "pending upstream" and "gap" narrative are gone. Published misprints are described mathematically at the citation.
