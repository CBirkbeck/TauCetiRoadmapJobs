## R01 (P2) — The reduced cone of 1.0 is owned by HomotopySpheres 3A.2 on main, with the other end of the interval collapsed

Two roadmaps would each build the reduced cone of a pointed compactly generated space and its comparison with the raw quotient, in opposite conventions, so the library would get two cones that differ by `t ↦ 1 − t`. Here, 1.0 says "the reduced cone is `CX = X ∧ I` with `I` based at `1`" and that for compact Hausdorff `X` it "is the raw quotient `(X × I)/(X × {1} ∪ {x₀} × I)`" (README lines 189–191), and "Owned here" lists "reduced cones and cylinders" (line 1485). On main, `TauCetiRoadmap/HomotopySpheres/README.md` §3A.2, *Pointed cones and Puppe sequences*, which that roadmap says it owns (line 970), builds the reduced cone on AlgebraicTopology Stage 4 item 8's category with "base at t = 1 and apex at t = 0", collapsing `A × {0}` and the basepoint meridian, and compares these quotients with the ordinary ones through the `k`-counit (lines 993–998). The cone was among the targets the previous review listed as not stated in AlgebraicTopology §4.8, which is true; HomotopySpheres' section was not read then.

No later statement uses the cone: the words "cone" and `CX` occur in the README only in that bullet and in the "Owned here" line, and 1.1 and 1.2 need only based homotopies, which Stage 4 item 8 supplies with its adjunction on based homotopy classes. The smallest change: drop the reduced-cone clause and its raw-quotient sentence from the bullet (the notation `I₊`, `S¹ = I/∂I` and the based homotopy `X ∧ I₊ → Y` can stay as notation), and drop "reduced cones and cylinders" from "Owned here" and "reduced cones" from the description's bullet for 1.0. If a cone is wanted later, it is HomotopySpheres 3A.2's, in its convention.

## C01 — The `*Needs:*` line of 4.3 omits 4.1, whose definition both propositions conclude

An implementer who reads the line would start Propositions 6.29 and 6.33 without the notion their conclusion is stated in. Both conclude that `M` is "abundant" (README lines 1101–1103), which is the definition of 4.1 (Definition 6.11), and the proof must verify its condition (ii) on the class `Φ_*(A × α)`; the line at 1121–1125 lists 1.4, the base roadmap's items, GeometricTopology and AlgebraicTopology, and not 4.1. The lines of 4.2 and 4.4 do list 4.1. Add 4.1.

## N01 — One Tau Ceti predicate is cited under two names

`IsAspherical` in the `*Needs:*` line of 4.3 and in Layer 4's Dependencies (lines 1123, 1392), `TauCeti.IsAspherical` in 4.5 and in the Tau Ceti contract (lines 1258, 1630). One spelling, with the namespace, would let a reader search for it.
