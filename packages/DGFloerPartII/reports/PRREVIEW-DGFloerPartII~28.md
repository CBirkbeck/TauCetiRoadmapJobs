## R01 (P2) — Section 1.0 and `compactlyGeneratedToTop_isLeftAdjoint` plan again what AlgebraicTopology Stage 4 item 8 owns on main

Two roadmaps now own the same functor and the same pointed constructions, so an implementer would build them twice, and 1.0 treats AlgebraicTopology's coreflection as a possibly different one: "every other compact-generation coreflection on these carriers, in particular the one through which AlgebraicTopology Stage 4.4 states its compact reduced-suspension carrier comparison, is identified with `k`" (README lines 139–142). It is the same functor by definition.

On main, `TauCetiRoadmap/AlgebraicTopology/README.md` §4.8, *Compact generation and the pointed adjunction* (lines 1795–1836), defines `k` as Mathlib's `GeneratedByTopCat.adj` for the family of `CompHaus.{u}`, proves through `TopologicalSpace.generatedBy_eq_coinduced` that the two full subcategories agree, and transports the adjunction to a right adjoint of `CompactlyGenerated.compactlyGeneratedToTop`, with colimits computed in `TopCat` and limits `k` of the limits. It then states the products `k(X×Y)`, the mapping spaces (compact-open followed by `k`), currying through `Topology.ContinuousMapGeneratedBy.curryEquiv`, the smash quotient, `ΣX = X∧S¹` with the interval last, the suspension–loop adjunction on maps and on homotopy classes, the pointed exponential law, and it owns "the three comparisons of raw quotients with their convenient counterparts" (compact smash, compact reduced suspension, the suspension of a pointed Hausdorff CW complex). The section is unchanged at the head of the open pull request that revises AlgebraicTopology, and HomotopySpheres on main cites it as the owner (its README line 480). Here, the bullets of 1.0 from "The coreflection" to "Comparison with the raw quotients" (lines 127–210) state the same targets by the same route, the first bullet of "Owned here" (lines 1502–1505) claims them, `Suggested.lean` line 86 states the adjoint, and Stage 4 item 8 is cited nowhere in the README.

The smallest change: in 1.0 cite AlgebraicTopology Stage 4 item 8 for `k`, the convenient product and mapping space, the currying bijection, the smash product, the suspension–loop adjunction, the pointed exponential law and the three raw comparisons, and keep as targets only what §4.8 does not state: the product criterion for a locally compact Hausdorff factor (`compactlyGeneratedSpace_prod`), the homeomorphism form of the exponential law with cartesian closedness, the preservation of quotient maps by `− ×_k Y`, the closed symmetric monoidal structure, the reduced cones and cylinders, the agreement with `CompactCoherentification` on Hausdorff spaces, and the metrizable comparison with the raw mapping spaces. Replace the quoted sentence by one saying that `k` is Stage 4 item 8's. Narrow the "Owned here" bullet, add the row to "Left to named roadmaps", add Stage 4 item 8 to the `*Needs:*` lines of 1.0–1.2 and to the AlgebraicTopology contract, and drop `compactlyGeneratedToTop_isLeftAdjoint` with its line of the module docstring. The description's bullet for 1.0 and its list of `Suggested.lean` statements then change with it.

## C01 — The `*Needs:*` line of 4.6 omits 4.2 and 4.3, whose classes the route of Theorem G uses

An implementer who reads the line would start Theorem G without the two inputs its route names. The route applies Lemma 7.7 "to the image `β` of the class `α₀ ∈ H_*(Map(S², S^k))` of Example 6.16 … or of Proposition 6.34" (README lines 1324–1326): these are the even-sphere family of 4.2 and the rotation-family class of 4.3 (with 1.2 and 1.4 through them), and the source uses them the same way (`bdho-cotangent` lines 5429–5431). The line at 1350–1358 lists 1.5 and the base roadmap's items only. Add 4.2 and 4.3.

## C02 — The scope paragraph claims every numbered statement of §§6.3–6.4 and §7 except Lemma 6.35, but Example 6.28 is not stated and half of Proposition 6.39 is excluded

"every numbered statement of its §§6.3–6.4 and §7 except Lemma 6.35" (line 1491) is contradicted two paragraphs later by the exclusion of the Cayley plane, and Example 6.28 of the source (a section of `Diff(M) → M` makes `M` covered by diffeomorphisms; `bdho-cotangent` lines 4930–4934) appears nowhere in the README, although the third Check of 1.4 uses an instance of it for `S³`. Either state Example 6.28 in 4.3 in one sentence (the class is `s_*[M]` for a section `s`), or except it, and name the Cayley half of Proposition 6.39 in the same sentence.

## C03 — Two pointers describe an earlier state of the text

The Hatcher entry of the References credits "the homotopy groups of Lie groups and Stiefel manifolds used in 4.3" (line 1889), but 4.3 no longer uses them: its only homotopy inputs are `π_k(Sⁿ)` from Tau Ceti and the base roadmap's torus criterion. The GeometricTopology contract gives "Layer 1 tubular neighbourhoods (1.0, 1.5)" (line 1853), but 1.0 uses no tubular neighbourhood and its `*Needs:*` line names no GeometricTopology item. Drop the first clause and write "(1.5)" in the second.

## C04 — The description's summary says Theorem I lists homogeneous spaces

The summary block reads "Theorem I lists examples (spheres, sphere bundles over highly connected bases, non-torus compact Lie groups, homogeneous spaces, CPᵈ and HPᵈ)". The source's Theorem I has no homogeneous spaces (`bdho-cotangent` lines 430–445), and the README says so in 4.5: Proposition 6.36 "adds the orientable homogeneous spaces …, which the source's Theorem I does not list". A text that would be right: "Theorem I lists examples (spheres, sphere bundles over highly connected bases, non-torus compact Lie groups, manifolds covered by diffeomorphisms, CPᵈ and HPᵈ, and products), to which Proposition 6.36 adds homogeneous spaces".

## N01 — 4.4 is a bold run-in paragraph inside 4.3, while the text and the base roadmap's revision cite it as a section

"4.4" is cited as a section in the Conventions, in 2.6, 4.2, 4.5 and the source coverage table, and it has no anchor. A `### 4.4` heading would give it one; nothing else changes.
