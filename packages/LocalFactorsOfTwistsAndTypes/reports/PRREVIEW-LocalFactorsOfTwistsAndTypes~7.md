## N01 — The subquotient clause of 4.1 (i) has a short route, valid over every nonarchimedean field

The Route says "a route for its subquotient clause is not given here" (line 442), and the clause is stated for `F` of characteristic zero only. It would be easy to give one, and it does not use the characteristic: writing `λ(𝔪)` for the standard module `L(Δ₁) × ⋯ × L(Δ_r)` in an order in which no `Δ_i` precedes a later `Δ_j`, the constituents of `λ(𝔪)` other than `L(𝔪)` are `L(𝔪″)` with `𝔪″` strictly smaller in the order generated by replacing a linked pair by its union and intersection, and `L(𝔪)` has multiplicity one; `λ(𝔪) × λ(𝔫)` and `λ(𝔪 + 𝔫)` have the same semisimplification; so `L(𝔪 + 𝔫)` occurs in `λ(𝔪) × λ(𝔫)`, and not in `L(𝔪″) × L(𝔫″)` unless `𝔪″ = 𝔪` and `𝔫″ = 𝔫`, since `𝔪″ + 𝔫″ < 𝔪 + 𝔫` otherwise. The roadmap could state the clause for every `F` with this route, keeping the formal proof's form for characteristic zero.
