**Verification.** dittmann_pop line 210–211 attributes resolution of threefolds to Cossart–Piltant [C-P], whose bibliographic entry is dittmann_pop line 786–787 (J. Algebra 321 (2009), 1836–1976). The manifest and the README's References carried no entry for it; the only resolution citation was Stacks, *Resolution of Surfaces*. The work was fetched through the sources service from HAL (hal-00139445v2, open repository, `status = "certified"`); its theorem of the introduction is cossart_piltant line 162–167 (page 5 of the HAL version): for `k` differentially finite over a perfect field and `Z/k` a reduced quasi-projective threefold there is a projective morphism `π : Z̃ → Z` with `Z̃` regular, an isomorphism off the singular locus, and strict-normal-crossings exceptional divisor.
