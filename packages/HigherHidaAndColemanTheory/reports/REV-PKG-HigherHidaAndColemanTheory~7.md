1. **F01** (P1) The declared standard pairs fail the allowed-support definition

   **Location:** `packages/HigherHidaAndColemanTheory/README.md:839`, repeated with analytic depths at line 1020; the contradictory assertion is line 848: “U=B(s,−1), Z=B(cl 0,cl s) is a standard positive pair for every integer s≥0”.

   **Claim:** These pairs satisfy the four inclusions defining allowed support, for the chosen arbitrary strictly positive t with min(t)≥1 and C=max(t).

   **Witness:** Take GL₂, lower Borel, w=id, p=3, t=diag(1,9), C=2, K_(p,4,0), and standard depth 1. For m=0 the lower inclusion wrongly includes z=3; for m≥1 the upper inclusion wrongly excludes z=9.

   Here the negative-root coordinate z parametrizes the upper-unipotent chart of B\GL₂. There are no positive-root coordinates in this chart, so the second depth and its partial closure do nothing. Thus U=B(1,−1) has v₃(z)>1, while Z=B(cl 0,cl 1) contains this U. At m=0, B(m,cl(n+s)) contains z=3, of valuation 1, and cannot be contained in Z∩U. At every integer m≥1, U contains z=9, of valuation 2, whereas B(Cm,−1)=B(2m,−1) requires valuation strictly greater than 2m. No choice of n or s repairs either failure.

   These are also witnesses after K-saturation: K_(p,4,0) is generated by diagonal units, lower-unipotent integral elements and upper-unipotent translations divisible by 3⁴. The coordinate transformations multiply z by a unit, send it to z/(1+cz), or translate it by 3⁴b. They preserve valuations 1 and 2. Both rational flags have ordinary modular-tower preimages, by GL₂(ℚ₃)-equivariance from an ordinary point. Partial closures introduce no change at these rank-one points. The tame level may be any neat prime-to-3 level.

   This is an inconsistency in the cited source too, not an OCR accident: BP-Coleman line 5210, Definition 5.4.10, printed pp. 111–112, and line 5230, Example 5.4.11, p. 112, have these same bounds and standard pairs. Both PDF pages were visually checked. The root extrema and congruence subgroup are at BP-Coleman line 2177 and line 2218.

   **Exact repair:** Keep the standard pairs and full range of t. Replace the first lower bound by its intersection with U in each sign:

   - Positive: U∩B(m,cl(n+s)) ⊆ Z∩U ⊆ B(0,cl(Cn)).
   - Negative: U∩B(cl(m+s),n) ⊆ Z∩U ⊆ B(cl(Cm),0).

   Keep the second sandwich, open/closed conditions and finite quasi-Stein hypotheses. Make the same replacement in §6.2, with l replacing the geometric depth n; keep its separate coefficient-domain condition U∩Z⊆B(n,n).

   Explain this as a corrected relative-support condition, with its proof from BP-Coleman Corollary 5.3.4, p. 106, line 4963, and the estimates in the proof of Theorem 5.4.12, p. 112, line 5260. In the source's notation U_m=T^m(U₀), Z_n=(Tᵗ)^n(Z₀), those estimates give U⊆U_m, U_(m+s)∩Z_n⊆U, and U∩Z_(n+s)⊆Z∩U⊆Z_n. These are precisely the relative hypotheses for applying Corollary 5.3.4 with T^s. On arbitrary supported models retain that power action; transport the full Hecke action on finite slopes through continuation. This does not require a single-step geometric action to preserve every chosen support pair.

   **Repair test:** For the refuting standard pair choose m=n=0,s=1. Its first lower bound becomes U∩B(0,cl 1)⊆Z∩U, which holds, while B(1,cl 0)⊆U⊆B(0,−1) holds. More generally a standard pair of depth a uses m=n=0,s≥a, in either sign. Add the GL₂ depth-one computation as a negative control for the former un-intersected bound. This repairs the source-level error without restricting to C=1, discarding primes, or deleting the standard neighborhoods used downstream.
