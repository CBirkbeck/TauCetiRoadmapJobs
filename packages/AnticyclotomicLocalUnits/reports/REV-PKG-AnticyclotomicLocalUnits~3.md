The opening explicitly identifies this as ColemanPowerSeries, Part II and states where the multiplicative cyclotomic roadmap stops. It retains every p≥5, including 5,7,11; the small-prime existence cases are proved separately, not excluded. The source issues below are corrected or explicitly avoided by the mathematical statements here.

The native domains matter: p≥5, characteristic zero, an unramified quadratic complete DVR, actual finite field extensions, abelian pro-p groups, compact Hausdorff unit modules and discrete point modules. The zero ring, equal-characteristic coefficients, ramified replacement coefficients, nonreduced bases, rank-zero towers and nonabelian S₃ or Q₈ do not satisfy those hypotheses.
