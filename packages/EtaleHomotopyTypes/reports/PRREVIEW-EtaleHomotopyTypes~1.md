The intake announces the plan's sentence, so the supplier is never asked for the characterization the route uses.

## R02 (P3) — the §3.9b detection lemma needs its basepoints

**Location.** `TauCetiRoadmap/EtaleHomotopyTypes/README.md:680`: "between connected pro-spaces whose positive homotopy pro-groups are profinite, a map inducing an isomorphism on π₁ and on H^m …".

**Claim.** The lemma is stated for unpointed connected pro-spaces, but `π₁` of a pro-space is only defined at a compatible vertex (Conventions, line 19; §0.1, line 175), and AM Theorem 4.3, the cited source, is a statement in the pointed connected pro-homotopy category, as is §0.4, the roadmap's own detection theorem.

**Witness.** A connected pro-space need not admit any compatible vertex: a tower of connected simplicial sets whose vertex sets are `ℕ` with transition maps `n ↦ n + 1` on vertices (for instance the nerves of the posets `ℕ` with their order, each contractible, with the shift functors) has empty vertex limit, since a compatible family `(n_i)` would satisfy `n_i = n_0 − i ≥ 0` for every `i`. For such an `X` the phrase "isomorphism on π₁" has no referent. Both uses of the lemma in the roadmap are pointed: the pro-universal cover of pointed `Et(X)` in §3.9b and `Et(X_y)` against the pointed pro-homotopy fibre in §3.9d (line 717).

**Repair.** State the lemma for connected pointed pro-spaces and pointed maps: "between connected pointed pro-spaces `(X,x)`, `(Y,y)` whose positive homotopy pro-groups are profinite, a pointed map inducing an isomorphism of pro-groups `π₁(X,x) → π₁(Y,y)` and isomorphisms `H^m(Y,L) → H^m(X,f^*L)` for every `m ≥ 0` and every finite abelian local system `L` on `Y` is a weak equivalence". Add the vertex-free tower above as a negative-control Check of §0.1 or §2.1 if a Check on compatible vertices is wanted; otherwise no Check changes.

## R03 (P3) — §3.8a (a) is narrowed to characteristic zero although its source has no such hypothesis

**Location.** `TauCetiRoadmap/EtaleHomotopyTypes/README.md:629`: "Let k be a field of characteristic zero … prove: (a) (F,f) is cohomologically proper in dimension ≤ 0 for every étale sheaf of sets F on X (SGA1 XIII Corollary 3.2(1))".

**Claim.** Assertion (a) holds over every field, with no resolution hypothesis and no restriction on the characteristic, and the roadmap states it only in characteristic zero; the route given for (a) (symmetric-group torsors `Q/H`, SGA1 line 10656) is itself a characteristic-zero route, because the torsor input it uses is the characteristic-zero cohomological-properness target.

**Witness.** SGA1 line 11938–11941: Corollary 3.2 is stated for "k un corps de caractéristique p ≥ 0, f coherent", and assertion 1) is "pour tout faisceau d'ensembles F, (F,f) est cohomologiquement propre en dimension ≤ 0" with the resolution hypotheses a), b) attached only to assertion 2). Its proof for sets is Theorem 3.1(1)(a)–(b) (SGA1 lines 11582–11669: normalization of the reduced fibre and Corollary 1.9 for the constant case, then the constructible embedding and Proposition 1.13(1)), and the limit step at SGA1 lines 11961–11982, none of which uses resolution or the characteristic.

**Repair.** State (a) for an arbitrary field `k` (keeping (b) and (c) in characteristic zero), cite Corollary 3.2(1) with Theorem 3.1(1)(a)–(b) and the limit step as the route for positive characteristic, and keep the symmetric-group route as the characteristic-zero instance. Add one positive-characteristic Check, for example: over an algebraic closure of 𝔽_p, the constant two-element sheaf on 𝔸¹ has two sections on 𝔸¹ and two on 𝔸¹_A for every strictly henselian local algebra A, while the Artin–Schreier covers that break the dimension ≤ 1 product formula (line 657) do not enter dimension ≤ 0. If the roadmap prefers to keep (a) in characteristic zero, say in the target that the general form is Corollary 3.2(1) and why it is not stated here; the current text gives neither.
