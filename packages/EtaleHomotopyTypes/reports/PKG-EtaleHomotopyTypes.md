## Corrections and support boundaries

- The properness reading leaf is resolved at the level of source identification: IStr line 507–539 contains the level-presentation repair and Remark 4.16 in arXiv v1. The design’s claim that this version lacks the correction was false. The roadmap states properness for Isaksen’s homotopy-pro-group model, and adapts that repair; it does not infer it merely from properness of the strict model. The published Remark 4.14 locator is not mixed with preprint numbering.
- SS line 1619 labels a product of cosimplicial degrees as a partial totalization. The roadmap uses actual compatible partial totalizations. For the arrow * → {0,1}, Tot₀ has two candidate tuples and Tot₁ retains only (*,0), not (*,1).
- SS line 1648–1652 calls the relative cylinder projection a trivial fibration. On the third unmarked point the fiber is Δ¹, whose inverse-edge horn has no filler. The valid target retains the endpoint cofibration and the projection weak equivalence.
- The finite-type p-materialization criterion retains H⁰=𝔽ₚ via the unit, H¹=0, and degreewise finite-dimensional cohomology; DAG13 line 4146–4150 and line 3736 give the criterion and the separate comparison. The correct theorem is 3.4.12, not 3.4.2. Infinite specified gradings are completed and materialized by grade, then given the ordinary coproduct. The infinite-discrete counterexample has π₀=βℕ after ungraded completion/materialization, and a nonprincipal ultrafilter singleton indicator gives an extra ordinary H⁰ class.
- The smooth-submersion proof requires a smooth target. The identity on a singular node is a smooth surjective scheme map, while its analytic source and target are not smooth curve manifolds. Analytic étale hypercover descent over singular spaces uses local homeomorphisms instead. FGV is cited by locator without text; the package does not claim an independent rereading or a newly discovered published erratum.
- The p-profinite adjunction has completion of the constant-space functor on the left. An arbitrary constant space need not be p-finite. Its right adjoint is materialization. This corrects an ambiguous constant/limit formulation in the design.
- A nontrivial stabilizer alone does not prove failure of a geometric-kernel quotient. The discriminating relative model uses X=BC₃ → BC₂ trivial and Y=BS₃ → BC₂ sign: there are three pointed relative maps and three geometric-kernel orbits, but two full-stabilizer/unpointed orbits. The roadmap gives this computed model explicitly.
- Rigid hypercovers may be infinite disjoint unions and need not be quasi-projective. The comparison zigzag analytifies them using ComplexComparisonPartII C−1.1’s locally finite-type domain; the algebraic evaluation theorem keeps its quasi-projective X hypothesis.
- The characteristic-zero geometric finite-cover product proof in SGA1 line 12282–12294 assumes strong desingularizability. Its exact resolution/principalization/SNC compactification suppliers are retained. Prime-to-characteristic means every prime in this use, so the result is not restricted to a selected set of primes.
- Supplier locators were repaired by reading the actual sections: generic Pro is AdicSpacesPartII R0.19.9, rather than DiamondsAndVStacks’ geometric bounded application; hypercover descent is EnhancedDerivedSheaves E2.6, not E2.5; étale cohomology limits are SchemeAndStackFoundations 2.15, not 2.5. SchemeAndStackFoundations 0.3 is Proj and is not an étale-neighborhood supplier; use 0.9–0.12. The bounded generic Pro predicate is PerfectoidSpaces P6.10, not its subsequent geometric P6.11–P6.12 results.
- Analytification integration is a direct contract with ComplexComparisonPartII C−1.1. That supplier states the analytic carrier and functor; the old integration leaf tied to another proposal does not justify introducing a second carrier.

The README cannot claim legal rereading of BK’s complete-convergence arguments, AM’s raw profiniteness/detection proofs, Fr’s arbitrary-local-system comparison and nonproper all-prime fiber theorem, or FGV’s published appendix. Those works are explicitly uncertified and cited by locator, as the brief permits. The unimplemented mathematical theorems remain full targets, rather than asserted closed proofs. No missing-text request is pending: all twelve cited works have manifest entries, eight with certified text and four with citation-only status. For Fr the service followed the correct DOI to an unclassified publisher landing host and fetched no text; the manifest records that limitation without manufactured provenance. The initial BK DOI resolved to a different work; bibliography verification corrected the DOI under the same BK key to 10.1007/978-3-540-38117-4 before the final source check.
