2. **F02** (P2) §3.5's higher-group route asserts that every positive-degree integral class dies on a finite cover; ℙ¹ refutes it

   **Location.** `packages/EtaleHomotopyTypes/README.md:574` (§3.5, *Higher groups* bullet): "Consequently every integral class of positive degree on X_i is torsion and is killed on a further finite cover, by the torsor classification in degree one and the Bockstein sequence for ℚ/ℤ in degree two."

   **Claim.** For a noetherian geometrically unibranch `X_i`, every class in `H^q(X_i, ℤ)`, `q ≥ 1`, becomes zero on some finite étale cover; the finite-image property of `H_n` of the pro-universal cover is then deduced from this.

   **Witness.** `X = ℙ¹` over `ℂ` is smooth, hence noetherian and geometrically unibranch, and simply connected, so every finite étale cover is a disjoint union of copies of `ℙ¹` and pullback is injective on cohomology. Rational vanishing (the bullet's own, correct, step) gives `H³(ℙ¹, ℤ) ≅ H²(ℙ¹, ℚ/ℤ) = colimₙ H²(ℙ¹, ℤ/n) = colimₙ ℤ/n = ℚ/ℤ ≠ 0` (Kummer: `Pic(ℙ¹)/n = ℤ/n`, `Br(ℙ¹) = 0`). No nonzero class of `H³(ℙ¹, ℤ)` is killed on any finite étale cover. The two tools named (degree-one torsors, degree-two Bockstein) justify only degrees 1 and 2, and the sentence is false from degree 3 on. The conclusion drawn from it ("whose maps to discrete abelian groups therefore have finite image") is true, but for a different reason.

   **Repair.** Replace the sentence by the argument that actually gives finite images, which needs no cover: for a discrete abelian group `A`, a homomorphism `H_n(X̃) → A` is, by universal coefficients (with `H_{n−1}(X̃) = 0` by Hurewicz), a class of `colimᵢ Hⁿ(Et(X_i), A) = colimᵢ Hⁿ_et(X_i, A)` (§3.4); its composite with `A → A ⊗ ℚ` is a class with coefficients in a ℚ-vector space, zero by rational vanishing, so the image lies in `A_tors`; and since `Hⁿ_et(X_i, −)` on a noetherian scheme commutes with filtered colimits of coefficient groups, the class comes from a finite subgroup of `A_tors`, so the homomorphism has finite image. Keep the rest of the bullet. Test on the refuting instance: for `ℙ¹`, `H₂(X̃) = H₂(Et ℙ¹)` is the system `(ℤ/n)` and every homomorphism to `ℚ/ℤ` factors through a finite subgroup; nothing is asked of `H³(ℙ¹, ℤ)`.
