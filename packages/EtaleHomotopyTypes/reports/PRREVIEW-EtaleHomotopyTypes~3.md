## R01 (P3) — the fibre-criterion route takes the C_i among the stalks of F, which Stacks 09Z7 does not give on a non-noetherian base

**Location.** `TauCetiRoadmap/EtaleHomotopyTypes/README.md:628`, part (iii) of the base-change criterion: "Embed F in G = ∏ p_(i*)C_i with p_i:Z_i → T finite and C_i finite constant groups taken among the stalks of F, by the construction of Stacks, Lemmas 59.74.3 and 59.74.4".

**Claim.** The route needs each C_i to be a finite L-group, because it then applies the fibre criterion for the constant group C_i on the base change W ×_T Z_i → Z_i, whose geometric fibres carry no nontrivial torsors under finite L-groups only. On a noetherian T, Lemma 59.74.3 does take each C_i to be a stalk (StacksEtale line 6018–6019: E is the value of F on a finite separable extension of κ(x)). On a quasi-compact quasi-separated T that is not noetherian, Lemma 59.74.4 obtains the embedding by descending F to a constructible F_t on a finite-type ℤ-scheme X_t and applying 59.74.3 there (StacksEtale line 6073–6089): the C_i are stalks of F_t at geometric points of X_t, and a point of X_t outside the image of T may carry a stalk that is not an L-group. The base T to which (i) applies (iii) is S″ ×_(S′) S̃′ for an arbitrary base change S′ of S, so the non-noetherian case is the one used.

**Witness.** StacksEtale line 6087–6089: the proof of 59.74.4 concludes by pulling back the embedding found for F_t, with no statement about the stalks of F. StacksEtale line 6018–6019 for the stalk claim in the noetherian case.

**Repair.** One clause: after descending F to F_t, shrink the stage. The locus of X_t where the stalk of the constructible F_t is not an L-group is constructible (on a finite partition into constructible locally closed pieces on which F_t is finite locally constant, the isomorphism type of the stalk is open and closed in each piece), and its preimage in T is empty, so by Stacks, Limits, Lemma 32.4.10 (tag 05F4; StacksLimits line 241) the image of a later stage X_(t′) avoids it; apply Lemma 59.74.3 on X_(t′). Alternatively run the pointwise construction of 59.74.3 directly on the affine T (the stalk-injective maps F → p_*E exist without the noetherian hypothesis, and finitely many suffice by quasi-compactness of the constructible topology). Add tag 05F4 to the Needs line and to the StacksLimits reference entry.
