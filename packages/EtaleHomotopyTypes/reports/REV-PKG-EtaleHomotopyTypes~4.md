2. **F02** (P3) §3.9a states the product formula below the generality of its source

   **Location.** `packages/EtaleHomotopyTypes/README.md:593`: “geometrically connected geometrically unibranch finite-type k-varieties Y₁,Y₂”.

   **Claim.** Both factors must be geometrically unibranch and of finite type.

   **Witness.** SGA1 line 12282–12294 (XIII, Proposition 4.6, p. 310): `k` separably closed, `X` and `Y` connected `k`-schemes, with condition (a) only on `X` (finite type and the desingularizability bound in dimension ≤ dim X); no unibranch hypothesis and no finiteness on `Y`.    **Repair.** State §3.9a for geometrically connected finite-type `k`-schemes `Y₁` and geometrically connected quasi-compact quasi-separated `Y₂` (or finite-type both, if the authors prefer one adapter), drop “geometrically unibranch” from §3.9a, and keep unibranchness exactly where it is used: §§3.9b–3.9c and 4.7 (profiniteness through §3.5). Add the nodal-cubic instance as a Check of §3.9a.
