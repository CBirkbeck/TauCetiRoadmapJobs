## Draft pull request body

This roadmap builds the uniformly hyperbolic theory of diffeomorphisms and flows on manifolds and joins it to the thermodynamic formalism of ClassicalErgodicTheory. Its abstract half proves Bowen's uniqueness criterion under weak specification with bounded gluing times, for homeomorphisms and for flows, with upper semicontinuity of entropy for expansive flows and the behaviour of pressure and equilibrium states under time-t maps and time rescalings. Because it assumes only weak specification, the criterion covers transitive systems that are not mixing, such as the swap of two points and the roof-one suspension of the full shift, which ClassicalErgodicTheory's specification with periodic shadowing excludes. It then abstracts uniform hyperbolicity as a hyperbolic local product structure, from which expansivity, closing, spectral decomposition, weak specification and the Bowen property of Hölder potentials follow. Its smooth half defines hyperbolic sets and Anosov systems through a continuous Riemannian metric. It proves metric independence, the characterisation, continuity and adapted metrics of the splitting, the uniform Hadamard–Perron theorem, local stable, unstable, strong and weak manifolds, nontriviality and Hölder continuity of the bundles, and the local product structure of locally maximal hyperbolic sets. The end theorems are Bowen's theorem for basic sets of C¹ diffeomorphisms; the Bowen–Ruelle theorem for basic sets of C¹ flows, with invariance under constants, flow coboundaries, time-t maps and rescaling; uniqueness of the Bowen and Bowen–Margulis measures, with positive entropy and Haar measure as Bowen's measure of a hyperbolic toral automorphism; and the Bowen–Ruelle SRB theorem for attractors of flows generated by C² vector fields, under which Lebesgue-almost every point of the basin is generic for the equilibrium state of the geometric potential (in particular almost every point of a transitive Anosov flow). Hyperbolic toral automorphisms, suspensions and time changes of Anosov diffeomorphisms, and geodesic flows in negative curvature (with the connection map and the Jacobi-field form of the derivative of the geodesic flow) are the examples. The route goes through Bowen's criterion and the abstract axioms, not through Markov partitions, so every uniqueness theorem holds for every compact metric system with the abstract properties.

## Fixes from the upstream-reviewer read

1. §9.3 and the supplier contracts: the unowned gap became a stated, sourced target with route and
   Checks (above). Before, the README depended on something no roadmap plans.
2. §6.6, weak specification for flows: the route bounded the gluing times only from above
   ("stay below τ₀ + 1"). A connecting time 0 plus a negative junction shift (|v| ≤ δ from (F2))
   gives a negative gluing time, which the definition forbids. The route now takes connecting
   segments of length between 1 and τ₀ (free, because the point with dense forward orbit passes
   between any two balls at arbitrarily large times), so for δ < 1 the gluing times lie in
   [1 − δ, τ₀ + δ] ⊆ [0, τ₀ + 1].
3. `Suggested.lean` docstring, geodesic-flow item: it said the restricted flow "rests on [README
   §9.3's] theorems that the geodesic spray of a compact manifold is complete". Completeness and
   constant speed are Tau Ceti's theorems. The sentence now says the flow is assembled from those,
   and that packaging it as a `Flow ℝ` on the sphere bundle needs the flow law and joint continuity
   proved, so it is not a simple definition.
