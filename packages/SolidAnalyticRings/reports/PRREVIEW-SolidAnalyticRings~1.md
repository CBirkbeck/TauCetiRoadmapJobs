Claim: the Check presents the degree-one profile colimit ⊕_ℕ ℤ/2 as a witness separating the marked-fiber model `K_r ≃ A_p` from the model that uses image subgroups `p^r·π_1A_p`.

Witness (computed): at p = 2, π₁A_p(*) = ℤ/2. Marked model: π₁K_r = ℤ/2 for every r, transition f→g is multiplication by 2^(f(j)−g(j)); lowering every positive value by one kills those coordinates, so colim_f ∏_j π₁K_(f(j)) = ⊕_ℕ ℤ/2. Image model: 2^r·ℤ/2 = 0 for r ≥ 1, so ∏_j 2^(f(j))ℤ/2 = ∏_(f(j)=0) ℤ/2, a finite product; its colimit over proper f under inclusions is again ⊕_ℕ ℤ/2. Both models give the stated value, and both agree with π₁ of the 2-completion of ⊕_ℕ 𝕊^∧_2 (Ext(ℤ/2^∞, ⊕ℤ/2) = ⊕ℤ/2, Hom(ℤ/2^∞, ⊕ℤ₂) = 0). The two models differ only at a fixed stage: for f(j) = j, π₁V_f(*) = ∏_ℕ ℤ/2 in the marked model and ℤ/2 in the image model.

Repair: replace the sentence by the stage-level witness, for example "For f(j)=j the stage V_f has π₁(*)=∏_ℕℤ/2 in the marked-fiber model, whereas image subgroups give the single coordinate ℤ/2; the colimits agree, so only the marked stages carry the coherent transitions".
