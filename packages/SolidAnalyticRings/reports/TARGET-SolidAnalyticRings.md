## Verdict and supply direction

The new direction is a reusable spectral coefficient library. The geometric VStack roadmap and the trace-methods roadmap each supply or use part of it; neither is an appropriate Part II base for this combined direction. Their existing plans are preserved. The already occupied VStack Part II name also denotes an actual, different geometric continuation. SolidAnalyticRings was unused as a folder across main, completed roadmaps, open roadmap PRs and the indexed board packages before creation here.

Read `merge-order/ORDER.txt` and the relevant EnhancedDerivedSheaves, GeneralizedCohomology and StableHomotopyKTheory supplier sections. The proposed placement is after VStackSheavesAndLisseCategories and before HabiroRings. RefinedTraceMethods is later than, and explicitly consumes, HabiroRings. An import from this supplier to RefinedTraceMethods §6.2 would therefore close a cycle. The brief resolves this by relocating only the existing generic §6.2 block to the earlier supplier without replanning it; the later roadmap imports that block. This triage changes neither the order nor another package. The later design integration must make the ownership move concrete before declaring the supply graph closed.

The new endpoints retain full spectral coefficients, all countable index sets, all primes where the p-adic comparison applies, arbitrary allowed coefficient morphisms, and explicit uniform boundedness. They do not claim that tensor or the discrete functor preserves arbitrary inverse limits. The proper-profile index is only filtered: f_k(n)=max(n−k,0) has no common target proper function under reverse pointwise order. Endpoint 4 cannot justify exchanging its colimit with a countable limit.

The general filtered null-family theorem and spectral analytic extension are identified as new proof obligations, with explicit hypotheses and source models. In particular, neither the abelian p-adic statement nor its π₀ calculation is treated as a spectral proof. Consumer-specific factorial identities and the resulting arithmetic multiplication comparison remain with HabiroRings and its suppliers.
