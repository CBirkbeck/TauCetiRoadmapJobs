## H01 (roed-math) — applied

Q6.2 used to compare "the N7 Fourier-Dirac seminorm" in every dimension d. NoncommutativeGeometry N7 (on main) defines only the two-dimensional triple `D=−i(σ₁δ₁−σ₂δ₂)` on `ℓ²(ℤ²)⊗ℂ²`, so at d=3, for example, there was nothing to compare against. Q6.2 now restricts the comparison to d=2, where N7's D is transported along Φ_θ and V (see H02). It states the coefficient estimate explicitly: for self-adjoint a, with x=δ₁a and y=δ₂a, `‖[D,a⊗1]‖=max(‖x+iy‖,‖x−iy‖)`, which lies in `[max(‖x‖,‖y‖), ‖x‖+‖y‖]`. It also says that for d≠2 no Dirac seminorm is compared. The Lip-norm, the derivative formula and the continuity theorem stay all-dimensional. The NCG supplier bullet in Conventions now says the N5–N7 objects are two-dimensional and that no d-dimensional Dirac operator is planned. The alternative repair, a d-dimensional Clifford triple, was not adopted. It would make this roadmap plan a spectral-triple construction (domain, self-adjointness, compact resolvent) that no metric theorem here needs, and which belongs to NoncommutativeGeometry's scope.

## H02 (roed-math) — applied differently

The phase formula did not define a map between the two C*-algebras. Q6.1 now specifies the isometric *-isomorphism `Φ_θ : A_Θ(θ) → A_θ` with `Φ_θ(W_(m,n))=exp(πiθmn)U₁^mU₂^n`, where Θ₁₂=−θ. This is the reviewer's direction and sign. The construction does not use a universal property of A_θ:
- The trace-GNS representation π_τ on `ℓ²(ℤ²)`, in N7's ordered basis, is injective because N6's trace is faithful.
- The diagonal unitary `Vδ_(m,n)=exp(πiθmn)e_(m,n)` conjugates π_τ(U₁) and π_τ(U₂) to W_(1,0) and W_(0,1).

Both sides were computed on δ_(m,n): they give `exp(−πiθn)δ_(m+1,n)` and `exp(πiθm)δ_(m,n+1)` respectively.

The listed compatibilities are:
- the gauge actions (α_z with N6's β_z);
- the traces;
- the Fourier-coefficient rule (a factor of `exp(πiθmn)`);
- finite and rapidly decreasing series (onto N6's 𝒜_θ);
- the intertwining of λ_Θ(θ) with π_τ;
- V commuting with δ_j and N7's D.

Together these transport the commutator seminorm.

In addition, Q6's carrier was "D1's scalar-twisted full C*-algebra" (OperatorAlgebraDynamics D1.CentralTwists/D2.CentralTwists). These sections exist only in an open pull request that revises OperatorAlgebraDynamics, not on main, so they are not a valid supplier. No roadmap on main and no new-folder pull request owns the twisted group C*-algebra of ℤᵈ (searched by object: twisted group C*-algebra, rotation algebra, noncommutative tori, σ_Θ). Q6 now owns it as the C*-algebra generated by the twisted regular representation `λ_Θ(p)δ_q=σ_Θ(p,q)δ_(p+q)`. It proves the universal property by twisted Fell absorption (`ξ⊗δ_q↦V_qξ⊗δ_q` conjugates `1⊗λ_Θ(p)` to `V_p⊗λ_0(p)`) and D2's almost-invariant vectors (on main), and implements the gauge action spatially. The supplier bullet now cites OperatorAlgebraDynamics D2 and `../AmenableGroups/README.md` Layers 5–6, both on main.

## H04 (roed-math) — applied

The objection is correct: normalizing the Jordan parts only gives `λ=t(φ′−ψ′)` with `t=μ⁺(S)=μ⁻(S)≤1`. Q0.4's old text was "normalize the equal positive/negative masses; treat λ=0 separately". The new text derives `t≤1` from ‖λ‖≤2 and λ(e)=0, sets `φ=tφ′+(1−t)ω` and `ψ=tψ′+(1−t)ω` for an arbitrary state ω, so that `λ=φ−ψ` exactly, and takes φ=ψ=ω when t=0.
