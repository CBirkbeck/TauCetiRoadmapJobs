1. **F01** (P3) Galois extension can be stated for locally admissible π; Šⁱ of §1.2 otherwise has no Gal_F action

   **Location.** `packages/ScholzeFunctorAndModpCohomologyOfTheLubinTateTower/README.md` line 380: "For admissible π prove that it extends uniquely to a continuous Gal_F-action"; line 309 defines Šⁱ on the dual category of locally admissible modules.

   **Claim.** The Gal_F extension of the Weil action is stated for admissible π only. Šⁱ(M) in §1.2 is defined on duals of locally admissible modules, and DPS23 line 1337–1338 (p. 30) asserts a continuous Gal_F-action on Šⁱ(M) in that generality; as written, the roadmap gives Šⁱ(M) a Gal_F action only when M∨ is admissible.

   **Witness.** π = ⊕_{m≥1} k[t,t⁻¹]/(tᵐ−1), r = 1, O_Fˣ trivial, ϖ ↦ t. Its O_Fˣ-invariants are all of π (infinite-dimensional), so π is not admissible; but it is the union of its admissible subrepresentations ⊕_{m≤M}, on each of which the geometric-Frobenius orbits have at most M! elements, and Sⁱ commutes with filtered colimits (§1.2, SCH18 line 819–826), so Gal_F acts continuously on S⁰(π) = π. The negative control k[t,t⁻¹] (line 393) has no nonzero admissible subrepresentation (every nonzero k[Fˣ]-submodule is an ideal of k[t,t⁻¹], infinite-dimensional), so it stays a negative control for the locally admissible statement.

   **Repair.** In §1.6 state the extension for locally admissible π (a filtered colimit of admissible subrepresentations, using the colimit law of §1.2), keep the admissible case as the source's statement (SCH18 Proposition 4.6), and add the direct-sum instance above as a positive-control Check; then say in §1.2 that Šⁱ carries the Gal_F action on the whole dual category. Tested: on the instance above the extension exists through the colimit; on k[t,t⁻¹] it does not.
