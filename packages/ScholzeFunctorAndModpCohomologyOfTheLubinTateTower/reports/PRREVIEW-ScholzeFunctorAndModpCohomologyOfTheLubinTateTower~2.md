## R01 (P2) — Qualify the new evaluation-at-one claims

**Location.** `TauCetiRoadmap/ScholzeFunctorAndModpCohomologyOfTheLubinTateTower/Suggested.lean:88`–91, especially “for J = H it is an isomorphism onto X”; `TauCetiRoadmap/ScholzeFunctorAndModpCohomologyOfTheLubinTateTower/README.md:434`, “not injective once H has two points”.

**Claim.** The new definition's documentation asserts surjectivity for an arbitrary continuous homomorphism φ:J→H, and an isomorphism whenever the groups coincide. Its signature neither makes φ an inclusion nor requires joint continuity. The README's additional noninjectivity assertion also allows the zero coefficient module. These claims exceed the hypotheses; the definition itself and the repaired Shapiro theorem are correctly typed.

**Witness.** For J=H=C₂, φ trivial and X=𝔽₃ with sign action, f(h)=−f(h) forces f=0: evaluation has image {0}⊊𝔽₃. For J=1, H=C₂, V=0, evaluation 0→0 is injective.

**Repair.** Describe the general `coindEvalOne` only as a continuous equivariant linear map **to** X. Qualify its isomorphism statement by φ=id and joint continuity of the action; reserve surjectivity for the closed inclusion with a continuous quotient section and jointly continuous action used in §2.1. Add V≠0 to the README's noninjectivity assertion, and retain the zero-module and noninjective-homomorphism examples as negative controls.

**Repair tested.** With φ=id and the same sign module, the allowed functions (f(1),f(t)) are (0,0), (1,2), (2,1); evaluation is a bijection onto 𝔽₃.
