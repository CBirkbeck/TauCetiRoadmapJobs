## R01 (P2) — Three sentences say for every field what the roadmap's own statements contradict

A reader takes each as a fact about every field, and an implementer who relies on them would wrongly rule out statements the roadmap itself makes.

- Convention 5 (`README.md`, lines 105–107): the full-weight parametrization has, "over a field that is not algebraically closed", an image "smaller than `T_D(k)`".
- The Layer 5 introduction (lines 772–774): the adjoint items "do not extend to the adjoint points over an arbitrary field, where the torus `T_D(k) = Hom(Q, kˣ)` of §2.5 is larger than the image of that parametrization and is not generated by root subgroups".
- Scope and ownership (lines 1243–1244): "Everything in this roadmap is about the ambient group and holds over an arbitrary field or ring".

What I computed:

- For `E₈`, `F₄` and `G₂`, `P = Q` (§2.3, lines 364–366), so `Hom(P, kˣ) → Hom(Q, kˣ)` is the identity over every field, the Geck carrier is `G_sc(t)`, and `exists_scSpecialIsogeny` gives the exceptional isogeny on the adjoint points of `F₄` and `G₂` over every field of characteristic `p`. That contradicts the second sentence.
- Over `𝔽₂`, `T_D(𝔽₂) = Hom(Λ(D), 1)` is trivial for every datum (the Check of §3.5, line 578), so the image is all of it. For adjoint `A₁` over `𝔽₄`, `s ↦ s²` is bijective on the group `𝔽₄ˣ` of order 3. More generally, for adjoint `B₂` over a perfect field of characteristic two the cokernel `kˣ/kˣ²` is trivial. None of these fields is algebraically closed.
- §4.5, §5.6 and §5.7 assume an algebraically closed field, and the Check of §4.5 shows §4.5 false over `ℚ`. That contradicts the third sentence.

The smallest change, one clause each:

- Convention 5: "its image can be smaller than `T_D(k)` (for adjoint `A₁` over `ℚ` it misses `[diag(2, 1)]`)".
- Layer 5 introduction: "They do not extend to the adjoint points over every field: unless `t` is `E₈`, `F₄` or `G₂`, the torus `T_D(k) = Hom(Q, kˣ)` of §2.5 can be larger than the image of that parametrization and then is not generated by root subgroups."
- Scope: "… is about the ambient group, over the fields and rings its statements name, not only about fixed points of an endomorphism."

## C01 — The comparison is stated on points only, and the statement over `ℤ` also elaborates

The fourth headline of the Summary is a homomorphism "over `ℤ`". `exists_scToGeckPoints` (`Suggested.lean`, line 266) states, for each field separately, a homomorphism of point groups; it does not say that these maps come from one morphism of group schemes. The module docstring gives the reason (the scheme map is ReductiveGroups §9B.2's), and the points statement is what §§6.1–6.3 prove here, so I do not hold this as a finding. But the file states smoothness on the same two carriers, which is taken from ReductiveGroups §9B.1 in the same way.

Both carriers and their root subgroups are pinned as objects and morphisms of `Grp (Over (Spec ℤ))`: `kostantToralGroupScheme` and `kostantRootSubgroupToToral` (`ToralClosure/Basic.lean`, line 250), `geckGroupScheme` and `geckRootSubgroup` (`GeckLattice/GroupScheme.lean`, lines 148 and 222). I elaborated, at this head, the statement "there is `f : G_sc(t) ⟶ geckGroupScheme t ht` with `kostantRootSubgroupToToral … i ≫ f = geckRootSubgroup ht i` for every `i`", with `hnil` as in the other statements and `open CategoryTheory`: one `sorry` warning, no error. Adding it beside the points statement would make the acceptance test cover the "over `ℤ`" clause; the docstring bullet on Layer 6 would then say "general Chevalley data" only.
