## H02 (CBirkbeck) — applied
C6 (old l. 606–616) is right that Steinberg, *Lectures*, Ch. 3, Cor. 3–5 to Thm 4′ present the group generated by the root subgroups only, and that the words `h_α(t)` do not reach `[diag(2, 1)]`. The proof plan now has the first of the two shapes the review suggested, in two steps:
1. **On the elementary subgroup.** Construct `φ' : E'_D(k) → E'_{D'}(k)` on the root-generated subgroup `E'_D(k)` by Cor. 5.
2. **Extension by the actual torus.** Prove that:
   - `E(k) = E'_D(k) T_D(k)`;
   - `E'_D(k) ∩ T_D(k) = H_D(k)`, the subgroup generated by the `h_α(s)`, using uniqueness in the Bruhat decomposition;
   - `res` sends `h_α(s)` to `h_α(s)`;
   - `φ'` intertwines conjugation by `t` with conjugation by `res(t)` (pinning equation, with `α ∈ Q ⊆ Λ(D')`).

   Then `e t ↦ φ'(e) res(t)` is the homomorphism out of `E'_D(k) ⋊ T_D(k)` that kills the antidiagonal copy of `H_D(k)`, and `E(k)` is the quotient by that copy.
