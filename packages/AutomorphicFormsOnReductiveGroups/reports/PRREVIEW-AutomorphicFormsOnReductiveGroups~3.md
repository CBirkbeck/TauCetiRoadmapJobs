## R01 (P2) — The trace of §7.4 is summed over the wrong coset space

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md` line 1205 ("when `[J:J']` is finite, the trace `(tr f)(g)=Σ_{J'u∈J'\J}f(gu)|u⁻¹`, well defined by the `J'`-covariance of `f`").

Claim: for `f∈S_Γ(J',M)` the term `f(gu)|u⁻¹` depends only on the coset `J'u`, so the sum over `J'\J` is well defined.

Witness: the covariance `f(gu u')=f(gu)|u'` for `u'∈J'` makes the term constant on `uJ'`, not on `J'u`. Take `G₀=J=S₃`, `Γ` trivial, `J'={1,(01)}`, `M=ℤ` with trivial action, so `f` is any function constant on the cosets `xJ'`: `{1,(01)}`, `{(012),(02)}`, `{(021),(12)}` with values `a,b,c`. The cosets `J'x` are `{1,(01)}`, `{(012),(12)}`, `{(021),(02)}`. The transversal `{1,(012),(021)}` of `J'\J` gives `(tr f)(1)=a+b+c`; the transversal `{1,(12),(021)}` gives `a+2c`. With `a=b=0`, `c=1` the two values are `1` and `2`. Over `J/J'` every transversal gives `a+b+c`, and `tr f` is then `J`-covariant: `(tr f)(gu₀)=Σ_{uJ'}f(gu₀u)|u⁻¹=Σ_{u'J'}f(gu')|u'⁻¹u₀=(tr f)(g)|u₀`. The identity `tr∘res=[J:J']·id` holds for either index set, because for `f∈S_Γ(J,M)` every term equals `f(g)`, so the C₂ Check at line 1218 does not detect the error (its two coset spaces coincide).

Repair: write the trace as `(tr f)(g)=Σ_{uJ'∈J/J'}f(gu)|u⁻¹` and say it is well defined because the term is constant on `uJ'`. Add the discriminating Check above (`S₃`, `J'={1,(01)}`, trivial coefficients, the two transversals with values `a+b+c` and `a+2c`). QuaternionicModularForms §2.1 at its head states "level inclusion/trace maps" without a formula, so nothing there fixes the convention either way.
