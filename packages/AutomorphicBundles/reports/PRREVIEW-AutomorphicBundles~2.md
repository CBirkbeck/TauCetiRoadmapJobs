The flat and locally-free clauses are only needed to identify the two *coefficient modules*, not to transfer the principle. As written, the partner's §7.15 (which states its map for arbitrary `M`) receives the expansion principle only conditionally.

Witness: 12.2 lines 4582–4590 (the identity and that `FJ^C` is defined for every `M`); 12.8 lines 4824–4827 (injectivity of `FJ^C_I` for every `R`-module `M`).
