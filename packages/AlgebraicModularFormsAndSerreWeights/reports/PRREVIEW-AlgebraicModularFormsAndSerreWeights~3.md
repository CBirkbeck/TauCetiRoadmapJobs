## R03 (P3) — The dyadic Ext step of §4.7 misdescribes Edixhoven's argument

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/AlgebraicModularFormsAndSerreWeights/README.md:1084` ("uses functoriality of Ext and the nonzero map from the constant `F`-scheme to its Cartier dual, as in the end of Edixhoven §8.3").

**Claim:** The dyadic comparison uses a nonzero morphism from the constant `F`-scheme to its Cartier dual.

**Witness:** `edixhoven1992` lines 1146–1149 (journal p. 589): the step uses Ext functoriality together with a nonzero morphism `𝔽_p → 𝔽` of the coefficient fields (equivalently of the constant group schemes `𝔽_{p,D} → 𝔽_D`), producing an extension of `𝔽_D` by the Cartier dual `𝔽_D^D`. No morphism from `𝔽_D` to its Cartier dual appears; such a map would change the shape (8.3.1) that the comparison is about.

**Repair:** Replace the clause by "uses functoriality of Ext along a nonzero `𝔽_p`-linear map `𝔽_p → F` of coefficient fields, as at the end of Edixhoven §8.3", and add the journal locator (§8.3, p. 589) next to the DVI page.
