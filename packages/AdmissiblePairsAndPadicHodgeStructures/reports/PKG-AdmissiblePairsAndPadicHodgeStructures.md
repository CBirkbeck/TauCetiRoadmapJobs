## Corrections and qualifications

1. Corrected the type-jumping disc examples to the C_p base and the fixed period generator of HKII Example 3.1.1, p. 21, HKII line 961. The Kummer-cover descent uses ε−1 divisible by ξ, so the changed pole coefficient is integral. A generator on a geometric cover does not automatically descend over every discretely valued base. The GL₃ parabolic jumping family is likewise stated over C_p. This separates these examples from the discrete-field type theorem.
2. Distinguished the integral Katz–Igusa Z_p× frame reduction from the full rational Q_p× crystalline-frame torsor. The latter is the contracted product with Q_p× and has valuation components indexed by Z; multiplication by p is a rational frame change and is not an integral frame change. HKII line 2885's identification needs this qualification under the rational-frame definition at HKII line 2860. The obstruction to rigid representability is local finite type, not just non-quasicompactness: an infinite profinite geometric frame fibre is nondiscrete. The supplier gap now requests the correctly normalized integral reduction comparison.
3. Corrected the Borel example's description of the e1 direction: for b=diag(p,1), its isocrystal slope is +1 and its associated bundle slope is −1. The inequalities a≠0 and c=0, and the different reversed-diagonal A¹ example, are retained.
4. Preserved the accepted sign corrections: weight equals twice Newton slope; the isocrystal bundle reverses Newton slope; pure weight w has bundle slope −w/2; Tate index n has étale/de Rham lattice exponents n and −n. The rank-one and two-term examples recompute these choices. In particular the printed sign in the basic-pair grading formula at HKI line 1960 does not override these definitions.
5. Made the arbitrary-field full-rank de Rham criterion explicit at the principal downstream theorem statements and in the summary. HKII lines 1904–1940 uses a rigidity theorem whose finite-Q_p base assumption is visible at LiuZhu line 86; LiuZhu lines 1154–1175 does not state the requested arbitrary-field replacement. Resolution addresses singularities, not this field scope. The criterion is a supplier obligation, and no claim of its proof is made here.
6. Retained the connected-base distinction for neutral subgroup reductions. The fixed H-isocrystal already exists globally; a disconnected base can instead change its étale H-fibre form in the nontrivial H¹ kernel. The norm-one torus test has class group Z/2, while ambient SL₂ has trivial H¹.
7. Retained the global isocrystal-frame obstruction for Hodge families, the neutral piece of a subgroup Hodge locus, and the even-weight Chevalley-line requirement. None is inferred solely from geometric fibre classification.
8. Retained the empty-index alternative for general analytic intersections, basicness for duality, the one-sided bounds for non-reductive groups, irreducibility in the bi-analytic definition, and nonbasic period-image density on the flag of the actual generic group.
9. Deleted the design's representative Frobenius identities, opposite-weight arithmetic, Baire consequence and arbitrary intersection signatures from the proposed Lean scope. They are neither deep headline theorems on the roadmap's carriers nor missing library foundations. Their mathematical witnesses remain in the README. All proposed API labels are prose, not claims of phantom Lean declarations.
10. Corrected the negative-exponent line-locus negative control: Fil^(−1)⊆Fil^0 is false, while B_dR+⊆Fil^0 is true. The replacement gives false positives, not the false rejection claimed by the design. The valid matching Tate line is accepted by both; the corrected Check uses the invalid exponent-zero model.
11. The crystalline-frame construction in §12.2 starts from a de Rham torsor, so its filtered comparison and good type come from the given de Rham datum and relative goodness; it does not need the arbitrary-field recognition criterion for an initially unfiltered neutral family. The proof dependency is §4.5 rather than §6.4. Uniformization applications of Ax–Lindemann retain that theorem’s explicit criterion.
12. Made the abstract DVR convention exclude the zero ring and fields by requiring a nonzero nonunit uniformizer. Characteristic-p DVR examples remain valid for the abstract bilattice layer. No p=2 restriction was introduced.
