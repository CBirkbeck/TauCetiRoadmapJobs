1. **F01** (P1) An almost-everywhere estimate does not bound the chosen Jacobian pointwise

   **Location.** `packages/SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds/README.md:1443`: “Then `J_f` is locally bounded, since `|J_f| ≤ ‖mfderiv f‖ⁿ` almost everywhere”.

   **Claim.** The Jacobian constructed from the chosen weak differential of a C¹ open embedding is locally bounded.

   **Witness.** On oriented flat ℝ take `f = id`, `D(x)v = a(x)v`, where `a(1/k)=k²` for integers `k≥2`, and `a=1` elsewhere. This allowed weak differential has Jacobian `a`, unbounded near zero.

   The exceptional set is countable and null. The package expressly permits null modifications of weak differentials (`README.md:1025`) and a total choice (`README.md:1031`); it only identifies them with the classical differential almost everywhere (`README.md:1358`). At the pin, `TauCeti.HasWeakFDerivOn.congr_ae_deriv`, `TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/WeakDeriv/Basic.lean:453`, preserves the weak-derivative relation under exactly this modification. Here the classical norm is 1, the essential supremum of `|J_f|` is 1, but `J_f(1/2)=4`, `J_f(1/10)=100`, and `J_f(1/100)=10000`. Thus the stated inference fails even for the identity map. The subsequent change-of-variables statement and its Lean signature are not refuted: their measures ignore the exceptional set, and the Lean statement uses the classical differential.

   **Exact repair.** Replace “`J_f` is locally bounded” by “`J_f` is locally essentially bounded”. Keep the a.e. estimate and explain that local essential boundedness and local finiteness of Riemannian volume give local integrability. Add the preceding null-modified identity as a Check distinguishing pointwise from essential boundedness. Tested on this instance, `J_f=1` a.e., `∫_[0,1] J_f=1`, `μ_f⁺([0,1])=1`, and `μ_f⁻([0,1])=0`, while pointwise boundedness still fails.

2. **F02** (P3) The discontinuity Check has no discontinuous member

   **Location.** `packages/SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds/README.md:956`: “Discontinuous members: `sign(x₁)` lies in every class on the open submanifold `ℝ² ∖ {x₁ = 0}`, with `Du = 0`, and in none on `ℝ²`”.

   **Claim.** This Check distinguishes the vector-valued class, which need not have continuous members, from a class requiring continuity.

   **Witness.** On the complement of the line, `sign(x₁)` is locally constant and continuous. On the whole plane it is discontinuous but excluded. Adding continuity to the definition leaves both membership answers unchanged.

   **Exact repair.** Replace this Check by the actual discontinuous member `u(x)=log|x|` for `x≠0` in ℝ², with `u(0)=0`, at `p=1`. Its weak gradient has norm `1/|x|`; on the unit disc `∫|u|=π/2` and `∫|Du|=2π`, so it belongs to the vector-valued class but is not continuous at zero. The wrong definition requiring continuity rejects it. The logarithm example already used in §1.2 supplies the same witness. The existing sign example can remain in §1.3 as a gluing negative control.
