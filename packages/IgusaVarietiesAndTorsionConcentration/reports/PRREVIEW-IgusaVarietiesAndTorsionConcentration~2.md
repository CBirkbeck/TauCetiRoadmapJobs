## R01 (P3) — 0.18a: "s even" does not remove the signs on an even-length cycle

Location: README line 440, "Take `s` divisible by the cycles of the combined weight-line and coefficient-embedding permutation, and even to remove the signs. On each cycle `Fˢ` is `pᵃσˢ`".

Claim: for `F = ẋσ` with `ẋ` a signed monomial matrix, `s` divisible by every cycle length and even makes `F^s` act on each coordinate line by `p^a σ^s`.

Witness: the README's own second Check of 0.18a (line 446): `F = [[0,−p],[1,0]]σ` has a single cycle of length 2, `s = 2` is even and divisible by the cycle length, yet `F² = −pσ²`; only `F⁴ = p²σ⁴` is sign-free. In general `ẋ^{mc}` on a cycle of length `c` is the `m`-th power of the product of the entries around the cycle, so a sign survives whenever `m = s/c` is odd.

Repair: replace "and even to remove the signs" by "and divisible by twice every cycle length (so that each cycle is traversed an even number of times), which removes the signs"; the Check already uses `s = 4`.
