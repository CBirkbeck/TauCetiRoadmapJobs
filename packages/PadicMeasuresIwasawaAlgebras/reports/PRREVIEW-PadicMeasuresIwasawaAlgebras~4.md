## C03 — The discrete case of the length statement is also asserted in AInfCohomology on main, and neither text points at the other

The scope bullet (lines 4117–4121) claims "the lengths that Fitting ideals compute at points of an arbitrary commutative ring with values in discrete valuation rings". AInfCohomology on `main`, §AI.1.3 (README line 856), computes Fitt₀ of ⊕ V/a_iV over a valuation ring as (a_1⋯a_s), and §AI.6.7 defines a real-valued normalised length as the valuation of Fitt₀, ordinary length in the discrete case. The objects differ (here `Module.length` over a discrete valuation ring of any characteristic, non-torsion modules and higher Fitting ideals included; there a normalised valuation over rank-one valuation rings), so each roadmap keeps its statement. One clause in the scope bullet would tell a reader where the other case lives: "the normalised length over rank-one valuation rings is AInfCohomology §AI.6.7". Raised on the previous head; not applied.
