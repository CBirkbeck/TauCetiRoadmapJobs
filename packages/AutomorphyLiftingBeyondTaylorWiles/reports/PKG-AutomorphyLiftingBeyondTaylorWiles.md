## Revisions and limits

All source fetches used the sources service. CG18 was read in the author-hosted published layout; CGH20 and KT17 in the exact preprint versions recorded by the manifest. Their cache line locators are retained in the README. The CG18 preprint and published paper are one work, so there is one lifetime key, not a second CG18v2 record. All three manifest records are certified. No missing legal text is being requested.

The following corrections affect the mathematics or a promised interface.

1. The diamond coordinate is det(B)a^{1−n}. An inertia-line character θ therefore sends deck δ to θ(δ^b), with b inverse to 1−n on the p-group. For n=3, p=5 the exponents are 3 and 2, whose product is 1 modulo 5. Using θ(δ) without this change was incompatible with the design's clause (6). The augmentation quotient is unchanged.
2. A selected polynomial root need not occur in the Hecke support. The ideal imposing that root is required to be proper when it is called maximal; otherwise its localized module is zero. The projector keeps every local factor where its polynomial is a unit. The later complexes now use the selected-factor projection as well as that projector, transported by adjunction on homology.
3. A point R_S→O′ has quotient its image order, not necessarily O′. Its characteristic-zero fibre is formed at the corresponding prime and then extended to the chosen coefficient field. It is not identified merely with H⊗_O E.
4. E denotes the coefficient field and K an adelic level. The native positive Hodge–Tate convention is expressed through the contravariant Fontaine–Laffaille dictionary; the ambiguous positive-index covariant de Rham graded-piece formula is not retained. The crystalline period object is rational, not an integral O-module.
5. The standing norm congruence is imposed only on R_triv. The stray stronger hypothesis on all of R in the Theorem 9.19 node is corrected. Balanced oddness is retained, with the n=2, p=3 identity negative control.
6. The generic finite-model theorem ALS.1.6 requires a full free cell action. Arbitrary orbifold levels can have p-torsion stabilizers even when p>n. This is not repaired by restricting primes or replacing groupoid cohomology by coarse cohomology. The localized perfect-model and duality extension is recorded as a supplier gap, as is finiteness of the simultaneous all-degree Hecke image at those levels. No proof is claimed from finiteness in each individual degree alone.
7. The rank-two amalgam glues adjacent vertices by h=diag(1,π), while g=diag(π,π⁻¹) is a distance-two element. The Lyndon second restriction uses h, not g. The compatible tree-chain extension is made explicit: its two vertex augmentations are q+1−q=1 and q(q+1)−q(q+1)+1=1, while the edge degree is q². This supplies a calculation missing from the design proof sketch. The normalized scalar is q⁻². A bare GgG=G observation by itself does not prove the sequence's equivariance. Integral U-eigenvalues retain norm denominators; only their residual reductions equal the arithmetic α,β.
8. The local tower used for smooth representations is a direct limit under pullback, not the design's inverse limit. The block condition is imposed on every irreducible subquotient, not only on subrepresentations; local admissibility is retained. The parahoric coordinate is transported together with its operator. The GLₙ/PGLₙ descent is a separate owned target, §3.7, rather than an assertion that their cohomology groups coincide. CGH20 Theorem 3.2's use of this comparison is stated with that qualification.
9. The finite-cutoff patching variant of DeformationAndDerivedPatchingAlgebra §8.6 asks for all coefficient/framing-quotient actions and diagrams. Layer 4 supplies them on the geometric finite coefficient systems before cohomology; it does not tensor an ordinary cohomology action through a nonflat quotient. A module O[[z]] --z→ O[[z]] acquires a new H⁰ after z=0. The paired cohomology-action version is a supplier gap; the derived-action variant cannot replace it without stronger input.
10. D_N reduced modulo ϖᴺ is perfect over S_N/ϖᴺ. It is not advertised as perfect over S_N. The cohomological indexing is reflection c↦q₀+ℓ₀−c; the kernel condition uses the original level M. The report retains the nonflat framing-quotient tests and the full top-degree reduction diagrams.
11. Total residual support does not immediately imply nonzero H^{q₀}. The nonzero minimal residual seed survives patching; codimension concentration gives a nonzero top patched module, and local Nakayama gives the nonzero augmented module. This avoids adding a hidden lowest-degree nonvanishing hypothesis to Theorem 9.19.
12. “Minimal level” means the precise hyperspecial-off-support/Iwahori-on-support cohomological predicate. Its conductor-n−1 interpretation uses regular single-block inertia; arbitrary unipotent partitions do not justify that conductor statement. The rank-two classical Check is restricted to squarefree conductor and the specified weight.
13. Restricting a conjecture at Q to a subset requires the actual level maps and corresponding clauses. An implication between arbitrary unrelated completed Hecke algebras is not asserted. The empty-Q bundle still contains the residual and completed Galois realization clauses.
14. R=T with a nonzero free module does not alone prove complete-intersection descent in positive defect. The design's unqualified complete-intersection Check has been replaced by the precise requirement of a regular augmentation sequence; the R=T and freeness targets are retained.
15. The finite character twisting at R is consistent with the original correspondence domain: generators can be chosen trivial at R, with arbitrary local units recovered by their coefficient-character intertwiner. Homology uses transfer/translation/pushforward, not a cohomological pullback/trace formula with the same labels.

16. The small-rank argument needs an upper bound on all localized degrees and finite-level duality before duality can prove the narrow interval. At arbitrary orbifold level these do not follow from manifold dimension alone. The supplier gap explicitly requests an independent 0≤i≤dim Y bound and duality at the standing big-image ideals; §2.8 names that input and does not infer it from the range it is proving.

The README cannot certify the eight supplier extensions listed in Exact supplier contracts; those needs are added only to the top-level gaps list of `design/plan.json`. They cover arbitrary orbifold models and duality, simultaneous Hecke finiteness, localized congruence-character H¹ vanishing, rank-n big-image Taylor–Wiles selection, the big-image predicate and detector, completed products of the literal character rings, paired cohomology-action patching, and the proper-support boundary-or-residual dichotomy. The last is not implied merely by isobaric realization. The number-field residual-block classification, by contrast, is present in AutomorphicSpectralTheory §4.14a and no gap is requested for it. The original self-owned PGLₙ source gap remains in the accepted design's gaps data and is expanded as target §3.7.

No notion has been imported from a later roadmap. The PGLₙ comparison is owned here because the accepted design routes it here and its consumer is later; it is not supplied by that consumer. The arithmetic two-system application is owned here, while its generic algebra remains with the earlier patching supplier.
