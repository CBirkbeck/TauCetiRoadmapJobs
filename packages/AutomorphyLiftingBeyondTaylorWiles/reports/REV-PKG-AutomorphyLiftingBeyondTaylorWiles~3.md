1. **F01** (P1) The cohomological Hecke correspondence represents the inverse double coset

   **Location:** `packages/AutomorphyLiftingBeyondTaylorWiles/README.md:189`, “(pullback, translation by g, trace)”; the identification with the characteristic function of the positive double coset is in the same paragraph. Its suppliers are cited at lines 193 and 229.

   **Claim:** The displayed cohomological composite is the action of `[KgK]` in the supplied Hecke convention. Put K_g=K∩gKg⁻¹ and L_g=g⁻¹Kg∩K. Right translation r_g goes from Y(K_g) to Y(L_g). The displayed middle cohomology arrow goes in that same direction, so it is the inverse of `r_g^*` (equivalently, pushforward along this isomorphism). With p₁ the natural projection from Y(K_g) and p₂ the projection after r_g, the displayed composite is `p_{2*} p_1^*`. ALS.3.5 instead supplies `p_{1*} c_g p_2^*`. Subscript stars denote trace; superscript stars denote pullback. These are inverse correspondences.

   **Witness:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces/README.md:2806` specifies the latter direction. For PGL₃, q=2 and Satake roots (2,3,1/6), T₁=31/3 and T₂=41/3. Inverting diag(2,1,1) exchanges these distinct operators.

   **Exact repair:** Define the cohomological action using the supplier's named geometric maps and coefficient morphism: `p_{1*} c_g p_2^*`. Equivalently, first pull back to Y(L_g), then apply r_g* to reach Y(K_g), then trace to Y(K). State the homology action and its adjoint separately, preserving the inverse character and adjoint ideal in §§0.5 and 4.1. Reconcile the deck-action convention in §§0.2, 1.4 and 2.2 with this choice, and check the oriented rank-two correspondence in §§3.3–3.5 against it. Keep the stated unnormalized characteristic polynomial. Add a rank-three negative-control Check: at p=5 and q=2 the intended polynomial is X³−(31/3)X²+(82/3)X−8, whereas the displayed inverse correspondence gives X³−(41/3)X²+(62/3)X−8. The repaired formula gives the first polynomial. The existing spherical rank-two Checks cannot detect the error because the relevant PGL₂ double coset is self-inverse. The finite cyclic direction test below also distinguishes the two maps without Satake theory.

   This is a new convention mismatch, not a re-raised settled finding. All nine findings in the last repair report are repaired as specified below. One finding, one blocking P1; no P2 or P3 findings. The package is repairable within its stated scope.
