## Source issues

All six are already in the design's register; I confirmed each in the text.

- **Error** in BS at Lemma 7.6, printed p.944: the source says the double-loop Betti formula holds for n>2; it should be n>3, since at n=3 the double loop of a bouquet of 2-spheres has ℤ^N components and the first factor degenerates; affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in BS at proof of Lemma 7.4, printed p.942: the source says the first trace sum uses compactly supported cohomology with ordinary weights q^(m−i); it should be ordinary cohomology H^i, dualised to H_c only afterwards; affects the proof.
- **Misprint** in BS at proof of Lemma 7.4, printed p.943: the source says the final Euler product has factors (1−qU^k) with no exponent; it should carry the exponent −e_k(N) on each factor; affects the proof.
- **Misprint** in BS at proof of Lemma 7.6, printed p.944: the source says the hypersurface is homotopic to a smash of N spheres; it should be the wedge of N copies of S^(n−1); affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in B19 at Lemma 7.3, printed p.98: the source says the integral lift is p*η plus ψ(ζ); it should be p*η minus ψ(ζ), because the class is η−φ(ζ) and ψ reduces to p*φ; affects a stated result.
- **Gap** in B19 at proof of Lemma 7.3, printed p.99: the source says two maps out of Q agree because Q injects into j_*j*Q; it should use injectivity of the target restriction p_*(ℤ/n)→j_*j*p_*(ℤ/n); affects the proof.
