## Draft pull request body

Develops the metric and arithmetic theory of Berkovich curves from norm-sensitive Banach spectra and the existing analytic-space foundations: rank-one semistable skeleta, all-point divisors and Riemann–Roch on metric graphs and metrized complexes, valued tropical multiplicities and faithful tropicalizations, model measures and local heights, and the skeleton–Jacobian and Berkovich–Coleman/abelian integration comparisons. The conventions and worked examples tie these developments to one logarithmic length, Laplacian and period normalization, including nonreduced fibers, inseparable degrees and arbitrary rank-one valuations.
