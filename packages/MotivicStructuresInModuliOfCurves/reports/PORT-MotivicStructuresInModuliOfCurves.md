## Draft pull request body

This roadmap develops the rational cohomology and Chow theory of the moduli stacks `M̄_{g,n}` of stable pointed curves from the operations that build it out of smaller moduli spaces: forgetful and clutching maps, cotangent lines and the Hodge bundle. On that base it builds tautological rings and kappa classes, the Arbarello–Cornalba boundary induction, the marking filtration and its local-system quotient, the semi-tautological extensions of Canning–Larson–Payne and their finite generation, and the Chow–Künneth generation property. That property is established on `M_{7,n}` through the tetragonal Hurwitz stack and Mukai's half-spin model of pentagonal curves. The point-count side covers the hyperelliptic and genus-four quadric sieve of Bergström–Faber–Payne, the weight-graded equivariant Euler characteristic, the Getzler–Kapranov formula, and the cusp-decorated graph bases in genera one and two. The end theorems are:

- odd cohomology of every `M̄_{g,n}` vanishes through degree nine;
- `M̄_{4,n}` has polynomial point count and pure Tate cohomology for `n ≤ 3`;
- in each degree only finitely many simple Hodge structures or Galois representations occur;
- `H⁴` is always tautological, `H⁶` is tautological for `g ≥ 10`, and even homology through degree fourteen is tautological;
- homology in degrees thirteen and fifteen is generated by the genus-one cusp classes;
- `H¹³`, `H¹⁴` and (for `g ≥ 2`) `H¹⁵` are sums of `L S₁₂`, `L⁷` and `L² S₁₂`, as Hodge structures and as Galois representations;
- the cohomology of `M̄_{3,n}` for `n ≤ 11` is a polynomial in `L` and `S₁₂`.

The roadmap consumes the moduli stacks, clutching and the boundary normalization from SchemeAndStackFoundations and StableReductionPartII, and Chow groups of stacks from VirtualFundamentalClass and EquivariantIntersectionTheory. Weights and purity come from DeligneWeightsAndPurity, the mixed Hodge theory of stacks from MixedHodgeModulesAndIrregularHodgeTheory, and stack point counts and the Hasse–Weil sieve from WeilConjectures. Nothing in it is statable at the pins, because neither library has stacks of stable curves. `Suggested.lean` is therefore a docstring that lists each headline theorem and the carrier it lacks.
