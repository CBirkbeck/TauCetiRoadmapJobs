1. **F01** (P1) §6.4: `−Ẽ(t,z)/E(t,z)` is not on the boundary of the Weyl disc

   Location: `design/README.md` line 1164–1165 ("`D_t(z)` is a closed disc of radius …, decreasing in `t`, with `−Ẽ(t, z)/E(t, z)` on its boundary"); the same sentence is the first API entry of target 6.4 in `design/plan.json` (line 1717) and the route sentence at line 1176 draws the same conclusion from `(u − iv)(t) = Ẽ + wE`.

   Witness: the boundary is `{w : h(t) = 0}` with `h = Im(conj u · v)`, `u = B + wA`, `v = D + wC`, i.e. the `w` with `u(t)/v(t) ∈ ℝ ∪ {∞}` (the points `−D/C`, `−B/A` and their real combinations).
