# Design report: K3SurfacesAndSymplecticBoundedness

The design consists of `packages/K3SurfacesAndSymplecticBoundedness/design/README.md`, the matching `design/plan.json`, and `packages/K3SurfacesAndSymplecticBoundedness/sources.toml`. It specifies all six endpoint families in the target, across eight layers and 50 targets. There were no merged-in targets to omit. Seven definitions have named APIs and at least three discriminating Checks each; the constructions also retain their universal, evaluation, base-change and compatibility interfaces. The plan contains proof sketches, explicit hypotheses and locators for every target. The final specification has 26 recorded gaps: a dependency gate success does not discharge their mathematical contracts.

Contraction sends an ordered two-frame to the signed dual frame, and the quartic cycle-pairing value is+4. These witnesses cover map direction, signs, half-norm and projective-dimension conventions.

No full Torelli theorem is used as a hidden proof floor for cohomological faithfulness. A cohomologically trivial automorphism first fixes an ample Picard class and hence has finite order; Hodge/crystalline comparison and the tame or wild quotient arguments then apply. No arbitrary automorphism is inferred trivial from identity characteristic polynomial alone.
