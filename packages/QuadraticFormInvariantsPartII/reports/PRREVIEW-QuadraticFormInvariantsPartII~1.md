## Source issues

- **Error** in dp at Proposition 3.2(3), proof of the Claim, printed p. 7: the source bounds the cohomological dimension of the local compositum by that of `E′`, then by `dim(E′) + 1`; for a formally real `E′` (for instance `E′ = ℚ`) the 2-cohomological dimension of `E′` is infinite, so the middle inequality is false; it should bound `cd₂(E′k′_{v′})` directly by `cd₂(k′_{v′}) + trdeg(E′/k′) ≤ t + 2` at finite places; affects the proof (the conclusion survives with the corrected bound).
- **Misprint** in dp at §3, definition of the characteristic-two Pfister form, printed p. 6: the outer factors are written with the quadratic two-angle notation; they should be the symmetric bilinear forms `⟨1, −aᵢ⟩_b` tensored with the terminal binary quadratic form; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in dp at proof of Proposition 3.2(2), printed p. 7: the subset-indexed coefficients are written as plain products of the `πᵢ`; with the stated convention `⟨⟨a⟩⟩ = x₁² − a x₂²` they carry the signs `(−1)^{|χ|}`; affects nothing (units).
- **Misprint** in dp at proof of Lemma 3.9(2), printed p. 11: the open set is called a neighbourhood of the quadratic form; it should be a neighbourhood of the valuation `w` in `Val_F`; affects nothing.
