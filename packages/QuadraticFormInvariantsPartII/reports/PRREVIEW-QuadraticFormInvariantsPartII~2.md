## R02 (P3) — 7.1 reads a henselian generator of `E` off SchemeAndStackFoundations §0.12, which gives only `E ⊆ F(η)`

**Location.** `README.md` lines 1950–1953, 7.1: "A finite subextension of `F_w^h` lies in an étale neighbourhood (`SchemeAndStackFoundations` §0.12), so `E = F(η)` with `η` integral, monic minimal polynomial `p` with `w`-integral coefficients and `w(p′(η)) = 0`".

**Claim.** The colimit description of §0.12 (the henselization of a pair as a filtered colimit of étale neighbourhoods with trivial residue extension; SchemeAndStackFoundations README line 969 ff.) places the finitely many generators of `E` in one étale local `O_w`-algebra `B`, and the standard-étale structure of `B` (Stacks 00UE) gives `B = (O_w[X]/(g))_h` with `g` monic and `g′(x̄)` a unit, hence `E ⊆ Frac(B) = F(x̄)`. It does not give a generator of `E` itself with a simple residual root: that stronger statement is what Dittmann–Pop cite for this step, "by Hilbert decomposition theory ([K-No], Thm 1.2), `E = F[η]` with `w(η) = w(p′(η)) = 0`" (dp lines 501–503), a theorem of Kuhlmann–Novacoski that the README neither cites nor proves. The choice matters: `η′ = 1 + 7√2` generates `ℚ(√2) ⊆ ℚ^h_7`, is `7`-integral, but its minimal polynomial `(X − 1)² − 98` reduces to `(X − 1)²` modulo `7`, so `w(p′(η′)) = w(14√2) > 0`; a generator must be chosen, and §0.12 chooses `x̄`, a generator of the possibly larger field `F(x̄)`.

**Witness.** dp line 502–503 (the cited [K-No, Thm 1.2]); SchemeAndStackFoundations README line 969–1031 (§0.12 states the colimit and the universal property, nothing about generators of subextensions).

**Repair.** The openness proof needs only the weaker statement, so restate the step: "every finite subextension `E` of `F_w^h` lies in `F(η)` for the generator `η` of a standard étale neighbourhood (§0.12 with Stacks 00UE): `η` is a root of a monic `g ∈ O_w[X]` with `g′(η) ∈ (O_w^h)ˣ`, so its minimal polynomial `p` over `F`, a monic factor of `g` in `O_w[X]` by Gauss's lemma, has `w(p′(η)) = 0` because `g′(η) = p′(η) · (g/p)(η)`; since `F_w^h/F` is immediate there is `x ∈ F` with `w(x − η) > 0`, hence `w(p(x)) > 0` and `w(p′(x)) = 0`, and on the clopen set `{ŵ(aᵢ) ≥ 0, ŵ(p(x)) > 0, ŵ(p′(x)) = 0}` the polynomial `p` has a simple Hensel root in `F_ŵ^h`, which embeds `F(η) ⊇ E`." Alternatively keep `E = F(η)` and cite Kuhlmann–Novacoski, *Henselian elements*, Theorem 1.2, adding it to `sources.toml`; the first repair needs no new source. Tested on the README's own instance: `F = ℚ`, `w` the `7`-adic valuation, `η = √2` (the standard étale generator of `ℤ_(7)[X]/(X² − 2)`), `p = X² − 2`, `x = 3`, `p(3) = 7`, `p′(3) = 6`, the clopen set `{ŵ(7) > 0, ŵ(6) = 0}` of the 7.1 Check.

- **Misprint** in dp at §3, definition of the characteristic-two Pfister form, printed p. 6: the outer factors are written with the quadratic two-angle notation; they should be the symmetric bilinear forms `⟨1, −aᵢ⟩_b` tensored with the terminal binary quadratic form; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in dp at proof of Proposition 3.2(2), printed p. 7: the subset-indexed coefficients `π_χ` are written as plain products of the `πᵢ`; with the stated convention `⟨⟨a⟩⟩ = x₁² − a x₂²` they carry the signs `(−1)^{|χ|}`; affects nothing (units).
- **Misprint** in dp at proof of Lemma 3.9(2), printed p. 11: the open set is called a neighbourhood of the quadratic form; it should be a neighbourhood of the valuation `w` in `Val_F`; affects nothing.
