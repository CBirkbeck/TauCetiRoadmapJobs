**R02, the rewritten route of 7.1, step by step.**
1. *Generators at one stage.* SchemeAndStackFoundations §0.12 on main (README line 969 ff.) defines an étale neighbourhood of `(R, I)` as an étale `B` with `R/I → B/IB` bijective. It defines the henselization as the filtered colimit of these, and `local_henselization` identifies `algebra m` with the henselization of a local ring. LocalFieldsRamificationPartII §GF.1 (board design line 248) sets `K^h = Frac(V^h)`, immediate over `K`. Every element of `F_w^h` is therefore `a/c` with `a ∈ O_w^h` and `c ∈ O_w ∖ 0`. Generators of `E` can be taken in `O_w^h`, and filteredness puts finitely many of them at one stage `B`.
2. *Standard étale chart.* `B/m_wB ≅ κ_w` makes `m_wB` the unique prime over `m_w`. Mathlib `Algebra.IsEtaleAt.exists_isStandardEtale` (`RingTheory/Unramified/LocalStructure.lean:376`, tagged Stacks 00UE) gives `g ∉ m_wB` with `B[1/g]` standard étale. It needs only `[FinitePresentation R S] [IsEtaleAt R Q]`, with no Noetherian hypothesis, so it applies over a valuation ring of any rank. Since `g` is a unit modulo `m_wB`, it maps to a unit of the local ring `O_w^h`, so `B → O_w^h` factors through `B[1/g]`.
3. *The pair.* `StandardEtalePair` (`RingTheory/Etale/StandardEtale.lean:48`) has a monic `f` and `cond : ∃ p₁ p₂ n, f′ p₁ + f p₂ = g^n`, so `f′(η)` is a unit at the image `η` of `X`. This matches the README's gloss "a monic `f` and a `g` with `f′` invertible in `R[X][1/g]/(f)`" and the Mathlib docstring at line 46.
4. *Inclusion.* The image of `B[1/g]` is `O_w[η, g(η)⁻¹] ⊆ F(η)`, hence `E ⊆ F(η)`.
5. *Integrality of `p`.* A monic factor `p` of `f` over the integrally closed `O_w` has coefficients in `O_w`, and so does the cofactor `f/p`.
6. *Simple root.* From `f′(η) = p′(η) · (f/p)(η)`, with both factors in `O_w^h` and the product of value `0`, it follows that `w(p′(η)) = 0`.
7. *Approximation.* The residue fields of `O_w` and `O_w^h` agree, which gives `x`.
8. *Neighbourhood.* `ŵ(x) ≥ 0` is implied, because a monic integral `p` with `ŵ(x) < 0` has `ŵ(p(x)) = deg p · ŵ(x) < 0`. So Hensel's lemma applies on the clopen set, and an `F`-embedding of `F(η) ≅ F[X]/(p)` restricts to `E`.

Each step is correct at the generality stated: arbitrary rank, any residue characteristic, the trivial valuation included. The route now needs neither the stronger `E = F(η)` nor Kuhlmann–Novacoski. The parenthetical attribution matches dp lines 499–503: dp obtain `E = F[η]` with `w(η) = w(p′(η)) = 0` "by Hilbert decomposition theory", citing Kuhlmann–Novacoski, Theorem 1.2. It is a remark on the source's route and supports no claim. The README's step is the repair proposed in the previous report, carried out with Mathlib's local structure theorem in place of a bare Stacks tag.
