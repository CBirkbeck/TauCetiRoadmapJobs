## R08 (P3) — §1.7's proof route rests on a remark; the `+1` is redundant

Witness: Bennett–Siksek print the log-free form "Σ_{q≤Q}Σ*_χ N(σ,T,χ) ≪_ε (Q^{5+ε}T^{3+ε})^{1−σ}" (bennettsiksek2020 lines 1420–1429, p.386) attributed to "a result of Selberg (referenced immediately below … Théorème 14 of [7])"; neither text proves it.
