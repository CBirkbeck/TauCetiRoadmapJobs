## R01 (P2) — The Kosters–Wan conductor formula (§4.4) is statable at the pins but declared not statable

**Location.** `TauCetiRoadmap/FunctionFieldArithmetic/Suggested.lean`, lines 26–28: "the Kosters–Wan conductor formula (§4.4) needs those reduced representatives and the field `K(℘⁻¹x)` as a finite separable extension carrying the pinned conductor exponent". The README states the target at §4.4, lines 1527–1535 (`aswConductor_eq`).

**Claim.** The docstring lists this theorem among the remaining headline theorems and says it is not statable. Both of its stated reasons fail:
- The formula is a finite-length statement. At length `n` a reduced representative has only finitely many pole terms (README §3.2, lines 1161–1162), so no convergent sum in `W(k((T)))` is needed.
- The pinned conductor exponent asks nothing of the extension beyond being a finite separable field extension.

So the headline is statable and absent.

**Witness.** Tau Ceti `TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Conductor/Basic.lean:80–82`: `conductorExponent K L` takes only `[Field L] [Algebra K L] [FiniteDimensional K L] [Algebra.IsSeparable K L]`. The statement below elaborates with `lake env lean`, and `sorry` is its only warning.

1. Add the theorem below to `Suggested.lean` under `Layer 4`, with the imports `TauCeti.NumberTheory.ClassFieldTheory.Local.Conductor.Basic` and `TauCeti.RingTheory.LaurentSeries.LocalField`. Both modules exist at the pin; the second had to be built first with `lake build`.

3. In README §4.4, say that `π_n(x)` involves only the finitely many terms with `v(c_i) < n`, so that the README's generality equals the Lean statement, and backtick `aswConductor_eq`.
