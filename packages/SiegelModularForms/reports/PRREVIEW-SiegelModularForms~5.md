## R01 (P1) — The standard-pole criterion is stated as an "iff" that is false, and its "rank `2k`, not `2g`" paragraph contradicts the source it corrects

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/SiegelModularForms/README.md:2126`–2132 ("the pole at `s = 1` occurs exactly when `F` lies in the span of the theta series … of rank `2k` … The rank is `2k` … and not `2g`"), repeated at 2177 and 2195.

**Claim:** Membership in the span of unweighted rank-`2k` theta series does not force a pole, so the polar divisors exported in 8.4–8.5 are wrong; and the source states the sufficient direction only, with rank `2g`.

**Witness:** Genus one (the standing hypothesis is `g ≥ 1`), `k = 12`: `Δ = (θ_{E₈³} − θ_{Leech})/720` lies in the rank-24 span, while `L(s, Δ, st)` is the symmetric-square `L`-function of a form with no nontrivial self-twist, entire by `gelbartjacquet1978 line 2151` (Theorem 9.3(1)): pole order at `s = 1` is `0`, not `1`. `vdg line 1770`–1773 says only "holomorphic *if* the eigenform does not lie in the space generated by theta series coming from unimodular lattices of rank `2g`"; those are the harmonic theta series of `vdg line 673`–686, so "rank-8 theta series have weight `4 ≠ 8`" (README 2132) forgets the pluriharmonic coefficients the roadmap itself defines in 5.3.

**Repair:** State the sufficient direction as the source has it (holomorphic off the span of rank-`2g` lattice theta series with pluriharmonic coefficients, 5.3), delete the converse and the "not `2g`" paragraph, and make the exact criterion (Böcherer's harmonic theta-image criterion, which `LevelOneAutomorphicFormsForClassicalGroups` LO.6/boecherer-criterion already names in a specialized form) a target with the hypotheses of the primary source once it is read; until then no exact polar divisor may be exported. The repaired statement assigns `Δ` no pole and keeps the pole of `L(s, I(Δ), st)`, whose `ζ(s)` factor is checked below.

## R02 (P1) — Two lost signs make the displayed `χ₁₉` non-modular

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/SiegelModularForms/README.md:1683`: "`θ' := (θ₁₀₀₀¹² + θ₁₀₀₁¹² + θ₁₁₀₀¹² + θ₁₁₁₁¹²)/1536`".

**Claim:** The second and third terms carry minus signs.

**Witness:** Recomputed with 15×15 lattice sums: at `τ = i[[1.2, 0.2],[0.2, 1.5]]` the displayed `χ₁₉` is `−4.3217·10⁻¹⁴` and after `τ ↦ τ + diag(0, 1)` (an element of `Γ₀(2)` with automorphy factor `1`) it is `+4.2994·10⁻¹⁴`; with signs `(+, −, −, +)` both values are `−1.08445·10⁻¹⁴`. `X, Y, Z, K, T` are invariant in both runs. `ibukiyama1991 line 593` prints the pattern `(+, −, −, +)`.

**Repair:** Use `θ' := (θ₁₀₀₀¹² − θ₁₀₀₁¹² − θ₁₁₀₀¹² + θ₁₁₁₁¹²)/1536` and add this translation test as a Check.

## R03 (P1) — The diagonal-restriction exact sequence has the wrong kernel

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/SiegelModularForms/README.md:1476`: "`0 → χ₁₀ M_{k−10}(Γ₂) → M_k^{≥2n}(Γ₂) → Sym²(M_{k+2n}^{≥n}(Γ₁))`".

**Claim:** The kernel of the restriction of the `z^{2n}` coefficient is `M_k^{≥2n+2}(Γ₂)`, not a term independent of `n`.

**Witness:** `k = 10`, `n = 1`: `χ₁₀ = Δ(τ₁)Δ(τ₂) z² + O(z⁴)` (`vdg line 878`), so its image under the `z²`-coefficient map is `Δ ⊗ Δ ≠ 0`, yet it lies in the displayed kernel. `vdg line 875` has `0 → M_k^{≥2n+2}(Γ₂) → M_k^{≥2n}(Γ₂) → Sym²(M_{k+2n}^{≥n}(Γ₁)) → 0`.

**Repair:** Replace the sequence by the source's, with surjectivity for even `k`, and derive the divisibility by `χ₁₀` afterwards.

## R04 (P1) — Containing some `Γ_g(N)` does not make a subgroup of `Sp_{2g}(ℝ)` arithmetic

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/SiegelModularForms/README.md:561`: "every subgroup containing some `Γ_g(N)` is arithmetic".

**Claim:** The declared class is subgroups of `Sp_{2g}(ℝ)` commensurable with `Γ_g`; the statement quantifies over all of them.

**Witness:** `Γ = Sp₂(ℝ) ⊇ Γ₁(1)`; `Γ ∩ Γ₁ = SL₂(ℤ)` has infinite index in `Γ`, so `Subgroup.Commensurable Γ Γ_g` is false.

**Repair:** State it for `Γ_g(N) ≤ Γ ≤ Γ_g` with `N ≥ 1` (the knowl's congruence subgroups) or add finite index of `Γ ∩ Γ_g` in `Γ` as a hypothesis; `Γ₀(2)` still passes with index `15`.

## R05 (P1) — A Gaussian with an arbitrary positive `Y` is not a Fourier eigenfunction

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/SiegelModularForms/README.md:1290`: "`P(X) exp(−π tr(XᵀX Y))` an eigenfunction of the Fourier transform".

**Claim:** The transform replaces `Y` by `Y⁻¹` and multiplies by `det(Y)^{−m/2}`.

**Witness:** `m = g = 1`, `P = 1`, `Y = 2`: the transform of `exp(−2πx²)` is `2^{−1/2} exp(−πξ²/2)`; its ratio to `exp(−2πξ²)` is `0.7071` at `ξ = 0` and `78.71` at `ξ = 1`.

**Repair:** State the Gaussian transform identity with `Y⁻¹`, the determinant factor and the transformed polynomial, and reserve "eigenfunction" for `Y = 1` with the harmonic degree.

## R06 (P1) — Odd-weight cancellation is argued on a quotient where the summand is not well defined

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/SiegelModularForms/README.md:1058` and 1067: "for odd `k` the sum is `0` since `(C, D)` and `(−C, −D)` both occur".

**Claim:** `(C, D)` and `(−C, −D)` are one `GL_g(ℤ)`-orbit, and for odd `k` the summand depends on the representative, so the quotient sum is not a function.

**Witness:** `g = 2`, `k = 5`, the identity coset: representatives `(0, 1₂)` and `(0, diag(−1, 1))` give `det(Cτ + D)^{−5} = 1` and `−1`; also `det(−1₂) = 1`, so negating a representative does not change the sign in even genus.

**Repair:** Define the series for even `k` only and replace the odd-`k` Check by this failure of representative independence.
