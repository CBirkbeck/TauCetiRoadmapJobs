## R03 (P1) — Disjoint-union monodromy need not be a product

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/BelyiMaps/README.md:703`: “monodromy the product acting componentwise.”

## R04 (P1) — The spherical parameter classification omits valid parameters

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/BelyiMaps/README.md:1418`: “the parameter triples are exactly (1, m, m)”.

Claim: Positive ordered parameters with positive orbifold characteristic have the displayed five forms, and their group order is always `2/χᵒʳᵇ`.

Witness: `(a,b,c)=(1,2,3)` has `χᵒʳᵇ=5/6`. Its presentation forces `x=y=z=1`, so its group order is 1, not `12/5`.

Repair: Include every (1,b,c), with group C_gcd(b,c). Restrict 2/χ to admissible exact-order families, explicitly. Keep general presented groups and add (1,2,3): order gcd(2,3)=1.

## R05 (P1) — The trace argument includes a false rotation criterion
