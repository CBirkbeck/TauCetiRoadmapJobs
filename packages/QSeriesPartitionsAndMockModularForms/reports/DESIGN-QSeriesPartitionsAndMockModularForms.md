# Design report: QSeriesPartitionsAndMockModularForms

The design is in `packages/QSeriesPartitionsAndMockModularForms/design/README.md` and `packages/QSeriesPartitionsAndMockModularForms/design/plan.json`. It has 11 layers, 65 targets (33 definitions, 6 constructions and 26 theorems), 236 named public laws and 190 discriminating Checks. Every definition and construction has at least three Checks. `packages/QSeriesPartitionsAndMockModularForms/sources.toml` records 17 works: 12 certified, 3 uncertified and 2 waived.

The mathematical specification is supplied for independent review with **nine explicit gaps**. They are not assumed facts, discharged hypotheses, or implementation milestones. In particular, this is not a claim that every endpoint has a closed proof chain. Every chain either reaches a declaration read at its pin, an identified supplier section, an earlier target, or one of these gaps. No merged target was supplied in the brief, and none has been silently dropped.

## Mathematical corrections and evidence

- The large-base unilateral radius retains the factor q contributed by `(q;q)_n`. At q=2,a=1/2 the radius is 4; the adjacent-term calculation contradicts the smaller radius suggested by the extraction at Gasper1995 line 780. Zero parameters change the degree of the ratio and remain included.
- Integer-index products and regular quotient coefficients have different domains. At q=2, Q₋₁([3];[2])=0, although the individual denominator P₋₁(2;2) has a pole. The unilateral/bilateral definitions stop before an inadmissible 0/0 rather than evaluating it.
- The ordinary crank is −1 for the singleton partition. Its coefficient is z⁻¹; the weighted vector coefficient is z+z⁻¹−1. The exact correction is `(1-z)X`, and ordinary symmetry excludes size one.
- Powers of 7 use floor(r/2)+1. The integer witness p(243) is 245 modulo 343 and zero modulo 49, agreeing with Watson1938 line 108. No stronger uniform exponent is asserted.
- Odd theta has derivative −2πη³, R is even, and the μ-to-m adapter contains exp(−πiτ/4). The thesis and survey μ functions differ by i. These facts fix the completion coefficient and the xi normalization.
- Q⁺ in Zwegers2002 Lemma 2.6, line 1409, requires two strictly negative independent cone vectors. For the two primitive hyperbolic boundary vectors the same expression is zero, not positive definite. Boundary convergence uses an auxiliary negative vector and mixed estimates; it is not narrowed away.
- Bruinier–Funke §2 assumes ambient dimension at least three (BruinierFunke2003 line 190). Adding two even unimodular hyperbolic planes preserves the discriminant module and signature modulo eight, so the design states the stabilization comparison explicitly instead of silently dropping the assumption. The k≠1 hypothesis remains on the quoted Fourier and surjectivity results; the general definition includes the independent log y mode at k=1.
- The full exponential-growth harmonic space can have a polar shadow. The weight-zero modular function conj(j−744) has zero normalized holomorphic part and nonzero polar shadow. Consequently standard mock holomorphic-part faithfulness is not asserted on that whole space. Its correct holomorphic-shadow version, with representation hypotheses, is an explicit gap.
- At higher Jacobi poles, the mixed residue is a Wirtinger derivative. A contour residue of a nonholomorphic product is not silently substituted. The sgn(λ+1/2) convention retains R_m,0(0)=1.
- The first third-order completion is q^(−1/24)f−2C(1/6,0;3τ), fixed by BringmannOno2006 Theorem 2.1, line 308. It is not obtained by blindly adopting the illustrative coefficient at Zagier2009 line 352.
- Direct splitting of the bilateral rank sum at Zagier2009 line 937 gives the signs and q^(−1/6) factor in target 11.2. The expression displayed in the cached extraction at Zagier2009 line 948 disagrees with this normalization. At q=0.1, α=1/5, the corrected uncompleted Lerch expression had absolute discrepancy about 1.45·10⁻¹⁸, while the extracted expression differed by about 1.88. This is a diagnostic finite-tail computation, not a proof or a claim that optical inspection located a typesetting error. The independent ξ=−1 completion supplies a second sign/scale witness.
