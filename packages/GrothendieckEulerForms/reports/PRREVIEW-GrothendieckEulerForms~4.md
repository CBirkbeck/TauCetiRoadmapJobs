## R01 (P3) — "no hypothesis on the grading of `A`" overstates the pinned forward lemma

**Location.** `TauCetiRoadmap/GrothendieckEulerForms/README.md` lines 400–403: "Forward: a linear resolution of `M` forces `Ext^i(M, N{j}) = 0` for `i ≠ j` whenever `N` is concentrated in internal degree zero, with no hypothesis on the grading of `A` (landed as `TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear.subsingleton_ext_of_ne`)".

**Claim.** The pinned forward direction needs nothing of the grading of `A`.

**Witness.** The elaborated type of `IsLinear.subsingleton_ext_of_ne` at the pin (`Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/LinearResolution.lean:73`) carries `[DirectSum.Decomposition 𝒜]` and `[HasExt (GradedModuleCat 𝒜)]`, and its target is a module concentrated in one degree `j` rather than a shift `N{j}` of a degree-zero module; the file's header (lines 19–21) claims only "neither a field nor a nonnegative algebra grading". The `N{j}` form is the separate pinned `IsLinear.subsingleton_ext_shiftObj_of_ne` (line 80), which the README does not name.

**Repair.** Replace "with no hypothesis on the grading of `A`" by "with no nonnegativity hypothesis on the grading of `A`, assuming only that `𝒜` is a direct-sum decomposition of `A`", and cite the `N{j}` form by its pinned name `TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear.subsingleton_ext_shiftObj_of_ne` next to `subsingleton_ext_of_ne`. Both names elaborate at the pin (scratch `#check`); the converse paragraph that follows needs no change.
