## Corrections and limits

1. **Duality direction:** the design wrote the cohomological scalar on the dual Ψ map in the same direction. Starting with ΦB = x¹⁻ⁿA∨Φ gives B∨Ψ = x¹⁻ⁿΨA. The README now states T_hom Ψ = x¹⁻ⁿΨT_sections, equivalently ΨT_sections = xⁿ⁻¹T_hom Ψ. At n = 2, x = 2, A = 2 and B = 1, the true equation is 1 = (1/2)·2; the old direction would give 2 = 1/2. At n = 1 the downstream specialization is unchanged. Evidence: cg18 line 1059–1064 and AlgebraicModularFormsAndSerreWeights R15.3, Twisted duality and the supersingular boundary.
2. **Exact contemporary sections:** the ordinary eigenvalue ring is LocalGaloisDeformationRings §6.7, its type-three theorem §6.18 and the ideal equality §7.5; the old L7/L8 labels are not used as section numbers. Away-p complete intersections are §2.19. Balanced modules and patching are DeformationAndDerivedPatchingAlgebra §§5.1 and 5.5. Full-Hecke Jacobian sequence and Katz witness are SerreWeightAndLevelOptimisation §§3.10 and 3.13. These locators were read at the supplied heads.
3. **Patching systems:** no transition maps between unrelated original Taylor–Wiles prime sets are imposed. The actual finite coefficient and deck maps preserve the displayed actions and coinvariant identifications; compatible cutoffs are supplied by the existing algebra theorem. The original-level maps from A∞ may differ. For the Hasse-choice API, equality of maps is asserted only with a fixed high-weight target and equal all-cusp expansions; different target weights do not give equal functions by definition.
4. **Missing supplier generality is explicit:** the integral ordinary source states its generic determinant theorem for O-flat Hecke families and distinguishes torsion/nonflat extensions. Its required p-anaemic Hasse specialization is registered, not inferred from reduced eigenform points. The orbifold source ALS.5a.6 assumes an invertible-order neat-cover quotient; the weaker invertible-stabilizer-order interface needed here is separately registered. Its failure to state the needed interface is not a claim that tame orbifold duality is false.
5. **Local conditions are spelled out:** the README gives CG18 Definition 3.1's four minimality clauses, the ordinary εχ₁/χ₂ filtration with unramified characters, finite-flat precedence over the local arithmetic integer ring, and the S/P/M/H lift conditions on every Artinian quotient. Invariant dimension alone, a nearly ordinary character, or finite flatness over the coefficient ring is insufficient.
6. **Three original mathematical gaps remain:** M is variable-determinant scalar type comparison, O is localized 3-primary stabilizer homology, P is the integral principal-type coefficient model. The general scope retains all three, through exact supplier contracts. Neither this package nor CG18 supplies a proof of those repairs. The general scalar type-three/multiplicity statements and the p = 3/principal-type Bianchi conclusions are expressly conditional on them. Conjecture A remains an all-auxiliary-level hypothesis, including nilpotents and the matched coefficient spaces.

No notion was moved from a later roadmap. All named suppliers are on main or earlier than this roadmap in the supplied order. The generic missing constructions remain at their earlier mathematical owners; fourteen supplier-gap entries record their exact needs. The original M/O/P entries keep their detail and acquire slug/supplier/section/needs fields; eleven additional entries expose missing specializations. No claim is made that numerical Checks prove those supplier gaps.
