## C01 — The target "sheaves representable after base change" (§1.1a) allows any subcanonical `J`, but Lemma 35.39.1 (02W5) is stated without the fpqc topology

A reader may take the locator to cover an fpqc `J`. The bullet (README lines 3007–3016) lets `J` be any subcanonical topology in which the sieve generated by `p` covers; for `p` quasi-compact and not locally of finite presentation that is an fpqc-type topology. Stacks states 02W5 for the Zariski, fppf, étale, smooth and syntomic sites only, and its footnote says the omission of fpqc is deliberate (stacks-project book, Descent, Lemma 35.39.1, p. 3066–3067).

The statement itself is true for every such `J`, by the argument the bullet gives: `T ×_S S' → T` generates a covering sieve of each `T → S`, and for a sieve generated by one map with pullbacks the sheaf condition is the equalizer `F(T) → F(T') ⇉ F(T' ×_T T')`, along which the identification of base changes descends. Both uses in this README (§1.2 and §1.9a) are fppf. Either add "(stated there for the Zariski, étale, smooth, syntomic and fppf sites; for general `J` it is the sheaf-condition argument above)", or restrict `J` to `Scheme.fppfTopology`.

## C02 — The second clause of `fibre_dimension_semicontinuity` (§0.7) is contained in the first, and its locator says something else

The target reads "for `f` flat and locally of finite presentation the loci `{ x : dim_x X_{f(x)} ≤ n }` are open" (README lines 1180–1181). That is already the first clause: upper semicontinuity for `f` locally of finite type is the openness of exactly these loci (Lemma 29.29.4 (02FZ)), so flatness and finite presentation buy nothing, and an implementer has nothing to prove for the clause. The locators cited for it say different things: Lemma 29.29.6 (02G0) is that, for `f` locally of finite presentation (no flatness), the open `{dim ≤ n}` is retrocompact in `X`; Lemma 29.29.5 (0A3V) is that fibre dimensions are bounded for `f` of finite type over a quasi-compact base; Lemma 29.29.3 (02FY) is invariance under base change.

The smallest repair is to state what the locators give: "for `f` locally of finite presentation these opens are retrocompact (02G0), and for `f` of finite type with `Y` quasi-compact the fibre dimensions are bounded (0A3V)". If a statement special to flat morphisms was meant, it needs its own locator.

## N01 — In §1.1a's affine bullet the reduction of the locally finitely presented case ends at affine `S` and `S'`, where Tau Ceti already proves effectiveness

Tau Ceti's `TauCeti.DescentDatum.exists_isAffine_baseChange_hom_isIso` (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Affine.lean:532`: `S`, `S'`, `X` affine, `p` flat) applies at the end of that reduction. Saying so would show that the fppf chain (§§1.2, 1.9a) does not wait on ModularCurves 0E.
