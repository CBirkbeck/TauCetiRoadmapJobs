## C01 — The new target "sheaves representable after base change" (§1.1a) allows any subcanonical `J`, but Lemma 35.39.1 (02W5) excludes the fpqc topology

A reader may take the locator to cover an fpqc `J`. The bullet (README lines 3007–3016) lets `J` be any subcanonical topology in which `{p}` covers. For `p` quasi-compact and not locally of finite presentation, that is an fpqc-type topology. Stacks states 02W5 only for the Zariski, étale, smooth, syntomic and fppf sites, and its footnote says the omission of fpqc is deliberate (Descent, §35.39, book p. 3066).

The statement is still true for every subcanonical `J`, by the argument the bullet gives. Each `T → S` is covered by the sieve generated by `T ×_S S' → T`. For a sieve generated by one map, the sheaf condition is the equalizer `F(T) → F(T') ⇉ F(T' ×_T T')`. So the identification of base changes descends. Both uses in this README (§1.2 with `Scheme.fppfTopology`, and §1.9a along an étale surjection) are fppf.

Either of these would fix the citation:

- add "(stated there for the Zariski, étale, smooth, syntomic and fppf sites; for general `J` it is the sheaf-condition argument above)";
- or restrict `J` to `Scheme.fppfTopology`.

## N01 — In the locally finitely presented case of §1.1a's affine bullet, the reduction ends at affine `S` and `S'`, where Tau Ceti already proves effectiveness

Tau Ceti's `TauCeti.DescentDatum.exists_isAffine_baseChange_hom_isIso` (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Affine.lean:532`) applies there. Saying so would show that the fppf chain (§§1.2 and 1.9a, through quasi-affine and quasi-finite descent) does not wait on ModularCurves 0E. Only the quasi-compact, non-finitely-presented case would then need 0E in §1.1a's *Needs:* line.
