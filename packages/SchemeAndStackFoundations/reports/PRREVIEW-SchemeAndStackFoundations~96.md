## C01 — The route for `integral_point_descent` (§0.9) cites EGA and Stacks for two steps the pinned Mathlib proves, and its *Needs:* line names neither

An implementer following the route in §0.9 (README lines 1299–1302) is sent to Stacks, Lemma 29.42.9 (03GN) and EGA IV₃, Theorem 8.11.1, and would reprove both. Mathlib at the pin has them:

- `AlgebraicGeometry.UniversallyClosed.of_comp_surjective` (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/UniversallyClosed.lean:75`): if `f ≫ g` is universally closed and `f` is surjective then `g` is universally closed; with `S' → S` integral this is the properness of `Z → S`.
- `AlgebraicGeometry.IsFinite.of_isProper_of_locallyQuasiFinite` (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/ZariskisMainTheorem.lean:373`): a proper locally quasi-finite morphism of schemes is finite.

The *Needs:* line (line 1307) lists only `Limits`, `LocallyOfFinitePresentation` and `Scheme.Hom.normalization`. Naming the two declarations in the route, beside the citations, and in the *Needs:* line would be enough. This is the small correction of the previous review; the head has not changed since.

## C02 — The *Needs:* line of §1.22a omits what the last step of *IsCohomologicallyAffine.iff_isAffine* uses

The route for Serre's criterion ends (README lines 3913–3916) with "so `H¹(X, F) = 0`, and `X` is affine by Stacks, Proposition 69.16.7 (07V6)". That proposition is about the cohomology of quasi-coherent modules on a quasi-compact quasi-separated algebraic space, so the step needs §1.10 (the small étale site of an algebraic space), §1.11 (quasi-coherent modules on it) and the sheaf cohomology of that site (Mathlib's `Sheaf.H`, as in §2.1). The *Needs:* line of the subsection reads "§1.1, §1.9, §1.18–§1.22, §2.6", so an implementer of this one target cannot tell from it that those two sections come first. Adding "§1.10, §1.11" and Mathlib `Sheaf.H` to the line would repair it; no cycle arises, since neither section depends on §1.22a, and the sentence of the Layer 1 dependencies on §2.6 stays true.
