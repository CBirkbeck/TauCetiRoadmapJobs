## R01 (P2) — The Fréchet open mapping theorem is owned by LocallyAnalyticDistributions §0.2 and is neither cited nor distinguished

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/SpectralManifolds/README.md` line 82 ("Prove the open mapping theorem `isOpenMap_of_surjective`: a continuous surjective linear map between complete metrizable Hausdorff topological vector spaces over ℝ or ℂ is open … Local convexity is not needed, so Fréchet spaces are the locally convex case"); the supplier table lines 19–36 (no LocallyAnalyticDistributions row); `TauCetiRoadmap/SpectralManifolds/Suggested.lean` lines 12–19 (docstring: "Mathlib at the pin proves the open mapping theorem for normed spaces … and for σ-compact topological groups …; neither covers the statement") and lines 40–57 (`isOpenMap_of_surjective`).

Claim: the README presents SM3 as the owner of the open mapping theorem for complete metrizable spaces over ℝ or ℂ and names only Mathlib as prior art; the Lean file states it as the roadmap's headline.

Witness: LocallyAnalyticDistributions (merge order line 63, before SpectralManifolds at line 214; open pull request #854, a new roadmap folder, head 444887a) states in its §0.2, README line 1107, "**Fréchet open mapping** (theorem; `naFrechet_openMapping` in Suggested.lean). A continuous surjective K-linear map between Fréchet spaces over a complete nontrivially valued field is open … the statement carries no ultrametric hypothesis on K or on the seminorms", and its `Suggested.lean` line 73 declares `naFrechet_openMapping` for `[NontriviallyNormedField K] [CompleteSpace K]`, uniform additive groups `V`, `W` with `T2Space`, `MetrizableSpace`, `CompleteSpace` and `WithSeminorms` families, `(f : V →L[K] W)`, `Function.Surjective f → IsOpenMap f`. With `K = ℝ` or `ℂ` this is exactly the statement at every instance this roadmap uses it on: H∞ with its `Λ^m` seminorms (SM1), the δ-seminorm completeness and Sobolev comparison of 𝒜 (SM3 line 88), the nuclear Hilbert scales (line 90) and the closed-subspace quotients (line 82), all locally convex. The only consumer outside the roadmap, AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII (its README line 219), consumes SM3 "for the open mapping theorem between complete metrizable locally convex complex spaces", again the locally convex case. What LocallyAnalyticDistributions does not state is the non-locally-convex F-space form (its carriers carry `WithSeminorms`) and the closed graph theorem (no closed graph statement in its README or Lean file).
