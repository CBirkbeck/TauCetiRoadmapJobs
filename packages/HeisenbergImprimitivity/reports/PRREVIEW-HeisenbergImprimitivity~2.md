## R01 (P2) — Euclidean lattice Poisson summation is planned by ThetaSeries Layer 1 on main and is restated here

**Location.** `README.md` line 25 (Scope: "the Schwartz–Bruhat space S(M), its Fourier theory, lattice Poisson summation and the fundamental identity (Layer 2)" owned here), lines 101–103 ("nothing multidimensional or for a general lattice exists at the pin"), §2.4 lines 323–325 and 330–332; `Suggested.lean` lines 52–56 (`symplecticFourier`) and 82–92 (`poisson_summation`).

**Claim.** `poisson_summation`: for a lattice `L ⊆ ℝ^k × ℝ^k` and Schwartz `F`, `Σ_(d∈L) F(d) = covol(L)⁻¹ Σ_(e∈L^⊥) (F_sF)(e)`, stated as a headline of this roadmap, with the Scope and the Mathlib contract presenting lattice Poisson summation as unowned elsewhere.

**Witness.** `TauCetiRoadmap/ThetaSeries/README.md` on main, lines 56–59: Poisson summation for a full-rank ℤ-lattice in a finite-dimensional real inner product space, for Schwartz functions, with summability and the covolume factor, is owned there, and "anyone who needs Poisson summation on a lattice consumes it from here"; Layer 1 (lines 423–447) states it as 1A with 1C (Fourier change of variables under a linear automorphism), 1D (dual-lattice transport, `covolume (A L) = |det A| covolume L`) and 1E (the two summability halves); `TauCetiRoadmap/ThetaSeries/Suggested.lean` declares `poissonSummation` (line 154), `summable_poisson_left`/`summable_poisson_right` (160, 164), `fourierIntegral_comp_equiv` (110), `dual_dual` (243) and `covolume_dual` (355). This roadmap's statement is 1A at `v = 0` in symplectic coordinates: with `J = [[0,I],[−I,0]]`, `σ(x,e) = e(⟨x,Je⟩)`, so `F_sF(e) = 𝓕F(−Je)`; `e ∈ L^⊥` iff `Je ∈ L^∨` (the Euclidean dual lattice), and `|det J| = 1`, so `Σ_(e∈L^⊥) F_sF(e) = Σ_(m∈L^∨) 𝓕F(−m) = Σ_(m∈L^∨) 𝓕F(m)`. Computed on the README's own Check (k=1, `L = ℤ×2ℤ`): `L^∨ = ℤ×(1/2)ℤ`, `J⁻¹L^∨ = (1/2)ℤ×ℤ = L^⊥`, covolume 2 on both routes. The README's §2.4 says this itself ("`F_sF(e)=(𝓕F)(−Je)` … transported to `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (2k))`"), which is precisely the consumer-side transport ThetaSeries' 1A docstring assigns to consumers whose space is not an inner product space (`(Fin k → ℝ) × (Fin k → ℝ)` carries the sup norm).

**Repair.** (1) Add `**From TauCetiRoadmap.ThetaSeries.**` to the supplier contracts: Layer 1 (1A `poissonSummation`, 1C `fourierIntegral_comp_equiv`, 1D, 1E `summable_poisson_left`, `summable_poisson_right`), 2A `dual_dual`, 2C `covolume_dual`. (2) In Scope, say that Euclidean lattice Poisson summation is consumed from ThetaSeries Layer 1, and that this roadmap owns the symplectic transport (`F_sF(e) = 𝓕F(−Je)`, `L^⊥ = J⁻¹L^∨`, `c_L c_(L^⊥) = 1` from 2C and `det J = 1`) and the finite-type statement of §2.3, whose `ℝ^k` factor consumes 1A. (3) Rewrite §2.4's first bullet as that transport target with `*Needs:* ThetaSeries Layer 1, 2A, 2C`, and make §2.3's `*Needs:*` name ThetaSeries 1A for the Euclidean factor. (4) Delete `poisson_summation` and the then-unused `symplecticFourier` from `Suggested.lean` and drop the Poisson clause from the module docstring (lines 16–17); `fundamental_identity`, `exists_parsevalFrame` and `parsevalFrame_trace` stay, as this roadmap's own. ## R04 (P3) — Unused hypothesis in the gauge-invariant-ideal bullet of §3.1

**Location.** `README.md` lines 384–385: "Every nonzero gauge-invariant closed ideal of `C*(L,c)` with L finitely generated is the whole algebra".

**Claim.** Finite generation of L is needed.

**Witness.** The argument given (the gauge average of a positive element is `τ(a)1`) uses only that `L̂` is compact, which holds for every discrete L. Instance without the hypothesis: `L = ℚ` discrete, `c ≡ 1`, `C*(ℚ) ≅ C(ℚ̂)` with the gauge action the translation action of the compact group `ℚ̂` on itself; a translation-invariant closed ideal of `C(K)` for a compact group K acting transitively is 0 or everything, so the conclusion holds. Finite generation is used in §5.1 (Lie group `L̂`, differentiation), not here.

**Repair.** Drop "with L finitely generated" from the bullet.
