## Scope

The roadmap owns the fully explicit numerical layer of multiplicative number theory on Mathlib's
`ArithmeticFunction` carrier: every inequality below is stated with its constant and threshold for
real arguments, with `f = g + O*(h)` meaning `|f − g| ≤ h`, and every large computation a source
relies on is isolated as a named `Prop` hypothesis that the theorem takes as an argument. It owns
the general conversion theorems behind the numbers (Balazard's identities, Ramaré's prime-to-Möbius
transfer, the comparison theorem of the convolution method, the explicit Levin–Fainleib theorem for
an arbitrary nonnegative multiplicative function), and the numerical instances as corollaries.

What it does not own:

- the explicit prime estimates (ψ, θ, π, Σ_{p≤x} 1/p, Σ_{p≤x} log p/p, Σ_{n≤x} Λ(n)/n with
  constants): the sibling target `ExplicitPrimeCounting` (#6066), consumed in Layers 1 and 8;
- zero-free regions, the explicit formula, zero counting and the semantics of verified zeros:
  `ZerosOfLFunctions` on main (Layers 6–9), consumed only through the explicit-primes roadmap;
- the explicit Pólya–Vinogradov and Burgess inequalities and the explicit sieve bounds: the
  sibling `ExplicitCharacterSumsAndSieves` (#6062). Layer 4 here takes a Pólya–Vinogradov bound as a
  parameter, so that nothing is consumed from that sibling (it consumes Layer 9 here, see
  Suppliers);
- the asymptotic theory already planned: Mertens' theorems with O(1) remainders, the ineffective
  Möbius cancellation, the divisor subpower bound τ(n) ≤ C_ε n^ε, the asymptotic maximal order of
  τ(n), the O(√x) Dirichlet divisor asymptotic and the asymptotic coprime Möbius sums of
  `AnalyticNumberTheoryPartI` (#1126, §§0.11, 0.19, 0.21, 0.25, 0.26, 0.31, 1.2, 3.1, 3.7, 3.9, 3.12,
  3.22, 3.26, 3.35); the asymptotic Levin–Fainleib mean value and the GGPY uniform dimension-one
  form of `SieveMethodsAndPrimePatterns` §1.1 and its weighted Rankin bounds §0.5; the Rankin-type
  bound for f(p^ν) = f(p) ≥ 1 of `ProbabilisticAndMetricNumberTheory` Layer 3. These are cited by
  section where a qualitative input is needed and never re-planned;
- any computational algorithm (sieving M(x), locating zeros, evaluating constants): the pages on
  exact computation contribute verified values only, as hypotheses, and `ComputationalNumberTheory`
  (#1408) Layers CN.4–CN.5 is where a hypothesis is discharged by certified computation.

Conventions fixed for the design:

- Carriers. `M D = Σ_{d ≤ D} μ d`, `m D = Σ_{d ≤ D} μ d / d`, `m̌ D = Σ_{d ≤ D} μ d · log(D/d) / d`
  for real `D ≥ 0`, as sums over `Finset.Icc 1 ⌊D⌋₊`; `Δ x = Σ_{n ≤ x} τ n − x (log x + 2γ − 1)`,
  `R x = Σ_{n ≤ x} μ² n − 6x/π²`, `R₁ X = Σ_{n ≤ X} 1/n − log X − γ`; `τ_k = ζ^{⋆k}`
  (`ArithmeticFunction.zeta` convolved k times), so `τ_1 = 1`, `τ_2 = τ = σ 0`; `σ* n` the unitary
  divisor sum. Coprimality restrictions are stated for the multiplicative semigroup ⟨S⟩ generated by
  an arbitrary set `S` of primes (Tao's generality), with `q/φ(q)` read as `∏_{p ∉ S}(1 − 1/p)^{−1}`
  over the finitely many excluded primes; the modulus form `(d, q) = 1` is the instance `S = {p : p ∤ q}`.
  Character twists use `DirichletCharacter ℂ q`.
- The statement shape of explicit inequalities (constant, threshold, named hypothesis on verified
  data) is the one of `AnalyticNumberTheoryPartI` §§0.3–0.5 and `ErdosProgressionPowers` §0.9
  (`…FiniteRangeInput` Props discharged elsewhere). The PNT+ repository PrimeNumberTheoremAnd is not
  a pinned dependency (`lake-manifest.json` lists only TauCeti and mathlib): its Chebyshev, Abel
  summation and harmonic lemmas are consulted for API shape only; nothing is imported from it.
- Computational hypotheses, one `Prop` each, with the verified range as data:
  `MoebiusTableVerified c H : ∀ D, 33 ≤ D → D ≤ H → |M D| ≤ c √D` (instances `c = 0.571`,
  `H = 10^12, 10^14, 10^16`); `MoebiusCoprimeInitialRangeVerified` (the finite check over an initial
  range of `D/q` behind the Ramaré 2015 and de Camargo 2025 coprime constants);
  `StieltjesGammaOneValue : |γ₁ + 0.0728158454836767248605863758749013191377| ≤ 10^{−40}`;
  `SquarefreeRemainderInitialRangeVerified`, `DivisorRemainderInitialRangeVerified` (the finite
  ranges of `R` and `Δ` checked by Cohen–Dress–El Marraki, Ramaré 2019, Berkane–Bordellès–Ramaré,
  Cully-Hugill–Trudgian); `ArithmeticalConstantsValues` (enclosures of `c₀`, `Σ_p log p/(p²−1)`,
  `A₃`, `A₄`); `MeisselMertensConstantValue`; `BordellesProgressionProductTableValid`;
  `SuperiorHighlyCompositeTablesValid`; and `EulerGammaEnclosure`, an enclosure of Euler's constant
  to the digits the thresholds need (Mathlib knows only `1/2 < γ < 2/3`), which the design may
  instead prove from `ComputationalNumberTheory` §4.17a's explicit digamma remainders. Verified zeros
  of ζ up to height T are the Prop *AllOnCriticalLine* of `ZerosOfLFunctions` Layer 9 on the
  rectangle `Icc 0 1 ×ℂ Icc 0 T`, named `RiemannZerosVerified T` by the explicit-primes roadmap and
  never restated here.
- Mathlib's constants are used where they exist: `Real.eulerMascheroniConstant`,
  `hasSum_zeta_two` (6/π²), `pi_gt_d6`, `exp_one_gt_d9`, `log_two_gt_d9`;
  `Chebyshev.psi`/`theta`; `sum_mul_eq_sub_sub_integral_mul`; `harmonic_le_one_add_log`.

Layers (as filed, with the corrections noted):

- Layer 0: carriers, `R₁` and the sharper harmonic remainder, `Σ_{q≤x} q = x²/2 + O*(x^c/2)`,
  Euler-product formulas for `H(0), H'(0), H''(0)` of a Dirichlet series `H(s) = Σ h(n) n^{−s}`.
- Layer 1: `M(D)`: elementary linear bounds (MacLeod, Costa Pereira, Dress–El Marraki,
  Cohen–Dress–El Marraki), decaying bounds (Schoenfeld, El Marraki, Ramaré 2013), coprime variants
  (Ramaré 2015 with corrigendum; de Camargo 2025).
- Layer 2: `m(D)`: the uniform bound ≤ 1 over any ⟨S⟩ (Tao), its mollification (Ramaré 2013b),
  thresholded and decaying bounds (Ramaré 2015, El Marraki, Bordellès), coprime variants (Ramaré
  2014, de Camargo), Treviño's tail bound; Balazard's conversion theorem from `M` to `m`.
- Layer 3: `m̌(D)` (Ramaré 2015), including the coprime positivity statement.
- Layer 4: Möbius twisted by a non-principal character (Bordellès 2015, Proposition 3), stated for
  general χ with a Pólya–Vinogradov bound `max_N |Σ_{n≤N} χ(n)| ≤ P(q)` as a hypothesis.
- Layer 5: the convolution method: comparison theorem and its weighted variant (Ramaré 1995,
  Berment–Ramaré 2012), the models `k = 1` and `k = τ` (Berkane–Bordellès–Ramaré Corollary 2.2),
  the second-model theorem (Ramaré 2016 Lemma 4.3), beyond the main term (Akhilesh–Ramaré 2017),
  explicit Levin–Fainleib (Ramaré 2009 Theorem 21.1).
- Layer 6: upper bounds for nonnegative multiplicative functions (trivial product bound,
  Daboussi–Rivat Lemma 4, Hall–Tenenbaum Theorem 01, Bordellès Theorem 4.28, van Lint–Richert).
- Layer 7: special averages of μ², μ²/φ, τ, τ_k, τ², τ_4, 2^ω, φ, nφ.
- Layer 8: explicit Euler products over primes (Rosser–Schoenfeld, Dusart, Vanlalngaia, Bordellès
  2005), proved from the explicit Mertens sums consumed from the explicit-primes roadmap, or, in the
  general theorem, from hypotheses of that shape.
- Layer 9: pointwise bounds for ω(n), τ_k(n), σ(n), σ*(n) (Robin, Nicolas–Robin, Duras–Nicolas–Robin,
  Teo, Nicolas 2008, Ivić, Eum–Koo).

## Risks

- **Supplier not yet placed.** The numerical instances of T1.2, T2.2, T3.1 and T8.1–T8.2 need the
  explicit `ψ` and Mertens-sum constants of the sibling `ExplicitPrimeCounting`, still in triage.
  Mitigation built into the targets: the general theorems T1.3 and T8.1 take those estimates as
  hypotheses, so the roadmap can be designed and its general theorems proved before the sibling
  lands; only the final constants wait.
- **Unreadable sources.** Fifteen works are paywalled or unpublished (list above). The explicit
  Levin–Fainleib theorem (Ramaré 2009, a book), Hall–Tenenbaum Theorem 01 (a book), Bordellès'
  Theorem 4.28 (a book), the Cohen–Dress 1988 preprint and the El Marraki 1996 preprint carry
  statements that the design can only take from the issue text and the TME-EMT wiki unless a human
  names a legal copy; their constants must be marked as unverified in the README until then.
  Ramaré 2015's constants are read from the author's offprint and corrected by the 2019 corrigendum
  (AMS open archive); de Camargo 2025 is behind the AMS paywall.
- **Numerical constants in Lean.** Thresholds like `2 160 535` and constants like `0.0144` require
  certified real arithmetic (`norm_num` with `Real.log`/`Real.exp` bounds, Mathlib's `pi_gt_d6`,
  `exp_one_gt_d9`); an enclosure of Euler's constant beyond `1/2 < γ < 2/3` does not exist in
  Mathlib and is either a hypothesis or a theorem via ComputationalNumberTheory §4.17a. The design
  must budget for this.
- **Hypothesis granularity.** Several papers verify finite ranges by computation without stating
  the verified predicate as a theorem (Hurst's `0.571 √x` bound is reported, not numbered); the
  design must phrase each `Prop` from the computation actually described, not from the wiki's
  summary, and keep one `Prop` per distinct computation.
- **Interaction with AnalyticNumberTheoryPartI.** That pull request already plans Rosser–Schoenfeld
  numerics under `…FiniteRangeInput` Props; the explicit-primes sibling must reconcile with it, and
  this roadmap should consume whichever lands first for T8.1's reciprocal-prime input without
  stating the sum again.
