## Source issues

- **Error** in BHS19 at Lemma 2.3.2, printed page 315: the source says a morphism from a CM Noetherian scheme to a regular one with fibres equidimensional of dimension `dim Y − dim Z` is flat; it should require the local equality `dim O_{Y,y} = dim O_{Z,z} + dim O_{Y_z,y}` (or `Y` equidimensional of finite type over a field), since a point disjoint union a line over `A¹` is a counterexample; affects the proof (the applications in the paper have equidimensional sources).
- **Gap** in BHS19 at Proposition 2.3.3, proof, printed page 316: the source says `X_{A,w}` is CM because it is `A`-flat with CM special fibre; it should also treat generic-fibre points whose closure misses the special fibre (for instance by the `G_m`-scaling into the special fibre), since `Spec A ⊔ Spec ℚ[x,y]/(x²,xy)` is `A`-flat with CM special fibre and not CM; affects the proof.
- **Error** in BHS19 at Lemma 2.4.8 and Remark 2.4.9, printed page 324: the source says the cycle `[L(ww₀·0)]` is invariant under `P_w(k)` acting by left translation on the first flag; it should say the cycle is saturated along the fibres of `G/B × G/B → G/P_w × G/B` (equivalently, its slice over the base flag is stable under `P_w` acting on the second flag and by `Ad` on `ψ`), since for SL₂, `w = e`, `P_e = B` and `u = ((1,1),(0,1))` the point `(ke₂, ke₂, 0)` of `Z_e` moves to `(k(e₁+e₂), ke₂, 0) ∉ Z_e`; affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in BHS19 at Lemma 2.5.2, proof, printed page 326: the source says the generic point `η̂_{T_w}` lies in `Spec Ô_{X,x}`; it should be `Spec Ô_{T,(κ₁,κ₂)(x)}`; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in M23 at Theorem 2.11, proof, printed page 3: the source says the inclusion `F_pD_U ⊆ F′_pD_U` is the easy one that follows from the examples; it should be `F′_pD_U ⊆ F_pD_U` (monomials have bounded order), the reverse inclusion being the content of the theorem; affects nothing.

- **Misprint** in M23 at Proposition 6.24, proof, printed page 50: the source says `Tor₁^R(N, R/(z)) ≃ N/zN = 0`; it should say `Tor₀^R(N, R/(z)) ≃ N/zN = 0`, the next sentence correctly using `Tor₁` for `N₀ = ker z`; affects nothing.
