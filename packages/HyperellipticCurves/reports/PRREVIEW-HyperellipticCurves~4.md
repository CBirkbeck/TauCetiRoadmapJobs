## R01 (P1) — Subtract `ε_ℛ` from the toric part only for an übereven root cluster

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/HyperellipticCurves/README.md:1763`: “`H¹_t = ⊕_𝔰 Ind_{G_𝔰}^{G_K} ε_𝔰 ⊖ ε_ℛ` over orbit representatives of even non-übereven clusters”; convention 12 at line 330: “for odd `|ℛ|` the term `⊖ ε_ℛ` of 4.7 removes nothing”.

**Claim:** The toric part of `H¹_ét(C)` is the induced sum over even non-übereven clusters minus `ε_ℛ`, for every hyperelliptic `C/K`, and the subtraction is void whenever `|ℛ|` is odd.

**Witness:** Over `ℚ_p`, `p ≥ 5`, take `y² = (x−1)(x−p)(x−2p)(x−3p)(x−4p)`. The picture is `ℛ` (depth 0) with children `{1}` and `𝔰₀ = {p,2p,3p,4p}` (depth 1). `ℛ` has odd size 5 and a child of size `2g = 4`, so by convention 12 it is a cotwin with `ℛ^* = 𝔰₀` and `ε_ℛ = ε_{𝔰₀} ≠ 0`.

The two sentences quoted contradict each other on this curve: convention 12 defines `ε_ℛ ≠ 0` for a cotwin and then asserts it is `0` because `|ℛ|` is odd. Computed values: the curve is semistable (4.4: `K(ℛ) = K`, `d_ℛ = 0`, `ν_ℛ = 0`, `d_{𝔰₀} = 1`, `ν_{𝔰₀} = 4`); 4.5(7) gives potential toric rank `#{𝔰₀} = 1` (`ℛ` not übereven); 4.9(iii) gives `n_C = 1`; the 5.4 toric factor is `1 − ε_{𝔰₀}(Frob)T`, degree 1; the special fibre of 4.6 is `Γ_{𝔰₀}` (genus 1, four odd children) with one loop of `2δ_{𝔰₀} = 2` edges (the cotwin row of the 4.6 table). The 4.7 formula as written gives `Ind ε_{𝔰₀} ⊖ ε_ℛ = ε_{𝔰₀} ⊖ ε_{𝔰₀} = 0`, dimension 0. The source has the same slip: DDMM Theorem 10.1 and Corollary 9.6(2) print `⊖ ε_ℛ` unconditionally, but the proof of 9.6(2) (ddmm lines 2519–2531, p. 54) subtracts `ε_ℛ` only when `B ≠ ∅`, i.e. when `ℛ` is übereven, and the last step there silently assumes `ε_ℛ = 0` for odd `ℛ`, which fails for an odd cotwin.

**Repair:** In 4.7 write `H¹_t = ⊕_{𝔰 ∈ A/G_K} Ind_{G_𝔰}^{G_K} ε_𝔰 ⊖ [ℛ übereven] · ε_ℛ` with `A` the even non-übereven clusters other than `ℛ` (the set already used in 4.5, 4.9 and 5.4), and in convention 12 replace “for odd `|ℛ|` the term removes nothing” by “the term is present only for übereven `ℛ`; a cotwin `ℛ` has `ε_ℛ = ε_{ℛ^*}` but nothing is subtracted”. On the witness the repaired formula gives `ε_{𝔰₀}`, dimension 1, agreeing with 4.5(7), 4.9(iii) and 5.4; for the übereven three-twin picture it still gives `3 − 1 = 2`; for a good quintic it gives `0`. Add the witness as a Check of 4.7 and record the DDMM slip in the references entry.
