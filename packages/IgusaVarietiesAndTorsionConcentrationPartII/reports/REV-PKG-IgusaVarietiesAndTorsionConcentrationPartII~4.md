1. **F01** (P1) Integral localization need not be free

   **Location:** `packages/IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentrationPartII/README.md:360`, §8.9(2): “for every maximal ideal”; also its proof at line 364, API at line 370, and §11.8 at lines 667–675. The integral lifting argument in §11.4 needs the same localization convention.

   **Claim:** A finite free integral cohomology module remains free over ℤ_ℓ after localization at any maximal ideal of the abstract integral Hecke algebra. Mod-ℓ concentration alone then implies integral concentration and freeness.

   **Witness:** Rank-one torus, ℓ=3, constant Hecke eigencharacter λ:𝕋^S→ℤ, and 𝔪=λ⁻¹(2ℤ): the localized constant submodule is ℚ₃, whereas its mod-3 localization is zero. A nonzero 3-divisible ℤ₃-module is not free.

   For completeness, take the package's Case 1 datum B=F=𝒦=ℚ(i), any neat level, and its nonempty finite set of geometric points. The constant function embeds ℤ₃ into H⁰. Every torus double coset acts on constants by 1, giving λ and the maximal ideal above 2. Exactness of localization preserves the constant injection; its localization is ℤ₃⊗ℤℤ_(2)=ℚ₃. Moreover 3 is inverted on the entire localized cohomology module. That module is nonzero and has zero quotient by 3; no nonzero free ℤ₃-module has that property. Every mod-3 localized group is zero, so §11.8's written hypothesis holds, including when d=0. Its freeness conclusion still fails. The source's Remark 1.3 refers back to the residual eigensystem of Theorem 1.1 (CS17 lines 89–103), not to arbitrary maximal ideals in other residue characteristics.

   There is a second precision issue in the proposed proof: the finite algebra must be the **ℤ_ℓ-algebra** generated by the operators, and localization must mean its local summand, not ordinary localization of an integral action followed by coefficient extension. For A=ℤ[i], M=A⊗ℤℤ₅ and 𝔭=(5,i−2), ordinary A-localization gives M_𝔭≅ℤ₅×ℚ₅. Indeed i+2∉𝔭 is a unit on the root congruent to 2 and has positive valuation on the conjugate root; inverting it leaves a rational factor. The completed local summand is ℤ₅ instead.

   **Exact repair:** Restrict integral residual localization to maximal ideals containing ℓ (or λ for O_λ coefficients). Define it explicitly as Rlim_r of the localizations with coefficients ℤ/ℓʳ, equivalently the corresponding idempotent summand for the finite ℤ_ℓ Hecke-action algebra at finite level. Use this convention in §§8.9, 11.4 and 11.8 and in their reduction and duality assertions. In §11.8 explicitly say that 𝔪 is maximal and contains ℓ; nonzero residual support may be assumed as in the main theorem, but is unnecessary once the completed-summand convention is fixed. Correct the proof at line 364 accordingly, and add the different-characteristic and split-coefficient controls.

   **Repair tested:** The ideal above 2 is excluded from the ℓ=3 integral assertion; its finite-coefficient localized inverse limit is zero. The ideal above 3 retains the constant ℤ₃ summand with reduction 𝔽₃. For A=ℤ[i], ℓ=5, the corrected local summand is ℤ₅, reduction 𝔽₅, instead of ℤ₅×ℚ₅. Thus the repaired modules are finite free and Nakayama applies.
