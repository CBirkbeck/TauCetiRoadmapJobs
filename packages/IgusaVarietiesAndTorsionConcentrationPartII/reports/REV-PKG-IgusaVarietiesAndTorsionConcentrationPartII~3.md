1. **F01** (P2) Wholly definite PEL data do not satisfy the Hodge-type supplier hypothesis

   **Location:** `packages/IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentrationPartII/README.md:555`, “a PEL datum of type (A) is of Hodge type”. The same missing qualification affects the canonical-model citation at line 105, §8.1's Shimura-variety identification, and the period-map application at lines 561–568.

   **Claim:** Every datum admitted in §8.1 can use PerfectoidShimuraVarieties §§2.2 and 3.5 as a Hodge-type Shimura datum. The package explicitly retains every signature, including wholly definite signatures of rank at least two.

   **Witness:** For a wholly definite rank-two datum, h(z)=zI₂, so h has trivial adjoint projection, although G^ad has dimension 3. SV3 fails. PELModuli explicitly excludes this case from its Shimura-datum adapter.

   A Case 1 realization is available inside the stated scope. Let D/ℚ be the quaternion algebra ramified precisely at ∞ and 5, let F=𝒦=ℚ(i), and take B=D⊗ℚF. Since 5 splits in F, B retains local index 2 at the two places above 5 and is a division algebra. Quaternion conjugation tensored with complex conjugation is a positive involution of the second kind; the regular left B-module with its associated positive PEL form gives the wholly definite rank-two datum. Its group is compact modulo centre over ℝ and anisotropic modulo centre over ℚ. Thus §8.1 does not exclude it. Its signature is (2,0), d=0, and μ is central. Conjugation by h is trivial on the nontrivial adjoint group, violating SV3, the condition that h project nontrivially to every ℚ-simple adjoint factor.

   This is a concrete supplier mismatch, not an inference from terminology. At open PR #966, `TauCetiRoadmap/PELModuli/README.md:948` (§M0.12), especially lines 954–968, requires connectedness and SV3 for the Shimura-datum and Hodge-type adapters and discusses this exact wholly definite exception. Its §M4.3 at line 3247 requires SV3 for the canonical-model identification. At open PR #1085, `TauCetiRoadmap/PerfectoidShimuraVarieties/README.md:1043` (§2.2) and line 1296 (§3.5) assume a Hodge-type Shimura datum. Properness or anisotropy does not imply SV3. The parent’s compact perversity input, `TauCetiRoadmap/IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration/README.md:1953` (§4.4, PR #1090), also assumes Hodge type.

   **Exact repair:** Keep all signatures. Distinguish the general PEL moduli problem from its Shimura-datum adapter. Apply PELModuli §§M0.12 and M4.3, PerfectoidShimuraVarieties §§2.2 and 3.5, and the Hodge-type perversity argument only on the SV3 branch. For the wholly definite higher-rank branch, add a named comparison target for the zero-dimensional PEL tower and its Hecke actions: the finite levels over C are finite étale; their inverse limit is the profinite set T of compatible points, represented by Spa(C⁰(T,C),C⁰(T,O_C)); the central cocharacter has flag variety a point, giving its unique equivariant period map. Cite the existing general profinite-product construction, `TauCetiRoadmap/PerfectoidSpaces/README.md:14460`, §P6.8 on main, rather than rebuilding it. State the identification with the PEL level tower and the required Igusa/cohomology comparisons explicitly, or prove the zero-dimensional concentration branch directly from finite-level cohomology. Use PELModuli §M3.4 for the arithmetic quotient without calling the SV3-failing datum a pure Shimura datum. Propagate the branch distinction through §§8.1–8.2 and 11.2–11.4 and their Needs lines; do not leave an unconditional invocation of the Hodge-type suppliers.

   **Repair tested on the witness:** For signature (2,0), d=0, the flag is a point and a finite level with N geometric points has H⁰(C,𝔽_ℓ)=𝔽_ℓ^N and H^i=0 for i>0.
