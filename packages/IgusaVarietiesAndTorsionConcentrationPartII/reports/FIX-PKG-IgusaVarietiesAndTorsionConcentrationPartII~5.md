## F01 (P1) — applied

**Verification.** The finding is correct. CS17 Remark 1.3 (CS17 lines 101–103) refers to the eigensystem of Theorem 1.1, and that eigensystem appears in `H^i(X_Γ, 𝔽_ℓ)` (CS17 lines 89–90), so it contains `ℓ`.

- *First witness.* Take Case 1 with `B = F = 𝒦 = ℚ(i)` and `ℓ = 3`. The group `G` is commutative, so every double coset is a single coset and acts on constants by `1`. This gives `λ : 𝕋^S → ℤ`. Put `𝔪_2 = λ⁻¹(2ℤ)`. Ordinary localization at `𝔪_2` inverts `3`, so the constants localize to `ℤ_3 ⊗ ℤ_(2) = ℚ_3`, while `H^0(𝔽_3)_{𝔪_2} = 0`. The old §8.9(2) and §11.8(1) both fail here.
- *Second witness.* Ordinary localization of an `ℓ`-adic module at an ideal of the abstract ring can also give `ℤ_5 ⊕ ℚ_5` (recomputed below).
