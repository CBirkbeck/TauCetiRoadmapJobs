## R01 (P2) — The null-homotopy `S_g` of the K-complex is mis-transcribed: its first entry is a product, not an element of `c`

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticStatisticsPartVIII/README.md:890`, in *The K-complex and its homology bound*, item (ii): “the map `S_g(g₀, …, g_q; s) := (g(g₀⋯g_q ∂s)⁻¹, g₀, …, g_q; s)` being a null-homotopy of right multiplication by `r_g`”.

**Claim:** As written, the first entry is the group element `g · (g₀⋯g_q ∂s)⁻¹`, which is not in `c` in general, so `S_g` does not land in `K(R)_{q+2} = k[c^{q+2}] ⊗ R[q+2]`; and where it happens to land there it is not a null-homotopy. The source's formula is a conjugate: `g^{(g₀⋯g_q ∂s)⁻¹}`, whose exponent the text extraction flattens (`ref-37 line 992`, and likewise `r(g^{(∂s)⁻¹})` at line 994). With the README's own convention `x^y := y⁻¹xy` (line 844) this is the element `(g₀⋯g_q ∂s) g (g₀⋯g_q ∂s)⁻¹ ∈ c`.

**Witness:** `G = S₃`, `c` the transpositions `a = (12), b = (23), c' = (13)`; `q = 0`, tuple `(a)`, `s` the empty orbit: the README's first entry is `b·a⁻¹ = ba`, a 3-cycle, not a transposition. Exhaustive check over all `(tup, s, g)` with `q ≤ 2` and `deg s ≤ 2` in the `S₃` K-complex (351 instances): with the conjugate `((g₀⋯g_q ∂s) g (g₀⋯g_q ∂s)⁻¹, g₀, …, g_q; s)` the identity `S_g d + d S_g = (· ; s r_g)` holds in all 351; with the README's product it is ill-typed in 144 instances and false in 132 of the remaining 207. By hand, for `q = 0`: `(S_g d + d S_g)(g₀; s) = (g₀; r(h^{g₀}) s)` with `h` the inserted entry, and `r(h^{g₀}) s = s r_g = r((∂s) g (∂s)⁻¹) s` forces `h = (g₀ ∂s) g (g₀ ∂s)⁻¹`. In general the inserted entry depends only on the total product `g₀⋯g_q ∂s`, which is why the `S_g d` and `d S_g` cross terms cancel.

**Exact repair:** Replace the displayed map by `S_g(g₀, …, g_q; s) := (g^{(g₀⋯g_q ∂s)⁻¹}, g₀, …, g_q; s) = ((g₀⋯g_q ∂s) g (g₀⋯g_q ∂s)⁻¹, g₀, …, g_q; s)`, and add to the proof sketch the two-line verification: the inserted entry depends only on `g₀⋯g_q ∂s`, which every face preserves, so `S_g d + d S_g` reduces to the single term `(g₀, …, g_q; r((∂s) g (∂s)⁻¹) s) = (g₀, …, g_q; s r_g)`. Add the `S₃` instance above as a negative-control Check (“a product in place of the conjugate leaves `c`”).
