## R01 (P3) — The Layer 5 contract describes a bigraded `Tor` that a single-graded derived tensor does not return

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticStatisticsPartVIII/README.md:108` (“Layer 5: the derived tensor product, whose homology in homological degree `i` is the graded `Tor^R_i(N, M)` … computed by a graded free resolution of either side”) and the `*Needs:*` line at 903 (“DGAInfinity Layer 5 (the derived tensor product and graded free resolutions)”).

**Claim:** DGAInfinity on main (`TauCetiRoadmap/DGAInfinity/README.md:293`–316, 431–465) builds DG algebras “on graded `k`-modules” with “constructors from an ordinary graded algebra with zero differential”, so the grading of `R` becomes the one DG grading, and Layer 5's derived tensor is a complex with a single grading. For `R` so regarded, the cohomology of `N ⊗^L_R M` in one degree is the sum over `i` of the pieces of `Tor^R_i(N, M)` in internal degree shifted by `i`, not the single bigraded piece `Tor^R_i(N, M)` with its internal grading that §5.16 uses (`deg Tor^R_i(N, M) ≤ … + A₁ i + 4A₁` is a statement about each `i` separately). What the section actually uses is its own definition at line 891 (`Tor^R_i(N, M)` as the `i`-th homology of `P_• ⊗_R M` for a graded free resolution, with the internal grading carried along) and its own balance statement (ii); from DGAInfinity it consumes only graded modules, free modules and the balanced tensor product (Layer 1), and from Layer 5 at most the fact that the single-graded total of that resolution is the derived tensor. Nothing is unowned: the mathematics of lines 889–911 is self-contained. Only the contract's description of what Layer 5 returns is wrong.

**Witness:** `R = k[x]` with `deg x = 1` as a DG algebra with zero differential, `N = M = k`: the derived tensor `k ⊗^L_R k` is the complex `k[1] → k` with zero differential, whose two classes sit in single degrees `0` and `1`; the bigraded reading `Tor_0 = k` (internal degree `0`), `Tor_1 = k[1]` (internal degree `1`) is recovered only by remembering the resolution degree separately, which a single-graded object does not record.

**Exact repair:** At line 108 write “Layer 5: the derived tensor product, consumed only as the single-graded total of `P_• ⊗_R M` for a graded free resolution `P_• → N`; the bigraded `Tor^R_i(N, M)`, with its internal grading and the balance between resolutions of either side, is defined and proved in *Tor spectral sequences over graded algebras*”, and at line 903 cite Layer 5 for the derived tensor product only, keeping “graded free resolutions” as the section's own first sentence already states them.
