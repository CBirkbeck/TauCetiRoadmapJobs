## R02 (P2) — Exclude even-order targets before taking the q-limits

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticStatisticsPartVIII/README.md:1725`: “Let `Γ` be finite and `H` a finite admissible `Γ`-group satisfying (SZ).” The same missing restriction occurs at line 1741.

**Claim:** These two numerical iterated-limit theorems quantify over H before restricting q. Admissibility and (SZ) do not make H odd. For an even H the allowed q-filter is empty, so it cannot specify the asserted real liminf and limsup. The Check at line 1737 notices that no q is allowed but does not restrict the outer H-quantifier. The convention about inhabited finite slices does not supply an unbounded set of q.
