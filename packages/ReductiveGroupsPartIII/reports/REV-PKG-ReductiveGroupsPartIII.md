1. **F01** (P3) §1.2 Check 3: the sextic example has no `ℚ_2`-point

   - **Location.** `README.md` line 626, §1.2 Checks: "`X=Spec ℚ[x]/((x²−2)(x²−3)(x²−6))` has a `ℚ_ℓ`-point for every `ℓ` and no rational point".
   - **Claim.** `X(ℚ_ℓ) ≠ ∅` for every prime `ℓ`.
   - **Witness.** In `ℚ_2` a unit is a square only when it is `≡ 1 (mod 8)`; `3 ≡ 3 (mod 8)`, and `2`, `6` have odd valuation. So none of `2, 3, 6` is a square in `ℚ_2` and `X(ℚ_2) = ∅`. (For odd `ℓ` the claim holds: the product of two non-squares mod `ℓ` is a square.)
   - **Repair.** Replace the polynomial by `(x²−13)(x²−17)(x²−221)`: `17 ≡ 1 (mod 8)` gives a `ℚ_2`-point, `17 ≡ 4` is a square mod `13`, `13 ≡ 8²` is a square mod `17`, for every other odd `ℓ` one of `13, 17, 221` is a square, and there is a real point; no rational point since none of `13, 17, 221` is a rational square. The second sentence of the Check (each `ℚ̄`-point rational over `ℚ_ℓ` for a set of primes of density one half) stays true for `±√13`, `±√17`, `±√221`. Tested on the refuting instance: `√17 ∈ ℚ_2`. (Alternatively keep `2, 3, 6` and write "for every odd `ℓ`", which is what §1.2's "all but finitely many" needs.)

## Source issues

- **Misprint** in KZ25 at proof of Lemma 6.2.6(2), arXiv v2 p.58: the source writes the conjugation hypothesis as `h⁻¹gh = g` and then uses `h₁ g h₁⁻¹ = g′`; it should be `h⁻¹gh = g′` with one consistent orientation; affects nothing.
- **Gap** in KZ25 at proof of Lemma 6.3.3, arXiv v2 p.60: the source takes an arbitrary complex lift of the normalized class and places it in a compact subgroup because its eigenvalues have modulus one; it should first choose a semisimple lift, since a unipotent lift with eigenvalues one has unbounded powers; affects the proof.
- **Error** in KMPS22 at Lemma 2.2.11, author PDF p.29: the source assumes only an irreducible scheme of finite type; it should assume reduced and separated, since `Spec k[ε]/ε²` and the doubled affine line give automorphisms agreeing with the identity on a dense set; affects a stated result.
- **Error** in KMPS22 at proof of Proposition 2.2.13, author PDF p.29: the source says the connected centralizer of a semisimple element is a Levi subgroup; it should say it is connected reductive (the long-root `A₁ × A₁` centralizer in `Sp₄` is not a Levi), which is all the argument uses; affects the proof.
- **Misprint** in KMPS22 at §2.2.3, author PDF p.25: the source defines an `l`-unit by boundedness at every place `v ∤ l`, archimedean included; it should say finite places, otherwise `l` is not an `l`-unit of `G_m(ℚ)` and Lemma 2.2.4 fails; affects a stated result.
