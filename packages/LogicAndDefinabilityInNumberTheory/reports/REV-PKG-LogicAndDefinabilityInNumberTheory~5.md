1. **F01** (P2) §2.5(b) asserts generic analyticity in regimes (i) and (iii) that neither its sources nor its route supply

   **Location.** `packages/LogicAndDefinabilityInNumberTheory/README.md`, line 425 (statement of (b), all three regimes): "f restricted to Z is strictly differentiable with invertible derivative, analytic on each piece of a finite definable partition"; line 429 (the route for regimes (i) and (iii)).

   **Claim.** In regime (i) (a fixed finite extension F/ℚ_p, Macintyre language, every p and e) and regime (iii) (fixed Denef–Pas data above a bound N, every allowed local field, including 𝔽_p((t)) for p>N), a definable bijection f:X→Y of n-dimensional definable subsets of Fⁿ is analytic on each piece of a finite definable partition of an open Z with lower-dimensional complements.

   **Witness.** The only source cited for regime (i) is `integrability_of_oscillatory_functions_on_local_fields_transfer_p` Proposition 3.3.5 (line 707, p.16): a one-variable statement, in one valued-field coordinate over a base Y, whose conclusion is the 1-Jacobian property, which by Definition 3.3.3 (lines 689–698) means C¹ with nonvanishing derivative, constant |f′| and the ball-to-ball norm identity; it asserts no analyticity and nothing about n variables. For regime (iii) the README's own route (line 429) transfers "a finite set of Denef–Pas sentences: the definable locus Z, strict differentiability with invertible Jacobian on Z, and a covering of the two complements by finitely many definable graphs"; analyticity is not a first-order sentence and is not among them. The only source that does give piecewise analyticity, `cluckers_loeser_2008` Theorem 3.2.1 (line 558, p.14) with Lemma 7.5.2 (line 1766, "Let K be a field of characteristic zero") and Theorem 7.5.3 (line 1786, Def_k), is stated in the category Def_k over a field k of characteristic zero, that is regime (ii) only. The repair report's own verification cites exactly these passages and no other.

   **Why it matters.** Nothing in the package consumes the analytic clause outside regime (ii): (a) needs strict differentiability with invertible derivative; §2.4 and §2.6 use (b) through (a); §3.7–3.8 use (b) in regime (ii). The clause is therefore an unconsumed assertion whose support is absent in two regimes, and in regime (iii) it concerns positive-characteristic fields where the roadmap has just been repaired for an unsupported regularity claim (round-4 F01).

   **Exact repair.** In the statement of (b), attach "analytic on each piece of a finite definable partition" to regime (ii) only, where CL Theorem 3.2.1 (applied to f and to f⁻¹, so that on the common analytic locus f is bianalytic and Df invertible by the chain rule) supplies it. For regimes (i) and (iii) state the conclusion the routes actually give: a definable Z⊆X open in Fⁿ with X∖Z and Y∖f(Z) of dimension <n, f|Z strictly differentiable with invertible derivative and the ball-to-ball (1-Jacobian) property cell by cell; in regime (i) say that the n-variable form is obtained coordinate by coordinate from CGH Theorem 3.3.2 and Proposition 3.3.5 as in CL §12, or add a primary source that proves generic analyticity of semialgebraic maps over a fixed finite extension of ℚ_p (CGH does not). Keep the sentence "No assertion (b) is made for a local field of positive characteristic outside regime (iii)".

   **Repair tested on the refuting instance.** The involution s on 𝔽₂((t)) stays excluded (char F=2 is outside (i) and (ii); its graph is not fixed data of residue characteristic >N for any N, and it is differentiable nowhere, so the retained first-order conclusion also fails for it). x↦tx on 𝔽₂((t)) stays admitted by (a): μ(tO)=1/2=|t|·1. The q=3 witnesses 1/3 and 1/27 are unchanged. §2.4 and §2.6 still obtain ∫_Y φ=∫_X φ∘f·|det Df| from (a) on Z plus Haar-nullity of the complements by §2.2. §3.8's use is regime (ii), where the analytic clause remains.
