   **Witness.** Regime (ii) (line 452) concludes only "dimension less than n", and a lower-dimensional definable subset of κ((t))ⁿ need not be covered by graphs over coordinate projections: the Denef–Pas 0-cells have centres depending on residue-field parameters (`pas_1989` lines 249–250; `cluckers_loeser_2008` lines 1531–1539, a 0-cell over a base in S[0,s,0]), so a 0-cell has finite fibres, not single-point ones, and the finitely many values are not definably selectable when the residue field has no definable roots. Computed instance, O=ℤ, κ=ℂ, n=2: let S′={(x,y)∈ℂ((t))² : y²=x³+x, ord x<0} and f(x,y)=(x,−y) on S′, f=id elsewhere, an L_ℤ-definable involution of X=Y=ℂ((t))². f is discontinuous at every point of S′ (S′ is closed with empty interior and y≠0 there since ord y=3·ord x/2<0), so every admissible Z has S′⊆X∖Z.
