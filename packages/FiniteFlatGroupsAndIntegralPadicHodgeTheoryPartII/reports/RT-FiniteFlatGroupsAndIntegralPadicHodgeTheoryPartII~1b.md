1. **F01** (P2) §4.2 asserts the connected-parahoric image for every smooth model, not only for a Bruhat–Tits stabilizer

   Location: `packages/FiniteFlatGroupsAndIntegralPadicHodgeTheoryPartII/README.md:1018`, "cutting out a smooth integral group 𝒢 with connected reductive generic G … With the fixer/parahoric component characterization, its image lies in 𝒢°(Z_p)."

   Claim: for any smooth ℤ_p-model 𝒢 ⊂ GL(L) cut out by Γ_K-invariant tensors of a crystalline lattice L, the Galois image lies in 𝒢°(ℤ_p).

   Witness: 𝒢 = dilatation of GL₂/ℤ_p along H₀={diag(a,b): a^{p−1}=b^{p−1}} (H₀°=scalars), closed in some GL(Λ′); L=Λ′ with Γ_{ℚ̆_p} acting through ℤ_p⊕ℤ_p(1). Image diag(1,χ): det a unit, so κ=0; reduction diag(1,χ̄)∉H₀°. Not in 𝒢°(ℤ_p).

   Computation. χ̄ is onto 𝔽_p^× because ℚ̆_p(ζ_p)/ℚ̆_p is totally ramified of degree p−1, so some γ has χ̄(γ)≠1; diag(1,χ̄(γ)) lies in H₀(𝔽_p) (both (p−1)-th powers are 1) but is not scalar, hence ρ(γ)∈𝒢(ℤ_p)∖𝒢°(ℤ_p). The lattice is crystalline (an algebraic representation applied to ℤ_p⊕ℤ_p(1)), 𝒢 is smooth affine with generic fibre GL₂ and is cut out in GL(Λ′) by tensors (closed flat subgroup). The identity 𝒢°(ℤ_p)=𝒢(ℤ_p)∩ker κ_G used for the second sentence is proved only for Bruhat–Tits stabilizers: KP18 line 2430 cites it for 𝒢=𝒢_x after the standing assumption at KP18 line 2437, and ReductiveGroupsPartII §RG2.3.3 (*parahoric-kottwitz-characterization*) states it for the fixer schemes 𝒢_Ω. The first conclusion (image in ker κ_G, KP18 Proposition 3.3.4, line 2398) holds for every 𝒢 and is unaffected. The Conventions paragraph reserves 𝒢 for "the smooth full integral fixer", and §§4.3–4.4 say "full fixer", but §4.2 widens the hypothesis explicitly and is then false at this generality.

   Repair text (§4.2). Replace "and L a crystalline lattice with Γ_K-invariant tensors cutting out a smooth integral group 𝒢 with connected reductive generic G" by "and L a crystalline lattice with Γ_K-invariant tensors cutting out the smooth full Bruhat–Tits stabilizer 𝒢=𝒢_x of a point x of B(G,ℚ_p), with connected reductive generic G, as in §2.1".
