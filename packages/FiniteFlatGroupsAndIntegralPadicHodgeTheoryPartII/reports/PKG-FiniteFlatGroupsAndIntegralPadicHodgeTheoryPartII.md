## Mathematical corrections and unsupported interfaces

- The ordinary modification is now an actual map: multiplication by c_E=pE(u)/E(0):φ*𝔐→F, followed by Θ:F→φ*𝔐 and the fixed frame φ*𝔐→𝔐. Its scalar is E(u)/a for E(0)=ap, and its value at zero is p. Θ lifts σ⁻¹(b/p) on D₁; the source and target identifications are explicit. This fills the formula omitted from the design and uses the construction read in Zhou20 lines 598–624, combined with KZ25 lines 1202–1219.

- The quaternionic PEL form is written with φ₀(t₀′,t₀), not φ₀(t₀,t₀′). Over the division quaternion algebra (−1,3)/ℚ₃, the balanced tensors i⊗1=1⊗i both pair with 1⊗1 to reduced trace −2 in the correct convention. The wrong argument order gives +2 and −2. The formula was added and corrected during this pass against KPZ26 line 2981; the design had specified only a trace form.

- The characteristic-p kernel Check now tensors j with k from the Witt frame. It no longer calls 𝔽_p a strict frame quotient: φ₁(p)=1 could not descend through p=0. The legitimate module reduction has j⊗k=0:k→k and nonzero source.

- The universal filtration uses integers of a complete discretely valued F/ℚ_p, not an arbitrary possibly equal-characteristic DVR; Hoff23 Notation 1.25, line 656, supplies this hypothesis. Continuous PD thickenings now spell out nilpotence, compatibility and a fundamental system of quotient ideals carrying the divided powers, from Hoff23 lines 607–611. The admissible height-one shtuka signature explicitly includes rational invertibility.

- The normal-pair rank statements state the nonzero-quotient condition; the zero frame is treated with zero modules rather than a numerical free-rank assertion. d≤h is explicit. The n_y in the completed-ideal argument is defined as ker(y:R_G→O_K), matching KP18 lines 2321–2322.

- The parahoric deformation theorem states the set-valued functor of marked isomorphism classes, with rigidity of marked morphisms as its companion assertion, rather than an unqualified scheme representation of a groupoid. Hoff23 lines 658–666 and 1346–1394 supply that distinction.

- The accepted corrections to first-order transport, common reflex quotient, rational-section topology, very-goodness, integral tensor type, residue-field frame triviality, dual Tate lattice, Hodge framing and rational specialization were all retained. Kottwitz targets distinguish geometric π₁(G)_I from the hyperspecial σ-invariants and the period-torsor coinvariants. No claim that g₀ belongs to G(W) or that every centralizer element is an integral automorphism remains.

The README does not assert closure of the ten supplier extensions. It takes the normal flat scheme-local-model geometry as specified input, separates integral-section detection from tensor-isomorphism flatness, separates connected purity from full-fixer component descent, and retains the perfect-residue realization and integral global Hodge-realization contracts. The nonordinary Levi theorem is conditional on its specified M-filtration, good embedding and very-good compatibility; the ordinary theorem does not imply existence of those data. These are the mathematical limitations of the advertised applications, not missing targets hidden by weakened Lean statements.

- The contravariant σ-centralizer convention now states a right quasi-isogeny action, D(q_g)=g and q_{gh}=q_h∘q_g; inversion gives a left action. The two noncommuting étale GL₂ matrices in the README distinguish the orders. This makes the scalar-p quasi-isogeny and all tensor-functor variance conventions compatible.
