   **Location.** `README.md:136` (the contract: "For PEL moduli at a neat level over an integral base containing a prime at which ShimuraCompactifications Layer C5 does not apply … prove quasi-projectivity"); `:1701` (U7/localized-smoothness: "is a finite morphism to the PEL moduli of PELModuli §M2.8 for the product datum", asserted over O_E[1/d] in every case); `:1711` (step 3: "gap:bad-prime-projectivity when O_E[1/d] keeps a prime ramified in F⁺"); `:1866`, `:1876` (U7/exotic-global-smoothness: "it is projective in the compact Li–Liu setting"; "Apply gap:bad-prime-projectivity in the compact case"); `:2043` (U8/asd-completed-local-rings: "Quasi-projectivity … is part of gap:bad-prime-projectivity"). Same text in plan.json `gaps[3].needs` and the three nodes.

   **Claim.** The gap is a precise contract, and its three consumers obtain quasi-projectivity (and properness) of their models from it.

   **Witness.** PELModuli §M1.7 defines `M_H` only "for an integral datum, a set □ of good primes, S₀ = Spec O_{F₀,(□)}", and §M2.8 proves representability over that S₀; ShimuraCompactifications (C4-b) and (C5-a) use the same base, and §C5.18 says "bad primes are not treated". The other two consumers do not go through any PEL moduli at all: U7/exotic-global-smoothness applies the gap to the exotic model of U7/exotic-global-model and U8/asd-completed-local-rings to the model of U8/ltxzz-asd-moduli, both constructed in this roadmap, which is later than ShimuraCompactifications in the merge order, so the supplier's theorem cannot be about them.    **Repair.** (a) In U7/localized-smoothness restrict the finite-comparison and §C5.18 clause to "when every residue characteristic of O_E[1/d] is unramified in F⁺ (so every prime of the base is good for the product datum)", and drop the citation of Stacks Tag 0C4N (line 4273 of the cached text is the converse implication, not used by the route; the base change Tag 0B3G is what the route uses implicitly).
