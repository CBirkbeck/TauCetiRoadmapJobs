Expanded the accepted 51-node design into `packages/AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII/README.md`, `Suggested.lean`, `metadata.toml` and `sources.toml`. All 51 mathematical topics, all 103 design API facts and all design Checks and acceptance examples remain, as prose or as explicitly imported supplier interfaces. Each layer has Examples and Dependencies. The only design JSON change is its top-level `gaps` value.

The supplied separate `design/review.json` and report files were absent. The accepted review embedded in `design/plan.json`, its per-node verdicts and its upstream notes were read and applied. This report does not certify that the future mathematical library has been proved: the deliverable is the fully stated roadmap and its native acceptance signatures.

There are two simple definitions and three headline theorems, with no API padding, no admitted data and no substitute abstract algebra theorems. The field-torsion statement uses integral closed curves of dimension exactly one and the actual Euler degree of their restricted sheaves. Properness makes those restrictions the intended finite-dimensional curve cohomology invariant; the bare Euler-degree definition also has junk values away from that domain.

## Corrections and support boundaries

1. The design's baseline pins were superseded by the task's `lake-manifest.json`: Mathlib `6b7abb3c7686292736be2955bd3eb9ebf63b456a` and Tau Ceti `a91d3aafa8cd3e6bc33dfde0d7677ed0f1625039`. Line-bundle classes now form a group and have pullback/degree infrastructure. All library claims were checked against these copies.
2. Fixed projective-space statements at dimension zero: Num(P⁰)=0, restriction 0→0 and cone {0}; rank-one assertions require n≥1. Kept the elliptic-product ranks 3,4,6, with supersingular rank 6.
3. Distinguished the geometric curve factor d_C=m r from the usual gcd index of a curve. The pointless real conic has geometric factor 1 but gcd index 2. The Das–Waldron factor is a quotient of field degrees; no product replacement is made.
4. Made the all-fibre definition of numerical triviality independent of the later closed-point equivalence, removing the design's forward-reference cycle at the definition level. Degree and nefness specialization do not assume that the residue extension of a dominating DVR is finite.
5. Located G1's full proper finite-coefficient input in EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.3, `VanishingCycles.etaleFiniteness`, including reducible and nonreduced proper schemes. No new cohomological finiteness theorem is planned here.
6. G2, G3, G4 and G5 become named supplier extensions with one-sentence needs, roadmap and section. The presumed `SF.5/nef-big-numerical` target does not occur in SchemeAndStackFoundations as read on main; its exact reduced-projective criterion is a separate named gap on §5.3. The proper bigness extension remains a target, with the parent's birational comparison as input.
7. Read CT20 Lemma 2.17 through its proof: lines 667–702 use connected components, a section and a proper curve meeting its zero set. The design's G6 assertion about a missing surface-cutting proof input is incorrect. The README supplies this actual route, using Chow's lemma for the curve, and closes G6 without restricting excellent schemes to projective surface models. Corrected the conclusion's undefined Y to the actual target S.
8. Retained the accepted corrections to SCH23: positive exponent d≥1; τ-torsors rather than full Picard torsors; no unnecessary R¹-pushforward vanishing; Leray edge H²(base,h_*G_m)→H²(source,G_m); edges may collapse; pure one-dimensionality in the curve criterion; the surface contraction to a point; pairwise separation of fibre-component classes; full Cartier pullback for labels. Negative-curve Hilbert rigidity retains its exact supplier gap and proves the stronger residue conclusion κ(a)=k.
9. Corrected the Hilbert locator: 0CZX is §99.9, while Proposition 99.9.4 is tag 0D01; tangent deformations are Remark 99.7.10, 0CZV. Used chapter-qualified STACKS extraction lines to avoid ambiguity among chapters.
10. Recomputed the elliptic example: affine counts (0,1,2,0,2,2,2), hence 10 points over 𝔽₇; rational group order 5. It obstructs exact lifting of some special sheaves but does not obstruct every positive power. HW23 lines 1528 and the continuation on p.34 use numerical equivalence already; the inherited accusation against them is rejected.
11. Required a nonzero nonunit in the exceptional-divisor principal-divisor argument. Added the contraction-to-a-point case up to k-isomorphism, not equality of chosen schemes. Kept the correct μ₂ Enriques terminology and nonreduced structure.
12. Corrected AHK slicing to n−e−2, and positivity to positive-dimensional projective varieties. The relative positive-characteristic comparison is a separate target with specified projective regular resolutions, common regular dominations and an exact exceptional-degree witness input. It is not inferred from resolution existence. The complex proof now names ResolutionOfSingularitiesCharacteristicZero §7.8 for common smooth domination.

The six supplier gaps are recorded verbatim under Exact supplier contracts and in the design's top-level `gaps` list. The README supports every accepted target as a mathematical library target, but cannot claim proof closure of those supplier extensions. It does not claim arbitrary proper Picard representability from the universal-functions theorem, arbitrary proper bigness from the projective case, or positive-characteristic AHK comparison from the complex theorem.
