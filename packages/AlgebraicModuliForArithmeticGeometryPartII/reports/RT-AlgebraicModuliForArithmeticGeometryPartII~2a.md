   **Claim:** In the proper, numerically surjective situation of §5.2, completeness and vanishing of H² of the special fibre imply exact lifting of every line bundle.

   **Witness:** In characteristic 3, a square-zero thickening of P³ with ideal O_D for a smooth quartic D has Picard restriction image 3ℤ⊂ℤ. It is numerically surjective over k[[t]], with H²(O_P³)=0, but O(1) does not lift.

   **Exact repair:** Replace the Check by: “When 𝒳 → Spec R is also flat, H²(X, O_X) = 0 and R is complete, every invertible sheaf on X has an actual lift (§5.3).”

Using t=1_D makes it a proper k[[t]]-scheme with special fibre P³, numerical restriction image 3ℤ⊂ℤ and H²(P³,O)=0, but O(1) does not lift.”

   **Verification of the witness and repair:** Take k algebraically closed of characteristic 3, X=P³_k and D the smooth Fermat quartic. Put I=i_*O_D. The restricted Euler sequence gives H¹(T_X⊗I)≅H²(I)=k: both H¹(O_D(1)) and H²(O_D(1)) vanish. Glue split square-zero extensions O_U⊕I_U using a derivation cocycle whose image under this isomorphism is 1. The obstruction to lifting O_X(1) is then 1∈H²(I); that of O_X(n) is n∈k. Its real numerical restriction map is an isomorphism, but O(1) does not lift. This family is not flat: t² annihilates O_𝒳. The added hypothesis excludes it; §5.3 and packages/AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII/Suggested.lean:309 already retain flatness.
