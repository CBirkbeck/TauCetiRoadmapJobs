## R01 (P3) — §1.2 locators support finite dimensionality, not integral finite generation

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII/README.md` line 251, "(KLE05, Remark 6.19, pp. 59–60; KLE05 line 2840; BMP23, Remark 2.22, p. 19; BMP23 line 825)".

Claim: the headline `numericalClassGroup_free_finite` (Num(X/k) free abelian of finite rank for X proper over any field) is located in these two remarks.

Witness: KLE05 line 2840–2847 states that `Pic^τ_{X/S}` is an open subgroup of finite type and that the torsion group (6.17.1) is finite; it says nothing about finite generation of Pic/Pic^τ. Neither remark states that the integral quotient is finitely generated, which is what freeness of finite rank needs (a torsion-free group with finite-dimensional real span need not be finitely generated: ℚ).

## Source issues

- **Misprint** in SCH23 at Lemma 1.1, printed p. 5: the source says the uniform exponent d satisfies d ≥ 0; it should be d ≥ 1, since d = 0 makes the condition vacuous; affects a stated result.
- **Gap** in SCH23 at Proposition 1.2, printed p. 6: the source says X one-dimensional; it should say every irreducible component of X is one-dimensional, as its proof uses (P¹_ℝ ⊔ Spec ℂ has constant Num and a non-bijective component map); affects a stated result.
