   **Claim.** The statement is in the roadmap's own words.

   **Witness.** B19 lines 510–513: the same three clauses in the same order (finiteness, the union named, the complement as the maximal open), with "integral" → "prime" and "biggest" → "largest"; eight consecutive words are shared ("their union is the ramification divisor of α").

   **Exact repair.** Replace the two sentences from "For α ∈ Br(k(X)) only finitely many …" to "… ⊂ Br(k(X))." by:

   "For α ∈ Br(k(X)), call a prime divisor Γ ⊂ X ramified for α when res_Γ(α) ∈ H¹(k(Γ), ℚ/ℤ) is nonzero. The ramified divisors are finite in number, and the ramification divisor of α is their sum. An open V ⊂ X satisfies α ∈ Br(V) exactly when V meets no ramified divisor; so the unramified locus U_α, the complement of the ramification divisor, contains every such V."

   **Repair test.** α = (x, y) on 𝔸²: ramified divisors {x = 0}, {y = 0}; U_α = 𝔸² ∖ {xy = 0}; V = 𝔸² fails, V = {xy ≠ 0} holds, as the Checks of §1.1 say. α ∈ Br(X): no ramified divisor, U_α = X. The new text agrees with API bullets 1–3 and shares no run of more than four words with B19 lines 510–513.

2. **F02** (P3) a Check of §5.1 is about a class that does not exist

   **Location.** `packages/RealSurfacePeriodIndex/README.md:1413` (§5.1 Checks): "On S = ℙ²_ℝ with R a real line, Ψ = U(ℝ) ≅ ℝ² is open and closed in U(ℝ) but not closed in S(ℝ); condition (ii) can hold …"

   **Claim.** There is α of period 2 on ℙ²_ℝ whose ramification divisor is a line R.

   **Witness.** U = ℙ² ∖ R = 𝔸²_ℝ and Br(𝔸²_ℝ) = Br(ℝ) = Br(ℙ²_ℝ), so every class of Br(U) is unramified along R: no α has ramification divisor exactly a line. (For (−1, f), deg f = d, the line at infinity is ramified iff d is odd, and then {f = 0} is ramified too.)

   **Exact repair.** Replace the Check by: "On S = ℙ²_ℝ take α = (−1, x/z), ramified exactly along R = {x = 0} ∪ {z = 0}, an SNC divisor. Then U(ℝ) is two open half-planes and Ψ = Θ(α) = {x/z < 0} ≅ ℝ² is open and closed in U(ℝ) but not closed in S(ℝ); condition (ii) can hold, the generator of H¹(ℝℙ², 𝔽₂) being cl_R of the line {x = 0}, whose real locus misses Ψ."

   **Repair test.** Residues of (−1, x/z): the class of −1 along {x = 0} and along {z = 0} (valuations 1 and −1), nonzero because ℝ(ℙ¹) is real; zero elsewhere. Period 2. α_p = (−1, x/z(p)) ≠ 0 iff x/z(p) < 0. H¹(ℝℙ²) → H¹(ℝ²) = 0 has kernel 𝔽₂, generated by the class of a line; {x = 0}(ℝ) ∩ Ψ = ∅.
