1. **F01** (P3) Brauer corestriction is already stated by a supplier; the ownership claim overstates

   **Location.** README:77–80, quoting "It also owns corestriction and the period and index of a Brauer class of a field, which no roadmap states"; the construction PI.0/brauer-corestriction (README:475–510) and its four uses (README:490).

   **Claim.** No roadmap states a corestriction of Brauer classes, so the cohomological transport along `TauCeti.brauerCohomologyEquiv` is owned here.

   **Witness.** HeightsRationalPointsAndObstructions §2.19a (open head 82cf8af, earlier in the merge order; already cited by this design for §2.16b) defines `finiteFlatBrauerCorestriction` for a finite locally free f : Y → X of rank d, with `pullback_comp` = d and `fieldAgreement` (on a finite F-algebra it is Σ eᵢ cor_{Fᵢ/F}); for Spec L → Spec K this is Br(L) → Br(K) with cor ∘ res = [L : K]. QuadraticFormInvariants Layer 7A on main already builds the embedding-independent, tower-functorial transfer `galoisCor` along finite separable L/K from ProfiniteCohomology's `corestriction`, for 𝔽₂ coefficients. Every use in this design (PI.0/brauer-period-index step 4, PI.7/period-induction-step, PI.9/enriques-complex-brauer) needs only cor ∘ res = [L : K].

   **Repair.** In Ownership, replace "which no roadmap states" by a clause citing HeightsRationalPointsAndObstructions §2.19a for the map and cor ∘ res = [L : K], and QuadraticFormInvariants Layer 7A for the Galois-cohomological transfer pattern. Make PI.0/brauer-corestriction consume `finiteFlatBrauerCorestriction` and `pullback_comp` for Spec L → Spec K (any finite L/K), keeping as its own content only the identification with the corestriction of continuous cohomology under `TauCeti.brauerCohomologyEquiv` (`brauerCohomologyEquiv_baseChange`), which no roadmap states; or delete the construction and repoint the three consumers to §2.19a. Test: PI.0/brauer-period-index step 4 computes ind(α)·α = cor(res α) = cor(0) = 0 from `pullback_comp` alone, and the Sylow step uses only [L : F]·α_F = 0.

3. **F03** (P3) The lattice step of PI.6/degree-two-splitting-uniform names the wrong subgroup

   **Location.** README:2252, step 5, quoting "the invariant equivariant classes map to a full lattice of H²(T, ℝ(1))^G_van (AlgebraicTopologyPartII AT2.16/finite-index-lattice) … ε₂ = 2δ + ν".

   **Claim.** The gap gives a lattice in the vanishing summand, and δ may be any equivariant class.

   **Witness.** The gap (needs: the image of H²_G(T, ℤ(j)) in H²(T, ℤ(j))^G has finite index) lands in H²(T, ℝ(1))^G, not in its vanishing summand; and with δ unconstrained, step 6 fails: the constant component of β − 2γ − 2δ is ε₁ − p*p_*δ, and p_*δ need not be of type (1,1), whereas "ε₁ is of type (1,1) because p_*(β − 2γ) = cl(θ)" uses p_*δ = 0. B19 line 1271–1274 defines H²_G(T(ℂ), ℤ(1))_van as the classes with vanishing component in p*H²(S(ℂ), ℝ(1))^G, derives from the finite-index statement that its image in H²(T(ℂ), ℝ(1))^G_van is a lattice, and takes δ in that subgroup. Computed: for δ ∈ _van the constant component of 2δ is 0, so ε₁ is unchanged; the lattice claim follows because the image I of H²_G has finite index m in Λ = H²(T, ℤ(1))^G, and for y ∈ Λ ∩ V (V = ker p_* ⊗ ℝ, a rational subspace) my ∈ I ∩ V, so I ∩ V is a full lattice of V.

   **Repair.** Rewrite step 5: "the subgroup H²_G(T(ℂ), ℤ(1))_van of classes with vanishing component in p*H²(S(ℂ), ℝ(1))^G maps onto a lattice of H²(T(ℂ), ℝ(1))^G_van (gap:at2-finite-index-lattice applied to H²(T, ℤ(1)), intersected with the rational subspace ker p_*); choose δ in that subgroup and ν in the cone with ε₂ = 2δ + ν." Test: step 6 then reads β − 2γ − 2δ = ε₁ + ν with p_*(β − 2γ − 2δ) = cl(θ) unchanged.
