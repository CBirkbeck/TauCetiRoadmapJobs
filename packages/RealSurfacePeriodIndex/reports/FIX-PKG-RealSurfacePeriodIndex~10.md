## F05 (P3) — applied differently

Verified that the statement was garbled: the doubled parentheses left ε̂ unrelated to ê. B19 lines 1541–1543 define ε̂ as the class of the pullback, along ι : 𝕊¹ → V(ℂ), of the complex covering V̂(ℂ) → V(ℂ).

Old → new (§8.6 statement): "… alternating double cover ((ι*ê))" → "the class of the double cover of 𝕊¹ obtained by pulling back along ι the complex points of the alternating double cover of §8.2 over V". The new text then gives the identity ε̂ = ι*[ê]₁, with its reason.

Why this differs from the review's text: the review writes ε̂ = ι*ê ∈ H¹(𝕊¹, 𝔽₂). But ι*ê lies in H¹_G(𝕊¹, 𝔽₂) = H⁰(𝕊¹) ⊕ H¹(𝕊¹), since G fixes 𝕊¹ ⊂ V(ℝ). The underlying topological double cover is the image under the forgetful map, and that map is the projection onto the degree-one component (the generator of H¹(G, 𝔽₂) restricts to 0 at a point). So ε̂ = ι*[ê]₁, which is the form §8.8 step 2 already uses. The §8.6 Check "no branch component meets 𝕊¹ gives ε̃ = ε̂" is unchanged. The decomposition is cited to MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles §7.6b, which §8.6 already lists in its *Needs:*.
