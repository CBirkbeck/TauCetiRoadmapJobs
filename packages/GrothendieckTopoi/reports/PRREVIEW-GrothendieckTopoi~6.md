## R01 (P2) — 6.3 claims `Sheaf (coherentTopology C)` is locally coherent (= `Topos.IsAlgebraic`) with only pullbacks in `C`; the proof route does not supply the diagonal condition that `IsAlgebraic` requires

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/GrothendieckTopoi/README.md` lines 848–853 ("*locally coherent* when it is algebraic. Prove that … `Sheaf (coherentTopology C) (Type u)` is locally coherent for small `C` with `[Precoherent C]` and `[HasPullbacks C]`") and 869–872 (6.4 derives `hasEnoughPoints_coherentTopology` from it, "in particular"); `Suggested.lean` lines 430–433.

Claim: for `C` precoherent with pullbacks, the sheaf topos of the coherent topology satisfies AdicSpacesPartII's `Topos.IsAlgebraic`, hence (SGA 4 VI 9.0) has enough points.

Witness: AdicSpacesPartII R0.19.10 on main (`TauCetiRoadmap/AdicSpacesPartII/README.md` lines 8009–8015, 8028–8032) defines an object as quasiseparated by "`Y ×_X Z` quasicompact for all quasicompact `Y`, `Z` over `X`", a morphism as quasicompact by pullback along maps from quasicompact objects, a morphism as quasiseparated by "its diagonal is quasicompact", and `IsAlgebraic` as a generating family of qcqs objects, closed under fibre products, "such that `X → *` is quasiseparated for every `X` in `C`". With only pullbacks in `C`, the generators `ℓc` are quasicompact and quasiseparated as objects (a quasicompact `Y → ℓc` is a quotient of a finite coproduct of `ℓd_i`, so `Y ×_{ℓc} Z` is a quotient of a finite coproduct of `ℓ(d_i ×_c e_j)`), and they are closed under fibre products since `ℓ` preserves pullbacks; that is what the README's route gives. The last condition is different: `ℓc → *` quasiseparated means the diagonal `ℓc → ℓc × ℓc` is quasicompact, and its fibre over a test object `ℓt → ℓc × ℓc`, that is over a parallel pair `f, g : t ⇉ c`, is the equalizer subsheaf `{h : s → t | f h = g h}` of `ℓt`. That subsheaf is `ℓ(eq(f, g))`, hence quasicompact, only when `C` has equalizers; with pullbacks alone (no terminal object, so no products, so no equalizers in general) nothing in 6.3 or in R0.19.10 makes it quasicompact. The object-level condition does not imply it: `Eq(f, g)` is the pullback of the diagonal of `ℓt` along `ℓ(t ×_c t) → ℓt × ℓt`, which is circular. I did not find a counterexample; the step is unsupported, and the Lean headline 6.4 inherits it through "in particular".

Repair (two parts, both tested for elaboration where Lean is involved):

- In 6.3, state the local-coherence claim for small `C` with `[Precoherent C]`, `[HasPullbacks C]` and `[HasEqualizers C]` (or with finite limits), whose route is the one written plus "`ℓ` preserves equalizers, so the fibre of the diagonal of `ℓc` over `ℓt` is `ℓ(eq(f, g))`"; or keep the hypotheses and add to 6.3 the lemma that every equalizer subsheaf of a representable in `Sheaf (coherentTopology C) (Type u)` is quasicompact, with its proof.
- Keep the headline `hasEnoughPoints_coherentTopology` at `[Precoherent C] [HasPullbacks C]` (it is true there) and give 6.4 the route that proves it at that generality: for `c : C`, `Over c` has finite limits (products over `c` are pullbacks in `C`, the equalizer of `f, g : (d → c) ⇉ (e → c)` is the pullback of `(f, g) : d → e ×_c e` along the diagonal of `e`), the induced topology `(coherentTopology C).over c` is generated by finite families, so `Sheaf (coherentTopology C) / ℓc ≌ Sheaf ((coherentTopology C).over c)` (3.1) is a coherent topos in the sense of Mac Lane–Moerdijk IX.11 and has enough points; since the `ℓc` generate, a morphism of sheaves is an isomorphism when its pullback to every `E/ℓc` is (bijectivity on maps from generators plus `E` balanced), so the points of the slices form a `u`-small conservative family. Replace "in particular" by this route and add 3.1 and 6.2 to 6.4's `*Needs:*`.

## R02 (P3) — 3.1 Check: `yoneda.obj d` is a sheaf there; it is `yoneda.obj c` that is not

Location: `README.md` lines 489–491, "`yoneda.obj d` itself is not a sheaf there."

Claim: on `Discrete {c, d}` with the empty sieve covering `d`, the representable of `d` is not a sheaf.

Witness: the sheaf condition for the empty covering sieve on `d` says `F(d)` is a one-element type; `(yoneda.obj d).obj d = (d ⟶ d)` has one element, and the only sieve covering `c` is the top sieve, so `yoneda.obj d` is a sheaf and `ℓ d = yoneda.obj d` (the initial sheaf, `∅` at `c`). The representable that fails is `yoneda.obj c`, whose value at `d` is `(d ⟶ c) = ∅`, not a one-element type; so the topology is not subcanonical, as the Check wants.

Repair: replace the clause by "`yoneda.obj c` is not a sheaf there (its value at `d` is `∅`), so the topology is not subcanonical, while `ℓ d = yoneda.obj d`".

## R03 (P3) — 3.3 Check: the witness shows non-faithfulness, not non-fullness
