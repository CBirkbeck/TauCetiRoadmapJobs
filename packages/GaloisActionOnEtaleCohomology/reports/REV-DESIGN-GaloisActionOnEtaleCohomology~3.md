## R01 (P2) — The neighbourhood category needs the opposite field poset

**Location.** `packages/GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology/design/README.md:157`, and plan target 0.3: “equivalent to the poset of finite subextensions of `K^sep/K`”. The same paragraph specifies arrows from the larger extension's spectrum to the smaller one's.

**Claim.** The category of connected pointed neighbourhoods, with the geometric morphisms written in this paragraph, is equivalent to the inclusion-ordered field poset used in §2.1.

**Witness.** For K=ℚ₃ and L=K(√3), the pointed Hom from Spa(L) to Spa(K) has cardinality 1; the reverse Hom has cardinality 0. Inclusion gives Hom(L,K)=∅ and Hom(K,L) a singleton.

This is not just a choice of names for the objects. The whole neighbourhood category has a terminal object Spa(K). The inclusion poset of finite subextensions of ℚ₃^sep has no terminal object: no finite extension contains all finite extensions. Thus those categories cannot be equivalent as stated. The arrow criterion later in line 157 is correct, but contradicts the claimed direction of the equivalence. The source itself takes the stalk colimit over the opposite neighbourhood category (`stacks03N1 line 3745`), and calls the affine refinement initial (`stacks03N1 line 3746`).

**Exact repair.** Identify the connected pointed neighbourhood category with the **opposite** of the inclusion-ordered finite-subextension poset. Say that its inclusion in all pointed neighbourhoods is initial (equivalently, cofinal after taking opposites for stalk colimits). Write the stalk colimit in §2.3 over N(ξ_C)^op before identifying it with the inclusion-filtered field colimit. Synchronize plan target 0.3 and add the two unequal Hom counts as a direction Check.

**Repair tested.** In the opposite field poset Hom(L,K) has cardinality 1 and Hom(K,L) has cardinality 0, exactly as for pointed spectra. Taking opposites again gives H^i(X_K)→H^i(X_L), the transition maps required in §§2.1,2.3. The two-component refinement and embedding action remain valid.

## R02 (P2) — The representation endpoint incorrectly excludes torsion modules

**Location.** `packages/GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology/design/README.md:484`: “not a finite projective `ℤ_2`-module and not a `ContinuousRep`”; related §§3.2,3.3,4.3(c), supplier contracts, and `design/plan.json:1127`.

**Claim.** Torsion prevents the integral cohomology from being a continuous representation, requiring passage to its torsion-free quotient for that carrier.

**Witness.** `TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/ContinuousRep.lean:82` requires a representation and a continuous action map, with no projectivity, finite-generation or torsion-free field. The trivial action on discrete ℤ₂/2 has action map (g,x)↦x, which is continuous.

The native declaration is `TauCeti.ContinuousRep Γ A M`, including the module argument. The older finite-projective application in main EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.4.0 does not restrict that native carrier. The other cited supplier explicitly identifies its carrier with this more general native one: #856, `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations/README.md:783`, continuing through line 786. The design should distinguish the finite-projective subcategory from continuous representations themselves, and use the pinned carrier rather than carry forward a restrictive description of it. Its own §3.2 joint-continuity theorem already supplies the native structure on the full integral group.

**Exact repair.** Cite `TauCeti.ContinuousRep` and `toContRepresentation` directly in Exact supplier contracts. State that §3.2 gives `TauCeti.ContinuousRep G_K ℤ_ℓ H` on the whole integral group H, and §3.3 gives its rational counterpart on the stated topology. In §4.3 retain finite generation of H; state separately that H modulo torsion is finite free and rationalisation finite-dimensional. Change the Enriques Check to say that H is a continuous representation with nonzero torsion, hence is outside the finite-projective subcategory. Synchronize the plan; do not construct another representation carrier.

**Repair tested.** The trivial action on ℤ₂/2 is accepted, with 2·1=0 and 1≠0, while finite projectivity remains false. In the Enriques example the ℤ/2 torsion survives in H with its continuous action and dies in H⊗ℚ₂. Thus both the integral representation and the intended finite-projective quotient are retained.

## R03 (P1) — The top-degree non-Mittag–Leffler Check cannot occur

**Location.** `packages/GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology/design/README.md:485`: “the finiteness hypothesis in degree `N` is what removes it”; §4.3(d), line 479; `design/plan.json:1099` and its `milnor_top` test.

**Claim.** Under (d)'s vanishing H^q(X_C,ℤ/ℓ)=0 for q>N, the top cohomology tower can be non-Mittag–Leffler and leave a nonzero H_cont^(N+1), unless one assumes finiteness in degree N.

**Witness.** The coefficient sequence 0→ℤ/ℓ(j)→ℤ/ℓ^(n+1)(j)→ℤ/ℓ^n(j)→0 gives H^N_(n+1)→H^N_n→H^(N+1)_1=0. Every transition is surjective, so lim¹ H^N_n=0, without finiteness.

Twists are constant rank-one coefficient modules on X_C under the standing hypotheses, so untwisted mod-ℓ vanishing gives the twisted vanishing used here. The statement that a completely arbitrary infinite tower can have nonzero lim¹ is true, but such a tower cannot be the tower in (d). This Check incorrectly places that possibility inside (d). The weaker bound in (d) is true but discards a consequence forced by its own hypotheses.

**Exact repair.** Strengthen (d) to H_cont^q(X_C,ℤ_ℓ(j))=0 for all q>N, without the extra degree-N finiteness assumption. Prove surjectivity of the top transition maps by the displayed coefficient sequence, then apply DerivedModuleAlgebra §1.T.1's ML vanishing. The pinned inputs are `TauCeti.ModuleTower.isMittagLeffler_of_surjective` and `subsingleton_limOne_of_isMittagLeffler`, in `TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Tower/MittagLeffler.lean:104` and line 191. Replace `milnor_top` with a Check of this stronger conclusion. Keep §3.1's general multiplication-by-ℓ tower as the separate illustration that arbitrary towers need not be ML.

**Repair tested.** Take K=𝔽₂((t)), X the closed unit disc, j=0 and N=1. Artin–Schreier and affinoid coherent acyclicity give H^q(X_C,𝔽₂)=0 for q>1, whereas H¹ is infinite: the classes T,T³,T⁵,… are independent over 𝔽₂. The reduction maps H¹(ℤ/2^(n+1))→H¹(ℤ/2^n) are surjective, so lim¹=0 and H_cont²=0 despite infinite H¹(𝔽₂). For the point, N=0, reduction ℤ/ℓ^(n+1)→ℤ/ℓ^n is likewise surjective and H_cont¹=0. These are tests of the repaired mathematical claim, not Lean executions.
