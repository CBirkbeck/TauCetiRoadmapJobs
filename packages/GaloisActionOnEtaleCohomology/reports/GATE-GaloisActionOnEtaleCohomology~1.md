The new section follows the precedent of ModularCurves on main (`## Provenance: existing Lean work and source audit`) and of ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomology (`## Existing formalizations outside Tau Ceti`). It also checks that the conventions do not conflict: Weil's stalk action has the direction fixed in Conventions, and its Frobenius is the geometric one that §2.6 inverts.
