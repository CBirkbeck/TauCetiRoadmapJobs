Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/AnalyticStacks/README.md` line 296, the first API bullet of 0.9: "D-suave and D-prim maps are stable under base change and composition, contain the equivalences, and ω and δ are compatible with base change and composition: ω_(g∘f) ≅ ω_f ⊗ f*ω_g (HM Lemmas 4.5.4–4.5.5, HM line 3536, HM line 3544)".

Witness: HM line 3536 (Lemma 4.5.4) is the criterion "f is D-suave iff f^!1 commutes with base change along f, and then ω_f = f^!1"; HM line 3544 (Lemma 4.5.5) is the prim criterion "f_!π₂_*Δ_!1 → f_*1 is an isomorphism, δ_f = π₂_*Δ_!1". Repair: replace the parenthesis by "(HM Lemma 4.5.9, HM line 3583; the formula for ω_(g∘f) is HM Lemma 4.4.9(i), HM line 3353, with P = Q = 1, and compatibility with base change is HM Lemma 4.4.8(i), HM line 3339)".
