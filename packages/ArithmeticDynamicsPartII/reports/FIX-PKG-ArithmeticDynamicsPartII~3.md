### Source and mathematical evidence

The proof gives exactly one parabolic q-cycle with every other rotation cycle prime to q. If L is the lcm of those other lengths, q∤L; hence its L-th power is a single q-cycle. Since q≥5, q(q+3)/4−(q+3)=(q+3)(q−4)/4≥0. The new target consumes the generic theorem rather than re-owning it.

For the Kummer estimate, let r be the number of geometric points of C̄ with odd valuation of f. The smooth projective double cover has genus g′ with 2g′−2=2(2g−2)+r. Its anti-invariant first cohomology therefore has dimension 2g′−2g=2g−2+r. Every unramified point of S adds one anti-invariant boundary dimension, while a ramified point adds zero; this gives (2g−2+r)+(s−r)=2g−2+s. The projective part has eigenvalues of absolute value √q and the finite boundary part has eigenvalues of absolute value 1. Geometric nonsquareness makes the cover geometrically connected and removes anti-invariant H⁰ and H². Thus the trace bound has the stated dimension and includes every original puncture. This uses SchemeAndStackFoundations §3.2 (tame Riemann–Hurwitz) and the actual curve/Frobenius comparison of WeilConjectures WC.5 and WC.7.
