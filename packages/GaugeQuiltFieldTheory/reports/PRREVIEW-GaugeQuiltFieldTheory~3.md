## R02 (P3) — The determinant-line order of GQ2c is the opposite of its supplier's, with no stated comparison

**Location.** `README.md` line 160: "Use determinant lines in the order `det(coker D)*⊗det(ker D)`, with parity `ind D mod2`; exchanging two entire determinant blocks gives `(-1)^(ind D₁ ind D₂)`"; line 174 `*Needs:* … HeegaardFloer F1.4 (determinant lines)`; line 31 "F0 (the nonlinear Fredholm/Sard–Smale substrate and determinant lines)".

**Claim.** The cited supplier orders the factors the other way. HeegaardFloer on main (`TauCetiRoadmap/HeegaardFloer/README.md` line 1464, F0.0): "The stabilized determinant charts identify fibers with det ker T tensor (det coker T)*"; F1.4 (line 2227) consumes "F0.0's single Fredholm determinant line" without reordering. GQ2c's order is that of `ww-orientations` line 189 (`det(D) = Λmax(coker(D)∨)⊗Λmax(ker(D))`). The two orders differ pointwise by the braiding sign `(−1)^(dim ker D · dim coker D)`, which is not locally constant on a Fredholm family (at an index-zero operator with one-dimensional kernel and cokernel the sign is `−1`; at an isomorphism it is `+1`), so the identification of the two determinant line bundles is a constructed isomorphism compatible with F0.0's stabilization charts, not a relabeling. The README states none, and every gluing and block-exchange sign it derives "in this order" is read against the supplier's bundle.

**Witness.** `TauCetiRoadmap/HeegaardFloer/README.md` line 1464 versus `README.md` line 160; `ww-orientations` line 189 and line 210 (the block-exchange sign `(−1)^(dim coker(D₂)·Ind(D₁))` in their order).

**Repair.** At line 160, after the order sentence, add: "HeegaardFloer F0.0 orders its fibers as `det(ker D)⊗det(coker D)*`; construct the isomorphism of determinant line bundles over the Fredholm family that reorders by the braiding sign `(−1)^(dim ker D·dim coker D)` on each fiber, and prove it compatible with F0.0's stabilization charts and with the gluing exact sequence." Add two Checks: an invertible `D` (sign `+1`, both orders agree) and an index-zero `D` with one-dimensional kernel and cokernel (sign `−1`; a map that ignored the reorder would change the sign of every gluing comparison through such an operator).
