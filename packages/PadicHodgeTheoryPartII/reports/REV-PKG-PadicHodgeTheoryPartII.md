## Source issues

- **Misprint** in Chen14 at Lemme 3.3.5, p. 744: the source writes the rank-one object as a sum of Q_{p^s}-lines e_i; it should be K_0-lines with Q_{p^s} acting through σ^i, since the underlying K_0-space has dimension s; affects nothing.
- **Error** in GLX26 at Proposition 5.7 and equation (5.29), pp. 36–38: the source indexes the Γ_E-orbits inside a Γ_K-orbit by τ ∈ Gal(E/K) and writes v_{O_λ} as the sum over τ; it should run over the Γ_E-orbits C ⊆ O, since for nonnormal E orbits are missed and for Galois E repeated; affects the proof (the bound (5.28) and Theorem 5.8 are unchanged).
- **Misprint** in GLX26 at §5.1 ¶14, p. 32: the source calls MT(b,μ_η), defined as the identity component of the Zariski closure, the Tannakian group of ω on T_(b,μ_η) and a pure inner form of MT_cris,s; it should be the full Zariski closure (Chen14 Proposition 3.2.3); affects nothing.
- **Gap** in GLX26 at Proposition 6.7, step (4), p. 47: the source treats ξ(Γ_K) as a subgroup of G̃_der and deduces openness upstairs from openness of the image downstairs; it should pass through Zariski density of the open image and central lifting of derived containment, since the image need not lie in G̃_der; affects the proof.
- **Misprint** in GLX26 at Lemma 5.10, p. 40: the source says the open neighbourhood is étale over affine n-space over Q_p; it should be affine n-space over the reflex field E; affects nothing.
