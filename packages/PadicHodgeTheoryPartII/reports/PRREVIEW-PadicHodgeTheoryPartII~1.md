## R02 (P3) — §3.4 re-derives a derived-image equality that Tau Ceti already states

**Repair:** In §3.4 replace "For the equality, f(J°) is the scheme-theoretic image … makes D(J°) → D(f(J°)) faithfully flat." with: "For the equality, f(J°) is the scheme-theoretic image of J°, so O(f(J°)) → O(J°) is injective, and `BialgHom.comap_derivedDefiningIdeal_of_injective` makes the scheme-theoretic image of D(J°), which is f(D(J°)), equal to D(f(J°))." ## R03 (P3) — §1.5 gives μ^◇ a second definition with no stated agreement

**Location:** `README.md:322`: "and μ^◇ = `(1/s)Σ_{0≤i<s}` σ_0^i(μ̄). Repetitions in the average are permitted when s is enlarged."

**Claim:** The Conventions define μ^◇ as the Γ-orbit average of BunGAndNewtonStrata §1.7, and that is the μ^◇ in the Hodge–Newton irreducibility used by §2.4. §1.5 uses the iterated inertia-then-Frobenius average. No target, supplier or locator states that the two agree.

**Witness:** GLX26 (2.5)–(2.7), arXiv v3 pp. 14–15, equates the two (citing HN18 A.4). BunGAndNewtonStrata §1.7 defines only the orbit average. §2.4 combines §1.5's bound with the Conventions' μ^◇.

**Repair:** After the formula in §1.5 add: "This is the Galois average μ^◇ of BunGAndNewtonStrata §1.7. The action of `Γ_{Q_p}` on X_*(T) through σ_0 factors through a finite group, in which the image of `Γ_{Q_{p^s}}` is normal with cyclic quotient generated by σ_0. An average over a finite group is the average over a normal subgroup followed by the average over the quotient (GLX26 (2.5)–(2.7), arXiv v3 pp. 14–15)." (Quartic torus of §1.5: (μ̄ + σ_0μ̄)/2 = (1/4)Σ_τ e_τ, which is the ⟨g⟩-orbit average.)
