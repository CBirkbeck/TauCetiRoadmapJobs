## Source issues

- **Error** in GLX26 at §5.1 ¶14, arXiv v3 p. 32: the source calls MT(b,μ_η), defined in Definition 5.1 as the identity component of the Zariski closure, the Tannakian group of the generated crystalline category; it should be the full Zariski closure, as in Chen14 Définition 3.2.2 and Proposition 3.2.3; affects the proof.
- **Gap** in GLX26 at Proposition 5.7, arXiv v3 pp. 36–37: the source indexes the refined isotypic pieces by τ ∈ Gal(E/K) although the reflex field need not be normal over K; it should run over all Γ_E-orbits inside the Γ_K-orbit (for Res_{Q_5(5^{1/3})/Q_5}𝔾_m the printed formula predicts generic jump 1, the true jump is 0); affects a stated result.
- **Error** in GLX26 at equation (5.29), arXiv v3 p. 38: the source sums the refined components over every τ ∈ Gal(E/K); it should sum once over the distinct Γ_E-orbits (a quadratic Galois E and the trivial weight give 2v); affects nothing.
