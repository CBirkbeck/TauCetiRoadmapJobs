## R01 (P1) — The two Spin definitions disagree in rank zero

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/OrthogonalTamagawaAndLatticeMass/README.md:20–23`, “the even invertible elements … with … spinor norm … = 1” and “for n = 0 trivial”.

**Claim:** The displayed functor agrees with native `spinGroup` in every stated rank and has a central μ₂-kernel.

**Witness:** For V=0 over ℚ, the displayed normalizer is {1,−1}, of order 2. Native `lipschitzGroup` is generated by an empty set of invertible vectors, so native Spin has order 1.

The pinned definitions are `Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/SpinGroup.lean:61,153,292`. The README's normalizer condition is vacuous on V=0; the even Clifford algebra is ℚ, and both scalars have norm one. Thus the problem is not that Mathlib's rank-zero answer is wrong: the proposed functor and its promised comparison cannot both be correct.

**Repair:** Restrict the normalizer description and μ₂-kernel assertion to positive rank, define the rank-zero branch separately as the trivial group, and state its kernel as trivial. Keep the semisimple/simply-connected assertions at n≥3. Add the computed rank-zero comparison as a Check. This repair gives order 1 on both sides at n=0 and preserves order-2 scalar kernels in positive rank.
