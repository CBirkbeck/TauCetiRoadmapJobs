## R02 (P3) — The new step (iv) of *Log Hodge–Tate reduction* draws the generic-fibre isomorphism before the input it needs

**Location:** line 2957 "on the proper smooth generic fibre its source and target are isomorphic vector bundles by … and analytic GAGA, so a determinant argument makes it an isomorphism there; with (iii) and Nakayama at the smooth points of the special fibre it is a surjection".

**Claim:** a map between isomorphic vector bundles on a proper smooth space is an isomorphism by a determinant argument.

**Witness:** the zero map: its determinant is the constant 0. The determinant is a nonzero constant on each component only because the map is already surjective somewhere on it, which is the Nakayama step placed afterwards (ck lines 1166, 1172–1174).

**Repair:** replace the two clauses by: "by (iii) and Nakayama the algebraised map is surjective at the smooth points of the special fibre, hence on an open set meeting every connected component of the proper smooth generic fibre; there its source and target are isomorphic vector bundles by *The first higher direct image of the completed structure sheaf* (§AI.3.1) and analytic GAGA, and its determinant, a global function, is constant on each component and nonzero, so the map is an isomorphism on the generic fibre; it is therefore a surjection between vector bundles of the same rank, hence an isomorphism, over the smooth locus, whose complement has codimension at least two;".
