## Draft pull request body

This roadmap develops rational algebraic representations of split symplectic-similitude groups over characteristic-zero fields, from their coordinate Hopf algebras and primitive exterior powers to integral character-ring presentations. It identifies both the full representation ring and the subring generated by standard tensor-power constituents with explicit reciprocal Weyl invariants, proves the exact tensor-occurrence criterion, and recovers the full ring by multiplier localization. The independent multiplier coordinate, central parity and rational tensor models extend ClassicalGroups; the integral Laurent-algebra results also supply counting applications independently of representation theory.
