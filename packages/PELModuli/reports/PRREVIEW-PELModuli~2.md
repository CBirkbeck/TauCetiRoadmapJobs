## R02 (P3) — The field-equivalence sentence of M1.3 names a nonexistent object

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/PELModuli/README.md` lines 1650–1652: "an element of `O_F` congruent to a generator of one residue field of `O_F ⊗ κ̄` and to `0` at the others separates the residue-field embeddings by unique factorization".

Claim: the separating element is chosen through the residue fields of `O_F ⊗ κ̄`.

Witness: with `p` unramified in `F` and `κ̄` algebraically closed of characteristic `p`, `O_F ⊗ κ̄ ≅ ∏_τ κ̄`, one copy per embedding `τ : O_F → κ̄`; its residue fields are copies of `κ̄`, which has no generator. The argument needs the residue fields of `O_F` itself: `O_F ⊗ 𝔽_p = ∏_{𝔭 | p} O_F/𝔭`, and an `a ∈ O_F` whose image is a primitive element of one factor `O_F/𝔭 = 𝔽_{p^f}` and `0` in the others has pairwise distinct `τ(a)` over `𝔭` and `τ(a) = 0` elsewhere, so unique factorization in `κ̄[T]` returns each `r_τ` over `𝔭` from `charpoly(i(a) | Lie)`. For `O_F = ℤ[i]`, `p = 3`, `κ = 𝔽₉`: `a = i` separates `diag(i, i)` from `diag(i, −i)` (charpolys `(T − i)²` and `T² + 1`), while `a = 1` gives `(T − 1)²` for both, so the primitive element is what does the work. The mathematics of the sentence is right (R04 of the previous report is repaired); only the object named is wrong.

Repair: replace "a generator of one residue field of `O_F ⊗ κ̄`" by "a primitive element of one residue field `O_F/𝔭` of `O_F` above `p`".
