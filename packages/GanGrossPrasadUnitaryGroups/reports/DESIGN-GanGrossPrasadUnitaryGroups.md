# Design report: GanGrossPrasadUnitaryGroups

The design has 39 targets (9 definitions, 8 constructions and 22 theorems) and 15 explicit supplier gaps. It covers every endpoint in `packages/GanGrossPrasadUnitaryGroups/target.md`. It has seven layers, including relative Hermitian geometry, all local odd-codimension multiplicity bounds, the actual tempered nonsplit packet criterion and its character, the general fixed-pair conjecture, adjacent local normalization, relative spectral contribution, endoscopic global nonvanishing and GGP.8. There are no merged targets to exclude. `parent` is null: the historical broad GGP proposal is not an existing base roadmap on main.

The files are `packages/GanGrossPrasadUnitaryGroups/design/README.md`, `packages/GanGrossPrasadUnitaryGroups/design/plan.json` and `packages/GanGrossPrasadUnitaryGroups/sources.toml`. Every target has a source locator, explicit hypotheses and recursively typed prerequisites. Every definition has at least three Checks and a named API. The README includes proof sketches; the JSON contains the same statements, dependencies, API and Checks. No `Suggested.lean` was requested or written. The planned Checks are mathematics and future Lean unit-test obligations, not claims of executed Lean tests. No Lean command was run.

The pinned checkouts match the manifest: Mathlib 6b7abb3c7686292736be2955bd3eb9ebf63b456a and Tau Ceti a91d3aafa8cd3e6bc33dfde0d7677ed0f1625039. The statements of the cited declarations were read at these pins. The direct declaration review covered `LinearMap.Nondegenerate`, `Submodule.orthogonalBilin`, `Representation.IntertwiningMap` and its extensionality/evaluation, `Representation.tprod`, `Representation.dual`, `Matrix.unitaryGroup` and `TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.pointsFunctor`. Searches by form, matrix, intertwiner, unitary, Bessel, Langlands and measure objects found no pinned relative GGP implementation. The identity-form matrix group is not an arbitrary-Hermitian supplier.

Main-branch roadmaps read for shape and boundaries were GlobalNumberFields and Completed RestrictedProducts, with ReductiveGroups Layers 0, 3, 5, 8 and RepresentationTheory/ClassicalGroups Layer 6 checked for their used interfaces. LocalFieldsRamification Layer 0 and OrthogonalSpinGroups boundaries were inspected. The named open roadmap heads have no required interface for this design, so none is a dependency. Board continuations checked for overlap include AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII, AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactorsPartII, SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroupsPartII, ModularityAndLanglandsExtensionsPartII, ReductiveGroupsPartIII, TwistedAutomorphicDescents, SelmerIwasawaCohomologyPartII and QuantitativeOrbitMethodAndMicrolocalAnalysisOnLieGroups. ## Adversarial mathematics pass

Each row covers the main statement and its API and Checks; the conventions above it are included in the relevant rows. Characteristic-p local fields, inseparable coefficient extensions, characteristic 2 algebraic forms, the zero ring and q=1 are outside the explicit domains. The finite-Galois/Weil L-group distinction was corrected in target 4.3: the connected dual is index two only in the finite-Galois model; R is inflated to the Weil L-group. Zero module, ranks 0/1/2, split coefficients, dyadic and ramified characteristic-zero places, noncompact groups, identity transport and zero vectors are tested inside the domains where applicable. The absence of these source hypotheses was not repaired by narrowing the endpoints to good primes or quasi-split forms.

The archimedean uniqueness reduction needed a material correction: JSZ Propositions 3.3–3.4 give an injection on each finite-dimensional subspace of the Hom space, with an inducing parameter chosen for that subspace. They do not assert one common parameter for the entire Hom space before finiteness is known. Target 2.5 states the finite-subspace form, and target 2.6 rules out a two-dimensional subspace. The real theta argument also retains the source’s integrated seesaw rather than assuming irreducibility of the big theta lift (xue-real line 329–341).

The source extraction’s final component-count line must be read with the earlier definitions: bpcz line 104 gives S_Π of order 2^r, while the spectral coefficient at bpcz line 4749 is 2^(-dim a_P). The coefficient is therefore the inverse order. Targets 6.3, 6.5 and 7.4 pin that direction with the three-constituent value 1/8. No assertion that |S_Π| itself is 2^(-dim a_P) is imported from the extraction.

## Scope boundaries, ownership and continuations

No existing roadmap theorem was replanned. The algebraic group, arithmetic adelic and restricted-product inputs are imported by their main-branch sections. Missing primary sections are represented only by `gap:` edges. The packet hypotheses in the older Beuzart-Plessis sources are not turned into theorem hypotheses on arbitrary packets: actual correspondence theorems, including normalization, are requested from the classification owner.

No notion was moved down from a later roadmap. The absent general supplier contracts remain explicit gaps with earlier owners, or with supplier `none` for generic distribution foundations. General unitary Bessel geometry, invariant distributions, relative tempered forms, geometric multiplicity and the star-generic relative spectral calculation are owned here because they are the relative theory required by these endpoints. Spectral multipliers and general doubling L-function theory already have board continuation owners and are not duplicated. The unassigned general distribution foundation is an explicit gap rather than a disguised GGP axiom.

Proposed continuations are: GanGrossPrasadUnitaryGroups, Part II for the excluded orthogonal/Fourier–Jacobi endpoints; ReductiveGroupsPartII’s arbitrary-form Hermitian/unitary extension; the signed-Asai and global norm extension of AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactors; the general local harmonic-analysis and Schwartz-isolation extensions of AutomorphicSpectralTheory; and EndoscopicTransferAndUnitaryTraceComparison, Part II for general Jacquet–Rallis geometric and local relative comparison beyond ordinary endoscopy. The existing AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII design owns the isolation extension; it must receive an eligible earlier placement and exact primary contract before discharging this design’s base-owner gap. Its absent placement is not concealed by a direct import.

SelmerIwasawaCohomologyPartII occurs before the last required classification supplier and therefore cannot gain a dependency on this roadmap in its existing place. Its §8.1 already accepts a period witness as a premise. A subsequent SelmerIwasawaCohomologyPartIII can apply target 7.1 after proving that the resulting inner form is its required definite form and obtaining its integral test vector. The report does not claim that existential global nonvanishing alone fills those arithmetic requirements. The two other broad-model consumers receive only the unitary Bessel portion of their contracts.
