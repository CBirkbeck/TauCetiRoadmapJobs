## Scope

Develop the unitary Bessel restriction library: relevant Hermitian pairs, Bessel functionals and periods, multiplicity one, the tempered local packet theorem with its specified character, global period nonvanishing, and the refined adjacent-rank period identity. Export **GGP.8** under this roadmap's name to PeriodsAndSpecialValues. The targets below specify endpoints, not an implementation sequence.

For the algebraic relative constructions use finite-dimensional nondegenerate Hermitian spaces over quadratic étale algebras over fields of characteristic different from two. A relevant inclusion has odd codimension `2r+1` and complement equal to `r` hyperbolic planes plus a nondegenerate line. The ambient spaces need not be quasi-split. Include all relevant pure inner forms, both possible signs of the real distinguished line, all allowed ranks, and transport under isometry and changes of auxiliary data. Local analytic theorems concern characteristic-zero local fields, with smooth admissible representations at finite places and Casselman–Wallach representations at infinite places. Retain split local models, including complex places, with the precise proved ranges below. Global theorems concern quadratic extensions of arbitrary number fields; no totally real, CM, unramified, odd-residue-characteristic or auxiliary-supercuspidal restriction applies throughout the library.

General Hermitian form theory, unitary algebraic groups, local representation categories, enhanced Langlands packets, endoscopy, base change, automorphic spectra, measures and L-functions remain upstream subjects. This roadmap owns their relative Bessel constructions and GGP assertions. Orthogonal pairs, symplectic/metaplectic pairs, Fourier–Jacobi models and function fields are reserved for a future Part II. Arithmetic cycles, Selmer ranks, heights and derivative identities remain with their arithmetic owners. Spectral isolation and microlocal period asymptotics remain with the existing designs identified below. A theta or trace-formula proof may consume upstream general theory without adding its other GGP endpoints here.

Conjectures must be named propositions, distinguished from theorems proving a covered instance. Never obtain a theorem by assuming its desired period identity or by supplying arbitrary packets as data. No Lean is requested at triage. Later `Suggested.lean` must use one `import Mathlib`, easy headline statements on existing carriers and proved checks of its own definitions; no sorry'd data or `set_option`.

## Final targets

Locators refer to the exact texts in `packages/GanGrossPrasadUnitaryGroups/sources.toml`; preprint numbering is not asserted to match a publication. Use a consistent bilinear convention: `m(πV,πW) = dim_C Hom_H(πV ⊗ πW, ν)`. Here `H = N ⋊ U(W)` embeds diagonally in `U(V) × U(W)`, and `ν` is the generic Bessel character extended trivially over `U(W)`. In the archimedean case use completed projective tensor products and continuous functionals. The older convention `Hom_(U(W),ν)(πV,πW)` requires replacing the second representation by its contragredient. The inverse character in a period integral is fixed by this same convention.

1. **Bessel models and multiplicity one.** For every relevant local Hermitian pair `W ⊂ V` of dimensions `n` and `n+2r+1`, where `n,r ≥ 0`, and irreducible representations in the categories just specified, construct the Bessel model and prove `m(πV,πW) ≤ 1`. This includes `r=0`, arbitrary ramification and residue characteristic two, and real signatures of either line sign. For nonarchimedean fields use AGRS Theorem 1′, preprint p. 2 (agrs line 41, agrs line 59), and the higher-codimension reduction of GGP Theorem 15.1, pp. 60–63, keeping its supercuspidal auxiliary representation and Bernstein-component exclusion in the reduction (ggp line 2152, ggp line 2158). At infinity use Sun–Zhu Theorem B, p. 2 (sun-zhu line 55, sun-zhu line 66), and Jiang–Sun–Zhu Theorem A, pp. 1–2, for general codimension (jsz-bessel line 11, jsz-bessel line 24, jsz-bessel line 38). Definitions and relevant inner forms: GGP §2, pp. 5–7, and §§12, 14 (ggp line 229, ggp line 249, ggp line 255, ggp line 2060). These reductions do not restrict the final representations to be supercuspidal or tempered.

2. **Tempered local GGP, including the distinguished character.** Let `E/F` be a quadratic field extension of nonarchimedean local fields of characteristic zero, or `C/R`. Fix a relevant Bessel complement, compatible Whittaker normalization and a nontrivial additive character `ψE` of `E` trivial on `F`. For tempered parameters `φV,φW` of dimensions `n+2r+1,n`, with each degree-m parameter conjugate-self-dual of sign `(-1)^(m-1)`, use the actual enhanced Vogan packets across all relevant pure inner forms. Exactly one pair has multiplicity one; all others have multiplicity zero. On each component-group generator corresponding to an irreducible conjugate-self-dual summand `ρ` of the appropriate sign, its distinguished enhancement has value `ε(1/2,ρ ⊗ φW,ψE)` on the V side, and `ε(1/2,φV ⊗ τ,ψE)` for a corresponding summand `τ` on the W side. The central-element restrictions select the relevant inner form; they are part of the assertion. Changes of Whittaker/additive data and scaling of the forms must transport both the model and the enhancement.

   The character construction is GGP Theorem 6.1, pp. 20–21, and the Hermitian clause preceding Conjecture 17.3, pp. 69–70 (ggp line 728, ggp line 754, ggp line 2433). The nonarchimedean refined result is Beuzart-Plessis Theorem 8.5.1, pp. 70–71 (bp-refined line 2855, bp-refined line 2869), with packet assumptions §7.2 and normalization §§7.7, 8.3; those properties must be imported as theorems about the supplier's correspondence. Its precursor is Theorem 18.4.1 under §18.1 in the tempered memoir (bp-unitary-tempered line 5284, bp-unitary-tempered line 5429). Beuzart-Plessis Theorem 12.4.1, pp. 289–290, gives packet uniqueness for real and p-adic fields, with the established packet properties explained in §12.3 (bp-arch line 11172, bp-arch line 11200). The explicit real character is Xue Theorem 2.2, published p. 1000, with normalization §2.1 and reduction Corollary 2.5, p. 1001 (xue-real line 117, xue-real line 174, xue-real line 213, xue-real line 254). Xue's fixed line sign is transported by simultaneous negation of the forms (xue-real line 179). For split local algebras formulate the tempered singleton-packet prediction in every relevant odd codimension, as a specialization of GGP Conjecture 17.1. Its certified GL theorem here is GGP Theorem 17.2: all odd codimensions nonarchimedean, adjacent ranks archimedean (ggp line 2394). Do not present this citation as a proof of the larger-codimension split archimedean criterion or extend the theorem to arbitrary nongeneric parameters.

3. **Global Bessel conjecture on a fixed pair.** For a quadratic extension `E/F` of number fields and any relevant odd-codimension Hermitian pair, construct the convergent cuspidal Bessel period `P(f) = ∫_[H] f(h) ν(h)^(-1) dh`, with a rationally trivial adelic Bessel character. For an irreducible everywhere tempered representation `π` occurring with multiplicity one in the cuspidal spectrum of `U(V) × U(W)`, formulate the equivalence: `P|π ≠ 0` if and only if every local Bessel model is nonzero and `L(1/2,π,R) ≠ 0`. Here `R` is the induced tensor representation of the unitary L-group in GGP §24, and the L-function includes infinite factors. The period/nonvanishing assertion at this full range is a conjectural proposition, not a consequence of multiplicity one alone. Locators: GGP §§23–24, pp. 86–90 (ggp line 3036, ggp line 3050, ggp line 3151), Conjecture 24.1 (ggp line 3175). The adjacent-rank theorems below are proved instances with their own hypotheses.

4. **Proved adjacent-rank global nonvanishing, including endoscopy.** For any quadratic number-field extension `E/F` and `n ≥ 1`, let `Πn,Π(n+1)` be Hermitian discrete Arthur parameters. In degree `m` this means the full parabolic induction of pairwise distinct conjugate-self-dual cuspidal constituents, each with a pole at `s=1` of `L(s,Πi,As^((-1)^(m+1)))`. Then `L(1/2,Πn × Π(n+1)) ≠ 0` exactly when some nondegenerate rank-n Hermitian space `h` admits a cuspidal automorphic subrepresentation `σ` of `U(h) × U(h ⊕ norm-line)` with this base change and nonzero diagonal period. The quantifier ranges over `h`; the theorem does not choose an arbitrary fixed inner form. No temperedness premise is added to this nonvanishing theorem. Locators: BPCZ §§1.1.2–1.1.4 and Theorem 1.1.5.1, pp. 2–3 (bpcz line 81, bpcz line 93, bpcz line 108, bpcz line 124). The stable case is BPLZZ Definition 1.5 and Theorem 1.8, pp. 3–4: both base changes are cuspidal, with the source's `n>1` convention (bplzz line 127, bplzz line 128, bplzz line 157). No auxiliary split supercuspidal place is required.

5. **Harris's refined conjecture and local normalization.** For adjacent Hermitian spaces over `E/F`, and irreducible everywhere tempered cuspidal `πn,π(n+1)`, formulate the squared-period identity with Tamagawa Petersson pairings, normalized local matrix-coefficient integrals, the completed Rankin–Selberg central value divided by the two completed adjoint values, and the inverse order of the parameter component groups. This is R. Neal Harris's Conjecture 1.3 in the version read, p. 3 (harris line 132), with pairings and local periods on pp. 2–3 (harris line 75, harris line 92, harris line 110). The explicit completed normalization and its proved scope are given in GGP.8 below. Include local absolute convergence for tempered representations and the almost-everywhere spherical evaluation: Harris Proposition 2.1, p. 5 (harris line 243), and Theorem 2.12, p. 23 (harris line 1289). The spherical calculation has the unramified, hyperspecial, normalized-vector and finite-place exclusions listed in §2 (harris line 170); those exclusions do not remove bad or infinite places from the identity.

6. **GGP.8 — unitary period/L-value identity.** Let `E/F` be any quadratic extension of number fields, `n ≥ 1`, `h` any nondegenerate rank-n Hermitian space, and `σ` an irreducible cuspidal automorphic representation of `G_h = U(h) × U(h ⊕ norm-line)` which is tempered at every place. Put `Π = BC(σ)`. If its degree-m base change has `r_m` distinct cuspidal constituents, set `S_Π = (Z/2Z)^(r_n+r_(n+1))`. With `η` the quadratic idele class character, use completed factors to define

   `C(σ) = (∏_(i=1..n+1) L(i,η^i)) · L(1/2,Πn × Π(n+1)) / L(1,σ,Ad)`.

   For each place define the corresponding quotient `C_v` of local factors and

   `α_v(f_v,f_v) = C_v^(-1) · ∫_(U(h)(F_v)) ⟨σ_v(t) f_v,f_v⟩_v dt`.

   For every nonzero factorizable `f = ⊗_v f_v`, prove

   `|P_h(f)|² / ⟨f,f⟩_Pet = |S_Π|^(-1) · C(σ) · ∏_v (α_v(f_v,f_v) / ⟨f_v,f_v⟩_v)`.

   Use Tamagawa measures for both global integrals and local Haar measures factoring the diagonal group's Tamagawa measure. Include convergence of the local integrals, regular nonzero local normalizing factors, and the almost-everywhere spherical evaluation that makes the product well-defined. Local invariant inner products may be chosen arbitrarily as in the source. All ramified, dyadic, split and infinite places remain. BPCZ §1.1.6 and Theorem 1.1.6.1, pp. 4–5 (bpcz line 148, bpcz line 159, bpcz line 179, bpcz line 188), with measures §2.3.2, p. 15 (bpcz line 679), is the precise export contract. Endoscopic base change is allowed; `|S_Π|^(-1)` equals `1/4` only in the stable cuspidal-base-change case recorded there (bpcz line 194). BPLZZ Theorem 1.10 supplies that stable everywhere-tempered case (bplzz line 185). This theorem is not an identity for arbitrary non-tempered representations or higher-codimension periods.

Checks should cover the adjacent split case, the compact real interlacing specialization of Xue's theorem, both real line signs, invariance under vector/local-pairing/measure rescaling, the spherical local normalization, and an endoscopic constituent count giving a constant different from `1/4`. They test the general definitions; they do not replace the general endpoints.
