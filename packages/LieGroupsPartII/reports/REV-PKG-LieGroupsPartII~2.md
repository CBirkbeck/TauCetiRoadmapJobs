1. **F01** (P3) The Check for `M` in `SL_2(ℝ)` gives a witness that also fits `N_K(𝔞)`

   **Location.** `packages/LieGroupsPartII/README.md` line 740 (§7.3, Checks, "The group `M`"): "For `SL_2(ℝ)` it is `{±1}`: the rotation `R_φ` sends `diag(1, −1)` to `((cos 2φ, sin 2φ), (sin 2φ, −cos 2φ))`, equal to `diag(1, −1)` only for `sin 2φ = 0`."

   **Claim.** The computed criterion identifies `M = Z_K(𝔞)` as `{±1}`.

   **Witness.** `sin 2φ = 0` holds at `φ = π/2`, where the image is `((−1, 0), (0, 1)) = −diag(1, −1)`; `R_{π/2} = ((0, −1), (1, 0)) ∉ M`. The solutions of `sin 2φ = 0` are the four elements of `N_K(𝔞)` (the next Check), not of `M`.

   **Repair.** Replace "equal to `diag(1, −1)` only for `sin 2φ = 0`" by "equal to `diag(1, −1)` exactly when `cos 2φ = 1`, that is, `R_φ = ±1`; `sin 2φ = 0` alone gives the four elements of `N_K(𝔞)` of the next Check". Tested on the refuting instance: `φ = π/2` has `cos 2φ = −1`, so it is excluded; `φ = 0, π` have `cos 2φ = 1` and give `±1`.
