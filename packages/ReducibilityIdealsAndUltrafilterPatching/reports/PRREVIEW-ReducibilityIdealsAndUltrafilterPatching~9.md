## C02 — The method label of 6.13 calls the closed normal subgroup `K_∞` the limit group

The method label (lines 635–636) says "The limit group is the closed normal subgroup that agrees with the kernels of almost all levels modulo every open normal subgroup". That subgroup is `K_∞`. In 6.13 (1) the limit group is `G_∞ := Ph/K_∞`, and the Examples of Layer 6 say that for a principal ultrafilter at `N₀` "the limit group is `Γ_{N₀}`". With that ultrafilter, the label's object is `ker π_{N₀}`, not `Γ_{N₀}`. Write "The limit group is the quotient of `Ph` by the closed normal subgroup that …".
