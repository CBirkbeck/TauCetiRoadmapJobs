## R02 (P3) — the `SO₀(n,1)` Check is false at `n = 1`, and the definition lacks the negative control that it forces `G⁰` semisimple

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/AdelicAlgebraicGroups/README.md:3428–3431` (“For `G = SO₀(n,1)`, `k` totally real and `f` a quadratic form over `k` in `n+1` variables of signature `(n,1)` … `SO(f)(𝒪_k) ∩ G` is an arithmetic lattice with `𝐇 = Res_{k/ℚ} SO(f)`”), and `:3399` (“Semisimplicity of `G⁰` is not part of the predicate”).

Claim: for every `n` the stated group is an arithmetic lattice in the sense of the definition.

Witness: `n = 1`, `k = ℚ(√2)`, `f = x² − √2 y²` (signature `(1,1)` at the identity embedding, definite at the other). `SO(f)` is the norm-one torus of `ℚ(2^{1/4})/ℚ(√2)`, of dimension 1, so `𝐇 = Res_{k/ℚ} SO(f)` is a two-dimensional torus, not semisimple, and the definition does not admit it. No other `𝐇` works either: `G⁰ = SO₀(1,1) ≅ ℝ_{>0}` is abelian and noncompact, and a continuous homomorphism from a connected semisimple Lie group to `ℝ_{>0}` is trivial (its differential kills `[𝔥,𝔥] = 𝔥`), so no surjective `p` exists. Yet `Γ = SO(f)(𝒪_k) ∩ SO₀(1,1)` is a lattice: it is the group of relative norm-one units of `ℚ(2^{1/4})` (unit rank `2`) over `ℚ(√2)` (unit rank `1`), infinite cyclic modulo torsion, cocompact in `ℝ_{>0}` by the Borel–Harish-Chandra criterion for the `ℚ`-anisotropic torus `𝐇`. So the Check fails at `n = 1`. For `n ≥ 2`, `SO(f)` is connected semisimple (absolutely simple for `n ≠ 3`), `𝐇(ℝ)⁰ = SO₀(n,1) × ∏_{σ ≠ id} SO(n+1)`, `ker p = ∏_{σ ≠ id} SO(n+1)` is compact, and the Check is correct as computed in the table below.

Repair: write “For `n ≥ 2` and `G = SO₀(n,1)` …”, and add the negative control the sentence at `:3399` calls for: “The predicate forces `G⁰` to be semisimple, since `G⁰ ≅ 𝐇(ℝ)⁰/ker p` with `ker p` compact: for `G = ℝ` and `Γ = ℤ = 𝔾_a(ℤ)`, `Γ` is a lattice and an arithmetic subgroup of `𝔾_a(ℚ)` (`Reduction.IsSArithmetic ∅`), but not an arithmetic lattice; likewise `SO(f)(𝒪_k) ∩ SO₀(1,1)` for a binary form `f`.”
