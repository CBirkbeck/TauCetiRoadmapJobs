## R01 (P3) — the Weyl-orbit bullet of AA.1.5 cites a Borel passage that contains no such statement

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/AdelicAlgebraicGroups/README.md:1373–1377` (“**Weyl orbit products lie in the split centre up to finite index.** … in particular some power of it lies in the split centre ([Borel], §5.5, p. 20)”).

Claim: Borel §5.5, p. 20 supports the statement that for `a ∈ A(E)` the product `∏_{w∈W} w(a)` lies in `(Z(G) ∩ A)(E)` up to an element of a finite group.

Witness: Borel §5.5 (cache lines 655–678, printed pp. 20–21) is the unimodularity lemma: the equivalence of unimodularity of `G_A`, `G_∞`, `G_A^∞`, `G_k^∞` with right invariance of a left-invariant top form, the sufficiency of `X_k(G°) = 1`, and the convergence-factor construction of the product measure. Neither it nor the proof of Theorem 5.8 (lines 716–733, which uses 5.5, 5.7 and 1.8 only) mentions Weyl orbits or products over the Weyl group; the text has no occurrence of “Weyl” at all. The statement itself is true: a `W`-fixed `s ∈ A` satisfies `r_α(s) = s`, hence `α^∨(α(s)) = 1` and `α(s)² = 1` for every relative root `α`, so `s²` lies in `⋂_α ker α`, whose identity component is `(Z(G) ∩ A)°` by Borel–Tits §5.3, p. 96 (cache lines 1711–1720: `Z° = (𝒵(G) ∩ S)°`); test: in `GL₂` with `a = diag(2,1)`, `∏_w w(a) = diag(2,1)·diag(1,2) = 2I`, central.

Repair: replace the locator by “[Borel–Tits], §5.3, p. 96, for `(⋂_α ker α)° = (Z(G) ∩ S)°`; the `W`-invariance step is an owned deduction”, keeping the `*Needs:*` line as it is. (The first bullet of AA.1.5, that the modular character is trivial on compact subgroups and on the centre, also cites Borel §5.5, which does not state it either; it is a general locally-compact-group fact and could cite Mathlib `modularCharacter` alone.)

## R02 (P3) — “changes both sides by the same finite product” misdescribes the change of presentation in the one-dimensional comparison

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/AdelicAlgebraicGroups/README.md:2037–2040` (“Presenting `ψ̃` at a multiple `𝔫` of `𝔪` changes both sides by the same finite product: `L_𝔫(s, ψ̃′) = L_𝔪(s, ψ̃) ∏ …`, and `S_ψ` grows by exactly those primes”).

Claim (as worded): both sides of the comparison identity change by the same finite product under a change of presentation.

Witness: the left side `L(ψ, s)` is the Euler product of the local Artin factors and does not depend on the presentation at all. What changes is the right side only, in two mutually cancelling ways: `L_𝔫 = L_𝔪 · ∏_{𝔭∣𝔫, 𝔭∤𝔪}(1 − ψ̃([𝔭]) N𝔭^(−s))` (LFunctions `heckeLFunctionC_induced`), and `S_ψ(𝔫) = S_ψ(𝔪) ∪ {𝔭 ∣ 𝔫, 𝔭 ∤ 𝔪}` contributes the reciprocal factors `(1 − ψ(Frob_𝔭) N𝔭^(−s))^(−1)` with `ψ(Frob_𝔭) = ψ̃([𝔭])`. Instance `χ₄` at `12∞` (the Check two bullets below): `L_{12∞} = L_{4∞}(1 + 3^(−s))` and `S` gains `3` with factor `(1 + 3^(−s))^(−1)`; the product is unchanged, the left side `L(χ₄, s)` never moved.

Repair: “Presenting `ψ̃` at a multiple `𝔫` of `𝔪` leaves the left side unchanged and changes the two factors of the right side inversely: `L_𝔫(s, ψ̃′) = L_𝔪(s, ψ̃) ∏_{𝔭 ∣ 𝔫, 𝔭 ∤ 𝔪} (1 − ψ̃([𝔭]) N𝔭^(−s))` (LFunctions, layer 5, `heckeLFunctionC_induced` with `eulerCorrection`), while `S_ψ` grows by exactly those primes, at which `ψ` is unramified since `F_𝔪/F` is, so the identity holds at every presentation.”
