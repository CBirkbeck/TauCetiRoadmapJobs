## Mathematical corrections

1. **Conjugation in the generalized datum.** The accepted prose wrote δe(g,ιg)∈M_P. CFK §3.3, p.13, CFK lines 510–518, uses left-superscript conjugation. The target and API now say δe(g,ιg)δ⁻¹∈M_P. At g=1 this gives 1; the former expression gives δ. In the Sp₂,k=2 example of CFK line 534, the Weyl representative has an off-Levi block, so δ is not in the Siegel Levi. This is a genuine correction to the plan's expanded notation, not an erratum in CFK.
2. **Distinguished central torus at low GSpin rank.** The plan conflated C_G^•=e₀∨(Gₘ) with the full connected center for every c>1. CFK §1, pp.6–7, CFK lines 264–271, identifies them only for c>2. For GSpin₂=Gₘ² their dimensions are 1 and 2 respectively. All local/global quotients, kernels and restricted central characters now use the distinguished torus, retaining GSpin₂. The quotient dimension is 1, whereas the old full-center quotient has dimension 0. This does not restrict the theorem to higher ranks.
3. **Cauchy action and Borel dictionary.** Section 4.4 now specifies the actual left polynomial action ((g₁,g₂)·f)(X)=f(g₁⁻¹Xg₂), instead of leaving the literal transposed matrix substitution as an ambiguous action. Its first Borel is lower triangular and its second upper triangular, so leading principal minors are highest vectors. The decomposition remains F_λ∨⊗F_λ. The two noncommuting unipotents in the added Check give composed inverse matrix [[1,−1],[−1,2]]; retaining the transposed substitution in this same left-pullback interpretation gives [[2,−1],[−1,1]] on one side. This corrects the literal plan formulation while retaining the intended Cauchy theorem and requiring its compact-Borel comparison. EHLS Lemma 4.4.8, p.76, EHLS lines 3515–3530, supplies the decomposition; it is not used to justify an unspecified group-action convention.
4. **No circular continuation dependency.** The integral construction in §6.1 now establishes its actual convergence-chamber definition and descent; §6.2 proves continuation. The construction no longer lists the subsequent continuation as an input to its own definition. Catchall references to source obligations G1–G6 were replaced with actual comparison/analytic construction targets and precise supplier gaps. The local Speh and theta targets have no self-reference in their Needs lines.
5. **Current ownership and locators.** Residual Speh is imported from AutomorphicSpectralTheory §4.14a, not the obsolete ET.7a label. Isobaric sums come from the base roadmap's Representation prerequisites, not a second constructor in AS.2. Nongeneric GL factors use EndoscopicTransferAndUnitaryTraceComparison §10.29. Real globalization, parameters, unitary exponents and cuspidal globalization use actual §§2.1–2.7 and 4.2, not the design's atlas-only AF.1/AF.3 labels. The native matrix/character locators were reread at the present pins.
6. **Exact domains and boundaries.** The auxiliary archimedean range is stated explicitly, finite-place rationality requires polynomial sections, holomorphy of Eisenstein values requires holomorphic section families, all-order conormal conditions are explicit, and finite-strip boundedness has the quantifiers ∀a≤b ∃C≥0 ∀s. Dimension-zero multiplier-recording GU and split quadratic-étale modules are included. The design's accepted inverse χ, true GU modulus, half-unit parameters, dual compact weights, weighted minor degree, order inequality and both bad-place L-factor conditions are preserved and given numerical witnesses.

The source review's rejected E1/E9/E29 allegations were not promoted to mathematical restrictions or source errata. In particular auxiliary multiplicativity uses its stated support/convergence argument, and EHLS Corollary 4.4.9 permits differential-operator coefficients off the diagonal. Its nonzero highest-line projection is the target retained here.
