## Corrections and unsupported inferences

1. Restricted microlocal splitting to a nonzero derived algebra with at least two simple factors. A rank-one skyscraper has Euler/Gauss degree 1 and connected group either trivial or a torus, so “not simple modulo the centre” without the nonzero-derived qualification cannot force two degrees greater than 1. The almost-simple hypersurface argument excludes a zero derived algebra separately using connected irreducibility and N≥6.
2. Corrected the coordinate-axis step of Frobenius avoidance. For an elliptic curve embedded by a↦(2a,3a), restriction is χ₁²χ₂³=1 and the first-axis kernel has four 2-torsion characters. It need not be one connected restriction kernel. A finite union of torsion translates suffices for the collision argument.
3. Kept the full arbitrary-character generic family theorem. A geometric-origin object twisted by an infinite-order character need not have geometric origin. The arbitrary semisimple proper-direct-image contract now appears in the relative-vanishing and generic-family proof routes, while torsion twists can use the geometric-origin theorem.
4. Replaced stale EDC/CA/R06 section references by the sections actually read. The tensor-isomorphism torsor addition in old R4 is supplied by MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles §6.8. The minuscule list and relative pointed Albanese property are not inferred from the weaker N2 and relative-Abel formulas and remain gaps.
5. HodgeStructuresPartII was not found in main, the indexed heads or the provided board files. Its proposed generic variation input is replaced by the earlier actual owner ShimuraData §§3.1–3.4, with the bounded-norm/orbit closedness addition explicitly registered there. No dependency on a nonexistent or later Hodge roadmap is retained.
6. Made the clean characteristic-cycle map the map respecting quotient convolution, distinguishing it from the full additive cycle. Added all five rational second-occupancy ratios with positive denominator conditions.
7. Defined the trace projector with its actual source, target and degree: u:V_m→V_m′, t:V_m′→V_m, r=(m′/m)^(2n)>0, e=ut/r on V_m′ and tu=r id. An unmarked total Hodge isomorphism can swap two equal Hodge summands and does not suffice.

8. Eliminated the extra general Chern–Gauss–Bonnet demand after finding EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.6’s rational étale Gauss–Bonnet theorem. ComplexComparisonPartII §C5.1 supplies the rational Betti comparison, so only conormal Gauss geometry remains a SchemeAndStackFoundations gap. Corrected the regular-immersion conormal citation to §5.5.
