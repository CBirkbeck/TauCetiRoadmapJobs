## Source issues

These issues occur in the legally retrieved source editions. The roadmap already avoids their problematic formulations; they are source errata, not additional findings against this folder.

- **Misprint** in FS at Proposition IV.1.8 proof, p.109 (FS line 4153): the source says the fibre-product atlas uses mismatched base and chart indices; it should be the atlas U₂×_(X₁)U₃ for X₂×_(X₁)X₃, with its projection; affects the proof.
- **Error** in FS at Remark IV.1.10, p.110 (FS line 4199): the source says the point map of a locally profinite classifying stack is nonsmooth unless the group is finite; it should be smooth also for infinite discrete groups, while infinite compact open subgroups obstruct smoothness; affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in FS at §IV.5 annular construction, pp.152–153 (FS lines 5885,5889): the source says the annular inequalities with t and u interchanged in consecutive definitions; it should be one consistent convention, such as ∣u∣ᵇ≤∣t∣≤∣u∣ᵃ, with reindexing when exchanging coordinates; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in FS at Proposition VII.7.10, p.276 (FS line 10699): the source says the lisse exterior product is compact in the étale category; it should be compact in the lisse category appearing in the displayed functor; affects a stated result.
- **Gap** in PQ at Lemma A.5 proof, p.90 (PQ lines 4148–4154): the source says an arbitrary profinite test map can be used in a square over a fixed finite stage; it should be factored through one common stage using Lemma A.1 before that square is formed; affects the proof.
- **Error** in PQ at Appendix A.1 opening, p.90 (PQ lines 4176–4178): the source says composing any accessible ring-valued functor of points with a condensed ring automatically gives a condensed set; it should be restricted to functors preserving the required sheaf conditions, such as representables, or followed by sheafification; affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in PQ at Lemma A.8 proof, p.91 (PQ lines 4184–4189): the source says a family of r polynomial equations defines a map Aⁿ→A; it should be a map Aⁿ→Aʳ with the zero section in Aʳ; affects the proof.
- **Gap** in PQ at Lemma A.8 proof, p.91 (PQ line 4184): the source says an arbitrary closed immersion into affine space is cut out by finitely many equations; it should be allowed arbitrary equations, using intersections of closed subsets of compact test spaces; affects the proof.
