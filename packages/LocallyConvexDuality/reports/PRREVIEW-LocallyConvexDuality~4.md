## R01 (P2) — The general notion of nuclear space already has owners

**Location.** `TauCetiRoadmap/LocallyConvexDuality/README.md` lines 44–46 ("AutomorphicSpectralTheory §§0.11 and 0.13 keep their nuclear Fréchet and LF test spaces and their kernel theorem, stated for those function spaces; the general notion of nuclear space of Layer 5 is the one they specialise") and §5.2 lines 564–567 ("Call a separated locally convex space `E` nuclear when …").

**Claim.** Layer 5 owns "the general notion of nuclear locally convex space with its characterisations and permanence properties" (line 31–32), and AutomorphicSpectralTheory §0.11 only defines particular test spaces.

**Witness.** AutomorphicSpectralTheory §0.11, at its open head, line 278: "For Hausdorff locally convex complex `E`, define Fréchet by completeness and a countable seminorm topology. Nuclear maps admit Banach factorizations … nuclear spaces require these factorizations for strengthened seminorm quotient maps." That is Grothendieck's criterion (c) of Théorème 1 (grothendieck1952 lines 1111–1113) stated as the definition of a nuclear space for an arbitrary Hausdorff locally convex complex space, not for a function space. SpectralManifolds (open, earlier in the merge order) treats it as the owner: its supplier table line 26 consumes "the definitions of Fréchet space, nuclear map and nuclear space by Banach factorisation of the strengthened seminorm quotient maps, for Hausdorff locally convex complex spaces; SM3 adds the real-scalar case, independence of the defining family, the finite-sum, closed-subspace and Hilbert-scale criteria", and its SM3 text (line 91) builds exactly that interface on native locally convex spaces over ℝ and ℂ. Both roadmaps precede LocallyConvexDuality in the merge order (AutomorphicSpectralTheory and SpectralManifolds are in the list of earlier roadmaps). So 5.2 defines, by a different but equivalent property (`E ⊗̂π F = E ⊗̂ε F`), an object two earlier open roadmaps already own, and the Scope paragraph misdescribes what §0.11 defines.

**Repair.** (i) In "Left to others", say that AutomorphicSpectralTheory §0.11 defines Fréchet spaces, nuclear maps and nuclear spaces (criterion (c), Banach factorisation of the maps `Ê_q → Ê_p` between seminorm completions) over ℂ and that SpectralManifolds SM3 extends the definition to ℝ and proves independence of the defining seminorm family, the finite-sum and the closed-subspace criteria. (ii) In §5.2, define nothing: consume that definition by name, and state Grothendieck's Théorème 1 as the equivalence of that definition with the two properties this roadmap owns, `E ⊗̂π F → E ⊗̂ε F` a topological isomorphism for every separated locally convex (equivalently every Banach) `F`, and every continuous linear map from `E` to a Banach space nuclear; "E nuclear iff its completion is" stays here. (iii) In §5.4, cite SpectralManifolds SM3 for closed subspaces and finite sums, and keep arbitrary subspaces, separated quotients, arbitrary products, countable direct sums, `⊗̂π` and the strong-dual clause as this roadmap's. (iv) Add AutomorphicSpectralTheory §0.11 and SpectralManifolds SM3 to `## Exact supplier contracts` and to Layer 5's `### Dependencies`, and rerun the dependency check (both are earlier, so no UPWARD line is expected). The Lean file needs no change: Layer 5 is already listed there as not statable.

## R06 (P3) — `dfSpace_strongDual` carries a hypothesis the theorem does not need

**Location.** `Suggested.lean` lines 218–220 and README §4.2 line 478.

**Claim.** The strong dual of a Fréchet space is a (DF)-space; the Lean statement assumes `[CompleteSpace E]`.

**Witness.** Grothendieck's theorem is about metrizable locally convex spaces: the polars `Uₙ°` of a countable basis of neighbourhoods are a fundamental sequence of bounded sets of `E′_b` because a metrizable space is bornological (§1.2) hence quasi-barrelled, so strongly bounded sets of `E′` are equicontinuous; and the countable quasi-barrelledness of `E′_b` uses only the countable basis. Completeness enters nowhere. Dropping it costs nothing and widens the theorem to the spaces the source covers (Grothendieck 1954, Part I; Köthe §29.3). I could not read either text, so this is a note: keep `CompleteSpace` only if the README says why.

**Repair.** Delete `[CompleteSpace E]` from `dfSpace_strongDual` and write "metrizable locally convex space" in §4.2 and the docstring, or leave both and add one clause in §4.2 saying that the metrizable case is the source's statement.
