## Decisions and corrections

- **Gestalten are not used.** ALRS uses Scholze's Gestalten (ALRS Construction 2.1.2) for two purposes, which ALRS Remark 2.1.1 lists. The first is to trivialize codualizing sheaves of 0-proper maps. The second is to make finitely presented maps of Berkovich spaces !-able (ALRS Proposition 6.5.3(3), 6.5.10). Remark 2.1.1 itself says the second purpose can be avoided by restricting to maps that are locally l.c.i. in the †-analytic topology. The design does this:
  - 12.1 states !-ability for locally †-l.c.i. maps, which include smooth and rigid smooth maps.
  - 12.4 states D-properness for locally †-l.c.i. maps with proper underlying arc-stack map.
  - Dualizing complexes are computed through the cone and modulus characters (ALRS Remarks 6.5.5–6.5.6) rather than through 0-affineness.
  - The 10.2 route avoids Gestalten via ALRS Lemma 5.2.7 and the Lazard–Serre resolution.
  - Nothing in T1–T14 is lost: every comparison and duality statement concerns smooth or rigid smooth maps.
- **"0-proper" is stated as D-proper.** This is the !-able class with f_! = f_* in the quasi-coherent formalism of the AnalyticStacks contract (Heyer–Mann Definitions 4.5.1, 4.6.1). It agrees with ALRS's 0-proper wherever the design uses it.
- **Correction to the target: ALRS Theorem 6.5.8.** target.md writes T12 as "f^{Div¹}_!O ≅ O{d}[2d]". The theorem is about the upper shriek: f^{Div¹,!}O ≅ O{d}[2d] (ALRS line 8875). The lower shriek is different; for P¹ it is O ⊕ O{−1}[−2] (12.3, 12.5). The design states the upper shriek and adds a Check that separates the two.
- **Wording of Theorem 5.1.1(1).** target.md and the body of ALRS call h 0-proper; the introduction of ALRS (Theorem 1.19(1)) says prim with trivial codualizing complex. The design follows the body, states D-proper, and records the introduction's form in 10.6.
- **ABLB numbering.** ALRS cites an earlier numbering of ABLB. The collation against v2, which is the version read:

  | ALRS cites ABLB | ABLB v2 |
  |---|---|
  | Definition 3.3.1 | Definition 3.1.1 |
  | Definition 2.3.1 | Definition 3.1.5 |
  | Lemma 3.6.2 | Lemma 3.1.17 |
  | Proposition 2.4.9 | Proposition 3.1.9 |
  | Corollary 3.4.10 | Corollary 4.4.10 |
  | Lemma 3.2.8 (in Remark 5.4.4) | Definition 3.2.8, with Proposition 3.2.11 |

  Every ABLB locator in the design uses v2, and every cited label was checked against the v2 text.
- **RC26a 3.2.27.** ALRS calls it a Theorem; in RC26a v3 it is a Proposition. The contract `analytic-cartier-duality` uses v3 numbering and records the difference.
- **Lazard.** ALRS Theorem 5.2.14 cites "Corollary I.3.9". The statement is Chapitre III, (1.3.9) Corollaire of Lazard's *Groupes analytiques p-adiques*. Chapitre III starts at Laz65 line 2538, and (1.3.9) is at line 2873, p. 77.
- **Label check.** I checked every "source + label" citation in the README against the source text. All are present after the ABLB 3.2.8 fix.
- **Coefficients and κ.** Coefficients are ℚ_p throughout, which is the source's own restriction. Every construction depending on the radius is made for the fixed κ of the Conventions.
