## R01 (P2) — 0-properness of BΠ^cont rests on a 2.2 law that its source proves only for finite covers by monomorphisms

**Location** `TauCetiRoadmap/RationalAnalyticPrismatization/README.md:1673` (10.2 route): "it is 0-proper because 0-properness can be checked along the D-proper, 0-proper !-cover f of the source (2.2)".

**Claim** The 2.2 law is sourced to HM Remark 4.4.11(iii). That remark covers only a finite family of monomorphisms. f: GSpec(ℚ_p) → BΠ^cont is neither, so the law does not reach this f. The step holds only because f_*ℚ_p is descendable, and the route does not use that.

**Witness** HM line 3409 needs a sieve "generated by a finite family of monomorphisms"; HM line 3407 says primness is "usually … not a local condition on the source". For Π = ℤ_p, f's diagonal (the unit section into ℤ_p^cont) is not an isomorphism.

**Repair** Replace the quoted clause, up to "(p. 127, ALRS line 6907);", with: "it is 0-proper by the argument of the proof of ALRS Proposition 5.7.3 (p. 127, ALRS line 6907). Since f_*ℚ_p is descendable, the cosimplicial object of pushforwards along (Π^cont)^n → GSpec(ℚ_p) that computes the right adjoint of pullback is pro-constant (AnalyticStacks §0.11(ii); HM Lemma 4.7.4(iii)). That right adjoint is therefore a retract of a finite limit of colimit-preserving ℚ_p-linear functors, as for primness in HM Corollary 4.7.5(ii). The iterated diagonals become maps of affinoids after base change along GSpec(ℚ_p) → (BΠ^cont)^k, so they are 0-proper by locality on the target (0.4);". Checked on Π = ℤ_p: the right adjoint is fib(γ − 1), a finite limit.
