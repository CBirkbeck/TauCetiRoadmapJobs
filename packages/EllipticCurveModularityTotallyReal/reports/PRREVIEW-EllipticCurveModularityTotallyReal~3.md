**Verdict:** needs changes

**Summary.** Changes are needed for one reason: the head now says, in four places, that EllipticCurveModularity derives its newform, parametrisation and L-function from the case `K = ℚ` of §4.4, while that roadmap, which is earlier in the merge order, proves its existence theorem itself from ClassicalSerreModularity §R27.6 and never cites this one; modularity over ℚ thus has two owners and a supply edge pointing the wrong way. ## R01 (P2) — EllipticCurveModularity does not derive its results from the case K = ℚ of §4.4, so the rational existence theorem now has two owners and an upward supply

A reader of the Summary, of the Check of §4.4, of Downstream consumers and of Scope and ownership is told that the newform of an elliptic curve over ℚ is obtained from this roadmap. An implementer of EllipticCurveModularity, or of any of the twenty-odd roadmaps that cite its R29 layers, would conclude that R29.2–R29.3 wait for the geometric prime switch; the plan says the opposite.

The four passages are: "EllipticCurveModularity derives from the case `K = ℚ` the newform of an elliptic curve over ℚ, its modular parametrisation and its L-function" (Summary, lines 38–40); the last sentence of the first Check of §4.4 (lines 1108–1109); "EllipticCurveModularity uses §4.4 with `K = ℚ` for the existence of the newform" (Downstream consumers, lines 1142–1144); and "The classical formulations over ℚ … are derived from the case K = ℚ of §4.4 in EllipticCurveModularity" (Scope and ownership, lines 1227–1229). At the head of its open pull request, EllipticCurveModularity says in its Summary that its existence theorem "uses the unconditional classical Serre modularity theorem supplied by ClassicalSerreModularity §R27.6", builds Serre witnesses in R29.2 and the exact-coefficient form in R29.3, and contains no mention of this roadmap or of a totally real field. ClassicalSerreModularity lists "`EllipticCurveModularity:R29.2` uses the finite-flat weight-two export of R27.6", and ModularityAndLanglandsExtensions states that ClassicalSerreModularity and EllipticCurveModularity "own rational modularity". In the merge order EllipticCurveModularity stands 26 places before this roadmap, so it cannot consume §4.4. The previous head had the consistent sentence: the rational case "is a Check of the general statement, not a new proof of EllipticCurveModularity's endpoint".

The smallest change keeps the new explicit statement for `K = ℚ` (the Summary's "and so is the field `K = ℚ`", the last sentence of the statement of §4.4, and the first two sentences of its Check, all of which are true) and rewrites the four sentences above to say what the two heads support: over ℚ, §4.4 gives a second proof, independent of [BCDT01] and of ClassicalSerreModularity, of the existence of `π_E`; the newform of level the conductor, the modular parametrisation and the analytic continuation over ℚ are owned by EllipticCurveModularity R29.3–R29.6 and are not restated here. The bullet for EllipticCurveModularity under Downstream consumers goes, since it is not a consumer. If the intention is instead to move ownership of rational modularity to this roadmap, that is a revision of EllipticCurveModularity (its R29.2–R29.3 and its place in the merge order), and this README can say so only once that roadmap does.
