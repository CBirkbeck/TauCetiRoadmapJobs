## Scope

Prove modularity of every elliptic curve over every totally real number field, including geometrically CM curves, with its entire compatible system and all finite local factors. There is no restriction on degree, ramification, reduction type or the original curve's small residual images. A Weierstrass equation is a presentation of the pointed smooth proper curve, not an extra hypothesis. The generality filed is already the right generality.

This is a new branch of the geometric prime switch. EllipticCurveModularityImaginaryQuadratic (#1048, abbreviated ECMIQ below) remains the owner of the cyclic covers, both degenerations, integral regular-module calculation, common-basis monodromy, character pieces and integral congruences. Import those results over arbitrary number fields by their existing sections. This roadmap owns reflection descent to the real cyclotomic field, the resulting totally odd systems, the totally real parameter specialization, the auxiliary elliptic seed application, and the three-lift assembly and its endpoint comparison. The general deformation, lifting, automorphic transfer and Hilbert local–global theories retain their earlier owners.

The extension from imaginary quadratic to arbitrary CM fields is already the separate design `packages/EllipticCurveModularityImaginaryQuadraticPartII/design/README.md`, arising from TARGET-EllipticCurveModularityCMFields. That design explicitly excludes totally real fields. Neither field class contains the other, and the reflection twist and totally real lifting chain are absent there and in ECMIQ. Sharing ECMIQ's geometry is a supplier relationship, not overlapping ownership. This target therefore neither joins that design nor becomes a second Part II of ECMIQ. The rational modularity roadmaps also retain their existing endpoints and proof directions; this roadmap consumes their available inputs.

No CM-field modularity lifting theorem is an input to this roadmap's chain. This describes the theorems invoked here; it is not a claim that the published proofs of every totally real supplier avoid auxiliary CM constructions. No new generic cyclic-cover, Hilbert modular-form, automorphic-induction or patching library is assigned here. The CM-field case, fields of mixed signature outside these classes, higher-dimensional modularity and computational algorithms are outside scope.

The primary draft states its theorem conditionally on geometric Inputs 2.1 and 2.2 (LeHung26 line 86), and explicitly retains that status (LeHung26 line 368). The finished library endpoint is unconditional only after importing proofs of those inputs from ECMIQ; they must not become assumptions or opaque certificates in the acceptance theorem.

## Final targets

Use cohomological H¹, with cyclotomic character εℓ of Hodge–Tate weight −1. Thus H¹(E) has determinant εℓ⁻¹ and labelled weights {0,1}. Residual congruence means isomorphism of semisimplified reductions of specified stable integral lattices over common finite coefficient extensions. Keep character labels compatible when changing coefficient prime.

1. **Totally real elliptic modularity (headline).** For every totally real number field K and every elliptic curve E/K there exists a regular algebraic cuspidal representation πE of GL₂(𝔸K), in the unitary parallel-weight-two normalization with trivial central character, such that for every prime ℓ and coefficient isomorphism ι: Q̄ℓ ≅ ℂ its attached cohomological representation is isomorphic to H¹ét(EK̄,Q̄ℓ). At every finite v∤ℓ require

   `ι WD(H¹(E)|G(Kv))^(F-ss) ≅ rec(πE,v ⊗ |det|v^(-1/2))`,

   with monodromy retained. At v|ℓ use the potentially semistable comparison, not an unramified trace assertion. Require equality of every finite Euler factor, including additive and multiplicative places, and the parallel-weight-two real parameters. Consequently `L(E/K,s) = L(πE,s−1/2)`; the Hilbert newform has rational Hecke eigenvalues and exact level equal to the conductor of E. Geometrically CM curves also give a cuspidal representation: their imaginary quadratic CM field T is not contained in totally real K, so the CM branch is quadratic automorphic induction from KT, with the same local comparison.

   Locators: LeHung26 Theorem 1.1, p.2 (LeHung26 line 86); §3.4, p.7 (LeHung26 line 263); Lemma 5.1 and the following compatibility and CM paragraphs, p.9 (LeHung26 line 357, LeHung26 line 361, LeHung26 line 364). The precise Hilbert compatible-system meaning is also FLHS15 §1, p.2 (FLHS15 line 66). Full local comparison is imported from the Hilbert attachment supplier below, not inferred from matching good primes.

2. **Reflection descent with integral congruences.** Let K be totally real, n≥3 odd, and t∈U(K) in ECMIQ's parameter open. Put M=K(μn), L=K(ζn+ζn⁻¹), and let δ be the character of M/L. For every m|n use ECMIQ's cover Cm(t) and its K-defined reflection w. Its twist by the cocycle associated to w and M/L has rotations and reflection defined over L. Every character piece Wθ is a rank-two compatible system over L, is totally odd, has determinant εℓ⁻¹ and labelled weights {0,1}, and retains the integral character-evaluation congruences at every coefficient prime, including primes dividing n. For n=3p, p>100 prime, η of order 3 and ψ of order p, require

   `Wη ≡p Wηψ ≡3 Wψ ≡p W1`,

   together with `Wη ≅ H¹(At)|G_L` and `W1 ≅ H¹(Bt)|G_L ⊗ δ`. Here At is ECMIQ's reflection-quotient elliptic curve and Bt its quadratic twist of E. In particular the final δ twist must be removed before descending the original elliptic representation. This is a descent and compatibility theorem for the entire family, not just one chosen modular fiber.

   Locators: LeHung26 §2.1–§2.2, p.5 (LeHung26 line 189, LeHung26 line 206); §3.2, Lemma 3.2 and equations (9)–(11), p.6 (LeHung26 line 236). The integral congruence and cover theorems themselves are imported from ECMIQ Layers 1–4.

3. **Totally real specialization and switch.** For every geometrically non-CM E/K with K totally real, there exist p>100 and t∈U(K) such that, over L=K(ζ3p+ζ3p⁻¹), the reflection-descended chain has an automorphic Wη, and the residual representations used to lift to Wηψ, Wψ and W1 are absolutely irreducible on G(L(ζℓ)) at the respective primes ℓ=p,3,p. The auxiliary elliptic curve's mod-five representation is absolutely irreducible on G(L(ζ5)). The systems satisfy the hypotheses of the totally real weight-two lifting theorem at each link, so all four systems are automorphic over L. This result includes the needed seed and local-hypothesis verification; it does not assume an independently supplied modularity witness for E.

   Locators: LeHung26 Proposition 3.1 and §3.3–§3.4, pp.6–7 (LeHung26 line 229, LeHung26 line 250, LeHung26 line 263); FLHS15 Theorem 3, p.3 (FLHS15 line 127); BD14 Théorème 3.2.2, pp.36–37 of arXiv v3 (BD14 line 2144). General lifting theorems and residual criteria already supplied elsewhere are imported; only the unowned totally real seed assembly and applications to this family belong here.

Checks include K=ℚ, arbitrary real quadratic and totally real cubic fields, and totally real quartic fields without any exclusion involving √5. The rational case recovers the existing rational modularity endpoint; it is not reproved by this roadmap's suppliers using the new headline. A geometrically CM example must exercise cuspidal induction. A multiplicative place must retain nonzero N. These are checks of the general statement, not replacement endpoints.

`Suggested.lean` is for a later design job. The pinned libraries have field and elliptic Tate-module carriers, but no GL₂ automorphic representation/Hilbert attachment on which to state these headlines. Do not create sorry'd data, an existential abstract “modularity” predicate or an API catalogue to conceal that gap. Record any unstatable headline and its missing carrier explicitly; any actual acceptance declarations must obey the single `import Mathlib`, simple-definition and theorem-with-`sorry` contract. This triage writes no Lean.

## Risks

- **Unrefereed conditional source.** LeHung26 and OpenAI26 are drafts, not independently certified mathematical proofs. LeHung26's theorem assumes geometric inputs; the roadmap must obtain them as proved ECMIQ results and verify the descent and lifting arguments. Legal certification alone establishes no correctness.
- **Exact lifting export is not yet bound.** GL2ModularityLifting §5.7 requires strong residual modularity and refined ordinary component matching, and §5.10 retains a residue-field restriction. Neither directly establishes LeHung26 Proposition 3.1. BD14 provides the broader local-type/ordinary-lift conclusion, while SW99 Theorem B and OrdinaryAutomorphicFormsAndModularityLifting §5.8 carry genuine extra hypotheses. The design must identify or obtain the necessary unconditional general export at the earlier lifting owner, including mixed ordinary/nonordinary and potentially multiplicative places and coefficient-enlarged character summands. It must not silently assume it, re-plan the general theory here, or weaken the endpoint to good reduction or favorable primes. The mod-five seed must likewise use FLHS15 Theorem 3 with its actual hypotheses.
- **Projectors, integrality and oddness.** Character eigenspaces use finite coefficient extensions and rational correspondence idempotents after coefficient extension, not invented rank-two data. Integral evaluation must still work at 3 and p. Reflection descent must preserve the lattices and prove oddness at every real place. The endpoint is twisted by δ.
- **Descent and all-place comparison.** Absolute irreducibility, cuspidality, the character adjustment in cyclic descent, and removal of both twists require proofs. Almost-all Frobenius matching does not establish local monodromy, coefficient-prime factors or the conductor.
- **Library distance and order.** The pins do not contain the automorphic/Hilbert attachment carriers for the headlines. Large upstream roadmaps must actually deliver them. Importing a later symmetric-power, CM-lifting or Hilbert comparison application as a supplier would create a cycle. Keep general theory upstream and the new endpoint downstream.
