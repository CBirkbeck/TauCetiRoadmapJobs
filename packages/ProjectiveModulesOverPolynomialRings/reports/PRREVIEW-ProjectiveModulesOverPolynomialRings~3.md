## R02 (P2) — The route of Lemma 2 uses a height theorem that nothing supplies

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ProjectiveModulesOverPolynomialRings/README.md:394-396`: “of dimension at most one because an ideal of `k[X]` of height greater than `dim k` contains a monic polynomial”.
**Claim:** The inputs of the route are 4.1's *Needs* (line 401: `Polynomial.Monic`, `Submonoid`, `Localization`, `IsPrincipalIdealRing`, `IsField`, flatness).
**Witness:** Quillen1976 lines 142–144 take this from Bass's Bourbaki exposé, for Noetherian rings. It is no target here, no supplier section, and `Mathlib/RingTheory` at the pin has no file relating `Ideal.height` to monic polynomials (two unrelated hits).
**Repair:** Replace the sentence by a route on pinned inputs: “Route: by `IsPrincipalIdealRing.of_prime` it suffices that every prime `Q` of `k[X]` containing no monic polynomial is principal.
