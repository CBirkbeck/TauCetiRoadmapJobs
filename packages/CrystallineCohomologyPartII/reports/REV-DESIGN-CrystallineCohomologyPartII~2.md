1. **F01** (P2) WV.5.9 drops the normality of S from "rational singularities", so "rational ⇒ Witt-rational" is false as written

   **Location.** `packages/CrystallineCohomologyPartII/design/README.md` line 1332 (section WV.5.9): "For an integral k-scheme S: rational singularities (a resolution f with R^if_\*O_X = 0 = R^if_\*ω_X for i > 0) ⇒ Witt-rational"; proof route step 1 (line 1340): "R^if_\*O_X = 0, R^if_\*W_nO_X = 0 and W_nO_S ≅ f_\*W_nO_X". plan.json node `WV.5/rational-implies-witt-rational`, `hypotheses` (only "S integral of finite type … the first implication assumes a resolution exists").

   **Claim.** The implication holds for every integral S admitting a resolution with vanishing higher direct images of O and ω.

   **Witness.** CR12 line 3881 (Definition 4.4.1, p. 81): the definition is for S a *normal* variety. Counterexample to the README's form: S the split nodal cubic, f: P¹ → S its normalization. f is finite, so R^{>0}f_\*O_{P¹} = 0 = R^{>0}f_\*ω_{P¹}; but S is not WO-rational: H¹(S, WO_K) = K ≠ 0 = H¹(P¹, WO_K), so WO_{S,ℚ} → Rf_\*WO_{P¹,ℚ} is not an isomorphism (condition (1) of WV.5.5 fails at the node, whose fibre is two points, so the converse of WV.0.5 (ii) applies; the README's own WV.5.2 Check at line 1173 records the two points). Step 1's "W_nO_S ≅ f_\*W_nO_X" also needs f_\*O_X = O_S, which is normality.

   **Repair.** In WV.5.9 define rational singularities with "S normal and f: X → S a resolution (projective birational, X smooth) with R^if_\*O_X = 0 = R^if_\*ω_X for i > 0" (CR12 Definition 4.4.1, p. 81), and add "S normal" to the plan.json `hypotheses` of `WV.5/rational-implies-witt-rational`. Add the split nodal cubic as a negative-control Check: finite normalization, all higher direct images zero, not WO-rational. Tested: with S normal, f_\*O_X = O_S gives W_nO_S ≅ f_\*W_nO_X by induction on the V-sequences, and CR12 lines 4073–4093 go through; the nodal cubic is excluded.

6. **F06** (P3) Tan18 locator for the base-change invariance of rational chain connectedness

   **Location.** README line 1672 (WV.6.11): "invariant under every field extension k′/k: X is rationally chain connected iff X ×_k k′ is (Tan18, Proposition A.4, arXiv v3, p. 30)".

   **Witness.** Tan18 line 1183 (Proposition A.4, p. 30) states that MRCC fibrations of X and of X ⊗ k′ have bases of the same dimension, for X proper, geometrically normal and geometrically connected. The equivalence as the README states it is Tan18 line 763 (Remark 5.3 (1), p. 20), which derives it from A.4.

   **Repair.** Cite "Tan18, Remark 5.3 (1), p. 20, from Proposition A.4, p. 30" and keep the README's hypotheses (which are A.4's). Tested on an elliptic curve E: both E and E ⊗ k′ have MRCC base of dimension 1, neither is RCC; on P^n both bases are points.

7. **F07** (P3) WV.3.6 proof step 3 misstates the dimension of the simplicial terms

   **Location.** README line 826: "which is an isomorphism on E_1 for i ≤ N because each U_i is an open of a smooth projective X_i of dimension d".

   **Witness.** The terms X_i of a simplicial resolution can have dimension above d: for X a threefold and X_0 → X the blowup of a point with exceptional divisor E ≅ P², X_0 ×_X X_0 contains E × E of dimension 4, and X_1 surjects onto it. BBE07 lines 1387–1393 argue instead that U_i has dimension d because it is étale over U, and apply the theorem to the closure of U_i in X_i (BBE07 lines 1319–1325).

   **Repair.** Write: "because each U_i, being étale over U, has dimension d and is a dense open of the union of the d-dimensional components of the smooth projective X_i, to which the case of opens of smooth proper schemes of dimension d applies". The theorem is unaffected.
