- **Misprint** in Bellaïche at §1.4.3 equation (5), arXiv p. 8: the Euler factors of the refinement `f_β` have `α` in both denominators; they should have `β`; affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in Bellaïche at the proof of Lemma 2.11, arXiv p. 18: the final extension group is `Ext¹_f(χ₂, χ₂)`; it should be `Ext¹_f(χ₂, χ₁)`; affects the proof.
- **Gap** in BD at Proposition 5.9, arXiv p. 35: the proof treats the normal Eisenstein case and leaves the exceptional `E_{2,ℓ}` case to the reader; the boundary symbol there has values `δ_0` at `0` and `−ℓ⁻¹·(f ↦ f(−1/ℓ))` at `1/ℓ` in Pollack–Stevens' conventions; affects nothing.
- **Error** in Pollack SS at Lemma 4.6, printed p. 538 (as recorded by Sprung lines 1120–1133): the half-logarithms are said to be invariant under `1 + T ↦ (1 + T)⁻¹`; they acquire the units `(1 + T)^{p/(p+1)}` and `(1 + T)^{1/(p+1)}`; affects a stated result; corrected in print at Sprung, Lemma 4.26, p. 908.
