## Corrections and mathematical limits

- Corrected the design’s David–Philippon exponent from (r+1)g⁵(dim X+1)² to (r+1)g^(5(dim X+1)²). For g=2, dim X=1, r=0 the values are 128 versus 1048576. This was read from the legally fetched PDF, p.643, as well as its numbered extraction (david-philippon-2002 lines 183–195).
- Corrected h₀(A) to [k:Q]max{1,h(A)} and the precise principal Mumford M¹⁶ embedding in the displayed explicit formula (david-philippon-2002 lines 54–57,88–94,129–136). The general uniform count and Mordell–Lang targets continue to cover every ample polarization. The unsupported claim that the same literal numerical formula holds for every symmetric ample L was cut.
- Separated David–Philippon 2002 (p.643 single-variety bound) from David–Philippon 2007 (Theorem 6.8 and (6.41), used by GGK). The source-service bibliographic search initially associated an incorrectly supplied DOI with an unrelated Giroux paper; its title was checked, its entry discarded, and the correct Rémond DOI recorded. No statement relies on that unrelated text.
- Corrected the packing API: translation preserves it for the same subgroup, while enlarging the subgroup requires its own hypothesis. A zero subgroup with c₀=1 on a genus-two curve passes; adding one Weierstrass two-torsion difference gives count 2>1 at the same rank.
- Corrected the pencil-height equality Check. A generic point of a constant elliptic curve has positive height over k and height zero after its field of definition becomes constant; descent to the pencil field does not force equality.
- Corrected the isogeny-descent Check: over an algebraically closed base, an isomorphism between constant varieties and its inverse descend by the same spreading argument. Nonconstant twists over a nonclosed base are a different statement.
- Corrected the torsion-fibre Check at t=0 to require exclusion of the jump locus; the subgroup example has e=0, empty jump locus and fibre {(0,0)} of height 0.
- Corrected the restricted-family API over reducible S: it is a flat proper closed family with geometrically integral fibres, irreducible only when S is irreducible. A multisection 2x=s shows why ordinary generic irreducibility cannot replace geometric irreducibility.
- Replaced an informal quotient N²=ĥ([N]P)/ĥ(P) by the identity ĥ([N]P)=N²ĥ(P) and division by positive N²; height-zero points no longer create 0/0.
- Made the large-height cutoff strictly larger than the Vojta–Mumford upper-bound constant before using a non-strict counting threshold; retained controlled-embedding hypotheses in every Faltings-height specialization.
- Clarified that Metric.closedBall on a pseudometric space has a distance-zero class at radius zero; the singleton statement uses the Euclidean metric, not an implicit separation axiom.
- Made the Betti measure assertion pointwise about its smooth density, rather than the measure of a singleton.
- Completed the joint-specialization route using a product of the two exceptional-set vanishing polynomials. Their degree bounds add polynomially in h, while the full bounded-degree parameter family has exponential vanishing degree (masser-1989 lines31–56; masser-1996 lines124–138). No intersection-of-dense-sets argument is used.
- Supplied the geometric constant-extension lemma and its noetherian induction for vertical characteristics in §11.8. Fixed model sections and fibre degrees survive flat constant extension; finite subsets prove finiteness. Zero-dimensional strata have no non-isotrivial points. Characteristics 2 and 3 use respectively levels 3 and 4.
- Expanded the bielliptic normal form into its hyperelliptic involution calculation rather than treating the Jacobi model as an unexplained input. The two quotient maps, four-element isogeny kernel, factor16 and exact torsion invariance of the same L_X are explicit.
- Replaced obsolete roadmap stage labels by resolved sections, including ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights §§3.4,4.2–4.4,5.5–5.8; AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §§1.5–1.6,2.1; NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties §§1.1–1.10,3.10–3.14,5.1–5.2; and JacobianChallengePartII §§5.1–5.6,7.1–7.2.
