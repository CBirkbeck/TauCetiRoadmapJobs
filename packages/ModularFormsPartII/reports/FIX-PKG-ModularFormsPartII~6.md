## REV-PKG-ModularFormsPartII~5/F01 (P2) — applied differently

Added theorems for §§1.8, 6.5, 8.2, 8.6 and 8.7, with definitions `weakFormAlgebra`, `quasiModularWeakAlgebra` and `HasCuspidalExpansionOver`. §6.5 uses Tau Ceti's `TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.laurentQExpansion` instead of the suggested `Δ^m` encoding. The docstring's claim that the pins lack Laurent expansions was false, so §6.1 (`R₂N = M₂N`) and the unconditional rank bound are now stated too.

**Verification.** The five signatures in the review do elaborate at the pins, and each statement is true (adversarial table below). While checking the "not statable" list in the module docstring, I found `TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/QExpansion/Laurent.lean` at the pinned Tau Ceti. Its `TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.laurentQExpansion h f : LaurentSeries ℂ` is the Laurent expansion at `∞` of any function whose cusp function is meromorphic at `0`. It equals `qExpansion` where the cusp function is analytic, and it is multiplicative, inverse-compatible and injective on periodic germs. The docstring's reason for leaving §§6.1, 5.3 and 3.8 unstated ("no Laurent `q`-expansion of a weakly holomorphic form at a cusp") was therefore false at the pin. Fixing the pattern, not only the five listed lines:

- §6.5 is stated on the native Laurent expansion: algebraic Laurent coefficients put all coefficients in one number field, and each conjugate series is the Laurent expansion of an h-periodic weak form for a finite-index subgroup. This keeps poles at `∞` and the original weight without encoding. The review's `Δ^m` version is equivalent, because `Δ^(±m)` have integral coefficients, but it states the Taylor expansion of a different function.
- §6.1 is stated as `boundedDenominatorSpan_eq_congruenceSpan`. It uses three definitions on native carriers. `lambdaField N` is the subfield of `LaurentSeries ℂ` generated by the expansion of λ, with period `2N`. `congruenceSpan N` (`M₂N`) and `boundedDenominatorSpan N` (`R₂N`) are `Submodule.span` over `lambdaField N`. They correspond to the new README §3.0 and to §§3.1–3.2.
- The holonomy rank bound is stated unconditionally as `boundedDenominatorSpan_finrank_le`. The statement includes `FiniteDimensional`, so a `finrank` of `0` cannot satisfy it vacuously. README §3.8 is conditional on the coverings `F_N` of ConformalMappingPartII, which the pins do not construct. The conclusion does not depend on `F_N`, and the proof sketch of §6.1 discharges the hypotheses, so §6.1 now names the unconditional form.
- Degree doubling (§5.3) and the leveraging contradiction (§5.5) are not stated. The docstring says why: §5.3 assumes `R₂N ≠ M₂N`, which the stated equality refutes, and §5.5 concludes that equality. Over these carriers both follow from §6.1 and test nothing.
- §§7.1–7.2 and §9.3 stay unstatable. I checked the pins again: there is no complex-analytic continuation or monodromy of linear ODE solutions (Tau Ceti `Analysis/ODE` has real flows only), and there is no minimal regular model of `X(N)` over `𝓞_K` (Tau Ceti `AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve` has `YOne` and `NaiveLevel` only).
