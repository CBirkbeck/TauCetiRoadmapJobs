## What the README can and cannot conclude

The README specifies the full general library and retains the accepted conditional endpoints. It does not claim that the all-index Fourier annihilation, scalar neighbor argument, selected rank inequality, singular Sen overlap comparison or arithmetic marked-point patching comparisons have been proved by this package. The CG statements retain these premises and the uniform H² hypothesis at a=2. The characteristic-zero branch retains the source's local, image, seed and packet hypotheses. Regular-point multiplicity is only claimed at nonzero fibers. Local freeness can have rank zero; support is proved separately. P12 remains a p-adic-form conclusion until a separate classicality theorem applies. The conjectural mod-2 class, weight-two interior-H² expectation and integral Hecke torsion-freeness expectation are kept as illustrations, with no use in a lifting proof.

The extra full p-polynomial comparison and the comparison of the Pilloni–Stroh modified lattice with the specified CG lattice are explicit conditional comparison targets. The former needs a normalized p-action comparison. The latter names the model morphism, boundary, coefficient and Hecke compatibility needed for that specific lattice; the characteristic-zero interpolation proof and independent CG Hasse route do not assume it.
