## Source issues

- **Misprint** in AHTW at Proposition 5.2.8(1), printed page 76 (repeated in Theorem 5.4.13 p.98 and Lemma 5.4.18(1) p.104): the source requires, for `v ∉ S` of residue characteristic `l`, that `l` be unramified in `F` and `S_l(F) ∩ S ≠ ∅`, or `l` split in an imaginary quadratic subfield; it should be `S_l(F) ∩ S = ∅` (the ACC+ Theorem 2.3.7 form), since by Chebotarev infinitely many `l` are inert in every imaginary quadratic subfield and have no place in a finite `S`; affects a stated result (the hypothesis as printed is never satisfied).
