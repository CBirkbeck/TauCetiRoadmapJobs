2. **F02** (P3) A §4.4 Check justifies a true algebraic identity by a false equation and is false for the metrized pairing

   Location: README line 743, "on ℙ¹×S, L = O(1)+π*M gives ⟨L,L⟩=2M since relative O(1)²=0; M-degree 1 gives pairing degree 2."

   Claim: O(1)·O(1) on a fibre ℙ¹ is 1, not 0. The algebraic identity ⟨L,L⟩ = 2M + ⟨O(1),O(1)⟩ holds, and ⟨O(1),O(1)⟩ for the constant family ℙ¹ × S → S is pulled back from Spec k, hence trivial in Pic(S) over a field; over k = ℤ with the Fubini–Study metric it is the pullback of ⟨O(1),O(1)⟩_{ℙ¹_ℤ/ℤ}, of degree 1/2 (the README's own §0.1 and HeightsRationalPointsAndObstructions Checks, lines 65 and 827), so the metrized pairing is 2M̄ + O(1/2), not 2M̄, and "pairing degree 2" is off by 1/2 in the arithmetic case.

   Witness: §0.2 line 176, ⟨L₁, …, Lₙ, f^*M⟩ ≅ M^{⊗(L₁···Lₙ)_fibre}; §3.3(2) line 632 with e = deg O(1) on the fibre = 1 gives ⟨O(1), π^*M⟩ = M; the remaining term is ⟨O(1),O(1)⟩, pulled back from the base of the constant family.

   Repair text: "For π = id, ⟨L⟩ = L. On ℙ¹ × S over a field, L = O(1) + π^*M gives ⟨L,L⟩ = ⟨O(1),O(1)⟩ + 2M = 2M, since ⟨O(1),O(1)⟩ is pulled back from Spec k and is trivial in Pic(S); M-degree 1 gives pairing degree 2.
