## Mathematical evidence and scope of the repair

The review's dimension-zero counterexample is valid. An integral finite-type zero-dimensional k-scheme is Spec F for a finite field extension F/k. Every line bundle is trivial, but its section space has k-dimension [F:k]. Thus 0!·h⁰(mL)/m⁰=[F:k]. A nullary intersection is a value attached to the scheme's fundamental cycle; it has no additive line-bundle input. A unary homomorphism from Pic(Spec F)=0 would give 0 instead. SchemeAndStackFoundations §5.2 on main supplies degree with residue-field degree weights and composition. Its degree is imported, not reconstructed here.

The nef volume identity and mixed Siu estimate occur at yuan-zhang-2025 lines 5460–5469. The repair separates the zero-dimensional equality from the mixed estimate rather than multiplying an undefined negative-exponent expression by zero.

The same exponent pattern occurs in the scalar Moriwaki specialization in §4.6. Its defining sentence and API now explicitly require model dimension d ≥ 1. The general vector-valued height remains available for finite F/k and takes values in the zero group P̂ic(F/k)_ℚ; its value 0 is distinct from the empty intersection [F:k]. No scalar-height substitute is introduced in dimension zero.

The proof of potential bigness at yuan-2026 lines 2044–2067 supplies the selected term a^(m−d)M^d. At d=0 it is a^m[F:k], where M is the relative Deligne pairing.
