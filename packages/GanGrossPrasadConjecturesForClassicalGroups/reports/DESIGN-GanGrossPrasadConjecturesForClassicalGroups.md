# Design report: GanGrossPrasadConjecturesForClassicalGroups

The design is in `packages/GanGrossPrasadConjecturesForClassicalGroups/design/README.md` and `packages/GanGrossPrasadConjecturesForClassicalGroups/design/plan.json`. It has 8 layers, 48 targets, 24 definition/construction targets with 84 discriminating Checks, and 207 explicit prerequisite edges. The mathematical design and verdicts were written by the primary author without helper agents.

It covers every endpoint in `packages/GanGrossPrasadConjecturesForClassicalGroups/target.md`: relative models and precise multiplicity ranges (§§1–3), the local character and selection propositions (§4), proved generic unitary selection (§5), classical global nonvanishing as a proposition (§6), orthogonal refinement as propositions (§7), and the endoscopic number-field unitary nonvanishing and refined identity (§8). GGP.8 is permanently identified with §8.6. There were no merged-in targets. No arithmetic Selmer, Abel–Jacobi, height or derivative statement is taken into this scope.

The recorded gaps are mathematical and supplier-interface obligations. The design does not claim they are discharged or that the roadmap can be implemented by assuming its endpoint as data. There are nine unbound upstream contracts and one local reduction proof interface. These are suitable review points before packaging; the absence of a direction defect does not certify those contracts.

## Source verification and mathematical corrections

All ten cited texts are legally readable and recorded with service metadata in `packages/GanGrossPrasadConjecturesForClassicalGroups/sources.toml`. The eight supplied cached texts were read at the endpoint, construction and proof-interface passages cited in the design. Two supplementary texts were fetched through the sources service: `bp-arch` (arXiv v3) and `harris` (arXiv v2). Both are certified. No source text or PDF was copied into the package, and all statements and proof routes are paraphrases. There is no unread required text and no missing-reference request. Missing roadmap texts are contract gaps, not missing-reference requests for papers.

The additional real-place theorem is weaker than explicit epsilon-character selection and exactly sufficient for the unitary global isolation argument: bp-arch line 11132 gives the STAB/TRANS/WHITT setup, and bp-arch line 11200 gives unique multiplicity one across relevant inner forms. F = C is outside this nonsplit local theorem and uses the split GL case. Harris’s local convergence is harris line 250 and its spherical computation is harris line 1289; the spherical finite set includes dyadic places (harris line 170), which does not remove those local integrals from GGP.8.

The older GGP reduction needs care. ggp line 2123 explicitly uses O in §15, while ggp line 2661 invokes it in the local selection reduction; ggp line 2699 omits the symplectic/skew-Hermitian variants. A connected SO and skew-unitary proof interface is therefore recorded rather than inferred. Likewise, Sun–Zhu uniqueness of each Hom space is not uniqueness of a member across a packet.

Reading the standing center convention at ggp line 2908 also prevents a finite-volume error in split GL cases: a full GL adelic quotient has an unbounded norm direction. The scalar automorphic theory uses fixed unitary central characters and the split-center quotient for the Petersson norm; the relative periods keep their displayed integrals with a convergence obligation. Split orthogonal planes remain excluded from the source’s unmodified global formulation.
