## Scope

Own the general Gan–Gross–Prasad restriction problem: relevant classical pairs and their Bessel or Fourier–Jacobi models, restriction multiplicities, the distinguished component-group character, the local packet prediction, global period nonvanishing, and the refined period identity. Preserve **GGP.8** as the unitary period/L-value supplier requested by PeriodsAndSpecialValues. The endpoint labels below are a brief, not a proposed sequence of implementation stages.

Use arbitrary nondegenerate quadratic, symplectic, Hermitian and skew-Hermitian spaces, their relevant pure inner forms, and genuine representations of the required metaplectic covers. Include every parity and codimension permitted by the sources, the split quadratic-algebra cases, and archimedean places. The ambient form is arbitrary; the split-complement condition belongs to the definition of a relevant GGP pair. Algebraic constructions have characteristic different from two; local statements retain the field and representation-category hypotheses of their sources. Global statements cover every number field, with no totally real, CM, unramified, or auxiliary supercuspidal restriction imposed across the library. Include global function fields of characteristic different from two where the cited formulations apply, without extending the number-field unitary theorems to them.

Import the existing algebraic-group, smooth-representation, Weil-representation, local Langlands, endoscopic transfer, automorphic-spectrum and local-factor theories from their owners. This roadmap supplies the relative models, multiplicity statements, character selection and period identities on those objects. Selmer groups, diagonal Abel–Jacobi classes, level raising, Euler-system reciprocity, arithmetic heights and derivative formulas remain outside. In particular, GGP §27 is background for later arithmetic consumers, not ownership of arithmetic GGP here.

Distinguish named conjectural propositions from proved theorems and from implications assuming those propositions. Neither a conjecture nor an arbitrary packet or period supplied as data may stand in for a mathematical construction. This triage writes no Lean. A later Suggested.lean must have one `import Mathlib`, easy headline statements on existing carriers, and proved checks of its own definitions, with no sorry'd data or `set_option`.

## Final targets

All locators refer to the versions recorded in `packages/GanGrossPrasadConjecturesForClassicalGroups/sources.toml`, not unchecked published numbering.

1. **Restriction models and multiplicity one.** Let the coefficient field have an involution with fixed field of characteristic different from two, allowing the trivial involution and the split quadratic algebra. For a nondegenerate epsilon-Hermitian space V and nondegenerate W contained in V, require `epsilon * (-1)^(dim W-perp) = -1` and W-perp split in the sense of GGP §2. Form G as the product of the connected isometry groups, H as the unipotent subgroup semidirect the diagonal smaller group, and the Bessel character or oscillator representation nu with its additive and splitting characters. Orthogonal G means SO; the Fourier–Jacobi construction uses the necessary covers and genuineness. A relevant inner form preserves the isometry class of the complement. Define `m(pi) = dim_C Hom_H(pi tensor nu, C)` with continuous Hom and completed projective tensor product in the archimedean category, and smooth representations in the nonarchimedean category. Own the multiplicity-one assertion `m(pi) <= 1`, its proved instances and its precise range of applicability. Locators: ggp §2, pp. 5–7 (ggp line 179, ggp line 229, ggp line 255); §§12 and 14, pp. 43–59, especially the topological convention (ggp line 2051) and Theorem 14.1 (ggp line 2068).

   The certified basic cases include nonarchimedean characteristic-zero Hermitian Bessel models from Theorem 14.1(ii), special orthogonal restriction from waldspurger Theorem 1, p. 1 (waldspurger line 10, waldspurger line 17), and symplectic/metaplectic and unitary Fourier–Jacobi multiplicity one from sun Theorems B and C, pp. 1–3 (sun line 15, sun line 31, sun line 102). At real and complex places use irreducible Casselman–Wallach representations: sun-zhu Theorem B, p. 2 (sun-zhu line 66), for the codimension-one classical pairs listed there, including SO; Theorem C, p. 3 (sun-zhu line 80, sun-zhu line 94), for the listed Jacobi groups and fixed nontrivial central character. Larger-codimension models must retain the actual hypotheses of the reduction theorems, ggp Theorems 15.1 and 16.1, pp. 61 and 65 (ggp line 2158, ggp line 2293). The older ggp v1 remarks about unresolved SO and archimedean Fourier–Jacobi uniqueness are historical, not present-day obstruction claims.

2. **Local packet selection and the distinguished character.** For every generic local Langlands parameter phi for a relevant pair, state the prediction that exactly one member of its Vogan packet, taken across the relevant pure inner forms, has nonzero m. Its enhancement under the chosen Whittaker normalization must be the explicitly constructed character chi, not an unspecified witness. Use the product of the two component-group characters of ggp §6: the conjugate-dual epsilon-factor characters in Theorem 6.1 and the determinant-corrected self-dual characters in Theorem 6.2; retain the determinant-one component subgroup where prescribed. For the symplectic/metaplectic pair use the orthogonal parameter enlarged by a trivial line before restricting the character; for the skew-Hermitian pair retain the inverse splitting-character twist. Prove the stated compatibility with changes of Whittaker, additive and splitting data, and with scaling the forms. Locators: ggp §§4–6, especially pp. 20–21 (ggp line 728, ggp line 780); Conjectures 17.1 and 17.3 and the intervening case definitions, pp. 68–70 (ggp line 2386, ggp line 2422, ggp line 2440, ggp line 2467, ggp line 2479); §18 and Theorem 19.1, pp. 71–76 (ggp line 2488, ggp line 2655).

   Include the proved unitary result: for any nonarchimedean local field F of characteristic zero, any quadratic field extension E/F, and every generic parameter for `U(V_n) x U(V_(n+2r+1))` or `U(W_n) x U(W_(n+2r))`, with r nonnegative, the packet prediction and specified character hold. Hermitian spaces give the Bessel problem; skew-Hermitian spaces give the Fourier–Jacobi problem with explicit psi and chi restricting to the quadratic character on F. No odd-residue-characteristic condition is added. Locators: gan-ichino §1.1, p. 1 (gan-ichino line 19), Theorem 1.3 and Corollary 1.4, pp. 3–4 (gan-ichino line 119, gan-ichino line 141), §3.2 and Proposition 9.3, p. 50 (gan-ichino line 2494). Other local packet predictions remain separately named propositions unless a theorem with the required hypotheses is certified; do not claim the generic criterion for arbitrary nongeneric parameters.

3. **Global nonvanishing for every covered pair.** For a global field F of characteristic different from two and a relevant pair as above, define the adelic Bessel period or Fourier–Jacobi period, including its theta input. In the orthogonal formulation exclude split two-dimensional V or W as the source does; a center-quotient extension would require a separate statement. For an irreducible everywhere tempered representation pi occurring with multiplicity one in the cuspidal spectrum, state the conjectural equivalence between a nonzero period on pi and the conjunction of local distinction at every place with `L(1/2, pi, R) != 0`. R is the specified tensor representation in the orthogonal and symplectic cases, the induced tensor representation in the Hermitian case, and its splitting-character-twisted version in the skew-Hermitian case. Include all local factors, including the infinite ones. Locators: ggp §§22–24, pp. 82–91, in particular ggp line 2993, ggp line 3036, ggp line 3044, ggp line 3123, ggp line 3151 and Conjecture 24.1 (ggp line 3175). Do not equate these different R representations or assume local distinction alone forces a nonzero global period.

4. **Proved unitary nonvanishing, including endoscopy.** For every quadratic extension E/F of number fields and n >= 1, let Pi_n and Pi_(n+1) be Hermitian discrete Arthur parameters: each is the full parabolic induction of pairwise distinct conjugate-self-dual cuspidal constituents, each constituent satisfying the appropriate Asai pole condition at 1. Then the completed Rankin–Selberg value at 1/2 is nonzero exactly when there exist a nondegenerate n-dimensional Hermitian h and a cuspidal automorphic subrepresentation sigma of `U(h) x U(h plus norm-line)` with that base change and nonzero diagonal period. The Asai sign for a degree-m parameter is `(-1)^(m+1)`. The conclusion ranges over h; it does not assert distinction on an arbitrarily fixed inner form. Locators: bpcz §§1.1.2–1.1.4, pp. 2–3 (bpcz line 81, bpcz line 93, bpcz line 108), Theorem 1.1.5.1, p. 3 (bpcz line 124). The stable special case, where both base changes are cuspidal, is bplzz Theorem 1.8, p. 4 (bplzz line 157), with its stated n > 1 convention (bplzz line 127). Do not import the auxiliary split supercuspidal assumptions of older results into either theorem.

5. **Orthogonal Ichino–Ikeda refinement.** For every global field of characteristic different from two, an isometric inclusion of nondegenerate quadratic spaces of dimensions n and n+1, n >= 2, and nonsplit smaller space if n=2, use the source's Tamagawa Petersson pairings and local matrix-coefficient periods. For irreducible cuspidal pi_i satisfying (ALG), meaning local genericity at almost every place, state Conjecture 1.5: the squared restriction period divided by the two Petersson norms is `2^beta * C_0 * Delta_(G_1) * L(1/2, pi_1 tensor pi_0) / (L(1,pi_1,Ad) * L(1,pi_0,Ad))` times the product of normalized local periods divided by the local vector norms. C_0 records the factorization of the smaller group's Tamagawa measure. Delta is the source's explicit product of zeta factors and, in even dimension, the discriminant-character factor. Include the local convergence and positivity clauses of Conjecture 1.4 rather than assuming (ALG) implies temperedness. For everywhere tempered pi_i with Arthur parameters psi_i, Conjecture 2.1 specifies `2^beta = 1/(|S_(psi_1)| * |S_(psi_0)|)`. These are conjectural assertions with analytic continuation as stipulated in §1, not an unconditional orthogonal identity. Locators: ichino-ikeda §1, pp. 4–7 (ichino-ikeda line 128, ichino-ikeda line 145, ichino-ikeda line 209, ichino-ikeda line 231, ichino-ikeda line 252, ichino-ikeda line 262); §2, p. 10 (ichino-ikeda line 395). Proved normalization checks are Proposition 1.1 and Theorem 1.2, p. 6 (ichino-ikeda line 234, ichino-ikeda line 237).

6. **GGP.8 — proved unitary period/L-value identity.** Let E/F be any quadratic extension of number fields, n >= 1, h any nondegenerate n-dimensional Hermitian space, and sigma an irreducible cuspidal automorphic representation of `G_h = U(h) x U(h plus norm-line)` tempered at every place. Let Pi be its base change. If Pi_m has r_m distinct cuspidal constituents, put `S_Pi = (Z/2Z)^(r_n+r_(n+1))`. With eta the quadratic idele class character, define

   `Delta_(n+1) = product_(i=1..n+1) L(i, eta^i)`,
   `C(sigma) = Delta_(n+1) * L(1/2, Pi_n x Pi_(n+1)) / L(1, sigma, Ad)`.

   All L-functions here are completed. For each place v set `C_v` to the corresponding quotient of local factors and define

   `alpha_v(f_v,f_v) = C_v^(-1) * integral_(U(h)(F_v)) <sigma_v(t) f_v, f_v>_v dt`.

   For every nonzero factorizable f, prove

   `|P_h(f)|^2 / <f,f>_Pet = |S_Pi|^(-1) * C(sigma) * product_v (alpha_v(f_v,f_v) / <f_v,f_v>_v)`.

   Use Tamagawa measures on the diagonal group and G_h and local measures factoring the former; local invariant inner products may be chosen freely with the source's normalization. Supply absolute convergence, the nonvanishing local normalizing factors, and the almost-everywhere unramified evaluation defining the product. Include ramified, dyadic and infinite places. Pi need not be cuspidal: the factor `|S_Pi|^(-1)` cannot uniformly be replaced by 1/4. Locators: bpcz §1.1.6, pp. 4–5, definitions and equation (1.1.6.1) (bpcz line 148, bpcz line 159, bpcz line 169), Theorem 1.1.6.1 (bpcz line 179, bpcz line 188), and measure conventions §2.3. Stable cuspidal-base-change specialization: bplzz Theorem 1.10, p. 5 (bplzz line 185). This is the contract exported to PeriodsAndSpecialValues; conjectural analogues for other pairs are not substitutes for it.

Checks should test the source's split local packet case (ggp Theorem 17.2, ggp line 2398), dependence on auxiliary characters, spherical local normalization, cancellation under vector and local-measure rescaling, and a stable versus endoscopic component-group factor. They test the general constructions; they do not replace ramified or archimedean theory.

## Risks

- **Unavailable supplier and consumer texts.** The named automorphic roadmaps, SelmerIwasawaCohomology and PeriodsAndSpecialValues are absent from main and the supplied packages; public-main reads of the three filed suppliers and the consumer returned 404. The verdict is supported by the available ownership search, not certification of those absent interfaces. Exact section binding and the claimed GGP.8 consumer reference must be resolved before detailed design is accepted. Their absence does not authorize re-planning them.
- **Large foundation distance.** The pinned libraries provide algebraic representations, intertwining maps, classical matrix groups and Clifford/Spin models, not a discovered GGP packet, relative model or period theorem. Smooth local representations, Casselman–Wallach analysis, local Langlands enhancements, endoscopic classification, Weil representations, Tamagawa measures and relative trace formulas require actual general producers. No theorem may be packaged as assumed packet or analytic data to bypass them.
- **False status or generality.** Generic is a condition on the parameter/packet, not a demand that every distinguished representation be generic. GGP.8 assumes everywhere tempered cuspidal sigma, but permits endoscopic Pi and arbitrary number fields. It does not establish every classical global conjecture, a non-tempered identity, a function-field unitary theorem, or an identity for every Fourier–Jacobi period. The known cases above are certified cases, not an exhaustive survey of subsequent literature.
- **Normalization defects.** Completed versus partial L-functions, conjugation/contragredients, genuine covers, additive characters, the extra trivial line in the metaplectic parameter, component-group quotients and Tamagawa factorization must remain explicit. Infinite-place topology cannot be replaced by algebraic tensor products. Spherical calculations are checks only.
- **Supply cycles.** Selmer or arithmetic-cycle results are not upstream proof assumptions for automorphic restriction. General trace-formula ownership must be assigned before this roadmap; a consumer's period formula cannot be imported to prove GGP.8.
