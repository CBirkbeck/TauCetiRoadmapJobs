## R01 (P2) — Give finite alphabets their discrete measurable structure

**Location:** `packages/DimerModelsAndLozengeTilings/design/README.md:827`, “measurable space (for 4.4–4.6 a finite set with the discrete topology)”; used at :896–905 and :960–962. Also `packages/DimerModelsAndLozengeTilings/design/plan.json:1017` and :1113.

**Claim:** The standing hypotheses suffice to use the native weak topology on `ProbabilityMeasure X`, its compactness, the clopen-cylinder criterion and the closedness theorem for Gibbs measures.

**Witness:** Take `Γ=I=ℤ`, `S={0,1}` with discrete topology but trivial σ-algebra, and `X={0̄,1̄}`. The trace σ-algebra has 2 sets; its Borel σ-algebra has 4. The clopen singleton `{1̄}` is not measurable.

The action is proper and `X` is closed and invariant, so this is permitted by the written hypotheses. Finiteness and a discrete topology do not force the existing `MeasurableSpace S` to be discrete. At the pin, `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Constructions/BorelSpace/Basic.lean:645` requires `BorelSpace` on each factor for `Pi.borelSpace`; `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Measure/ProbabilityMeasure.lean:301` and :310 require `OpensMeasurableSpace` for the weak-topology instance; `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Measure/Prokhorov.lean:65` and :176 require a Hausdorff Borel space for the compactness instance. These hypotheses fail in the witness. In particular the claimed clopen cylinder cannot be used as a measurable indicator in the conditional-expectation interface.

**Exact repair:** Keep arbitrary measurable alphabets in 4.1–4.3. For 4.4–4.6 explicitly equip the finite alphabet with both the discrete topology and the full/discrete σ-algebra (equivalently require its Borel measurable structure), and give `X` the induced structures. Put this convention in the README and in the hypotheses of the affected plan targets, and state the Borel-factor hypothesis in the `Pi.borelSpace` supplier contract. Add the above two-point negative control to 4.4.

**Repair tested:** With the full σ-algebra on `{0,1}`, the trace and Borel σ-algebras on `X` both have exactly 4 sets. `{1̄}` is clopen and measurable, its indicator takes values 0 and 1 measurably, and its mass under `(δ_{0̄}+δ_{1̄})/2` is `1/2`. The pinned Borel/product and probability-topology hypotheses are then available. This changes no dimer statement: the subsequent `Bool` instance already uses its native discrete measurable structure.
