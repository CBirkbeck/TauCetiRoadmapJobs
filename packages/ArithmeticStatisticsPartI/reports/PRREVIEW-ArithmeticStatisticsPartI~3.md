## R01 (P1) — the new Weil-polynomial bound omits the negative constant coefficient

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticStatisticsPartI/README.md:2536`, “such a polynomial f satisfies `q^g f(x) = x^(2g) f(q/x)`”; also the nine-polynomial Check at line 2549.

**Claim:** the root-modulus condition alone implies this reciprocity identity and the displayed product bound.

**Witness:** At g=1, q=4, the nine polynomials x²−ax+4 (−4≤a≤4) and x²−4 all qualify: 10>9=4√4+1. For f=x²−4, 4f(1)=−12 whereas f(4)=12.

**Repair:** retain finiteness for the full stated family, but distinguish f(0)=q^g from f(0)=−q^g. The functional equation has that additional sign. For g≥1, twice the displayed product bounds the full family; the original product bounds the positive-constant subfamily. Treat g=0 separately (only 1). State why abelian Frobenius polynomials have constant coefficient q^g, using the degree/characteristic-polynomial comparison. The corrected Check has ten members in the unrestricted family and nine in the positive-constant family: 10≤18 and 9≤9. Adding the constant-coefficient condition only to the sharper bound preserves the general finiteness target. The source itself has the same omitted-sign error; correcting its numerical constant alone did not fix it.

## R02 (P1) — a stable lattice need not be free over the product Frobenius order

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticStatisticsPartI/README.md:2542`, “every stable lattice is a free module over it”.

**Claim:** away from p·disc(m_π), every Frobenius-stable lattice is free over ℤ_ℓ[π], including non-isotypic abelian varieties.

**Witness:** Over 𝔽₅ take E₋:y²=x³−x, E₊:y²=x³+x and A=E₋×E₊². At ℓ=3, the maximal order has two unramified quadratic factors; T₃A has component ranks (1,2), hence ℤ₃-rank 6, whereas a free module has rank 4r.

**Repair:** write the maximal order as ∏ᵢOᵢ and the lattice as ∏ᵢLᵢ, with each Lᵢ free of its own fixed rank nᵢ over Oᵢ. Then ∏ᵢGL_(nᵢ)(Frac Oᵢ) acts transitively. For the witness the Frobenius factors are x²+2x+5 and x²−2x+5; their resultant is 80 and disc(m_π)=1638400, a 3-adic unit. Thus this is inside the asserted good-prime range, not an exceptional-prime issue. The corrected componentwise statement gives ranks (1,2) and one local orbit. Add this mixed-multiplicity Check. Lipnowski–Tsimerman §3.1.1–§3.1.2 treats the components separately (5ceed816 lines 241–267).

## R03 (P2) — the characteristic-prime lattice argument loses the Witt scalars

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticStatisticsPartI/README.md:2542`, “the order generated by F and V inside a maximal order of the semisimple `ℚ_p`-algebra they generate”.

**Claim:** the prime-field argument in [63], Remark 3.2 establishes finiteness of local lattice orbits for every 𝔽_q.

**Witness:** Over 𝔽₉, put K=ℚ₃(i), i²=−1, σ(i)=−i, and F=[[0,−3],[1,0]]σ on K²; V=−F. The ℚ₃-linear commutant of F,V has dimension 8; imposing K-linearity reduces it to 4.

**Repair:** use the image of the full noncommutative Dieudonné order generated by W(𝔽_q), F and V. Explicitly supply the general local-order argument: its rational algebra is semisimple; its module automorphism group is the W(𝔽_q)[1/p]-linear F-commutant; maximal-order lattices have finitely many types in the fixed rational module; and a conductor gives a finite interval cΛ⊆L⊆Λ. This proves the required finite-orbit statement with the actual group, including q=p^r, r>1. On the witness, retaining W(𝔽₉) recovers dimension 4, the quaternionic endomorphism algebra, rather than dimension 8. Keep contravariant endomorphism actions as opposite algebras (or explicitly invert the induced action on units).

[63] expressly restricts its argument to the prime field and leaves the semilinear case open in that remark (5ceed816 lines 399–406). The supplier defines morphisms to be W-linear (FiniteFlatGroupsAndIntegralPadicHodgeTheory §R07.2.5, `README.md:3169`), and Waterhouse–Milne uses the full skew algebra K[F] (waterhouse-milne lines 311–344). Neither licenses dropping the scalar action.

## R04 (P2) — rational irreducibility does not make the local commutant a field

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticStatisticsPartI/README.md:2551`, “with irreducible Frobenius polynomial … the commutant … is the field `ℚ_ℓ[π]`”.

**Claim:** irreducibility of an elliptic Frobenius polynomial over ℚ implies that its algebra over ℚ_ℓ is a field.

**Witness:** For y²=x³−x over 𝔽₅ the polynomial x²+2x+5 is irreducible over ℚ. Modulo 13 it has roots 2 and 9, with derivatives 6 and 7. Hensel lifting gives ℚ₁₃[π]≅ℚ₁₃×ℚ₁₃.

**Repair:** call the commutant a quadratic étale ℚ_ℓ-algebra, a field only when the polynomial remains irreducible over ℚ_ℓ. Keep its dimension 2 and add the split case above alongside an inert case (ℓ=3). The corrected values are ℚ₁₃×ℚ₁₃ and the unramified quadratic extension of ℚ₃, both of dimension 2.
