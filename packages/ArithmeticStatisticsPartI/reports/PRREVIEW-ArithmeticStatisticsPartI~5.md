## R01 (P2) — QuadraticFormsOverABase §6.1 is an upward supplier for an identity the pinned Mathlib already has

**Location.** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticStatisticsPartI/README.md:68–70` (the subsection "From TauCetiRoadmap.QuadraticFormsOverABase": "§6.1 supplies the signed resultant identity at formal degree … Applied to the universal form over `ℤ[a₀,…,aₙ]` … it gives the same identity for `binaryDisc`"), with the dependent mentions at :22 ("and QuadraticFormsOverABase §6.1"), :2401 ("is QuadraticFormsOverABase §6.1, transported to `binaryDisc` through the universal form"), :2411 ("take the resultant identity from QuadraticFormsOverABase §6.1 at the universal form"), :2413 (`*Needs:*` "resultant identity at formal degree (QuadraticFormsOverABase §6.1)") and :2607 ("QuadraticFormsOverABase §6.1 for the resultant identity at formal degree").

**Claim.** The identity `a₀·Δ_n(f) = (−1)^{n(n−1)/2} Res_{n,n−1}(f,f′)` for `binaryDisc` is consumed from QuadraticFormsOverABase §6.1.

**Witness.** (i) QuadraticFormsOverABase is not on main and not in the merge order: it is the new-folder pull request #1930 (another author), and `gate --depcheck` on its README prints `NOT IN ORDER: QuadraticFormsOverABase (insert it after its last supplier)`. Its suppliers are HyperellipticCurves (merge order line 394), GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmeticPartII (392), OrthogonalTamagawaAndLatticeMass (149), LefschetzPencilsAndVanishingCycles (68) and SchemeAndStackFoundations, so it is inserted after line 394, below ArithmeticStatisticsPartI (109): this citation is upward. The gate's `depcheck=0` on this README does not see it because an unlisted supplier is never placed; the brief's rule (an unlisted roadmap sits after its last supplier) does. The pull-request description's "Depends on" list omits #1930 as well. (ii) The identity needs no roadmap at all. `binaryDisc n a` is `Polynomial.discr` of the universal polynomial `Coefficients.binaryPolynomial (X i) = Σ X_i·Tⁿ⁻ⁱ` over `MvPolynomial (Fin (n+1)) ℤ`, evaluated at `a` (`Suggested.lean:146–155`). At the pin, `Polynomial.resultant_deriv` (`Mathlib/RingTheory/Polynomial/Resultant/Basic.lean:977`) states, for `0 < f.degree`, `resultant f f′ f.natDegree (f.natDegree − 1) = (−1)^(natDegree·(natDegree−1)/2) · f.leadingCoeff · f.discr`; the universal form has degree exactly `n ≥ 1` and leading coefficient `X 0 = a₀`, and `Polynomial.resultant_map_map` (:137, formal degrees, no degree hypothesis) with `Polynomial.derivative_map` (`Mathlib/Algebra/Polynomial/Derivative.lean:249`) specialise it to every coefficient vector, leading zeros included. This is exactly, and only, the case of §6.1 the README invokes ("applied to the universal form, whose degree is exactly n"): §6.1's extra content, the `aₙ = 0` case, is about HyperellipticCurves §0.1's `formDiscr`, which this README never uses. QuadraticFormsOverABase §6.1 itself routes the exact-degree case to `Polynomial.resultant_deriv`.

**Repair.** Delete the QuadraticFormsOverABase contract: the list item at :22, the subsection at :68–70, the clause "is QuadraticFormsOverABase §6.1, transported … supplier contract" at :2401, the proof sentence at :2411, the `*Needs:*` item at :2413 and the Dependencies clause at :2607. In their place, in the From Mathlib contract and at :2401, state: for `n ≥ 1` and every coefficient vector `a` over any commutative ring, `a₀·binaryDisc n a = (−1)^{n(n−1)/2} · resultant f f′ n (n−1)` with `f = binaryPolynomial a`, `a₀ = 0` included, by `Polynomial.resultant_deriv` at the universal form (degree exactly n, leading coefficient `a₀`) specialised with `Polynomial.resultant_map_map` and `Polynomial.derivative_map`; over the universal coefficient ring this exhibits `Δ_n` as the signed quotient `Res(F,F′)/a₀`. `*Needs:*` gains `Polynomial.resultant_deriv`, `Polynomial.resultant_map_map`, `Polynomial.derivative_map` (Mathlib) and loses the QuadraticFormsOverABase item. Tested on the refuting instances of the table: `(a,b,c)` at n = 2, `(0,b,c)` at n = 2, n = 1, `X³ − X`, `X⁴ − 1` all satisfy the Mathlib-derived identity, and n = 0 (excluded by `n ≥ 1`) fails it, as it should. No open pull request then needs to enter the description's dependency list for this identity.
