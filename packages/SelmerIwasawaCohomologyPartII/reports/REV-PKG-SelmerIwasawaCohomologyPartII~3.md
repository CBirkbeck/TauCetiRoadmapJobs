### Principles and scope

The package is a continuation of SelmerIwasawaCohomology, with the base boundary stated at README line 9. It imports general local conditions, weak semisimplicity, exponent algebra, Satake predicates, deformation conditions, PEL models and general geometry. Its new work concerns native unitary strata, diagonal cycles, reciprocity comparisons and their arithmetic applications. The finite-sum definition in Lean does not replace any of those geometric carriers.

The generality checks retained arbitrary CM extensions, including F⁺=ℚ where stated; ramified coefficient fields; both rank parities; arbitrary transferable levels on the declared transfer contract; and ordinary cohomology on the nonproper domain through an explicit comparison contract. The bounds on ℓ and the conditions on auxiliary p are source hypotheses, rather than an extra convenient-prime restriction. The general transfer gap explicitly avoids silently restricting all endpoints to the supplier's smaller S_FL domain.

The rank theorems retain a uniform realised Frobenius span assumption. This is substantive: a fixed coefficient line alone cannot imply that actual permitted Frobenius elements detect Selmer classes. The supplier EulerSystemsAndKolyvaginSystems §1.15 retains that same condition. The package neither replaces that group by the full Selmer extension group nor asserts a diagonalizing basis from a positive-loss scaled inverse. Its rank-one proof uses a primitive kernel vector instead.

The endpoints start from an actual definite period or an actual nonzero regulator. The README does not assert the conjectural implication from an L-derivative to a nonzero cycle, or remove L6/L7 using an announcement without a proof. General period nonvanishing is left to its consumer, with no upward dependency. I found no copied source passage, executable mathematical algorithm, sorry'd data, `set_option`, or theorem with its conclusion hidden in a hypothesis. No ownership move was needed.
