# Design report: SelmerIwasawaCohomologyPartII

The four requested artifacts are `packages/SelmerIwasawaCohomologyPartII/design/README.md`, `packages/SelmerIwasawaCohomologyPartII/design/plan.json`, `packages/SelmerIwasawaCohomologyPartII/sources.toml` and this report. The design has 8 layers, 44 targets, 9 definitions and 64 specified Checks. Every definition has at least three Checks, and every target has a legal-source locator. There are 33 explicit contract/proof gaps, including missing accepted supplier sections. This is a source-grounded design with recorded gaps, not a certification that the absent supplier interfaces exist or that packaging can treat them as implemented.

The parent is SelmerIwasawaCohomology. Its text is absent from the supplied main/completed trees and packages. Consequently the exact point where its accepted sections stop cannot be certified. The design keeps that base-ownership gap explicit, especially for coefficient exponents, weak semisimplicity, Bloch–Kato reduction and any generic bipartite machinery. Accepting these as new definitions before resolving that boundary would risk duplication.
