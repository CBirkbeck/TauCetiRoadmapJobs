## R02 (P3) — The valuation-ring Check of 5.4 does not say that `k` is a field

**Location.** `README.md` 5.4, Checks, lines 915–917: "Let `V` be a valuation ring whose maximal ideal `m` is not principal and satisfies `m² = m` (the localisation of `⋃_n k[x^(1/2ⁿ)]` at the ideal generated by the `x^(1/2ⁿ)`)".

**Claim.** 5.4 does not introduce `k`, and in Layer 5 (Convention 1) `k` is a commutative ring. The parenthetical construction gives a valuation ring only when `k` is a domain.

**Witness.** Take `k = ℤ/6`. The quotient of `⋃_n k[x^(1/2ⁿ)]` by the ideal of the `x^(1/2ⁿ)` is `ℤ/6`. So that ideal is not prime: `2 · 3 = 0` lies in it, but neither factor does. There is no localisation at it, and no valuation ring.

**Repair.** Write "for a field `k`". Tested for a field `k`:

- `V` is the union of the DVRs `k[x^(1/2ⁿ)]` localised at `(x^(1/2ⁿ))`, each dominating the last. So `V` is a valuation ring with value group `ℤ[1/2]`.
- `m = m²`, because `x^q = x^(q/2) x^(q/2)`.
- With `A = m` and `M = m/xV`, the element `x^(1/2) ⊗ x^(1/2)` is nonzero in `A ⊗_A M = m/xm`, since `v(x) = 1` while `xm` has values `> 1`. It maps to `x = 0` in `M`.
