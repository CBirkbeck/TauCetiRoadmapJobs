## Source issues

- **Error** in Gee at §3.23, printed pp. 17–18: the source says the degree-zero relative group is the one-dimensional scalar space whenever `ρ̄` is absolutely irreducible and displays `dim H¹_{S,T} = #T − …`; it should be zero for nonempty `T` (the scalars inject into the framing terms), giving `#T − 1`, as the source's own Proposition 3.24(3) and Proposition 5.10 use; affects a stated result (the displayed dimension formula), not Proposition 3.24.
- **Gap** in KW final at Lemma 5.1, printed p. 47: the source says one may find `g` with `χ(g) ≠ 1` and `tr ρ̄(g) ≠ 0` without argument; it should note that `ρ̄(ker χ)` contains a copy of `SL₂(𝔽_{2^r})` (perfect core, odd-order central kernel) whose span is `M₂(k)`, so some element of the coset has unit trace; affects the proof.
- **Gap** in ACC published at Proposition 6.2.33 (arXiv v2 Proposition 6.2.32), printed p. 1047: the source says a degree-one place forces the rational prime below it to split in `F₀`; it should exclude the finitely many primes ramified in `F₀`, where degree one does not imply splitting; affects the proof (repaired by discarding finitely many primes, as §8.7 does).
