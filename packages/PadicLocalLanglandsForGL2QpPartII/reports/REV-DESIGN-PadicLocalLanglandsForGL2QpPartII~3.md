2. **F02** (P1) §2.4 and §7.2 name a Galois representation V^{(k)}_{M,ℒ} that does not exist for k ≥ 2

   **Location.** README line 409: "Every line is weakly admissible (§0.3), so Δ_{k,ℒ} = D_rig(V^{(k)}_{M,ℒ}) for an absolutely irreducible de Rham representation V^{(k)}_{M,ℒ} of weights {0,k}"; line 412: "0 → ℒ⊗(LL(M)⊗Sym^{k−1}) → Π(M,k) → Π(V^{(k)}_{M,ℒ})^an → 0"; §7.2 line 1051: "the strong dual of Π(V^{(k+1)}_{M,ℒ})^an, with V^{(k+1)}_{M,ℒ} the representation of weights {0,k+1} of §2.4".

   **Claim.** For a fixed supercuspidal M and every k ≥ 1, Δ_{k,ℒ} is étale, the (φ,Γ)-module of a Galois representation of weights {0,k}.

   **Witness.** COLMEZ19POIDS line 211: Δ/Δ_{k,ℒ} ≅ R/t^kR. Degrees on the Fargues–Fontaine curve are additive, so deg Δ_{k,ℒ} = deg Δ − k, and an irreducible (φ,Γ)-module is étale exactly when its degree is 0: at most one k gives an étale Δ_{k,ℒ}. For M = M(π) of slope 1/2 (§0.3), Δ_{1,ℒ} = D_rig(V_{M,ℒ}) is étale, so deg Δ = 1 and deg Δ_{2,ℒ} = −1 ≠ 0: no V^{(2)}_{M,ℒ} exists. Colmez's Proposition 0.1 (lines 146–150) defines Π(Δ) for every rank-two Δ over R, étale or not, and Remarque 0.2(i) (lines 153–154) gives Π(Δ) = Π(V)^an only for étale Δ; Théorème 0.6(iii) (lines 226–236) is stated for Π(Δ_{k,ℒ}), never for a Galois representation. §0.3's weak admissibility is a weight-{0,1} statement (its line 184–186: weights {0,1}, D_pst = M[−1]). The hedge in §7.2, "up to the twist by the power of det that matches central characters", cannot repair this: a twist by x^a∘det corresponds to χ^a and shifts both weights; what is missing is an unramified twist of φ.

   **Repair.** In §2.4 replace the quoted sentence by: "Δ_{k,ℒ} has degree 1−k, so it is étale only for k = 1. For k ≥ 2 let δ_k be the unramified character with δ_k(p)² = p^{1−k} (enlarge L by √p when k is even; the sign of the exponent is fixed by deg(Δ_{k,ℒ}⊗δ_k) = 0). Then Δ_{k,ℒ}⊗δ_k = D_rig(V^{(k)}_{M,ℒ}) for an absolutely irreducible de Rham V^{(k)}_{M,ℒ} of weights {0,k} with D_pst(V^{(k)}_{M,ℒ}) = M[−1]⊗δ_k, and Π(Δ_{k,ℒ}) = Π(V^{(k)}_{M,ℒ})^an⊗δ_k⁻¹ by COLMEZ19POIDS Remarque 0.2(ii), line 162." Write Π(Δ_{k,ℒ}) in the two exact sequences of (ii). In §7.2 write "the strong dual of Π(Δ_{k+1,ℒ}), which is Π(V^{(k+1)}_{M,ℒ})^an⊗δ_{k+1}⁻¹" and say that these V^{(k+1)} are the representations of weights {0,k+1} of DLB17 Remarque 11.12 (line 2723). Add the negative-control Check: "At k = 2 and M of slope 1/2, Δ_{2,ℒ} has degree −1 and no Galois representation has it as D_rig; the twist δ_2 with δ_2(p)² = p⁻¹ restores degree 0." Tested on k = 2, in the convention where sub-objects have smaller degree (t^kΔ ⊂ Δ_{k,ℒ} ⊂ Δ forces deg(t^kΔ) = deg Δ − 2k, so R(x) = R·t has degree −1 and R(δ) has degree −v_p(δ(p))): deg(Δ_{2,ℒ}⊗δ_2) = −1 − 2·v_p(δ_2(p)) = −1 − 2·(−1/2) = 0 for δ_2(p)² = p⁻¹; the k = 1 case is unchanged (δ_1 = 1).
