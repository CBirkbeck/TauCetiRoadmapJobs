## Source issues

- **Error** in HW at Lemma 4.7 and Definition 3.2, printed pages 9 and 14: the source says the outer exponent of `U¹` equals `p` for every prime and `2 ≤ n ≤ 6`; it should be `p` for `p` odd and `1` for `p = 2`, since `(ℤ/2)ˣ` is trivial and the action then factors through `(ℤ/1)ˣ` (the proof shows `M ~ M⁻¹`); affects nothing (only the factorization through `(ℤ/p)ˣ` is used).
- **Misprint** in HW at proof of Lemma 4.7, printed page 14: the source says the exponent of `U¹` is divisible by `9` when `p = 3`, `n = 6`; it should be "divides `9`", which is what Lemma 4.6 gives; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in HW at proof of Lemma 4.13, printed page 17: the source says `Q = e_{n−2,n}^{b_{n−2}} ⋯ e_{0,2}^{b_0} e_{0,3}`; it should have `e_{0,3}^{a_2}`, since `Q₀₃ = a₂` is how `Q` was defined; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in HW at proof of Proposition 6.1, printed page 23: the source says the subgroup on the indices `j ≤ 4` is a copy of the unitriangular `4 × 4` matrices; it should be `5 × 5`; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in HW at §7, printed page 28: the source says `P_{r,s}` is the external direct sum of the last-column and first-row Hom summands; it should be the internal sum, the corner `Hom(N_n, N_0)` occurring once; affects nothing.
- **Gap** in HW at Proposition 5.3 and proof of Theorem 5.1, printed pages 19 and 22: the source says a local field is complete discretely valued with finite residue field, then applies Proposition 5.3 to every completion `k_v`; it should prove the real case too, nontrivial only for `p = 2`; affects the proof.
- **Misprint** in HW at proof of Proposition 5.3, printed page 19: the source says in the easy case any homomorphism `Γ_K → U/Z` lifts to `Z`; it should be "lifts to `U`"; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in HW at proof of Proposition 5.3, printed page 21: the source says `ρ_{v,u}` twice; it should be `ρ_{u,v}`, row first, as in (5.5); affects nothing.
- **Gap** in HW at Lemma 5.5, printed page 19: the source says the sections form a torsor under the `Γ`-module `Hom(C, A)` for any discrete `C`; it should say `Hom(C, A)` is discrete only for finitely generated `C`, which is the case used; affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in HW at Proposition 7.3, printed page 26: the source says defining systems for the classes `α_i` are in bijection with lifts of `α`; it should say defining systems with the fixed adjacent cocycles that define `α`, since another representative gives a lift of a conjugate character; affects nothing (the proof fixes the cocycles).
