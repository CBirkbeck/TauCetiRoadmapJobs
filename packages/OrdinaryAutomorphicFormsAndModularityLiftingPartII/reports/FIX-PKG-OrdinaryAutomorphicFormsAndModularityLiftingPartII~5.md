## F02 (P2) — applied

Verified. Ger19 Definition 2.3.4 (Ger19 lines 506–510) separates two algebras: T, generated without the U-operators, and T̃, generated with them. Ger19 lines 542–555 put e in T̃, and Definition 2.4.1 (Ger19 line 535) defines T^{T,ord} as the image of T in T̃^{ord}. BCGP25 lines 5963–5968 define eT inside End_O(S), so the source is correct. The false claim was this roadmap's own API bullet.

Change in §2.1:
- **Definition.** Old: "T^T … generated by T^j_w, (T^n_w)^{−1}, ⟨u⟩ … T^{T,ord} = eT^T". New: T^T ⊂ T̃^T, where T̃^T adds the U^j. T̃^T is finite over O and splits as the product of its localizations. Ordinary maximal ideals are defined, and e ∈ T̃^T with e = lim U(l)^{r!}. T^{T,ord} is the image of T^T in eT̃^T. Because eT̃ acts faithfully on eS, this equals the image of T^T in End(eS). T^T carries the universal algebra and the Galois surjection of 3.3.
- **API.** Old: "e is an idempotent of every T^T". New: e ∈ T̃^T commutes with T^T and need not lie in it. The bullet adds the facts from Ger19 §2.4: eS is the largest submodule on which every U^j is bijective, U(l) is topologically nilpotent off eS, both algebras act faithfully on eS, and e has a unique limit idempotent.
- **New two-refinement Check.** Take n = 2, F⁺_v = ℚ₂, π ι-ordinary and unramified at v. The polynomial is X² − tX + 2s; with t = s = 1 its roots are ≡ 27 and 6 mod 32. On this plane e = (U − β)/(α − β) = diag(1,0), while T^T acts by scalars there.

The 3.3 route already says that T_m is generated by T^j_w, characteristic-polynomial coefficients and diamond operators. That is consistent with the corrected definition, so no other occurrence needed repair.
