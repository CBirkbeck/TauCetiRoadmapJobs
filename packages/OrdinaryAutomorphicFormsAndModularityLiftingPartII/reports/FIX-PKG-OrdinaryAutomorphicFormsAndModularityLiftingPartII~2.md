## F02 (P3) — applied differently

The reviewer's sentence ("⟨o_1, ψ′⟩ = 0 holds exactly when the kernel K … is torsion-free …; no nontrivial tuple of 2-power roots of unity at T has trivial Artin image in Γ") was not adopted: the condition it names never holds, so writing it as a condition to verify would mislead. Witness: the diagonal tuple (−1)_{v∈T} lies in K for every F, F⁺, T, because the idele that is −1 at T and 1 elsewhere is the principal idele −1 times an idele whose components are units at the finite places outside S and archimedean (complex) elsewhere, both of which have trivial image in `G^{ab}_{F,S}`; and −1 ≠ 1 in `F_ṽ^×(2)`. Hence K_tors ≠ 0, d(K) = (h² − 1) + d(K_tors) = h² (injectivity of the adjoint of o_1 gives d(K) ≤ h²), the adjoint is an isomorphism and ⟨o_1, ψ′⟩ ≠ 0 for every nonzero ψ′. So the rank-one case refutes the old route "J generated by h² − 1 elements" outright, while (b) still holds in rank one (dim O⟦Γ⟧ = 1 + d).

New route of (b) (README 3.2, route paragraph): the h²-th relation is a torsion relation s_ζ − 1 = 0 in the scalar (determinant) direction, where s_ζ is the product of the local determinants at a tuple ζ of 2-power roots of unity with trivial global Artin image; it has 2-power order, so on each component of Spec P it is either identically 0 or a nonzero constant of ϖO, and it cuts no dimension. (b) then follows from two inputs: (★) some such s_ζ − 1 is a minimal generator of J (not in m_P J), and (C) every irreducible component of Spec P/ϖ lies on a component of Spec P on which the torsion acts trivially at ζ. Given (★) alone, every component of Spec `R^T_{S_χ}` not inside V(ϖ) gets the bound; (C) is needed only for components inside V(ϖ). (C) holds after the preparation of 4.1 (products of local components; §6.24 at v | 2 with a common special fibre for all `R^△_v/Q`; D^{χ_v}_v = D^1_v on Artinian k-algebras at R; sums of characters at v₁). At the unprepared field, (C) at v | 2 is now the second clause of gap:dyadic-flag-ring-dimension (README line 90 and `plan.json`), and at v ∈ T ∖ (R ∪ S_2) it is part of the target, since the Breuil–Mézard identity that would give it (Sho18 Theorem 4.6, line 801) is proved for l > 2 only. (★) is the unsourced step; in rank one it holds (a generator of the cyclic K_tors is not in 2K); the reduction through det : 𝒢_n → 𝒢_1 is kept and stated as sufficient only. The one-sentence pointer to GlobalGaloisDeformations §§4.8–4.11 (determinant map, free dyadic twisting, determinant torsor; verified at main: headings at its lines 889, 926, 954, 997) is added as the second route. The Check "Rank one" now records the torsion generator and `⟨o_1, ψ′⟩ ≠ 0`; the Lemma 5.7.7 correction and 4.8's Check name (★) and (C).
