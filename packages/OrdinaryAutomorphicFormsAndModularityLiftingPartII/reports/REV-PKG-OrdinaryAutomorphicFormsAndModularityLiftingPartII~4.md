1. **F01** (P1) Operator compatibility fails at spherical depth zero

   **Location:** `packages/OrdinaryAutomorphicFormsAndModularityLiftingPartII/README.md:236`, :237 and :244. The levels allow “0 ≤ b ≤ c”, and the API says the operators commute with inclusions “for b ≤ b′, c ≤ c′”.

   **Claim:** The normalized U-operator commutes with restriction from the spherical level c = 0 to a positive-depth Iwahori level.

   **Witness:** At n = 2, λ = 0 and F⁺_v = ℚ₂, the constant function maps to 3 under the spherical double coset and to 2 under the Iwahori double coset. Ger19 line 493 requires c > 0.

   **Exact repair:** Keep the level subgroup at (b,c) = (0,0), but require c ≥ 1 for the U-operator tower, its compatibility assertions and the ordinary constructions that use that tower. Explain separately that the spherical operator at depth zero is a different operator. Cite Ger19 Definition 2.3.1 and Lemma 2.3.3, pp. 10–11. Add the negative-control Check below. Do not restrict the coefficient prime or remove the spherical level from the general level theory.

   **Repair test:** Put K = GL₂(ℤ₂), I = Iw(2⁰,2¹), a = diag(2,1). The indices are [K:K∩aKa⁻¹] = 3 and [I:I∩aIa⁻¹] = 2. The inclusion sends 1 to 1, so its two composites give 3 and 2. At depths c = 1 and c′ = 2 both indices are 2, recovering the source's positive-depth statement. The constant function is an allowed algebraic modular form: take R empty, λ = 0, F⁺ = ℚ(√105), F = ℚ(√−7,√−15), n = 2. Here nd = 4; the relative discriminant has norm 105²/105² = 1 and the completions above 2 are ℚ₂. Neither a non-Eisenstein localization nor a cuspidality assumption appears in §2.1.

2. **F02** (P2) The ordinary projector is placed in the smaller Hecke algebra

   **Location:** `packages/OrdinaryAutomorphicFormsAndModularityLiftingPartII/README.md:238` and :245: “e is an idempotent of every” finite-level T-algebra. The generators in :238 omit the U-operators.

   **Claim:** The projector onto the unit-U-eigenvalue summands belongs to the algebra generated by away-from-2 Hecke operators and diamonds, before taking the ordinary image.

   **Witness:** Ger19 lines 508–510 distinguish T from T̃ = T[U^j]; lines 542–555 put e in T̃. On two Iwahori refinements of one ordinary unramified constituent, T acts scalarly while e acts as diag(1,0).

   **Exact repair:** Define the finite-level enlarged algebra T̃ by adjoining the normalized U-operators. Put e ∈ T̃ and define T^ord as the image of T in End(eS), equivalently eT inside eT̃. Retain the smaller T as the algebra used for the universal ordinary Hecke algebra and the Galois surjection. Cite Ger19 Definition 2.3.4 and Definition 2.4.1, pp. 11–12. Replace the membership API by membership in T̃ and commutation with T. Add a two-refinement Check; no Lean declaration is needed.

   **Repair test:** On the rank-two Iwahori space with U-polynomial X²−X+2, its two roots α,β in ℤ₂ have valuations 0 and 1. Modulo 32 they are 27 and 6; their difference is a unit. Thus e = (U−β)/(α−β) belongs to O[U], has eigenvalues (1,0), and cannot be a scalar. Away-place operators and diamonds on the two refinements of the same unramified constituent have the same eigenvalues. The corrected image algebra on eS is scalar and has identity e, without asserting e ∈ T on the original space. This also agrees with the earlier supplier's definition at `TauCetiRoadmap/PotentialAutomorphyInfrastructurePartII/README.md:894` (#2135): it takes the image in End(eS).
