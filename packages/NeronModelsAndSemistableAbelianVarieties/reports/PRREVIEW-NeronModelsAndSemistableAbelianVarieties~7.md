## R01 (P3) — The wrong-model Check of §1.9a states its dimensions at an arbitrary point of the fibre

**Location:** TauCetiRoadmap/NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties/README.md:1053–1061 "Let `R` be the local ring of `𝒜` at a point of the fibre over `s` … `V(ḡ)`, a proper closed subset of `Spec (R/π)` of dimension `dim A − 1` where it is non-empty … all of `Spec (R/π)`, of dimension `dim A`".

**Claim:** for the local ring at any point of `𝒜_s`, the saturated closure `V(g)` has fibre of dimension `dim A − 1` while the reduced generator `f̄ = 0` cuts out all of `Spec (R/π)`, of dimension `dim A`.

**Witness:** take the generic point `η` of `𝒜_s` (irreducible: smooth and geometrically connected). Then `R/π` is the function field of `𝒜_s`, `Spec (R/π)` is one point of dimension `0`, not `dim A`, and `ḡ ≠ 0` is a unit, so `V(ḡ)` is empty. At a point of codimension `c` in `𝒜_s` the two dimensions are `dim A − c − 1` and `dim A − c`. The saturation argument itself (`J ∩ R = (g)`, `π` prime because `R/π = O_{𝒜_s,x}` is regular, hence a domain) is correct at every point.

**Repair:** write "at a closed point of the fibre over `s`" (then `dim R/π = dim 𝒜_s = dim A`, and for `ḡ` a nonunit `dim V(ḡ) = dim A − 1` by catenarity of the regular local ring `R/π`). Instance that must hold afterwards: `𝒜 = E` an elliptic scheme, `x` the origin of `E_s`, `g = t` the parameter of the non-example instance Check: `V(t̄)` is the closed point, dimension `0 = dim A − 1`, and `Spec (R/π)` has dimension `1 = dim A`.
