- **Error** in `ramare2016density` at (1.1) p. 326 (the zero-density bound for `ζ` alone, `N(σ, T) ≤ 4.9 (3T)^{8(1−σ)/3}(log T)^{5−2σ} + 51.5 log² T`), as recorded in `kadirilumleyng2018` lines 93–95: the inequality as printed is incorrect; affects a stated result; the revised form rests on an unpublished proof.
- **Error** in `ramare2016perron` at Theorems 1.2 and 3.1, as recorded in `cullyhugilljohnston2025` lines 68–76: a factor `k + 1` is missing from the error term of Theorem 3.1, (3.1) contains a typo, and the measure-theoretic steps are flawed; affects the proof and the stated error terms; corrected in print at `cullyhugilljohnston2025` Theorem 2.1 and Corollary 3.2.
- **Gap** in `nemes2015` at Theorem 1.3, line 168: the case `N = 1` of the bound is left unproved; affects nothing here (only `N ≥ 2` is cited).
- **Misprint** in `kadiri2005` (arXiv v1) at Théorème 1.1, line 14: the constant is printed as `5.70176`; the published version has `5.69693` (as `jangkwon2014` line 13, `mossinghofftrudgian2015` line 111 and `mty2024` line 1279 quote); affects a stated result's constant, not its truth.
