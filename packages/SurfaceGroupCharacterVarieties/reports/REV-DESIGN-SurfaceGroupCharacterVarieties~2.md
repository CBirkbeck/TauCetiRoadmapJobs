1. **F01** (P2) §3.5 "the action does not lift to `Hom`" has no exact statement, and its literal reading is false

   Location: `packages/SurfaceGroupCharacterVarieties/design/README.md` §3.5, the theorem sentence "and that the action does not lift to `Hom(π₁(Σ_{g,n}, x), H)`: for nonabelian `H` the inner automorphisms of `π₁`, which are the images of point pushes, act nontrivially on homomorphisms with nonabelian image (§1.3)"; the same clause in `design/plan.json` target 3.5 and in the Summary ("compatibly with … the action on conjugacy classes of all representations").

   Claim: for nonabelian `H` the `Mod_{g,n}`-action on `Rep(π₁, H)` admits no lift to an action on `Hom(π₁, H)`.

   Witness: as a statement about actions on sets the claim is false for every `(g, n, H)`. For a `Mod_{g,n}`-orbit `O ⊆ Rep` with base point `c₀`, the fibres of `Hom → Rep` over `O` are the conjugation orbits `H·ρ_c`, pairwise in bijection through `ρ ↦ ρ ∘ Ψ_x[f]⁻¹`; choosing one bijection `X_{c₀} → X_c` per `c ∈ O` identifies `π⁻¹(O)` with `O × X_{c₀}`, on which `m·(c, x) = (m c, x)` is an action of `Mod_{g,n}` making `Hom → Rep` equivariant. Computed on `(g, n) = (0, 3)`, `H = S₃`: `Hom(F₂, S₃)` has `36` points, `Rep(F₂, S₃)` has `11` classes, and the construction above lifts the `S₃`-action on the `11` classes to the `36` points. LandesmanLitt2024 lines 49–50 say that `Mod_{g,n}` does not *naturally* act on the representation variety; the README dropped the qualifier, and what its sketch proves is a different, precise statement: the based action of the stabilizer does not descend along `Forget`. A reader formalizing the sentence as written (`¬ ∃` an action on `Hom` over the action on `Rep`) states a false theorem; a reader formalizing "no homomorphic lift `Mod_{g,n} → Aut(π₁)`" states one the sketch does not prove.

   Repair: replace the clause by the statement the sketch proves: "and the non-descent theorem: for `2g − 2 + n > 0` and `S = Σ_{g,n}` marked at `x`, the based action of `Mod(S*)` on `Hom(π₁(S, x), H)`, `[f]·ρ = ρ ∘ (f_{const})⁻¹` (§3.3), lifts the action on `Rep(π₁(S, x), H)` along `Mod(S*) → Mod_{g,n}` but does not factor through `Forget` whenever some homomorphism `π₁(S, x) → H` has nonabelian image: `Push(α)` acts by `ρ ↦ MulAut.conj (ρ[α])⁻¹ ∘ ρ` (§§1.3, 3.3), which moves such a `ρ` for a suitable `α`; in particular no action of `Mod_{g,n}` on `Hom(π₁(S, x), H)` by precomposition with automorphisms of `π₁(S, x)` lifting `Ψ_x` exists when `Ψ_x` has no homomorphic lift to `MulAut(π₁(S, x))`, and none is asserted here." Make the same change in `plan.json` target 3.5 and keep the LandesmanLitt2024 lines 49–50 locator. Tested on the instance that refuted the original: `(0, 3)`, `H = S₃`, `ρ` the surjection `a ↦ (1 2)`, `b ↦ (2 3)`, `α = a`: `MulAut.conj (ρ a)⁻¹ ∘ ρ` sends `b` to `(1 2)(2 3)(1 2) = (1 3) ≠ (2 3)`, so `Push(a)` moves `ρ`, while the set-theoretic lift above still exists; the restated theorem holds and the refuted one is no longer claimed.

2. **F02** (P3) §3.4's orientation-to-`H₂` sign step cites a section that does not make it, and two *Needs:* lines omit MordellLawrenceVenkatesh §5.19

   Location: README §3.4, proof sketch: "`Ψ_x[f]` acts on `H₂(π₁(Σ, x); ℤ)` as `f` acts on `H₂(Σ; ℤ) ≅ ℤ`, by `+1` if `f` preserves the orientation (MordellLawrenceVenkatesh §5.1) and by `−1` if it reverses it"; *Needs:* lines of §3.4 and §3.2.

   Claim: MordellLawrenceVenkatesh §5.1 supplies that an orientation-preserving homeomorphism acts by `+1` on `H₂(Σ; ℤ)` and an orientation-reversing one by `−1`.

   Witness: MordellLawrenceVenkatesh §5.1 at its head defines orientation as atlas data and says that "integral homology … are consequences, not fields of this structure"; it states nothing about `H₂`. The fundamental class `[S] ∈ H₂(S, ∂S; ℤ)` and its invariance under orientation-preserving homeomorphisms are MordellLawrenceVenkatesh §5.5 ("invariant under orientation-preserving homeomorphisms", proved by "naturality of cap products"), a section this roadmap does not cite. The `−1` clause follows from §5.5 only through a computation: with `r` the reflection of the polygon model, `r` maps the two-cell to itself with reversed orientation, so acts by `−1` on cellular `H₂`, and an orientation-reversing `f` equals `(f ∘ r) ∘ r` with `f ∘ r` orientation-preserving (the index-two structure of §2.1's API), so `f_* = r_* = −1`. Computed: torus, `A ∈ GL₂(ℤ)` acts on `Λ²ℤ² ≅ ℤ` by `det A`; `diag(1, −1)` reverses orientation and `det = −1`; genus two, the hyperelliptic involution preserves orientation and acts by `+1` on `H₂`, by `−1` on `H₁`.

   Repair: in §3.4's sketch write "by `+1` if `f` preserves the orientation, because it fixes the fundamental class (MordellLawrenceVenkatesh §5.5), and by `−1` if it reverses it, since `f = (f ∘ r) ∘ r` for the reflection `r` of the polygon model, which acts by `−1` on cellular `H₂`"; add "MordellLawrenceVenkatesh §5.5; MordellLawrenceVenkatesh §5.19" to §3.4's *Needs:* line and to the MordellLawrenceVenkatesh supplier contract (§5.5: the fundamental class of a compact oriented surface and its invariance under orientation-preserving homeomorphisms); add "MordellLawrenceVenkatesh §5.19" to README §3.2's *Needs:* line, as `plan.json` already has it.
