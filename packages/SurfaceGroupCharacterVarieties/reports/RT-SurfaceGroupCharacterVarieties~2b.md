1. **F01** (P3) §6.4's proof plan says the centralizer dimension drops; it rises

   **Location.** `packages/SurfaceGroupCharacterVarieties/README.md:1806-1809`: "a cocharacter limit
   into the closed orbit would lower the dimension of the centralizer, while its adjoint image is
   `GL(𝔤)`-conjugate to that of `Z` …, which preserves the dimension of the fixed space; a
   contradiction."

   **Claim.** Passing from a tuple with non-closed orbit to its cocharacter limit in the closed orbit
   lowers the dimension of the centralizer.

   **Witness.** `G = SL₂`, `m = 1`, `z = [[1,1],[0,1]]`: the limit under `diag(t, t⁻¹)` is `z' = 1`;
   `dim C_G(z) = 1`, `dim C_G(z') = 3`; the orbit dimension falls `2 → 0`. BateMartinRohrle2005 lines
   870–874 have the same direction (orbit smaller, centralizer larger).

   **Repair.** Replace the quoted clause by: "a cocharacter limit `(z'₁, …, z'_m)` in the closed orbit
   of the orbit closure would have an orbit of strictly smaller dimension, hence a centralizer of
   strictly larger dimension and, every subgroup being separable, a strictly larger fixed space on
   `𝔤`; but the adjoint tuple of the limit lies in the closure of the `GL(𝔤)`-orbit of the adjoint
   tuple of `Z`, which is closed by the `GL(𝔤)` case of the same criterion, `𝔤` being a semisimple
   `Z`-module, so the two adjoint tuples are `GL(𝔤)`-conjugate and their fixed spaces on `𝔤` have the
   same dimension; a contradiction." Tested on the instance: `Ad(z)` is not semisimple on `𝔰𝔩₂`, so the
   hypothesis fails there, as it must (`1 < 3`).

2. **F02** (P3) "the topological group of §2.1" for a group that carries no topology

   **Location.** `packages/SurfaceGroupCharacterVarieties/README.md:261`: "`Mod^±(X)` is the
   topological group of §2.1; TeichmullerGeometry §3.1's `Mod±(Σ) = π₀ Diff(Σ)` … is another carrier".

   **Claim.** §2.1 makes `Mod^±(X)` a topological group.

   **Witness.** `README.md:785`: "Define `Mod^±(X) := (X ≃ₜ X) ⧸ Homeo₀(X)`"; the only topology of
   §2.1 is on `X ≃ₜ X` (`:789-790`), for locally compact Hausdorff `X`. For `X = ℝ` the group has two
   elements (`:835`) and no topology is stated.

   **Repair.** Replace "`Mod^±(X)` is the topological group of §2.1" by "`Mod^±(X)` is §2.1's group of
   ambient isotopy classes of homeomorphisms of the topological space `X`, an abstract group on which
   no topology is put". Tested on `X = ℝ`: the sentence now describes the two-element group of §2.1's
   Check.

3. **F03** (P3) module docstring narrows `exists_outAction_characterVariety` to topological fields

   **Location.** `packages/SurfaceGroupCharacterVarieties/Suggested.lean:36-38`: "`Out(Γ)` acts by
   homeomorphisms on the character variety of every group `Γ` with values in a subgroup of the points
   of an affine group over a topological field".

   **Claim.** The headline is stated for affine groups over topological fields.

   **Witness.** The declaration (`Suggested.lean:362`) takes `[Field k] [TopologicalSpace k]`, and its
   docstring (`:358`) and `README.md:2152` say "every field `k` with a topology". `ℝ` with the
   lower-limit topology is admitted and is no topological field (`x ↦ −x` is discontinuous); for
   `𝔾_a`, `Γ = ℤ` the pointwise topology is generated by `x ↦ nx`, `n ∈ ℤ`, so `x ↦ −x` is a
   homeomorphism and the statement holds.

   **Repair.** In `Suggested.lean:38` replace "over a topological field" by "over a field with a
   topology". Tested on the instance: the docstring then covers the lower-limit line, as the
   declaration does.
