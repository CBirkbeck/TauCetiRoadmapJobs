3. **F03** (P3) §7.3 is stated only for finitely generated groups

   - **Repair.** State §7.3 for all groups, on §7.1's `CharacterVariety`: "Prove that for a
     homomorphism of real reductive data `φ : (𝐆, G^ℝ) → (𝐆', G'^ℝ)` and every group `Γ`,
