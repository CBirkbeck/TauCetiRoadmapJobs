same pattern is repaired at its three other occurrences:

  sequence;

- the §2.2 Check (line 234): `𝔽_p[[t]]/𝔽_p[[t]]^p` → `𝔽_p[[t]]/𝔽_p[[t^p]]`, and
  `H¹(𝔽_p,α_p)=𝔽_p/𝔽_p^p=0` → `H¹(𝔽_p,α_p)=0, Frobenius being bijective on 𝔽_p`.

nonzero" in the same sentence.
