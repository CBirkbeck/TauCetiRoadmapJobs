### Principles, shape and ownership

The package extends the ordinary foundation roadmap and states where that roadmap stops. It does not rebuild the imported perfect-base comparison, primary continuity, finite-ideal excision or fpqc hyperdescent. It retains arbitrary ordinary bases, bad residue characteristics, derived quotients, non-Noetherian hypotheses where applicable, and arbitrary topological completion with the stated ideal basis. There is no restriction to good primes, computational algorithm, copied source passage, invented opaque carrier, sorry'd data or option override.

The seven layers have mathematical titles and dependencies. The changed preliminary order no longer follows CS24's independent section sequence. Later order is explained by the actual inputs to continuity, henselian invariance and supported descent.
