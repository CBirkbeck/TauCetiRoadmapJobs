1. **F01** (P3) the slope-one twisting lemma's last step is not what §3.7 supplies

   **Location.** `packages/FourierDeligneTransform/design/README.md` line 479, last sentence: "every character of that quotient occurring in `V` is the restriction of `ℒ_ψ(−a/π)` for some `a`, by the Artin–Schreier classification of breaks of ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §3.7"; `plan.json` target 5.1.2 (prerequisites `§3.1`, `§3.7`). The autoequivalence of §5.5 (line 547, target 5.5.1) rests on this step.

   **Claim.** The lemma (for `k` algebraically closed and `V ≠ 0` purely of slope one, some `a` makes `V ⊗ ℒ_ψ(a/π)` have a nonzero part of slope `< 1`) follows from §3.7.

   **Witness.** ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §3.7 at its head (line 2865) classifies characters of `G_K` of conductor `m`; the step needs that every character of the subgroup quotient `G_K^1/G_K^{1+}` is the restriction of a character of `G_K`, equivalently that the fixed field of `G^{1+}` is the compositum of the fixed field of `G^1` with the extensions `α^p − α = a/π`, `a ∈ k`. That is not a statement about characters of `G_K`: for a finite Galois `L/K` with `H^{1+} = 1` and `L_0 = L^{H^1}`, the extension `L/L_0` is elementary abelian with the single upper jump `ψ_{L_0/K}(1)` relative to `L_0`, and this jump is `p + 1`, not the ramification index `2p`, when `L_0/K` is a dihedral extension of order `2p` with upper jump `1/2`; so the classes `a/π` in `L_0/℘(L_0)` are not literally of the form "`a` times a uniformizer to the `−1`". The lemma is nevertheless true: the leading coefficient at that jump of the class of `a/π` is an injective additive polynomial in `a` over the algebraically closed `k`, hence a monomial, hence onto `k`; the classes of smaller jump generate extensions inside the fixed field of `G^1`. I computed this on `V = W ⊗ ℒ_ψ(a/π)` with `W` of slope `1/2` and rank `2` (then `a` itself works) and on the dihedral `L_0` above.

   **Repair.** Replace the last clause of the sentence by: "and every character of that quotient is the restriction of some `ℒ_ψ(a/π)`, because the fixed field of `G^{1+}` is the compositum of the fixed field of `G^1` with the Artin–Schreier extensions `α^p − α = a/π`, `a ∈ k`: for finite Galois `L/K` with `H^{1+} = 1` and `L_0 = L^{H^1}`, `L/L_0` is elementary abelian with the single upper jump `ψ_{L_0/K}(1)`, prime to `p`; the leading coefficients at that jump of the classes of `a/π` in `L_0/℘(L_0)` form the image of an injective additive polynomial in `a` over the algebraically closed `k`, hence all of `k`; the classes of smaller jump lie in the fixed field of `G^1` (ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §3.1 for the Herbrand functions and the upper numbering of subgroups and quotients, §3.7 for the conductor of `α^p − α = a/π`)". In `plan.json` 5.1.2, add the same route sentence to the statement or a `route` field; prerequisites unchanged. Tested: on the two instances above the repaired route produces the required `a`.
