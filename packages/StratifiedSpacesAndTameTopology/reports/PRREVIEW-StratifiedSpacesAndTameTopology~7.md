## R02 (P3) — Two hypotheses of the "Not targets either" sentence are dropped

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/StratifiedSpacesAndTameTopology/README.md` 70–71, "definably compact (every definable curve in it has a limit in it)", and 75–76, "compact definable `Cᵖ` manifolds with corners have definable triangulations (Commelin–Huber, appendix to Huber 2022, Proposition A.1)".

Claim: the gloss of definable compactness and the triangulation statement are the sources' statements.

Witness: curve-compactness is convergence at both ends of the curve's interval: andujar-guerrero2025 line 642 defines a curve `γ : (a, b) → X` to be completable when it converges as `t → a` and as `t → b`, and line 667 defines curve-compactness as every definable curve being completable; Theorem A (7) at line 79 is this notion, and Remark 5.9 at lines 789–791 recalls Peterzil–Steinhorn Theorem 2.1 in this form. "Has a limit" reads as one limit. Proposition A.1 (huber2022 lines 959–962) is stated for `0 ≤ p < ∞` and, per lines 954–956, in a fixed o-minimal structure on the real field with parameters in a subfield; the README's sentence opens with "in an o-minimal structure" over a general `Rⁿ` and puts no bound on `p`. For `p = ∞` the source claims nothing (a `C^∞` definable manifold is `C^p` for every finite `p`, so a triangulation `C^p` on open faces exists for each finite `p`, but no `C^∞` one is asserted).

Repair: line 71, "(every definable curve in it has a limit in it)" → "(every definable curve `(a, b) → X` has limits in `X` at both ends)"; lines 75–76, "compact definable `Cᵖ` manifolds with corners have definable triangulations" → "for finite `p` and an o-minimal expansion of the real field, compact definable `Cᵖ` manifolds with corners have definable triangulations, `Cᵖ` on open faces". Both agree with the locators as quoted.
