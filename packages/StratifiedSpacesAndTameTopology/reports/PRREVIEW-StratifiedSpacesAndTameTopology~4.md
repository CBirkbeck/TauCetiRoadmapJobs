## R01 (P2) — The normalisation of 5.1 does not support 5.5(a), 7.3 and 7.4: tubes may have components away from the stratum, and tubes of incomparable strata may meet

Location: `README.md` lines 688 ("They are *normalised* when `T_p ∩ X_q ≠ ∅` implies `p ≤ q`"), 775–781 (5.5(a)–(c) "for normalised control data"), 965–970 (7.3), 983–984 (7.4), 1039–1044 (7.7), 1100–1103 (8.3).

Claim: 5.1 admits any open `T_p ⊇ X_p` with a continuous retraction and a continuous `ρ_p` vanishing exactly on `X_p`; a tube may therefore have a connected component disjoint from `X_p`, on which `ρ_p` is arbitrary, and `T_p(ε) = {ρ_p < ε ∘ π_p}` then has frontier points that lie on no level set `{ρ_p = ε ∘ π_p}`. Mather's abstract stratified sets have the same freedom ((A6)–(A9), mather2012 lines 821–842) and he removes it by passing to an equivalent structure satisfying (A10) `T_{X,Y} ≠ ∅ ⇒ X < Y` and (A11) `T_X ∩ T_Y ≠ ∅ ⇒ X, Y comparable` (lines 850–856); 5.1 takes only (A10). The unfolding of 7.3 and the local conical structure of 5.5(a) also need the sublevel sets `{ρ_p ≤ ε ∘ π_p}` to be closed in `X` over compact pieces of `X_p` (proper tubes), which Mather's Whitney control data have (his tubes are `|T|_ε`, images of `ε`-disc bundles, lines 697–700) and 5.1's data need not.

Witness (two strata). `X = ℝ²`, `P = {i < j}`, `X_i` the `x`-axis, `X_j = {y ≠ 0}`: a smooth stratified space with the frontier condition. `T_i = {|y| < 1} ∪ {2 < y < 3}`, `π_i(x, y) = (x, 0)`, `ρ_i = y²` on the first piece and `(y − 2)/(3 − y)` on the second; `T_j = X_j`, `π_j = id`, `ρ_j = 0`. Every condition of 5.1 holds: `T_i` is open and contains `X_i`, `π_i` is a continuous retraction, `ρ_i` is continuous with `ρ_i⁻¹(0) = X_i` (the second piece has `ρ_i > 0`), `(π_i, ρ_i)` is a submersion on `T_i ∩ X_j` (Jacobian `2y ≠ 0` on the first piece, `1/(3 − y)² > 0` on the second), the control conditions for `i < j` are trivial with `π_j = id`, and the data are normalised (`T_i` meets only `X_i` and `X_j`). Values: for any continuous `ε : X_i → (0, ∞)`, `Σ_j = ℝ² ∖ T_i(ε) = {y² ≥ ε(x), |y| < 1} ∪ {|y| ≥ 1} ∖ {2 < y < (2 + 3ε(x))/(1 + ε(x))}`, so the line `{y = 2}` lies in `Σ_j` and `Σ_j` is `{y ≤ 2}` near it: a boundary hypersurface of `Σ_j` contained in no `∂_iΣ_j = Σ_j ∩ {ρ_i = ε ∘ π_i}` (its points are not in `T_i`), contradicting 7.1's "every boundary hypersurface lies in exactly one `∂_iΣ_j`"; and the stratum `j` of `glue(Σ)`, `Σ_j ∖ ∂_iΣ_j`, has three components (`y < −√ε`, `√ε < y ≤ 2`, `y > (2 + 3ε)/(1 + ε)`) against two for `X_j`, so 7.4's stratum-preserving homeomorphism does not exist. For 5.5(a): `L_i = ρ_i⁻¹(ε) ∩ π_i⁻¹(x)` is three points for `ε < 1` and one point for `ε ≥ 1`, while a neighbourhood of `(x, 0)` is stratum-preservingly `ℝ × C(two points)`; `ℝ × C(L_i)` has three or one local components of the open stratum, never two.

Witness for (A11): `X = ℝ²` with `X_h = {0}`, `X_i` and `X_{i'}` the two punctured axes, `X_j = {xy ≠ 0}`, `h < i, i' < j`, `i ∥ i'`; `T_h` the unit disc, `T_i = {x ≠ 0, |y| < 1} ∪ {x ≠ 0, 2 < y < 3}` with `π_i = (sign(x)·r, 0)`, `ρ_i = arcsin(y/r)²` on the first piece and `(y − 2)/(3 − y)` on the second; `T_{i'} = {|x| < |y|}`, `π_{i'} = (0, sign(y)·r)`, `ρ_{i'} = arcsin(x/r)²`. Normalised in 5.1's sense (`T_i ∩ X_{i'} = ∅` since `x ≠ 0`; `T_{i'} ∩ X_i = ∅`), with `T_i ∩ T_{i'} ⊇ {0 < |x| < y, 2 < y < 3} ≠ ∅`. For every `ε_i, ε_{i'}`, the far level curve `{(y − 2)/(3 − y) = ε_i(π_i)}` (nonempty on each fibre, since `ρ_i` takes all values in `(0, ∞)` there) runs from inside `T_{i'}(ε_{i'})` near the `y`-axis to outside `T_{i'}` at `|x| ≥ y`, so either it crosses `{ρ_{i'} = ε_{i'} ∘ π_{i'}}` at a point of `∂_iΣ_j ∩ ∂_{i'}Σ_j` (forbidden by 7.1 for incomparable `i, i'`) or `T_{i'}(ε_{i'}) = T_{i'}` on that range and `{|x| = y}` is a boundary hypersurface of `Σ_j` in no `∂_·Σ_j`.
