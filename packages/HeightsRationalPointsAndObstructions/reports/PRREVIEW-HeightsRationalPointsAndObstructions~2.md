## R01 (P2) — Néron's theorem is stated in characteristic zero only, below its source and below this roadmap's own Lang–Néron

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/HeightsRationalPointsAndObstructions/Suggested.lean` lines 70–73, `theorem AbelianVariety.finitelyGeneratedField_points_fg {K : Type u} [Field K] [CharZero K] [Algebra.EssFiniteType ℚ K] (A : …) : AddGroup.FG (AbelianVariety.Points A K)`; its docstring lines 65–69 and the module docstring lines 12–13 ("Néron's theorem over finitely generated fields of characteristic zero"); `README.md` line 2627, "for a field E of characteristic zero finitely generated as a field extension of ℚ".

Claim: the headline is Néron's theorem for fields finitely generated over ℚ.

Witness: the cited source states it for every field finitely generated over its prime field, in every characteristic (conrad-kktrace line 1090, Corollary 7.2, printed p. 23), and §1.10 of this README (`fg_points_mod_trace`, regular finitely generated `K/k`, Conrad Theorem 7.1 line 1079) carries no characteristic restriction either. The statement holds without `CharZero`: for `K = 𝔽_p(t)` and any abelian variety `A/K`, the constant field `k` (the algebraic closure of `𝔽_p` in `K`) is finite, `K/k` is regular, §1.10 makes `A(K)/Tr(k)` finitely generated and `Tr(k) = Tr_{K/k}(A)(k)` is a finite group, so `A(K)` is finitely generated; this is exactly Conrad's proof of Corollary 7.2. The general form is statable at the pin: Mathlib has `Field.FG L` ("finitely generated over its prime subfield", `Mathlib/Algebra/Field/Subfield/Basic.lean` line 437, with `Field.fg_iff_essFiniteType : Field.FG F ↔ Algebra.EssFiniteType (⊥ : Subfield F) F` at `Mathlib/FieldTheory/IntermediateField/Adjoin/Algebra.lean` line 128), and on a scratch copy of the file the declaration `theorem … {K : Type u} [Field K] [Field.FG K] (A : TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianVariety K) : AddGroup.FG (AbelianVariety.Points A K)` elaborates with `sorry` as its only warning (so does the variant `[Algebra.EssFiniteType ℤ K]`). Restricting a headline to characteristic zero when the source, the roadmap's own supplier target and the pinned carriers all admit the general statement is the "good characteristic only" shortcut the principles exclude.

Repair (tested on the instance above): replace `[CharZero K] [Algebra.EssFiniteType ℚ K]` by `[Field.FG K]` and say in the docstring that `Field.FG` is finite generation over the prime subfield, in characteristic zero equivalent to `Algebra.EssFiniteType ℚ K` by `Field.fg_iff_essFiniteType` and `IntermediateField.fg_top_iff`; drop "of characteristic zero" from the module docstring (lines 12–13; line 29 can keep "over finitely generated fields").
