## R01 (P3) — A new Check calls `A(ℚ̄)` a torsion group

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/HeightsRationalPointsAndObstructions/README.md` line 2636, "A(E) contains the infinite torsion group A(ℚ̄) or A(𝔽̄_p), so finite generation of E cannot be weakened to algebraicity of the constant field".

Claim: for `E = ℚ̄` the group `A(E) = A(ℚ̄)` is an infinite torsion group.

Witness: `A(ℚ̄)` is divisible and contains points of infinite order (any non-torsion point of `A` over a number field, say `(3,5)` on `y² = x³ − 2`, whose double has `x = 129/100`), so it is not a torsion group; its torsion subgroup `A(ℚ̄)_tors` is infinite for `dim A ≥ 1` (`A[n](ℚ̄)` has `n^{2 dim A}` elements). For `dim A = 0` both `A(ℚ̄)` and `A(𝔽̄_p)` are trivial and finitely generated, so the Check needs `A` of positive dimension. The conclusion of the Check (the hypothesis `Field.FG E` fails for `E = ℚ̄` and `E = 𝔽̄_p(t)`, and `A(E)` is not finitely generated there) is correct: a nonzero divisible group is not finitely generated, and `A₀(𝔽̄_p)` is an infinite torsion group.

Repair: for `A` of positive dimension, say that `A(ℚ̄)` contains the infinite torsion subgroup `A(ℚ̄)_tors` (or that it is nonzero and divisible), and that `A(𝔽̄_p(t)) ⊇ A₀(𝔽̄_p)` is an infinite torsion group; the rest of the bullet stands.
