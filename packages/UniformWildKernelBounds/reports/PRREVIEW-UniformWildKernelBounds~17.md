## C01 — Five sections carry the origin "this roadmap's own formulation" for statements the formal proof has, which the family index defines that label to exclude

A reader who takes the family's taxonomy at its word will conclude that 2.3, 3.1, 3.17, 3.23 and 5.2 are not statements of [FormalProof]. The pull request's description, summing the labels over the family, then counts these five under the wrong heading. The family index (its section "Origin and method of each statement", line 194) reserves `this roadmap's own formulation:` for "a statement that is neither published nor a statement of the formal proof", and the description glosses it the same way. This README's Labels paragraph (lines 152–154) instead lets a statement that [FormalProof] has carry that label. For 5.2 the conflict is plain: its locator `UpperTheory.V.X.propZ` (`…/LevelCohomology/PropZ.lean`, line 11) proves `HV.PropZ`, which is 5.2 exactly (`5 ≤ l`, `2 ≤ e`, `e` even, `l − 1 ∣ 2e`, the trivial component vanishes).

The smallest repair would be one of two. Either relabel the five with the family's forms (5.2, say, as `particular to this proof:`, naming Kurihara's theorem and ArithmeticKTheory §7.5 as the nearest published results, which its origin line already does). Or widen the family index's definition to cover a formulation of this roadmap's own of a statement the proof has in another form, and say so in the description.

## Questions for the author

- 1.1's origin (line 300) calls the statement published for odd `ℓ` at every `e ≠ 0`. Kolster and Movahhedi (p. 60, §3) state the isomorphism of twisted coinvariants with the étale wild kernel only for `i ≥ 2`, that is `e ≥ 1`, and cite Schneider §6, Lemma 1 for it. Does Schneider's lemma also cover `e ≤ −1`? If not, the label would read "published for odd `ℓ` and `e ≥ 1`". What stopped me: no legal copy of Schneider (1979) was found.
