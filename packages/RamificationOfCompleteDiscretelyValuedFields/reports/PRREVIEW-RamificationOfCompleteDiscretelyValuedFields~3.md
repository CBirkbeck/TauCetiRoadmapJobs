## R01 (P2) — §3a.2 states Herbrand's theorem, tower transitivity and the intersection formula without the separable-residue hypothesis; an inseparable-residue tower refutes all three

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/RamificationOfCompleteDiscretelyValuedFields/README.md` lines 311–318, "In the setting of 3a.1, with `S = B` … prove: Herbrand's theorem: for `H` normal in `G`, `(G/H)^v = G^v H/H` …; tower transitivity … `φ_(M/K) = φ_(L/K) ∘ φ_(M/L)` …; `Gal(M/L) ∩ Gal(M/K)^v = Gal(M/L)^(ψ_(L/K)(v))`"; also the proof route, lines 342–344, "the lower index of a class `s ∈ G/H` is `(1/e(L/K′)) Σ_(σ ∈ s) lowerIndex B σ`, computed at generators of `B` over `A` and of the integer ring of `K′` over `A`"; and the Scope bullet, lines 40–43.

Claim: the three statements hold in the setting of 3a.1, which by the Conventions (line 88, "assumed separable wherever a statement says so") and by §3a.1's own inseparable Checks (lines 289–301) includes inseparable residue extensions. None of the three bullets says "separable".

Witness (`p = 2`; the same construction works for every `p`): `K = F_2(s)((t))`, `M = K(y, w)` with `y² − y = s·t^(−2)` and `w² − w = t^(−1)`; `G = C_2 × C_2` (the two Artin–Schreier classes are independent: `c·t^(−1) + d·s·t^(−2) = a² − a` forces, for `d ≠ 0`, `v(a) = −1` and `ds` a square in `F_2(s)`, and for `d = 0`, a class of odd valuation). Let `L₁ = K(y)`, `L₂ = K(w)`, `σ` the generator of `Gal(M/L₂)` (`y ↦ y + 1`), `τ` the generator of `Gal(M/L₁)` (`w ↦ w + 1`).
- `L₂/K`: totally ramified, `π₀ = 1/w` a uniformizer with Eisenstein polynomial `X² + tX − t`, `O_(L₂) = A[π₀]`.
- `M/L₂`: with `z = ty`, `z² − t z = s`; `O_(L₂)[z]/(π₀) = F_2(s)[Z]/(Z² − s)` is a field of degree `2 = [M : L₂]`, so `O_M = O_(L₂)[z] = A[π₀, z]`, `e(M/L₂) = 1`, `f(M/L₂) = 2`, `v_M(t) = 2`.

Hence `G_0 = G_1 = G`, `G_2 = 1`, `φ_(M/K)(u) = u` on `[0, 1]`, and `G^v = G` for every `v ≤ 1`.
- The quotient `G/H` for `H = Gal(M/L₁)`, that is `Gal(L₁/K)`: `L₁/K` is the inseparable-residue extension of the §3a.1 Check (line 289), `B₁ = A[z]`, `σ̄z = z + t`, `e(L₁/K) = 1`, `i(σ̄) = v_(L₁)(t) = 1`; so `(G/H)_0 = G/H`, `(G/H)_1 = 1`, `φ_(L₁/K)(u) = u/2` for `u ≥ 0`, and `(G/H)^(1/2) = (G/H)_1 = 1`.
