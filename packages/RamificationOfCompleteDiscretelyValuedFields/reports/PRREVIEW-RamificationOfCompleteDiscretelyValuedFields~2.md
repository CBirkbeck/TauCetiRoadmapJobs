## R01 (P2) — §3a.1 states monogenicity without the separable-residue hypothesis

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/RamificationOfCompleteDiscretelyValuedFields/README.md` line 210, "There is `x ∈ B` with `Algebra.adjoin A {x} = ⊤`, and for every such `x` …".

Claim: in the setting of the Conventions (A complete DVR, L/K finite Galois, B the integral closure), B is monogenic over A. No separability of the residue extension is asked at that point; it enters the paragraph only at line 215 ("For separable residue extension and a uniformizer …") and in the last sentence (line 226), which restates monogenicity correctly with the hypothesis and points at the pinned `TauCeti.IsDiscreteValuationRing.exists_adjoin_eq_top`, whose signature carries `[Algebra.IsSeparable (ResidueField R) (ResidueField S)]` (`TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Monogenic.lean:70`).

Witness: `K = F_p(s, u)((t))`, `L = K(y₁, y₂)` with `y₁^p − y₁ = s·t^(−p)`, `y₂^p − y₂ = u·t^(−p)`. `L/K` is Galois with group `C_p × C_p` (compositum of two Artin–Schreier extensions). With `z_i = t·y_i`, `z₁^p − t^(p−1) z₁ = s` and `z₂^p − t^(p−1) z₂ = u`, so the residue field of `B` is `F_p(s, u)(s^(1/p), u^(1/p))`, of degree `p²` and not generated by one element over `F_p(s, u)`; hence `e = 1`, `f = p²`, and no `x ∈ B` has `A[x] = B`, since `B/𝔪_B` would then be generated by one element. The sentence is false exactly where the §3a.1 Check at line 268 already shows the graded statements failing (a one-generator version of that Check).

Repair: at line 210 write "For separable residue extension there is `x ∈ B` with `Algebra.adjoin A {x} = ⊤`; for every `x` with `Algebra.adjoin A {x} = ⊤` and every `σ ∈ G`, `lowerIndex B σ = v_L(σx − x)`." The lower-index formula needs only the generator, not separability (`lowerIndex_eq_addVal_of_adjoin_singleton_eq_top`, `TauCeti/RingTheory/LocalRing/RamificationGroup.lean:440`). Tested on the witness: with the clause added the sentence no longer applies to it, and on `ℚ_2(ζ_8)/ℚ_2` (finite, hence separable, residue field) it still gives `x = ζ_8` with `i_G(σ) = v_L(σζ_8 − ζ_8)`.

## R03 (P3) — Unused hypotheses in the two headline signatures

Location: `Suggested.lean` lines 44–45 and 63–64, `[IsAdicComplete (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A) A]`; lines 46–47 `[Module.Finite A B] [FaithfulSMul A B] [IsLocalHom (algebraMap A B)]`; line 66 `[IsLocalHom (algebraMap A B)]`.

Claim: completeness of `A` is not needed for either statement, and the three structural instances of the first theorem follow from the others. Witness for completeness: `A = ℤ_((p))` (not complete), `B = ℤ[ζ_p]_((1−ζ_p))`, `G = (ℤ/p)ˣ` acting by `ζ_p ↦ ζ_p^a`: `B` is a DVR, `B^G = B ∩ ℚ = A`, `differentIdeal A B = (1 − ζ_p)^(p−2)` and `Σ_(i≥0)(#G_i − 1) = (p − 1) − 1 = p − 2`, so the first theorem holds; its Tau Ceti inputs (`exists_adjoin_eq_top`, `sum_addVal_smul_sub_eq_finsum_card_ramificationGroup_sub_one`) are completeness-free, and the route of §3a.1 uses completeness only to know that the integral closure is a DVR, which the signature assumes of `B`. For the second theorem: `Algebra.IsInvariant A B G` with `G` finite makes `B` integral over the image of `A`; the kernel of `A → B` is `0` or `𝔪_A`, and `𝔪_A` would make `B` a domain integral over a field, hence a field, contradicting `IsDiscreteValuationRing B`; so `A → B` is injective, `B` is the integral closure of `A` in `Frac B` (a DVR is maximal among proper subrings of its fraction field containing it), hence finite over `A`, and `IsLocalHom` follows from integrality. The two signatures are therefore consistent, but the first carries three instances the second does not.

Repair (take or leave): either drop `IsAdicComplete` from both and `Module.Finite A B`, `FaithfulSMul A B`, `IsLocalHom` from the first, or keep them for readability and say in the docstrings that they are the Conventions' standing hypotheses and are not needed by the statement. Nothing else changes; both forms elaborate.
