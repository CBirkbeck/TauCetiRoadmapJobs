## R01 (P1) — Rank-one modules are compatible, not a stability counterexample

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/PadicLocalLanglandsForGL2Qp/README.md:1064`: “For `D` of rank 1 the module `D^♮ ⊠ ℙ¹` is not `G`-stable in general”.

**Claim:** This Check gives a false reason for restricting the rank-two correspondence. For a rational rank-one étale module the natural submodule is stable for **every** unitary central character. The general quotient construction gives principal series in rank one. What distinguishes the rank-two theorem is not failure of rank-one stability.

**Exact repair:** Replace this negative Check by the rank-one principal-series comparison, retaining the rank-two scope of `Π(V)` and its reconstruction theorem. If a failure of compatibility is wanted, cite published Theorem III.15(ii), CD14 lines 1061–1062: an absolutely irreducible étale module of rank at least three has no compatible unitary character. The replacement passes the original test object `E(1)`: stability is true and the quotient above is defined. In particular it does not assert `D(Π(D))=D∨(1)` in rank one. No Lean declaration needs adding.
