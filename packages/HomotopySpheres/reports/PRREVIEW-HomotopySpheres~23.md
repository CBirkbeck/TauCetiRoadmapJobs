## R03 (P3) — `nonempty_diffeomorph_sphere_six` writes out the body of Mathlib's `NonemptyDiffeomorphSphere` instead of using it

**Location.** `Suggested.lean`:234–238; README:547–550, "includes Hausdorffness, but not compactness or second countability. The direct recognition theorem in Layer 10 carries all three hypotheses explicitly"; README:310–313.

**Claim.** Mathlib defines the smooth Poincaré statement as the predicate `ContinuousMap.HomotopyEquiv.NonemptyDiffeomorphSphere M n` (`Mathlib/Geometry/Manifold/PoincareConjecture.lean`:41, both pins). The unoriented headline writes out its body at n = 6 instead of citing it. The README implies that the predicate lacks needed hypotheses. In dimension six it does not: for a Hausdorff 6-manifold, M ≃ₕ S⁶ forces compactness, since a connected noncompact n-manifold has Hₙ = 0 (Hatcher, Proposition 3.29, p.239), and a compact locally Euclidean space is second countable.

**Witness.** In a scratch copy, `intro _ _ h; exact nonempty_diffeomorph_sphere_six M h` proves `NonemptyDiffeomorphSphere M 6` for compact, second-countable `[T2Space M]`. The converse also elaborates. `hatcher_at` line 10370.

**Repair.** State the corollary through the predicate, keeping the binders the route uses:

```lean
theorem nonempty_diffeomorph_sphere_six (M : Type u) [TopologicalSpace M] [T2Space M]
    [SecondCountableTopology M] [CompactSpace M] :
    ContinuousMap.HomotopyEquiv.NonemptyDiffeomorphSphere M 6 := by
  sorry
```

Replace README:547–550 with: "Layer 10's unoriented corollary is Mathlib's `ContinuousMap.HomotopyEquiv.NonemptyDiffeomorphSphere` at n = 6 for compact second-countable M; both binders follow from the homotopy equivalence (Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, Proposition 3.29, p.239) and are kept because the route of 10.1–10.3 uses them." Adjust :310–313 so that it no longer describes Mathlib's predicate as an unrestricted proposition.
