## Source issues

- **Error** in DP at Lemma 1.13, proof, printed p. 8 (arXiv v2): the source says a subgroup generated by a scalar and the antidiagonal involution is never normal in the image because no diagonal matrix with distinct entries normalises it; it should be that `diag(1,−1)` normalises `⟨−I, W⟩`, so that abelian normal subgroup must be diagonalised directly; affects the proof.
- **Misprint** in DP at Theorem 1.7, printed p. 4–5 (arXiv v2): the source says `det ρ = ψχ₃^{k−1}` with no condition on `ψ`; it should be `ψ` of finite order, as in the Skinner–Wiles theorem it quotes; affects a stated result.
