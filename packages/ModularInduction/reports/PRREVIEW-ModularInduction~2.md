## C01 — The scope section lists ModularCharactersAndBlocks and ClassFieldTheory under "Owned elsewhere and consumed here", but both of them consume this roadmap

A reader takes the two bullets on lines 736–739 as suppliers, which would put this roadmap in two cycles. In fact ModularCharactersAndBlocks cites this roadmap's Layers 0, 1 and 3 (its README on main, lines 234, 282, 1455 and its "From TauCetiRoadmap.RepresentationTheory.ModularInduction" contract at line 1571), and this README lists ClassFieldTheory's local Euler characteristic as a downstream consumer (line 687). No layer here uses either roadmap. Put the two bullets under their own lead-in, for example "Excluded here, owned by", or fold them into the exclusions sentence of "This roadmap owns" (line 705).

## C02 — In §2.1, the "in particular" sentence about irreducible representations of a group drops the finite dimensionality that the cited declaration assumes

Lines 368–370 say, for a group, that an irreducible representation in which every element acts with ℓ-power order is the trivial line. They cite `Representation.IsIrreducible.eq_trivial_of_forall_pow_expChar_pow_eq_one`, which at the pin takes `[FiniteDimensional k V]` from its section variables (`TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Unipotent/PowerOrder.lean`, lines 120–121, the theorem at line 138). Finite dimensionality is automatic for a finite group, but §2.1 is stated for arbitrary monoids and groups. Adding "finite-dimensional" fixes it, as the first sentence of §2.1 already does.

## C03 — Milne's Lemma 2.10 is cited in §4.6 for the multiplier |G| (line 620), but it states only the rational form, over 𝔽_p

Milne, *Arithmetic Duality Theorems*, I, Lemma 2.10, p. 32 (MilneADT line 1266), says that `R_{𝔽_p}(G) ⊗ ℚ` is generated by the images of induction from cyclic subgroups of order prime to p. It gives no multiplier and no field other than 𝔽_p, so it supports §4.7 at k = 𝔽_ℓ and not the |G| of §4.6. Nothing is left unsupported, because the pinned `TauCeti.modularArtin_natCard_nsmul_mem_indCyclicCoprimeK0` (`TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Artin/Spanning.lean`, line 76) proves §4.6; the locator just claims more than the lemma says. One option is to keep Milne in §4.7 and write "Milne, I, Lemma 2.10, for the rational form over 𝔽_p" in §4.6; the other is to drop the Milne citation from §4.6.

## C04 — InductionRestriction is cited as "Layers 0 and 6" (line 731) for finite-dimensional induction and for permutation modules as induced modules, which are in its Layer 2

In InductionRestriction on main, `TauCeti.indFDRep` and `TauCeti.indTrivialIso` belong to Layer 2 (the "Layer2 finite induction" row of its Tau Ceti table, line 179, and line 317). They are in Layer 2 at the head of its open revision as well (its rows "Layer 2: finite-dimensional induction and the left-coset model" and "Layer 2: permutation characters and the double-coset pairing"). The citation should read "Layers 0, 2 and 6".

## C05 — The Layer 4 introduction puts the projection-formula step in §4.5, but the layer carries it out in §4.4

Lines 539–541 say that Artin's identity is "spread to all classes by the projection formula (§4.5)". The spreading is the second half of §4.4 (lines 590–592, `TauCeti.natCard_nsmul_eq_sum_artinCoeff_indK0_resK0`); §4.5 only discards the non-cyclic terms. Suggested wording: "spread to all classes by the projection formula (§4.4), which places |G|·x among the classes induced from cyclic subgroups (§4.5)".

## C06 — The description does not say that CharacterTheory is no longer a supplier

The README on main named CharacterTheory as the supplier of the Green ring `TauCeti.repRing` (its "Dependencies and boundaries" section, line 66 of that file). The head names no CharacterTheory section and attributes the Green ring to Tau Ceti (line 734). The description lists the other supplier changes, so a reader would miss this one. Suggested addition to "What I found in the pinned library": "CharacterTheory is no longer named as a supplier; the Green ring is cited from Tau Ceti directly."

## C07 — "ClassFieldTheory §5.1" (line 687) resolves, by ClassFieldTheory's own correspondence table, to its worked example W.1 and not to the local Euler characteristic

A reader who follows the citation lands on the wrong passage. ClassFieldTheory on main says that a bare former section number means its row in the table "Section and name correspondence" (its README, lines 253–258), where §5.1 is "Worked example W.1, the trivial layer" (line 272), and that a citation of a layer subsection names its layer. The Euler characteristic formula for a finite extension of ℚ_p is the bullet "The Euler characteristic" of Layer 5, subsection 5.1 (lines 1056 and 1103). Write "ClassFieldTheory Layer 5, §5.1".

## N01 — The target name `modularArtin_natCard_nsmul_mem_indCyclic` (§4.5; `Suggested.lean`, line 40) uses the modular theorem's prefix for a statement over every field

Someone searching for Artin's theorem in characteristic zero could miss it. If you want to rename it, `natCard_nsmul_mem_indCyclicK0` would follow the pinned naming; keeping the current name is also fine.
