## C01 — The scope section lists ModularCharactersAndBlocks and ClassFieldTheory under "Owned elsewhere and consumed here", but both of them consume this roadmap

A reader takes the two bullets on lines 736–739 as suppliers, which would put this roadmap in two cycles. In fact ModularCharactersAndBlocks cites this roadmap's Layers 0, 1 and 3 (its README on main, line 1455, and §§1.2, 2.1, 2.4), and this README lists ClassFieldTheory's local Euler characteristic as a downstream consumer (line 687). No layer here uses either roadmap. Put the two bullets under their own lead-in, for example "Excluded here, owned by", or fold them into the exclusions sentence of "This roadmap owns" (line 705).

## C02 — In §2.1, the "in particular" sentence about irreducible representations of a group drops the finite dimensionality that the cited declaration assumes

Lines 368–370 say, for any group, that an irreducible representation in which every element acts with ℓ-power order is the trivial line. They cite `Representation.IsIrreducible.eq_trivial_of_forall_pow_expChar_pow_eq_one`, which at the pin takes `[FiniteDimensional k V]` from its section variables (`TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Unipotent/PowerOrder.lean`, line 121). Finite dimensionality is automatic for a finite group, but the sentence is about an arbitrary group. Adding "finite-dimensional" fixes it, as the first sentence of §2.1 already does.

## C03 — Milne's Lemma 2.10 is cited in §4.6 for the multiplier |G| (line 620), but it states only the rational form, over 𝔽_p

Milne, *Arithmetic Duality Theorems*, I, Lemma 2.10, p. 32 (MilneADT line 1266), says that `R_{𝔽_p}(G) ⊗ ℚ` is generated by classes induced from cyclic subgroups of order prime to p. It gives no multiplier, so it supports §4.7 at k = 𝔽_ℓ and not the |G| of §4.6. Nothing is left unsupported, because the pinned `TauCeti.modularArtin_natCard_nsmul_mem_indCyclicCoprimeK0` proves §4.6; the locator just claims more than the lemma says. One option is to keep Milne in §4.7 and write "Milne, I, Lemma 2.10, for the rational form" in §4.6. The other is to drop the Milne citation from §4.6.

## C04 — InductionRestriction is cited as "Layers 0 and 6" (line 731) for finite-dimensional induction and for permutation modules as induced modules, which are in its Layer 2

In InductionRestriction on main, `TauCeti.indFDRep` and `TauCeti.indTrivialIso` belong to Layer 2 (the Layer 2 heading and the "Layer2 finite induction" row of its Tau Ceti table). They are also in Layer 2 at the head of its open revision ("finite-dimensional induction and the left-coset model" and "permutation characters"). The citation should read "Layers 0, 2 and 6".

## C05 — The Layer 4 introduction puts the projection-formula step in §4.5, but the layer carries it out in §4.4

Lines 539–541 say that Artin's identity is "spread to all classes by the projection formula (§4.5)". The spreading is the second half of §4.4 (lines 590–592, `TauCeti.natCard_nsmul_eq_sum_artinCoeff_indK0_resK0`); §4.5 only discards the non-cyclic terms. Suggested wording: "spread to all classes by the projection formula (§4.4), which places |G|·x among the classes induced from cyclic subgroups (§4.5)".

## C06 — The description does not say that CharacterTheory is no longer a supplier

The README on main named CharacterTheory as the supplier of the Green ring `TauCeti.repRing` (in its "Dependencies and boundaries" section). The head names no CharacterTheory section and attributes the Green ring to Tau Ceti (line 734). The description lists the other supplier changes, so a reader would miss this one. Suggested addition to "What I found in the pinned library": "CharacterTheory is no longer named as a supplier; the Green ring is cited from Tau Ceti directly."

## N01 — The target name `modularArtin_natCard_nsmul_mem_indCyclic` (§4.5; `Suggested.lean`, line 40) uses the modular theorem's prefix for a statement over every field

Someone searching for Artin's theorem in characteristic zero could miss it. If you want to rename it, `natCard_nsmul_mem_indCyclicK0` would follow the pinned naming; keeping the current name is also fine.
