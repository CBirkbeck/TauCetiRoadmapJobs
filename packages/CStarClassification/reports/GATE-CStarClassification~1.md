## H01 (CBirkbeck) — applied

CL7 already separates the two notions at head `299afef` (README CL7, the paragraphs beginning "A *-homomorphism φ:A→B is full" and "Use OperatorKTheory K9's actual asymptotic morphisms"). Pointwise fullness (every nonzero positive a has full image) and image-fullness (φ(A) generates B as a closed ideal) are now distinct. The full-tail-projection test for a source with a full projection p defines image-fullness and recovers it for constant morphisms, because the ideals generated by φ(p) and φ(A) agree. Equivalence with pointwise fullness is claimed only for simple sources. The text includes the negative control A=ℂ⊕ℂ, B=ℂ, p=(1,1), φ(z,w)=z: φ(p)=1 is full, but φ(0,1)=0. It also includes the positive control (a nonzero map from a nonzero simple source to a simple target). The 2026-10-21 review confirms this resolution. No further change.

## H02 (CBirkbeck) — applied

CL5 lifting lemma: "for separable unital D,E and a nuclear closed ideal J⊲E" → "for separable unital D,E with D≠0 and a nuclear closed ideal J⊲E". The README now also says that, for a unital embedding φ:D→E/J, D≠0 is equivalent to J≠E (φ(1)=1 is nonzero exactly when E/J≠0). It records D=0, E=J=ℂ as a negative control: every other hypothesis holds, but no linear map 0→ℂ sends 1=0 to 1. I checked the other unital lifting and embedding statements in CL5 at zero for the same defect. These are the comparison maps η_n:B₁→B₂, the (O₂)∞→O₂ correction, the crossed-product lift into C(𝕋)⊗ℓ∞(E), and the dilation of u.c.p. maps M_n→B. In each one a zero domain forces a zero target (or makes the hypothesis impossible), so the zero map is a valid unital witness. None of them needed a change.
