## R01 (P3) — Proposition 1.2 quantifies over the carrier `ChiefSeries G`, which admits series that do not run from `⊥` to `⊤`

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/OddOrderTheorem/README.md` lines 393–395: "`F(G)` is the intersection of the centralizers `C_G(s_{i+1} ⧸ s_i)` of the factors of a chief series `s` of `G` (FiniteGroupInvariants §0.2, `ChiefSeries`) (Proposition 1.2)".

Claim: for every `s : ChiefSeries G` of a solvable `G`, `F(G)` is the intersection of the centralizers of the factors of `s`.

Witness: FiniteGroupInvariants, at the head of its pull request, defines `ChiefSeries G` as `CompositionSeries (NormalSubgroup G)` (its `Suggested.lean` line 66) with no endpoint condition, and its own `chiefFactors_eq` (line 87) carries the hypothesis `s.head = ⊥ ∧ s.last = ⊤`; its README §0.2 says "two chief series of a finite group from `⊥` to `⊤`". For `G = S₄` the series `V₄ < A₄ < S₄` is a member of `ChiefSeries S₄` (each step is maximal among normal subgroups), its factor centralizers are `A₄` and `S₄`, and their intersection is `A₄ ≠ V₄ = F(S₄)`. The §1.2 Check uses the full series `1 < V₄ < A₄ < S₄` (centralizers `V₄`, `A₄`, `S₄`, meet `V₄`), which is the intended statement.

Repair: write "of the factors of a chief series `s` of `G` from `⊥` to `⊤` (FiniteGroupInvariants §0.2, `ChiefSeries`, with the endpoint hypothesis of its `chiefFactors_eq`) (Proposition 1.2)". Tested: with the endpoints required, the `S₄` witness above is excluded and the §1.2 Check is the instance `1 < V₄ < A₄ < S₄`, meet `V₄ = F(S₄)`.
