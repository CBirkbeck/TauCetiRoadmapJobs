## R01 (P3) — §4.2 states the Frobenius complement in `G` instead of in `M`

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/OddOrderTheorem/README.md` lines 871–873: "state the conclusion as `TauCeti.IsFrobeniusComplement (E.map M.subtype)` inside `M` together with the identification of `TauCeti.frobeniusKernelSubgroup` with `H(M)`".

Claim: the Lean form of Theorem (12.7) is `TauCeti.IsFrobeniusComplement (E.map M.subtype)`.

Witness: `E.map M.subtype : Subgroup G`, and `TauCeti.IsFrobeniusComplement` is a predicate on subgroups of the ambient group whose field `isTISubgroup` quantifies over every `g : G` outside the subgroup (`TauCeti/GroupTheory/TrivialIntersection.lean` lines 115–116, 326–332). So the phrase asserts that `E` is a trivial-intersection subgroup of `G`, which neither Peterfalvi (12.7) nor `FTtype1_Frobenius` proves (MC-PFsection12 lines 1282–1283 conclude `[Frobenius M with kernel M`_\F]`, a statement internal to `M`); the "inside `M`" of the same sentence shows the intended carrier. No instance refutes it (the ambient hypothesis is a minimal odd simple group), but as written the proof obligation is unowned.

Repair: "state the conclusion as `TauCeti.IsFrobeniusComplement E` for `E : Subgroup ↥M`, with `TauCeti.frobeniusKernelSubgroup` (formed in `↥M`) identified with `H(M)` formed inside `↥M`, as the Conventions do for the cores; map into `G` along `M.subtype` only afterwards".
