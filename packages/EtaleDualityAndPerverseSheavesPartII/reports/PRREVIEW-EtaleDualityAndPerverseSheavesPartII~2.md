## R01 (P1) — Alteration-site hypercovers do not give the claimed hyper-base-change

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/EtaleDualityAndPerverseSheavesPartII/README.md:660`: “every alteration hypercovering from §0.2 gives one, since its generators are h-coverings.” The resulting false theorem is at line 718 (§3.6); its hypercovers are constructed at lines 570–574 (§2.4), using the modified products of line 146.

**Claim:** A hypercover formed using fibre products in alt/S can be used for ordinary étale hyper-base-change.

**Witness:** For π:Bl₀(𝔸²_ℂ)→𝔸²_ℂ, the constant simplicial blow-up is an alt/S hypercover. With f=g=id and Λ=ℤ/3, the closed-stalk H² of §3.6 is 0 on the left and ℤ/3(−1) on the right.

Indeed, π is a covering monomorphism in alt/S: two maps from a reduced maximally dominant object agree on its generic points, hence agree everywhere by separatedness. Consequently its modified Čech nerve is constant. The ordinary degree-one matching object, however, contains E×E for E=ℙ¹, and the diagonal misses pairs of distinct exceptional points. Thus it is not an h-hypercover of schemes. Proper base change computes the right-hand H² as H²(E,Λ)=Λ(−1). This uses regular excellent schemes and invertible coefficients; adding those hypotheses does not help.

The published source requires an **h-hypercover with ordinary matching objects**: ILO-final-XII, XIIA 2.1.1 and Theorem 2.2.5, pp. 241, 243–244, lines 218–223 and 290–297. The published finiteness proof expressly constructs this different object and allows components with empty inverse image of U: ILO-final-XIII, Theorem 2.2.2, p. 263, lines 99–108. Individual alteration coverings being h-coverings does not make the inclusion preserve matching objects.

**Exact repair:** Keep alt/S for uniformization, but distinguish its hypercovers from scheme h-hypercovers. In §2.4 construct the latter in finite-type X-schemes using ordinary products and coskeleta. Uniformize the reduced components of those matching schemes; permit each regular connected term's inverse image of U to be either an snc complement or empty. State the degree-controlled refinement relative to each matching scheme for the prime-to-ℓ version. Delete the implication at line 660 and require these genuine h-hypercovers in §3.6 and its uses in §§4.1 and 5.6. Update their Needs clauses and Checks, without changing the generality of finiteness or affine Lefschetz.

**Repair tested on the witness:** The ordinary Čech nerve of π contains the full E×E in degree one and its higher fibre powers. Its descent totalization has closed-stalk H²=0, agreeing with Λ, whereas the constant modified nerve retains H²=Λ(−1). Add this as the negative-control Check distinguishing the two constructions. The published §2.2.2 formulation also accounts for terms lying entirely over the boundary, whose U-term is empty and contributes zero. This is one underlying defect, not separate findings for each downstream use.
