## R01 (P3) — The justification of the small-site comparison in §0.8 fails for non-noetherian X

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/EtaleDualityAndPerverseSheavesPartII/README.md:324`: "since every refinement by generating families of a finite-type X-scheme consists of finite-type X-schemes."

**Claim:** A family of finite-type X-schemes is h-covering in the small category exactly when it is h-covering in 𝒮, because generating families of a finite-type X-scheme consist of finite-type X-schemes.

**Witness:** §0.8 imposes no noetherianity on X. For X = Spec k[x₁, x₂, …] and U = X ∖ V(x₁, x₂, …), the Zariski covering {U → X, id_X} is a generating family of the finite-type X-scheme X, but U is not quasi-compact, so U → X is not of finite type. The stated equivalence is still true: every Zariski covering is refined by affine opens, which are of finite type, and single proper surjections have finite-type source, so every covering sieve of either topology contains a composite family of finite-type X-schemes. Only the justifying clause is wrong.

**Exact repair:** Replace the clause by "since every Zariski covering is refined by affine opens and proper morphisms are of finite type, every covering sieve of a finite-type X-scheme contains a composite of generating families whose members are of finite type over X". Tested on the instance above: {U → X, id_X} is refined by {id_X}, which is of finite type.

## R02 (P3) — EnhancedDerivedSheaves §E2.8 owns the h-topology on perfect schemes, not on all schemes

**Location:** `README.md:21`: "EnhancedDerivedSheaves §E2.8 and ArcTopologyAndDescent §1.3 own the h-topology generated by fppf coverings and finitely presented proper surjections, the finitely presented v-covers of Bhatt–Scholze".

**Claim:** §E2.8 owns the Bhatt–Scholze h-topology on schemes.

**Witness:** EnhancedDerivedSheaves on main, `README.md:2399–2405`: §E2.8 is "h-descent on perfect schemes"; its object `perfectHTopology` is the topology on perfect qcqs 𝔽_p-schemes whose covers are perfections of h-covers; the scheme h-topology is recalled there only to define it, with the generators the scope sentence quotes. ArcTopologyAndDescent §1.3 (at its head, `README.md:282–296`) is the owner of the topology on finitely presented S-schemes, generated by fppf and abstract-blowup covers. The scope sentence attributes to §E2.8 more than it states; the sentence's conclusion (§0.8 is a different topology and no comparison is asserted) is unaffected.

**Exact repair:** Write "ArcTopologyAndDescent §1.3 owns the h-topology on finitely presented schemes over a qcqs base, the finitely presented v-covers of Bhatt–Scholze, generated by fppf coverings and abstract blowup covers; EnhancedDerivedSheaves §E2.8 owns its perfection on perfect 𝔽_p-schemes".
