## R01 (P2) — The "supplied" scheme h-topology is not the one ArcTopologyAndDescent §1.3 defines

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/EtaleDualityAndPerverseSheavesPartII/README.md:570`: "Here h is the supplied topology generated by proper surjections and Zariski coverings." Also `README.md:122` ("Section 1.3 supplies the h-site of finitely presented schemes over a qcqs base"), `README.md:590` (*Needs:* "ArcTopologyAndDescent §1.3 for the scheme h-site"), and `README.md:676` ("Section 2.4 supplies such hypercovers").

**Claim:** The h-topology in which §2.4 builds its hypercovers is ArcTopologyAndDescent §1.3's, and that topology is generated by proper surjections and Zariski coverings; §3.3's descent then applies to those hypercovers.

**Witness:** ArcTopologyAndDescent at its open head, `TauCetiRoadmap/ArcTopologyAndDescent/README.md:284`: the h-topology on finitely presented S-schemes is generated by finite families whose disjoint union is a v-cover; line 291: "Fppf covers and abstract blowup covers generate the h-topology." Nothing there mentions Zariski coverings and proper surjections as generators. Meanwhile §3.3 (`README.md:664`) defines its own h-topology on schemes "generated by Zariski coverings and proper surjective morphisms", following ILO-final-XII line 229–231 (XIIA 2.1.3, p. 241). So the roadmap carries two topologies under one name: §2.4 and its Needs line import the Bhatt–Scholze one, §§3.3, 3.5 and 3.6 use the Illusie–Laszlo–Orgogozo one, and the bullet at line 676 passes a hypercover from the first into a theorem stated for the second. The two do coincide on the noetherian finite-type schemes of §2.4 (Stacks, More on Flatness, Lemma 38.34.4, tag 0H45: over a locally noetherian base a family is an h covering iff it is a ph covering; with Lemma 38.34.1, tag 0ETR, for the identification of finitely presented v-covers), but that is a theorem and no layer, supplier section or pinned declaration states it. The supplier contract and §2.4 thus claim for ArcTopologyAndDescent §1.3 a statement it does not make, and the step the §3.3 bullet needs is unowned.

**Exact repair:** State the scheme h-topology once, in this roadmap, as the topology generated by Zariski coverings and proper surjective morphisms (ILO-final-XII, XIIA 2.1.3, p. 241; ILO-final-XII line 229), either as a Layer 0 interface item (a short §0.8, or a bullet of §0.2) or in §2.4's interface; record there that étale coverings and alteration coverings are h-coverings by §0.3 (ILO-final-XII line 239 refers to II-3.1.1; ILO-final-XIII line 107 to II-3.2.1), which is what the proof route of §2.4 actually uses. Make §3.3 cite that item instead of listing §2.4 in its *Needs:* line (§3.3's statement and route do not need §2.4; the sentence "Section 2.4 supplies such hypercovers" belongs in the interface bullet only). Then either delete the ArcTopologyAndDescent §1.3 citations at lines 122, 570, 590 and 604, or keep the site and add the owned comparison with its locator: on locally noetherian schemes its coverings are exactly the ph-coverings (Stacks tags 0H45 and 0ETR), so an h-hypercover in either sense is one in the other. Tested on the instance that exposed it: for the degree-one matching scheme T ×_X T of the blow-up Check, the covering map built in the route is "disjoint union of reduced components, then alterations, then affine opens"; each is a finite or proper surjection or a Zariski covering, hence a covering for the topology of §3.3 directly, and the §3.3 bullet applies without any appeal to the Bhatt–Scholze generators. For a Nisnevich covering produced by §0.3 form (1), the same holds after §0.3 with ℓ = 1 refines it by a finite surjection followed by a Zariski covering.

## R03 (P3) — §2.4 is stated below the generality of its published source

**Location:** `README.md:570`: "such that for every i ≤ N, X_i is a finite disjoint union of affine regular connected schemes T."

**Claim:** Regularity and boundary control are asserted only in degrees ≤ N.

**Witness:** ILO-final-XIII, Théorème 2.2.2, p. 263, lines 99–104, asserts both conditions for every i ≥ 0, and the degree-by-degree split construction in the roadmap's own route has no obstruction at any stage. The truncation came from the preprint (ILO14 lines 9331–9335, "pour chaque i ≤ q + 1"), which the published version removed. Only §5.6's Popescu approximation needs a finite truncation.

**Exact repair:** State the hypercover with control in every degree, as the published theorem does, and let §§4.1 and 5.6 take the truncation they need (q + 1 and N + 1 respectively) as a consequence. Tested on the constant hypercover of an affine regular X with snc Z: every term is X, so control holds in all degrees, and nothing in the existing Checks changes.
