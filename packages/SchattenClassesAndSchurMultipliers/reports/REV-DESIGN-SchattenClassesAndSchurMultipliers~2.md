2. **F02** (P3) §10.1 first Check does not separate the column and row seminorms

   - Location: `design/README.md` lines 1419–1422, "Column versus row: on `𝕋` let `r_k(z) = sign sin(2π 2^k z)` … `f = Σ_k r_k E_{k1}` … gives `√N`, while … gives `1`: a symmetrised definition would not see the difference"; `plan.json` 10.1 test (line 1403).
   - Claim: the example has different column and row seminorms.
   - Witness: the two numbers are the `Q = 𝕋` contributions only. Every `r_k` changes sign at `z = 1/4` (a multiple of `2^{−k−1}` for all `k ≥ 1`): just left of `1/4` the vector `(r_1, …, r_N)` is `(1, −1, …, −1)`, just right it is the negative. On `Q = (1/4 − ε, 1/4 + ε)` the mean is `0` and `⨍_Q |f* − f*_Q|² = r rᵀ` is constant, of norm `‖r‖² = N`.    - Repair: replace the example by `f = Σ_{k=1}^N 1_{I_k} E_{k1}` with `I_k = [(k−1)/N, k/N)`, `N ≥ 3`. With `a_k = |Q ∩ I_k|/|Q|`: `⨍_Q |f − f_Q|² = (1 − Σ_k a_k²) E₁₁`, maximal at `Q = 𝕋`, so `‖f‖_{BMO^c} = (1 − 1/N)^{1/2}`; `⨍_Q |f* − f*_Q|² = diag(a) − a aᵀ`, whose norm is the largest variance of a unit vector's coordinates under the law `a`, at most `1/2` (Popoviciu) and equal to `1/2` at `Q = I₁ ∪ I₂`, so `‖f‖_{BMO^r} = 2^{−1/2}`.
