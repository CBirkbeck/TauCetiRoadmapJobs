The strict endomorphism theorem is stated on the actual completion of a normed algebraic closure of `LaurentSeries k`. It fixes the composite image of k. The norm-order comparison in the hypothesis specifies the Laurent valuation, so an arbitrary normed field is not a stand-in for the theorem's carrier. KT16 line 34 states the result for every field of characteristic p. The unnecessary perfectness restriction in the design was therefore dropped for this theorem, in both README and Lean. The other seventeen headline families need heavy period or marked-untilt constructions; p-typical Witt vectors, algebraic ideal completion and the topological quotient do not construct them. No generic Bézout minimum-degree lemma, API lemma, numerical Check or free predicate was put in their place.
