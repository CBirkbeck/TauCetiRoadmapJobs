## R05 (P3) — The proof route of `theta_kernel_charP` needs one more step when `R♭` has zero divisors

Location: `README.md` lines 553–557: "Writing `p = ξa`, the first two Witt coordinates and the unit criteria show that `a` is a unit."

Claim: the route proves `ker θ = (p)` from a principal kernel for a nonzero ring of characteristic `p`.

Witness: the two coordinate equations are `ξ₀a₀ = 0` and `ξ₀^p a₁ + ξ₁ a₀^p = 1`; when `R♭` is not a domain they do not by themselves make `a₀` a unit. The statement is nevertheless true: since `[j] ∈ (ξ)` for every `j` in `J = ker(R♭ → R)`, one has `J = (ξ₀)`; multiplying the second equation by `ξ₀` gives `ξ₀(1 − e) = 0` with `e = ξ₀^p a₁ ∈ J` idempotent, and an idempotent of `lim_F R` with zeroth coordinate `0` is `0`, so `ξ₀ = 0`, `J = 0`, and then `ξ₁a₀^p = 1` makes `a` a unit.

Repair: add the idempotent step (or the equivalent observation `J = (ξ₀)` with `ξ₀ = ξ₀^{p+1}a₁`) to the route; no change to the statement or to `tilt_projection_injective_charP`.
