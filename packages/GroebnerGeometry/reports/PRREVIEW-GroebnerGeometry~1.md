**Verdict:** needs changes

**Summary.** The Lean headline statements are correct as stated, and every Check I recomputed holds. Four findings block. The new stabilizer statement of §2.5 says the finite part of an orbit stabilizer is a single `μ_m`, which fails when the torsion of `L_S ⧸ gp F` is not cyclic. Two headlines the module docstring calls not statable, the `SL₂`-invariant theorem and the Catalan degree, elaborate at the pins with short definitions. ## R01 (P2) — The finite part of an orbit stabilizer in §2.5 need not be a single `μ_m`

The third bullet of §2.5 (lines 480–483) says the torsion of `L_S ⧸ gp F` "gives a finite factor `μ_m` of the stabilizer". An implementer would be asked to prove that the torsion is cyclic, and that is false from rank 3 on.

A counterexample: in `L = ℤ³`, let `S` be generated by `(2,0,0)`, `(0,2,0)`, `(0,0,1)`, `(1,0,1)` and `(0,1,1)`. Then `L_S = ℤ³`, because `(1,0,0) = (1,0,1) − (0,0,1)` and likewise for `(0,1,0)`. Since the third coordinate is nonnegative on `S`, `F = S ∩ {z = 0}` is a face, generated by `(2,0,0)` and `(0,2,0)`. So `L_S ⧸ gp F ≅ ℤ ⊕ (ℤ/2)²`, and the stabilizer is `𝔾_m × μ₂ × μ₂`, which has no `μ_m` factor carrying all its torsion. (In rank 2 the torsion is always cyclic, which is why §§6.1 and 6.3 do not show this.)

The fix is to the clause only: the torsion subgroup `T`, of order the index of `gp F` in its saturation in `L_S`, gives a finite diagonalizable factor `D(T) ≅ ∏ μ_(m_i)` for `T ≅ ⊕ ℤ/m_i`, nonreduced exactly when the characteristic divides `|T|`. The pull-request description repeats the `μ_m` sentence under "Stabilizers (§2.5)".

## R02 (P2) — The `SL₂`-invariant theorem and the Catalan degree are statable at the pins, though the module docstring says they are not

`Suggested.lean`, lines 30–33, lists both as not statable: "the pins have no coaction of `SL₂` on that polynomial ring", and, for the Catalan degree, "no degree of a projective scheme". Both elaborate with nothing beyond `import Mathlib` and Tau Ceti modules at the pin. So the acceptance test omits two headline theorems it could state.

For `SL₂`, §5.5 already plans the coaction as an `aeval` map. The following elaborates with only the `sorry` warning (imports: `TauCeti.Algebra.AlgebraicGroup.SpecialLinear.StandardComodule` and `TauCeti.Algebra.AlgebraicGroup.GeneralLinear.Coordinate.HopfAlgebra`):

```lean
noncomputable def universalSL2 (k : Type) [Field k] :
    Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) (TauCeti.SpecialLinear.coordinateHopfAlgebra k 2) :=
  (TauCeti.GeneralLinear.genericMatrix k 2).map (TauCeti.SpecialLinear.coordinateMap k 2).hom

noncomputable def sl2Coaction (k : Type) [Field k] (n : ℕ) :
    MvPolynomial (Fin n × Fin 2) k →ₐ[k]
      MvPolynomial (Fin n × Fin 2) k ⊗[k] TauCeti.SpecialLinear.coordinateHopfAlgebra k 2 :=
  aeval fun ir ↦ ∑ s, X (ir.1, s) ⊗ₜ universalSL2 k s ir.2

theorem sl2_invariants (k : Type) [Field k] (n : ℕ) :
    AlgHom.equalizer (sl2Coaction k n) Algebra.TensorProduct.includeLeft =
      Algebra.adjoin k (Set.range fun p : {p : Fin n × Fin n // p.1 < p.2} ↦
        Matrix.mvPolynomialMinor k ![p.1.1, p.1.2] id) := sorry
```

For the Catalan degree, §6.6 itself states it through the Hilbert function. With the file's own `pluckerMap`, this elaborates:

```lean
theorem plucker_two_hilbertPolynomial (k : Type*) [Field k] (n : ℕ) (hn : 2 ≤ n) :
    ∃ P : Polynomial ℚ, P.natDegree = 2 * n - 4 ∧
      P.leadingCoeff = (catalan (n - 2) : ℚ) / (2 * n - 4).factorial ∧
      ∀ᶠ m in Filter.atTop, (Module.finrank k
        ((homogeneousSubmodule (PluckerIndex n 2) k m).map (pluckerMap k n 2).toLinearMap) : ℚ) =
          P.eval (m : ℚ) := sorry
```

The values check out for `n = 2, 3, 4`: a point (`P = 1`), `ℙ²` (leading coefficient `1/2`) and a quadric in `ℙ⁵` (`2/4!`). Either state both, or say in the docstring why these forms are not wanted. The docstring's reasons for the `K₀` comparison and the normalized volume (no `K₀` of coherent sheaves, no lattice-normalized volume at the pins) are correct. The description's list of four non-statable headlines would change to match.

## R03 (P2) — The completion theorems of §1.4 and §3.7 each omit the hypothesis that makes their conclusion true

§1.4 (lines 222–226) asserts that "every sequence … `G = G₀ ⊂ G₁ ⊂ ⋯` … is finite, and its last member is a Gröbner basis". The one-term sequence `G₀ = G` is such a sequence, and for `G = {x y − 1, y² − 1}` its last member is not a Gröbner basis: §1.4's own Check shows the S-polynomial `x − y` reduces to a nonzero remainder. The conclusion needs the sequence to be one that cannot be extended: every such sequence is finite, and one that cannot be extended ends at a Gröbner basis.

§3.7 (lines 663–668) never requires the starting family to generate `A`. Start from the empty family with `A = k[x]`. There is no binomial relation to process, so every fair completion stops at once at `∅`, which is not a SAGBI basis of `k[x]`, although the initial algebra `k[x]` is finitely generated. Bruns–Conca–Lembo start from a finite family generating `A` (BCL line 199, p. 5). Add "for a finite family generating `A`".

## C02 — The overview says everything after division is over a field

Line 12 says "the rest is over a field". But §2.1 works over every commutative ring, §2.4 over `ℤ` and every base, §4.2 over any commutative ring, and §5.1's identities hold over `ℤ`. "Normal forms, reduced bases and SAGBI theory are over a field" would match Convention 1.

## C03 — The Check of §6.7 calls 8 "the dimension of the `GL₃`-representation of highest weight `(2,1,0)`"

The roadmap allows every characteristic, and in characteristic 3 the irreducible module of highest weight `(2,1,0)` has dimension 7. The degree-`uv` piece of the flag algebra is the Weyl (or Schur) module of dimension 8. Naming that module (line 1237) makes the Check true in every characteristic.

## C04 — `ker_aeval_eq_span_sub_of_lifts` is more general in Lean than in the README

§§3.4–3.5 (lines 607, 611–612) take a finite family and finitely many nonzero binomials. The Lean statement allows any index types `ι` and `J`, and binomials with `u j = v j`. The Lean form is true (Bruns–Conca–Lembo allow countable families), so the README could say it holds at that generality.

## C05 — Links to the old Layer 4 anchor no longer resolve

Lines 64–65 map `Gk` to Layer `k` for readers. But DeformationAndDerivedPatchingAlgebra (line 78), DeformationAndDerivedPatchingAlgebraPartII (line 118) and ResolutionOfSingularitiesCharacteristicZero (line 33) on main link to `README.md#g4-rees-algebras-and-flat-initial-families`. That heading is gone, so the links land at the top of the file. An explicit anchor before the Layer 4 heading would keep them working.

## C06 — The description says other roadmaps cite G1, G4 and G5

G2 is cited too: by TensorProductCombinatorics and AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry on main, and by MixedHodgeModulesAndIrregularHodgeTheory in an open pull request. "Other roadmaps cite them as G1, G2, G4 and G5" would be right.

## C07 — The second half of the Check of §1.2 calls `y` the leading term of `x² − y`

For lex with `y > x`, the leading term is `−y`; its leading monomial is `y` (line 193). Convention 2 uses `m.leadingTerm` with its coefficient, so "leading monomial `y`" would match.

## N01 — `K0.toG0_bijective` (§4.11, line 904) is in code font but is a proposed name of SchemeAndStackFoundations §5.7

There it is the italic *Chow.K0.toG0_bijective*, specified in that README only. Writing it as the supplier contract at line 1407 does, in italics, avoids a search for a declaration that does not exist.
