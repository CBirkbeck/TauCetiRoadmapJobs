# Review of FieldOfNorms (pull request #1812 at 4384623)

**Verdict:** needs changes

**Summary.** Needs changes: three stated results are false as written, each by one missing hypothesis or one wrong set, and each has a one-line repair. The converse construction of 0.5 drops the source's condition that the upper breaks tend to infinity; the unit-root statement in 4.2 (Wintenberger 2004, Prop. 2) concludes `u ∈ ε^{ℤ_p}` where only `u ∈ ε^{ℚ_p}` holds; and the definition of `e(Γ)` that opens Layer 4 omits "wildly ramified". All nine findings of the previous review are repaired. There are six small corrections and two format notes, none blocking, and three points for the human reviewers.

## R01 (P2) — The converse construction of 0.5 is false without the condition that the upper breaks tend to infinity

An implementer asked to prove "has APF union" from the listed hypotheses cannot: a tower whose levels `i_n` tend to infinity slowly has bounded upper breaks, and its union is not APF. The item (line 176) asks only for `K_0/K` unramified, `K_1/K_0` tame and "`K_{n+1}/K_n` elementary of strictly increasing level `i_n` (tending to `∞` if the sequence is infinite)". Wintenberger 1983, Rem. 1.4.2 (lines 242–252 of the Numdam text) has a further condition: with `d = [K_1 : K_0]`, `p^{r_n} = [K_{n+1} : K_n]` and `b_n = d⁻¹[i_1 + (i_2 − i_1)p^{−r_1} + … + (i_n − i_{n−1})p^{−r_1−…−r_{n−1}}]`, the union is APF if and only if the tower is finite or `b_n → ∞`.

Instance: `K = 𝔽_2((t))`, `K_0 = K_1 = K`, and `K_{n+1} = K_n(y)` with `y² − y = π_n^{−(2n−1)}`, `π_n` a uniformizer of `K_n`. Each step is cyclic of degree `2` with break `2n − 1`, so it is elementary of level `i_n = 2n − 1`, strictly increasing to `∞`. Then `b_n = 1 + 2(2⁻¹ + … + 2^{−(n−1)}) < 3`, every `K_n/K` has all its upper breaks below `3`, so `G_K(3) ⊆ ⋂ G_{K_n} = G_L` and `G_K(3)·G_L = G_L` has infinite index.

Smallest change: add the hypothesis, "…and, if the sequence is infinite, `b_n → ∞` for the `b_n` above", and state the result as the equivalence of Rem. 1.4.2; the `b_n` are then the breaks and `ψ_{L/K}(b_n) = i_n` is the Herbrand function the last sentence refers to. The instance above would make a negative-control Check.

## R02 (P2) — In 4.2 the solutions of `τ(u) = u^{η(τ)}` are `ε^{ℚ_p}`, not `ε^{ℤ_p}`

The statement taken from Wintenberger 2004, Prop. 2 (line 353: "then `η = χ_cyc` and `u ∈ ε^{ℤ_p}`", repeated in the first sub-step, line 358, and in "`j(ε) = ε'^a` with `a ∈ ℤ_p`", line 366) is false as written. With the README's normalisation (`ε_0` a primitive `p`-th root of unity), `u = ε^{1/p} = (ε_1, ε_2, …)` is a principal unit of the perfect field `R(Ĉ)`, `u ≠ 1`, and `τ(u) = u^{χ_cyc(τ)}` for all `τ`; but `u_0 = ε_1` has order `p²`, while every element of `ε^{ℤ_p}` has `0`-th component in `μ_p`. The README's own proof gives the right set: it ends with "the `u_n` are compatible `p`-power roots of unity", and those families are exactly `ε^{ℚ_p}`. The source agrees: w2004 line 321 has `j(ε) = ε'^a` with `a ∈ ℚ_p`, and then deduces that `a` is a unit of `ℤ_p` from the valuations.

Smallest change: write `ε^{ℚ_p}` in the three places, and let the valuation step do the work it does in the source: `v_{X'}(ε'^a − 1) = p^{v_p(a)}·v_{X'}(ε' − 1)`, so the equality `v_{X'}(j(ε) − 1) = v_{X'}(ε' − 1)` already displayed gives `v_p(a) = 0`. The rest of the argument (the generator `∑_{i<p}[ε']^{ai}` of `ker θ'`) is unchanged.

## R03 (P2) — The definition of `e(Γ)` that opens Layer 4 needs `Γ` wildly ramified

The paragraph (line 313) asserts, for every group `Γ ≅ ℤ_p` of continuous automorphisms of a local field of characteristic `p`, that `i_{n+1} − i_n = p^{n+1}e(Γ)` for `n` large with an integer `e(Γ) ≥ 1`, or `i_n/p^n → ∞`. Wintenberger 2004 states this for a wildly ramified automorphism of infinite order (w2004 lines 61–74), and it fails otherwise. Instance, from the README's own statements: `K = ℚ_p`, `p` odd, `L/K` a `ℤ_p`-extension whose inertia subgroup has index `p` (the quotient `(x, y) ↦ x + py` of the unramified-by-cyclotomic `ℤ_p²`). `L/K` is abelian with finite residue extension, so `Γ = Gal(L/K)` acts faithfully on `X_K(L) = X_{K_1}(L)` (0.6, 2.3, 1.6), with `K_1/ℚ_p` unramified of degree `p`. For a generator `σ` and `τ = σ^p`, `e(⟨τ⟩) = e_{K_1} = 1`, so `i_{n+1}(σ) − i_n(σ) = i_n(τ) − i_{n−1}(τ) = p^n = p^{n+1}·(1/p)` for `n` large, and `i_n(σ)/p^n → 1/(p − 1)`: neither alternative holds.

Smallest change: "For a group `Γ ≅ ℤ_p` of wildly ramified continuous automorphisms (lower index at least `2`, as in 4.1)…".

## C01 — The faithfulness step of 4.2 names the wrong subfield

"Each `E` is generated by `π_E` over the unramified extension of `K` with the residue field of `E`, which `i` determines through Teichmüller representatives…" (line 336). In `ΓEXT` every `E` is totally ramified over `K`, so that extension is `K` itself, and Teichmüller representatives do not determine `i` on `K`: `ℚ_p(√p)` has two embeddings into itself that agree on them. The argument is sound once the subfield is the one the representatives generate: `E = W(k)[1/p](π_E)` in characteristic `0` and `E = k((π_E))` in characteristic `p`. Replace "the unramified extension of `K` with the residue field of `E`" by that subfield.

## C02 — The conventions attribute the steps of `F_K` to a different supplier section than FilteredLocalGaloisGroups does

The conventions (line 73) say `F_K` of FilteredLocalGaloisGroups Layer 2 is "`G_K^u` of RamificationOfCompleteDiscretelyValuedFields §4a.1". At its open head, FilteredLocalGaloisGroups 2.1 defines the steps of `F_K` as ClassFieldTheory Layer 7's `localAbsoluteUpperRamificationGroup K u`, and records that §4a.1's filtration agrees with it on a local field (§4a.1, fourth bullet). The identification is true, so nothing stated here changes; the sentence, and the §4a.1 entry of the supplier contracts, would say "which agrees with `G_K^u` of §4a.1" so that a reader lands on the definition FilteredLocalGaloisGroups actually uses.

## C05 — The Check against the Amice field holds for any uniformizer

"`ū ↦ π` identifies the reduction `𝔽_p((ū))` … with `X_{ℚ_p}(L)`" (line 483) is true of any two Laurent series fields over `𝔽_p` and any uniformizer, so it cannot tell this `π` from another. One that could fail: for `p` odd, `1 − π = (ζ_{p^n})_n` in `X_{ℚ_p}(L)` (the norm from level `m` to level `n` of `ζ_{p^m}` is `ζ_{p^n}`), so `γ(1 − π) = (1 − π)^{χ_cyc(γ)}`, to be matched with the cyclotomic action on the Amice reduction.
