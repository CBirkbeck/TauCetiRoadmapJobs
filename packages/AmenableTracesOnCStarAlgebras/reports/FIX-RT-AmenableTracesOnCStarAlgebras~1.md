## RT-AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras~1b/F02 (P3) — applied differently

§6.5: the clause "`‖ψ(x − ex)‖₂² ≤ tr ψ(x*(1−e)²x)`, which is close to `γ(...)`" was replaced. Put
`(1 − e)` on the right instead: `d₂(ψ, x(1−e)) ≤ tr ψ̃((1−e)x*x(1−e)) ≤ ‖x‖²(1 − tr φ(e))` by
positivity of `ψ̃`. Then `d₂(ψ, xe) = d₂(φ, xe)`, and §2.2 bounds the product defect. The trace is
recovered by Cauchy–Schwarz for `tr ∘ ψ̃`: the error is at most `2‖x‖(1 − tr φ(e))^{1/2}`.

Why not the suggested repair: the review's Stinespring bound is correct, but the same bound follows
from the positivity of `ψ̃` and the order `(1−e)x*x(1−e) ≤ ‖x‖²(1−e)²` alone, which §2.1–§2.2
already supply. No new supplier input is needed. The review's witness (`c₀ ⊆ c`,
`ψ = (1−η)δ_1 + η·lim`) satisfies the new bound: `|η lim x| ≤ ‖x‖η^{1/2}`. Needs now lists §2.1
and VonNeumannAlgebras V0 (Cauchy–Schwarz).
