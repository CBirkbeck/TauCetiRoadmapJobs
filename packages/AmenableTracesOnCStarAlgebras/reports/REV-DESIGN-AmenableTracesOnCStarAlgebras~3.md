## R01 (P3) — The unnormalized-trace negative control has the opposite failure

**Location:** `packages/AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras/design/README.md:989`, “and so would wrongly exclude `tr_n`”; also `packages/AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras/design/plan.json:219`, “an unnormalized-trace definition would exclude tr_n.”

**Claim:** Failure of the identity approximant under the wrong normalization does not exclude the trace from an existential approximation definition. The definitions permit completely positive contractions, not only unital maps.

**Witness:** For any tracial state τ, set φ_m(a)=τ(a)I_m/m. Then Tr_mφ_m=τ and the operator-norm multiplicativity defect is at most ‖a‖‖b‖(1/m+1/m²), tending to zero. These maps are completely positive contractions.

Consequently replacing the normalized recovery trace by Tr makes every tracial state pass, including the nonamenable canonical trace of C*_r(F₂). This remains true if the 2-norm is also changed to the unnormalized Hilbert–Schmidt norm: its defect is at most ‖a‖‖b‖(1/√m+1/m^(3/2)). Functional-norm recovery is exact too, so the same dilution defeats the purported uniform version. The three actual definitions in the design use the correct normalization and are unaffected.

**Exact repair:** Replace the exclusion assertion in both locations by the dilution negative control: “For φ_m(a)=τ(a)I_m/m, unnormalized trace recovery is exact and the defects tend to zero for every τ; this incorrect normalization would admit even the nonamenable canonical trace of C*_r(F₂). Normalized recovery rejects these maps since tr_mφ_m(1)=1/m.” The observation that the identity map fails its particular unnormalized recovery test may remain, but not its inference about existence of other approximants.

**Repair test:** On M₂ with τ=tr₂, a=e₁₂ and b=e₂₁, φ_m(a)=φ_m(b)=0 and φ_m(ab)=I_m/(2m). Recovery is Tr_mφ_m(ab)=1/2=τ(ab); operator and normalized 2-defects are 1/(2m), and the unnormalized Hilbert–Schmidt defect is 1/(2√m). At the unit, the correct recovery error is 1−1/m, whereas the incorrect recovery error is 0. These values verify the proposed replacement and refute the original exclusion claim.
