## R01 (P1) — Cartan involution data on a reductive group need not be conjugate by an inner automorphism

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md:1172–1173`: "and any two Cartan involution data on `G` are conjugate by an inner automorphism of `G`". The same claim appears in Scope, lines 123–124: "and §5.4 that of LieGroups `CartanInvolution` data on a group".

**Claim:** This is false for reductive `G` whose centre has both a compact and a split part. There a datum is not determined by its fixed group `K`, and inner automorphisms cannot carry one datum to another.

**Witness:** Take `G = ℂˣ = H ⊂ GL₂(ℝ)`. The identity cover gives `θ₀(z) = 1/z̄`. The cover `re^{iφ} ↦ re^{i(φ+log r)}` gives `θ₁(z) = |z|^{2i}/z̄`. Both are data with fixed group `U(1)`, yet `θ₁(2) = e^{2i·log 2}/2 ≠ 1/2`, and `ℂˣ` is abelian.

**Repair:**
- Lines 1172–1173: "and, when `𝔤₀` is semisimple, any two Cartan involution data on `G` are conjugate by an inner automorphism of `G` (a datum is then determined by `K`, through `𝔭 = 𝔨^{⊥_B}` and the polar decomposition); for reductive `𝔤₀` only their fixed groups are conjugate".
- Lines 123–124: "and §5.4 that of LieGroups `CartanInvolution` data on a group with semisimple Lie algebra, nonlinear finite covers included."
- Lines 1104–1105: replace "for `θ' = Ad(h)θAd(h)⁻¹`" with "for `K' = hKh⁻¹`".
- Add to the §5.4 Checks: "- `G = ℂˣ`: `θ₀(z) = 1/z̄` and `θ₁(z) = |z|^{2i}/z̄` are Cartan involution data. They lift transpose-inverse through the covers `z ↦ z` and `re^{iφ} ↦ re^{i(φ + log r)}` of `ℂˣ ⊂ GL₂(ℝ)` and have the same fixed group `U(1)`. They differ, and `ℂˣ` has no nontrivial inner automorphism, so the conjugacy of data needs semisimple `𝔤₀`."

On `ℂˣ`, the repaired clause asserts only that the two fixed groups `U(1)` are conjugate, which is true.

## R02 (P2) — The restricted Weyl group headline is statable at the pins but absent

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/Suggested.lean:30–32`: "the identification of the restricted Weyl group `N_K(𝔞)/Z_K(𝔞)` with the Weyl group of the restricted root system (§2.3): … a definition of it would be data with a `sorry` body".

**Claim:** The stated reason fails, for two reasons:
- No definition is needed: the pairing can be bound existentially, as `exists_restrictedRootPairing` already does.
- For connected `G`, `K` is the set of `g` whose `Ad g` commutes with `θ`, since §2.2 makes `K = Ad⁻¹(K_θ)`.

So this headline is statable, and it is absent.

**Witness:** The theorem below, inserted after line 174 of a scratch copy, elaborates with 12 `sorry` warnings and no error. On `SL₂(ℝ)`, `Ad g` commutes with `−ᵀ` exactly for `g ∈ SO(2)`, and `[[0,−1],[1,0]]` acts on `𝔞` by `−1`, the coreflection.

**Repair:** In `Suggested.lean`, insert after line 174:

```lean

          ∀ H : 𝔞, TauCeti.Lie.Ad (I := I) g (H : LeftInvariantDerivation I G) = (w H : _) := sorry
```

Then make these three edits:
