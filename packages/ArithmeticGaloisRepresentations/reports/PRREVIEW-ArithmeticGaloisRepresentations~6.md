## R05 (P3) — The invariance clause mis-scopes its fields and does not follow from the sentence before it

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations/README.md:1327–1328`, "Hence `χ_H` is constant on `H(K)`-conjugacy classes of `H(K')`-points for every field `K'` over the base".

**Claim:** `K` is bound in the preceding sentence as an algebraically closed field over the base; `K'` ranges over all fields over the base, with no relation to `K`, so "`H(K)`-conjugacy classes of `H(K')`-points" has no meaning unless `K' ⊆ K`. **Witness:** the parent target's universal property (`:1308–1310`: the quotient map is the invariant morphism) gives `χ_H(g h g⁻¹) = χ_H(h)` for `g, h ∈ H(R)` and every base algebra `R`; the semisimple-class description is used only for the bijection with classes over algebraically closed `K`.

**Repair:** replace the sentence by "`χ_H` is conjugation invariant: `χ_H(g h g⁻¹) = χ_H(h)` for all `g, h ∈ H(K')` and every field `K'` over the base, since `χ_H` is the quotient map of the conjugation action; so `χ_H` is constant on `H(K')`-conjugacy classes in `H(K')`, and two `K'`-points with the same image become conjugate over an algebraic closure exactly when both are semisimple".
