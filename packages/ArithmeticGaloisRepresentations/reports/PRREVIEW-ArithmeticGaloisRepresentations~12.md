## R02 (P3) — The characteristic-two clause of §4.1 is attributed to a lemma that states something else

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations/README.md:2940–2944`, §4.1 **Absolute irreducibility from oddness**: "and that for `char k = 2` the same holds when `ρ(c_v) ≠ 1` at a real place (KW (I), Lemma 6.1, p. 10)".

**Claim:** Khare–Wintenberger (I), Lemma 6.1, states that an irreducible `ρ : G_F → GL₂(k)` in characteristic two with `ρ(c_v) ≠ 1` is absolutely irreducible.

**Witness:** `3bf48fb9…` lines 358–366 (printed p. 10): Lemma 6.1 says that a finite solvable subgroup of `GL₂(F̄₂)` acting irreducibly has dihedral projective image, and its proof excludes `S₄` and `A₄` by Sylow considerations. Neither the lemma nor its proof mentions complex conjugation; the only oddness statements of the paper are "`det ρ(c) = −1`" (lines 49–50, 235). The paper never uses the words "absolutely irreducible" outside line 49.

**Repair:** attribute the characteristic-two clause to the roadmap's own first clause of the same target: for `char k = 2`, `ρ(c_v) ≠ 1` is a nonscalar involution, and `(ρ(c_v) − 1)² = ρ(c_v)² − 2ρ(c_v) + 1 = 0`, so it is unipotent with split characteristic polynomial `(X − 1)²`, and the "nonscalar element with split characteristic polynomial" clause gives absolute irreducibility; keep Serre 1987, §3.3, p. 198 (`8048919d…` lines 870–877) for `char k ≠ 2`, and drop the KW (I) locator or move it to §4.4, where the dihedral statement it proves is used.
