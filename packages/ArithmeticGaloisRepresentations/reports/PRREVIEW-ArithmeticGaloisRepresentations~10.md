- **Error** in BCGP21 (arXiv:1812.09269v3) at Remark 7.5.23, printed p. 205: the source says the order-128 Sylow 2-subgroup of `SL₂(F₃) ≀ ℤ/2` is not enormous; it should be enormous, as its own §7.5.20 list (an enormous class of order 128, necessarily the Sylow class) and the direct check of (E1)–(E3) show; affects a stated result (the remark's justification).
- **Error** in BCGP21 (arXiv:1812.09269v3) at Lemma 7.5.22, proof, printed p. 205: the source says the automorphism group of `PSL₂(k)` is `PGL₂(k)`, so distinct projective images force image `SL₂(k)²`; for `k ≠ F_p` the automorphism group is `PΓL₂(k)` and the graph of a field automorphism is not excluded; affects the proof.
