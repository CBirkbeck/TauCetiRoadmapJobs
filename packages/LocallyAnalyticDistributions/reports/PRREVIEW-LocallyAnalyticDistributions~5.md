## R02 (P3) — A Gauss-convergence Check that evaluates both sides at a root

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/LocallyAnalyticDistributions/README.md:454`: “`P_(u_s)(1) = ∏_(i<s)(1 − p^i) → ∏_i(1 − p^i) = P_u(1)`” for `u = diag(p^i)_(i∈ℕ)`.

**Claim:** the `i = 0` factor is `1 − p^0 = 0`, so `P_(u_s)(1) = 0` for every `s ≥ 1` and `P_u(1) = 0`: the displayed convergence is `0 → 0` and does not discriminate the imported statement (a wrong convergence law would pass it too). The `c_1` half of the Check (`−Σ_(i<s) p^i → −1/(1 − p)`) is fine.

**Witness:** `1 − p^0 = 0`; `P_u(T) = ∏_i(1 − p^i T)` vanishes at `T = 1`.

**Repair:** take `u = diag(p^(i+1))_(i∈ℕ)` and `u_s` its truncations: `‖u − u_s‖ = p^(−s−1) → 0` with majorant `b_j = p^(−j−1)`, `c_1(u_s) = −Σ_(i<s) p^(i+1) → −p/(1 − p)`, and `P_(u_s)(1) = ∏_(1≤i≤s)(1 − p^i) → ∏_(i≥1)(1 − p^i)`, a unit of `ℤ_p` (every partial product is `≡ 1 mod p`), so the limit is nonzero and the test is discriminating.
