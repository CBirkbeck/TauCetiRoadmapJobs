## R01 (P2) — `exists_entire_spectral_transform` is CompactOperators §3.3's theorem, not this roadmap's

**Location.** `TauCetiRoadmap/LocallyAnalyticDistributions/Suggested.lean` lines 134–144 (`theorem exists_entire_spectral_transform`), its module docstring line 12 ("the entire spectral limit (§4.13)"), and `README.md` line 35 ("states … the entire spectral limit") and line 2505 ("**Full entire spectral transform** (supplier; `exists_entire_spectral_transform` in Suggested.lean states its unique entire limit)").

**Claim.** The Lean file states, as a headline theorem of this roadmap, that for entire `B, P` with `B(0) = 0`, `P(0) = 1` the double truncations `D_(n,m)(trunc_(m+1) B, trunc_(n+1) P)` have a unique entire limit in every Gauss norm.

**Witness.** The README itself labels the target "supplier" (line 2505) and cites CompactOperators §3.3 **Convergence and topology** for it. On main, `TauCetiRoadmap/CompactOperators/Suggested.lean` lines 360–377 already declare `colemanDEntire` (the coefficientwise `limUnder` of exactly these double truncations) and `tendsto_gaussNorm_colemanDBounded` (convergence in every Gauss norm, same hypotheses `IsEntire B`, `coeff 0 B = 0`, `IsEntire F`, `coeff 0 F = 1`) and `colemanDEntire_isEntire`; its README line 710 ("**Convergence and topology.** … convergence in every Gauss norm. Define `colemanDEntire B F` by this limit and prove entirety") plans the same object. The definition `polynomialSpectralResultant` (Suggested.lean lines 105–108) is body-for-body `colemanDBounded` (CompactOperators Suggested.lean line 330). So the object has two owners, and this roadmap's acceptance test contains a theorem the finished roadmap proves by citation.

**Repair.** Delete `exists_entire_spectral_transform` from `Suggested.lean`; in the module docstring replace "the entire spectral limit (§4.13)" by a sentence saying that the entire spectral transform and its Gauss convergence are CompactOperators §3.3's (`colemanDEntire`, `tendsto_gaussNorm_colemanDBounded`); in README line 35 drop "the entire spectral limit" and in line 2505 drop the clause "`exists_entire_spectral_transform` in Suggested.lean states its unique entire limit". Keep `polynomialSpectralResultant`, `entireMonicQuotient` and `entirePolynomialSpectral`: they are still needed to state `tendsto_spectral_fixed_polynomial_gauss`, which is this roadmap's own fixed-rank statement (no `B(0) = 0`, rank fixed, only the functional truncation varies) and is not in CompactOperators. Tested: with the deletion the remaining five theorems elaborate unchanged (they do not mention the deleted name).

## R02 (P3) — Unused hypotheses in `naFrechet_openMapping`

**Location.** `Suggested.lean` lines 83–93.

**Claim.** The open mapping theorem for a continuous linear surjection between Fréchet spaces is stated with `[IsUltrametricDist K]` and the two ultrametric-seminorm hypotheses `huV`, `huW`.

**Witness.** The conclusion does not depend on them: Schneider's proof of Proposition 8.6 (Schneider line 2077–2088) uses only completeness, metrizability and the closed graph theorem, and the same statement holds over ℝ (instance: `K = ℝ`, `V = W = ℓ²`, `f = id`, which is open and satisfies none of the three hypotheses). Mathlib's Banach-space version `ContinuousLinearMap.isOpenMap` (`Mathlib/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Banach.lean` line 228) is likewise stated over any nontrivially normed field.

**Repair.** Either drop `[IsUltrametricDist K]`, `huV` and `huW`, or keep them and say in the docstring that they are the roadmap's standing setting and not used by the statement. Tested: the theorem elaborates without the three binders.

## Source issues

- **Error** in Coleman at Lemma A3.1, author manuscript p. 22 (published p. 432): the source says that if `G` is a polynomial with multiplicative leading coefficient and `H` is a restricted series on the closed unit disc with `GH` constant, then `G` is constant or `H = 0`; it should require `H` entire, since `G = 1 − pT`, `H = Σ pⁿTⁿ` over ℚ_p satisfy the printed hypotheses with `GH = 1`; affects a stated result (the README already states the entire form, §4.3).
- **Misprint** in Coleman at proof of Lemma A3.4, author manuscript p. 23 (published p. 433): the source writes the auxiliary polynomial `K(T)` as `Σ_{i=1}^n (−1)^i c_i T^{n−i}` without its leading term; it should be `T^n + Σ_{i=1}^n (−1)^i c_i T^{n−i}`, the monic polynomial the argument factors; affects nothing.
- **Error** in Loeffler at Definition 2.12 and Remark 2.13, p. 5: the source defines order `(r_1,…,r_g)` by the joint first-difference condition along the cofinite filter on `ℕ^g` and says the space is the completed tensor of the one-variable `C^{r_i}`; it should use the tensor-wavelet norm, since `f = 1 ⊗ 1_{pℤ_p}` on `ℤ_p²` with `r = (1/2, 0)` has `ε_{(m_1,0)}(f) = 0` for every `m_1`, so the cofinite condition fails although `f` is a locally constant pure tensor; affects a stated result (Remark 2.13's isomorphism).
- **Error** in Pilloni at §13.1.1, condition 4, p. 84: the source requires `Q⋆(U)` to be invertible on `M^{>h}` for polynomials `Q` whose roots have valuation strictly greater than `h`, and asserts uniqueness via Urban Cor. 2.3.3; it should require invertibility for `Q` whose eigenvalue-side roots have valuation `≤ h`, as in Urban §2.3.1, since for `M = K`, `U = p`, `h = 2` both `M^{≤2} = K` and `M^{≤2} = 0` satisfy the printed conditions; affects a stated result (uniqueness).
- **Misprint** in Pilloni at proof of Corollary 13.2.4.2, p. 87: the source extends a finite-slope class `f = P(U)f` to the smaller level by `P(Ũ)f`, where `Ũ` is a map between two different cohomology groups, not an endomorphism; it should be `a(Q(U)f)` with `P = XQ` and `Ũ = a` the raising map, after which `res∘ext = id`; affects the proof.
- **Error** in BCGP I at §6.1.1, p. 139: the source defines an `h`-slope decomposition with `Q(U)` invertible on `M^{>h}` for monic `Q` whose roots have valuation strictly less than `h`, and says such decompositions are unique; it should use valuation `≤ h`, since for `M = ℂ_p`, `U = p`, `h = 1` both `M^{≤1} = M` and `M^{≤1} = 0` satisfy the printed conditions; affects a stated result (uniqueness at the endpoint).
