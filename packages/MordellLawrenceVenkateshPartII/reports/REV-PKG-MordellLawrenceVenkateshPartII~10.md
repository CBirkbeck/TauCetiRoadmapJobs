1. **F01** (P2) §2.6 and §7.1 do not say over which local field the representation lives

   **Location.** `README.md:1350–1351` (§2.6): "For a crystalline local Galois representation with values in
   `G` (as in §2.5), applying `D_cris` as an exact tensor functor gives filtered φ-data with an inner form
   `G′` of `G`". `README.md:2292` (§7.1): "a crystalline p-adic representation `V`".

   **Claim.** The hypotheses of the crystalline tensor comparison are stated. Neither §2.5 nor §2.6 gives `G`
   or the Galois group a base field, and §§0.7 and 1.2 allow `K_v ≠ ℚ_p`.

   **Witness.** LS lines 2675–2677, 2686–2690: `G` over `ℚ_p` and `Gal(ℚ̄_p/ℚ_p)` only. For `K = ℚ_(p²)` and
   the trivial character, `D_cris = K` and `φ` is the Frobenius of `K`, not `K`-linear, so not in `G′`.

   **Repair.**

   1. §2.6, replace the first sentence by: "Let `G` be an algebraic group over `ℚ_p` with reductive identity
      component and a faithful representation `G → GL_N`, and let `ρ : Gal(ℚ̄_p/ℚ_p) → G(ℚ_p)` be continuous
      and crystalline, that is, crystalline in that representation (the property does not depend on the
      faithful representation chosen). Applying `D_cris` as an exact tensor functor gives a filtered φ-module
      with an inner form `G′` of `G` over `ℚ_p`, a Frobenius in `G′(ℚ_p)` and a `G′`-filtration."
   2. In the same paragraph write "A homomorphism `G₁ → G₂` over `ℚ_p`", and "In the coefficient-normalizer
      case, when the composite of `ρ` with `G → Aut_(ℚ_p)(E_et)` is the Galois action on `E_et`, `G′` is
      identified with …" (the hypothesis of LS line 2709). Add before the locator: "The base field is `ℚ_p`
      and not a finite extension: over `K ≠ ℚ_p` the functor `D_cris` takes values in `K₀`-vector spaces with
      a Frobenius that is only semilinear."
   3. Add the Check: "For the trivial character of `Gal(ℚ̄_p/K)` with `K = ℚ_(p²)`, `D_cris = K` and `φ` is the
      Frobenius of `K`, which moves every `a ∈ K ∖ ℚ_p` and is not `K`-linear; over `ℚ_p` the same character
      gives `D_cris = ℚ_p` with `φ = 1 ∈ GL₁(ℚ_p)`. In the arithmetic theorems the base is `ℤ[S⁻¹]`, the
      local field is `ℚ_p`, and a larger unramified field enters only through the coefficient algebra of
      §2.1."
   4. §7.1, replace "generated by a crystalline p-adic representation `V`" by "generated by a crystalline
      representation `V` of `Gal(K̄/K)` on a finite-dimensional `ℚ_p`-vector space, for `K` a finite
      unramified extension of `ℚ_p` (so that `D_cris` takes values in `K`-vector spaces)", write "become
      tensor-isomorphic over `ℚ̄_p`, after an embedding `K → ℚ̄_p`", and in the route "a nonempty scheme of
      finite type over `K`". This is the field of §7.2 and of gap:hodge-tate-image-cocharacter.

   Tested on the witness: with (1)–(3) the instance `K = ℚ_(p²)` is outside the statement and is the new
   negative control; the consumers still meet the hypothesis (§5.4 restricts `G_ℚ → G(ℚ_p)` to
   `Gal(ℚ̄_p/ℚ_p)`; §§4.5, 5.8 and 6.1 have base `ℤ[S⁻¹]`). With (4), for `K = ℚ_(p²)` and trivial `V` the
