## C01 — The `*Needs:*` line of §7.4 lists Milestone 2 "for the orientation convention of §7.5", and §7.5 states no orientation convention

An implementer reading the `*Needs:*` line of §7.4 (line 874 of `TauCetiRoadmap/ComplexManifolds/README.md`) is told to wait for Milestone 2 for something located in §7.5, but §7.5 (lines 890–907) defines the cotangent and canonical bundles, mentions no orientation, and its own `*Needs:*` line (line 899) does not list Milestone 2. Nothing in the statement or route of §7.4 uses an orientation either: the tangent map, the quotient line bundle, the transverse-chart cocycle and the comparison with the divisor bundle are all complex-linear. The edge comes from the sentence of the build guide (lines 53–54) that §§7.4–7.5 use "the orientation convention of Milestone 2", which the README on main also has, so the dependency itself is the author's to keep.

The smallest change is to say in the clause what is used, for instance "Milestone 2 (the ordered real coordinates `(Re z₁, Im z₁, …)` of transverse charts, where an orientation of the normal bundle is compared)", or to move the clause to the `*Needs:*` line of §7.5 if that is where the convention is meant to enter; either way the clause would name something the cited section contains.
