## Source issues

- **Error** in FKP22 at Lemma 3.7, pp.15–16: the source says the fixed fields of all the marked dual classes, over all new primes and basis indices, are strongly linearly disjoint over K; it should be assumed that the conjugates of inertia generate the whole product of the images, which fails for trivial SL₂ image over ℚ; affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in FKP22 at §9, the paragraph before Proposition 9.1, p.41: the source says the diagonal character ψ_i has Hodge–Tate weight minus (ξ_{τ,n−i+1}+i−1), and that this is less than the next; it should be plus that number, as the similitude κ^{1−2n} and the stated increase require; affects nothing.
- **Error** in FKP22 at Example A.16, p.50, used in Example 6.8, p.31: the source says the separating character of the Siegel Levi of GSp₄ is the determinant of the GL₂ factor; it should be that determinant divided by the similitude, the determinant being c² on the centre; affects a stated result.
- **Gap** in FKP22 at the proof of Proposition 9.1, p.43: the source says compatibility at the single Steinberg place gives a regular unipotent in every ℓ-adic member; it should use a second Steinberg place of another residue characteristic for ℓ equal to that of the first; affects the proof.
- **Gap** in FKP22 at the proof of Corollary 6.6, p.30: the source says the twisted adjoint of the new extension has no invariants because the two semisimplifications agree; it should use that the joint image with the cyclotomic character contains that of the Levi-valued representation; affects the proof.
- **Error** in FKP22 at Remark 8.2, p.38: the source says absolute irreducibility gives the hypotheses of Theorem 8.1 for all p large in terms of n; it should let the bound depend on the constant field, since for q≡−1 mod p an induced representation has a trivial constituent in the twisted adjoint; affects nothing.
