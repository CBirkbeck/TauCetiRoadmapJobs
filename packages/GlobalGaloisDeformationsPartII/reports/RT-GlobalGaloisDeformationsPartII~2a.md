1. **F01** (P3) The new Levi Check excludes absolutely irreducible groups incorrectly

   **Location:** packages/GlobalGaloisDeformationsPartII/README.md:165: “containing an element of order p in a unipotent subgroup of L(k), the projection is not absolutely L-irreducible”.

   **Claim:** Having such an element prevents absolute L-irreducibility.

   **Witness:** Take G=SL₃, L=S(GL₂×GL₁), k=𝔽_p, and Γ={(diag(A,1),1): A∈SL₂(𝔽_p)}. Its natural two-dimensional Levi module is absolutely irreducible, but I+E₁₂ has order p.

   **Exact repair text:** “For Γ generated by (diag(I+E₁₂,1),1), the projection fixes the first coordinate line and lies in a proper parabolic of L, so it is not absolutely L-irreducible. Merely containing a nontrivial unipotent element does not imply this.”

   **Repair test:** For the cyclic subgroup the invariant line is ke₁. For the full SL₂(𝔽_p), upper and lower unipotents have distinct unique invariant lines ke₁ and ke₂; there is no common invariant line, even over k̄. Both groups contain the stated order-p element. This Check was introduced by the repair of a-F03.

   **Exact repair text:** “The degree-one uniform torsion theorem of README §4.4 is stated below on a compact Hausdorff finite-dimensional analytic group over ℚ_p with semisimple GroupLieAlgebra, a finite extension coefficient DVR O, and a finite free continuous O-linear lattice V: there exists a natural a such that, for every r≥1, multiplication by pᵃ kills every class of the actual continuous H¹(Γ,V/ϖʳV).
