## Draft pull request body

This roadmap extends GlobalGaloisDeformations, which stops at deformation problems, obstruction presentations, restriction, twists and Taylor–Wiles data, with the G-valued lifting theory of Fakhruddin–Khare–Patrikis for residual representations of global Galois groups that may be reducible or have prime-to-p components. It builds cyclic prime-field ramification classes, doubling and controlled finite-level towers, relative Selmer images and their annihilation at auxiliary primes, and assembles a characteristic-zero fixed-multiplier lift ramified at finitely many places with prescribed local components, arbitrary approximation and full derived congruence image; a lattice change then recovers Levi-valued residual representations with derived-dense lifts. The applications are actual (extension-sensitive) modularity of reducible odd two-dimensional residual representations over ℚ at every weight and at Serre weight with prime-to-p level, the finiteness of actual reductions at fixed weight and level that shows why fixed-level lifting methods cannot reach that theorem, automorphy of reducible residual representations over global function fields through the Lafforgue correspondence, and ordinary symplectic lifts with two Steinberg places feeding the Allen–Newton–Thorne lifting theorem and a strictly pure compatible system. The general reducible results carry an explicit arithmetic selection hypothesis (§3.2) that the source's Lemma 3.7 does not establish, with the refuting example recorded as a Check; the irreducible number-field theorem (§6.6) and the symplectic applications rest on an independent route without it. The uniform finite-coefficient torsion theorem for compact p-adic analytic groups with semisimple Lie algebra (FKP19 Lemma B.1, degree one) is the one headline statable at the pins and is stated in `Suggested.lean`.

Dependencies still in pull requests, by name: GlobalGaloisDeformations, ArithmeticGaloisDuality, LocalGaloisDeformationRings, IntegralHeckeAndGaloisDeterminants, ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations, NoncommutativeAndEquivariantIwasawa, AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups, ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces, FunctionFieldArithmetic, AlgebraicModularFormsAndSerreWeights, AutomorphicGaloisRepresentations, AutomorphicGaloisRepresentationsPartII, ClassicalSerreModularity, OrdinaryAutomorphicFormsAndModularityLifting, GL2ModularityLifting, AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactors, ModularityAndLanglandsExtensions, AutomorphicSpectralTheory.
