## Mathematical corrections and unsupported inputs

The following changes supplement the accepted source corrections without changing the accepted endpoints.

- The unconditional all-primes/all-basis field-independence assertion is not asserted. The joint-inertia criterion remains §2.9, with a genuine extra generation premise. Section 3.2 requires a quantitative arithmetic replacement for arbitrary repeated constituents. Its conditional estimate explicitly has 0<ε≤1 and applies to a positive-upper-density family inside the common Frobenius prescription. Four independent binary failures have proportion 1/16; four repetitions of one binary test have proportion 1/2. This replacement is an unsolved target, not something supplied by Chebotarev or by the source’s false lemma. The lifting endpoints retain their intended full hypotheses.
- ArithmeticGaloisDuality now has prime-to-characteristic function-field Poitou–Tate and Sha duality in R02.4.5. The broad old gap was narrowed to function-field Greenberg–Wiles and nested-local-condition numerics at R02.5.1. Its nonempty-S hypothesis is retained, by harmless unramified enlargement of S. Compact lattice comparisons are imported from R02.1.2–R02.1.3 instead of requested again.
- A torus has no roots. Its Q_N is empty, of density 0, so the auxiliary-prime positive-density API requires positive derived rank. The torus lift itself is exactly its fixed quotient μ. Strictness of the central series is explicit; the trivial group has a zero-length series.
- The three-character joint-field control was recomputed. For independent order-10 κ̄,ψ, the ratio fields ψ, κ̄ψ, κ̄ψ² have cyclotomic intersection degrees 1,1,2 and remaining cyclotomic degrees 10,10,5. Their compositum contains κ̄ and leaves degree 1. The previously convenient claim that all three separate intersections have degree 1 is false.
- Hodge–Tate signs are consistent with HT(κ)=−1. The Siegel example has weights {0,1−a,w,w+a−1}, with w outside {0,1−a,2−2a}; a=2,w=2 gives {0,−1,2,3}. Both its main statement and its acceptance clause use these signs.
- The uniform reduction counterexample uses the finite analytic group C₃ acting by ζ₃ on ℤ₃[ζ₃], whose Lie algebra is zero and hence semisimple. Its H¹ has order 3 at every level, and the reduction is nonzero; its residual invariant dimension 1 explains the missing hypothesis. A ℤ₃ example with nonsemisimple Lie algebra would not isolate that hypothesis.
- The generic character-extension statement uses all its hypotheses explicitly: an infinite field, a finite group, the two actual H¹ dimensions, the two-dimensional middle representation, and the equivariant injection and quotient. On S₃ in characteristic 3 the sign line has H¹ dimension 1; it correctly fails the strict dimension threshold.
- Automorphic GLₙ and GSp₂ₙ applications require n≥1. Residue norms satisfy q≥2. Positive coefficient levels, odd p, finite residue fields and original-uniformizer accuracy are explicit. No zero-ring or q=1 instance is silently admitted.
- PotentialAutomorphyInfrastructurePartII’s readable design now includes PL.7/source-primitive-ant-lifting. Its primitivity is tested on the residual semisimplification over the algebraic closure; that is the consumed source-primitive variant. Strong primitivity of the older node is not silently inferred from the noninduction of an unsemisimplified representation. The supplier already states a finite-place Steinberg variant, so that construction is not requested again. The missing weight-zero ι-ordinarity export is recorded precisely.
- The reference historically keyed Ribet10 is Bellaïche’s *Ribet’s lemma, generalizations, and pseudocharacters*, Clay lectures from 2009. The design’s Ribet authorship and different title were corrected while retaining its lifetime key.

- GL2ModularityLifting §10.15 includes CC1–CC5 and LC. Pan22 Theorem 1.0.2 does not add them, and the intended modularity endpoint cannot inherit such extra conditional assumptions. The exact unconditional export is now gap:unconditional-pan-fontaine-mazur. At p=3 the local ratio 1 is allowed, while ω is excluded; the quadratic character of ℚ(√−2) remains the positive control.
- The Borel criterion uses the semisimplification of the actual residual action on the Lie centralizer, rather than an action of the full chosen torus. In sl₃(𝔽₁₁), diag(2,6,1) sends E₁₂+E₂₃ to 4E₁₂+6E₂₃; their bracket is −2E₁₃≠0, so the two-dimensional centralizer is not preserved by that torus point. The simultaneous root-position theorem explicitly requires both A and B nonzero; either zero gives the failed value 0.
- The density replacement includes the simultaneous finite tuples used by doubling, not only one coordinate. Positive upper density of the selected tuple family is a separate required part of that construction. Upper limits 1 and 1 for alternating complementary 0–1 sequences give product upper limit 0, so coordinatewise upper limits cannot be multiplied without a common density argument. This remains part of the intrinsic G1 proof obligation.
- Every use of minimally ramified local symplectic lifts retains LocalGaloisDeformationRings §2.28's inertia-splitting, coefficient-square-root and pure-nilpotent/smooth-centralizer hypotheses through its allowed coefficient extensions. The ANT input explicitly retains semisimplicity of the characteristic-zero representation; the full derived image here makes it absolutely irreducible.

The source cannot support the unconditional independence used by the printed doubling proof, automatic polarized-tangent agreement, an all-coefficient monodromy conclusion from BLGGT14 Lemma 5.3.1 alone, the omitted similitude factors, or a uniform inference from irreducibility before cyclotomic restriction. These are respectively explicit own targets, supplier obligations or corrected hypotheses. The ordinary symplectic endpoint prescribes two Steinberg places of distinct residue characteristics: at coefficient characteristic 2 use a place over 5, and at coefficient characteristic 5 use a place over 2.
