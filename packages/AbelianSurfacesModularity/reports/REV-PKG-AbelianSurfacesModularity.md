## Source issues

- **Misprint** in BCGP25 at proof of Lemma 9.1.6, item (2), p. 190: the source says the square of any lift of a 2C (respectively 2D) element is I (respectively −I); it should be −I for 2C and I for 2D, as the lift polynomials (x²+1)² and (x²−1)² force; affects nothing.
- **Error** in BCGP25 at Lemma 9.2.13, pp. 196–197: the source says unramified mod-3 action, class 2C or 6G and image in S₅(b) force purely toric reduction, but its good non-ordinary curve Y of §9.2.16 satisfies all three; it should be stated with the extra hypothesis that A has bad or semistable ordinary reduction, which the proof uses; affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in BCGP25 at §9.2.16, p. 198: the source says the Frobenius polynomial of Y at 2 is x⁴+2x²+1; it should be x⁴+2x²+4, the Weil polynomial given by the point counts 3 over 𝔽₂ and 9 over 𝔽₄; affects nothing.
- **Error** in BCGP25 at proof of Lemma 9.2.14, p. 197: the source says the mod-3 representation of the Tate curve X is trivial and Frobenius on A[3] is I⊗R, which has multiplier 1; it should be diag(1,−1)⊗R, since X[3] ≅ μ₃ ⊕ ℤ/3, with multiplier −1 and the same class 2C; affects the proof.
- **Error** in BCGP25 at proof of Lemma 9.2.14, p. 198: the source says the mod-2 Frobenius U⊗R has centralizer of order 16, hence is a double transposition in S₅(b); it should be centralizer of order 48 for the product pairing, a triple transposition fixing no odd theta characteristic, so outside S₅(b); affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in BCGP25 at proof of Lemma 9.2.7, p. 195: the source says the absence of eigenvalue ratio −1 gives vanishing of H² of the mod-3 representation itself; it should be vanishing of H² of its adjoint representation, which is what the ratio condition controls; affects nothing.
