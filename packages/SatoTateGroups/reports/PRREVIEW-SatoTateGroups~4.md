## R02 (P3) — Frobenius semisimplicity is used in 9.2 and called "expected" in 9.6

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/SatoTateGroups/README.md:2217-2221` (9.2, homotheties) and `:2386-2387` (9.6). Quotes: "the smallest algebraic subgroup containing `ρ(Frob_𝔭)` is diagonalizable with character group `ℤ^{2g}/R`"; "Remark 2.10 records that `g_𝔭` is expected to be semisimple".

Claim: the homothety route rests on the diagonalizability of the group generated by `ρ(Frob_𝔭)`, which is its semisimplicity; 9.6 then says semisimplicity is only expected.

Witness: for an abelian variety the Frobenius endomorphism `π` of the good reduction `A_𝔭` has `π† = q/π` under a Rosati involution, so `ℚ[π]` is a commutative Rosati-stable subalgebra of `End⁰(A_𝔭)`, hence semisimple by positivity (A6.14): `π` acts semisimply on `V_ℓ`. Serre's own argument (bdba7daa lines 4203–4206, p. 114) avoids the question by using the minimal algebraic subgroup containing `g_p`, which contains the diagonalizable group generated by the semisimple part. FKRS Remark 2.10 (d5e70f9e line 448) is written for the general motivic setting.

Repair: in 9.2 say "the diagonalizable group generated by the semisimple part of `ρ(Frob_𝔭)`" (Serre's route, no hypothesis), or state and cite the semisimplicity of Frobenius for abelian varieties (Rosati stability of `ℚ[π]`, A6.14); in 9.6 replace "expected to be semisimple" by the theorem, so that the Jordan-decomposition clause is visibly vacuous for abelian varieties.
