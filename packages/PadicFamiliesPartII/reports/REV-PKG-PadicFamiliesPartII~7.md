1. **F01** (P1) The partial Frobenius has the wrong polarization

   **Location.** `packages/PadicFamiliesPartII/README.md:783`, §5.2: “with polarization descended from `p² x_w λ`”.

   **Claim.** This polarization makes the quotient an object of the same Hilbert–Siegel moduli problem.

   **Witness.** Take F = ℚ, x_w = p and a principally polarized abelian surface. The quotient isogeny has degree p². Descending p³λ gives polarization degree p¹²/p⁴ = p⁸, not 1. BCGP21 line 6126 has p²/x_w.

   **Exact repair.** Replace the factor by `(p²/x_w) λ`. State that x_w has valuation one at w and zero at the other places above p, consistently with §5.3. Add this degree calculation as a Check. The source PDF, Definition 5.6.2, printed p. 126, was rendered and read: the fraction bar is essential and is lost in the linear extraction. This is a roadmap error, not a source error.

   **Repair tested.** For p = 3, the original gives degree 6561 on the quotient. The repaired pullback is 3λ, of degree 81; dividing by the square of the isogeny degree, 9², gives 1. In degree d, the quotient isogeny has degree p^(4d−2); the repaired polarization upstairs has degree p^(8d−4), again giving quotient degree 1. The wrong factor gives degree p⁸ instead. This calculation concerns the abelian-scheme isogeny, not the distinct degree p³ of the map on the moduli space.

3. **F03** (P2) The two-root algebra need not be local when M is zero

   **Location.** `packages/PadicFamiliesPartII/README.md:909`, §5.9: “the algebra E generated by the two operators is local Artinian”.

   **Claim.** This holds for every finite-dimensional stable subspace M satisfying the two nilpotence assumptions.

   **Witness.** At p = 3 take M = 0, α = 1, β = 2. Both shifted operators are zero and nilpotent, but E = End(0) is the zero ring. Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/Defs.lean:30 requires a local ring to be nontrivial.

   **Exact repair.** Treat M = 0 separately: the doubling and refinement maps have zero domain and are injective. Assert the local-Artinian and lifted-root construction for M ≠ 0. Add both cases as Checks. BCGP21 Remark 5.8.7, p. 135, lines 6547–6552, also omits this boundary hypothesis; copying its scope does not cure the issue.

   **Repair tested.** For nonzero M, scalars embed into End(M). The commuting nilpotent deviations generate a nilpotent ideal, and the quotient of E by it is the coefficient field, so E is local Artinian. On a one-dimensional space over 𝔽̄₃, take T = 0, U = 2; then E = 𝔽̄₃, its maximal ideal is zero, and the roots 1 and 2 differ by a unit. The zero case gives the unique injection 0 → 0 without claiming that the zero ring is local.

4. **F04** (P3) Three Hilbert weight Checks violate the standing parity

   **Location.** `packages/PadicFamiliesPartII/README.md:1002`, `:1004`, `:1022`: “At gap `k_v − l_v = 3`”; “at `k = 3`”; “Weight `(5, 2)`”.

Keep the theorem's general inequality “gap ≥ 3”; on the permitted lattice it already means gap ≥ 4.

   **Repair tested.** Sym² of a rank-two bundle has basis x², xy, y² and rank 3, including at p = 3; Sym⁰ has rank 1. Neither determinant twists nor the invertible boundary ideal changes these ranks.
