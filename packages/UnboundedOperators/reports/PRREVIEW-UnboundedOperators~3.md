## R03 (P3) — Teschl's Theorem 2.18 is cited for the four spectrum alternatives it does not state

**Location:** README `:437` ("prove the spectrum is either all of `ℂ`, the closed upper half-plane, the closed lower half-plane, or a subset of `ℝ`; … (Teschl (2009), Theorem 2.18 and Lemma 2.20, p.77)"); `Suggested.lean:71–74` (docstring "Spectrum alternatives … (Teschl, Theorem 2.18)"); and the References entry `:1330` ("Lemmas 6.18–6.19").

**Claim:** Teschl's Theorem 2.18 states that a symmetric operator is self-adjoint iff its spectrum is real (and the semibounded variant with the resolvent bounds); Lemma 2.20 gives reality of eigenvalues. Neither states that the spectrum of a closed symmetric operator is `ℂ`, a closed half-plane or real, and the word "half-plane" occurs nowhere in the 2009 text. The alternative is true (the defect dimension is constant on each open half-plane, Problem 2.28 gives the kernel map, and Lemma 2.3 gives self-adjointness from two surjective shifts), but the locator covers only the iff and the eigenvalue clause. Separately, the References line calls 6.19 a Lemma; it is Theorem 6.19 (Weyl), as §3.2 correctly says.

**Witness:** `teschl line 3170–3177` (Theorem 2.18, printed p.77: "A is self-adjoint if and only if σ(A) ⊆ ℝ …"), `teschl line 3187` (Lemma 2.20), `teschl line 3453–3456` (Problem 2.28), `teschl line 6321` (Theorem 6.19); a case-insensitive search of the whole text for "half" returns nothing. Instance: `S = −i d/dx` on `H₀¹(0,∞)`: `ker(S*−z̄) = span e^{i z̄ x}` is nonzero exactly for `Im z < 0`, so the resolvent set is the open upper half-plane and the spectrum the closed lower half-plane, which is the third alternative and is not a consequence of Theorem 2.18 alone.

**Repair:** Keep Theorem 2.18 and Lemma 2.20 for the iff and the eigenvalue clause, and cite the alternative to a source that states it, for example Reed–Simon II, Theorem X.1, p.136 (paywalled: record it `uncertified` by locator), or state in §4.2 that it is proved here from the half-plane constancy of §4.1 (Teschl Problem 2.28, p.84) and Lemma 2.3, p.63; correct the Lean docstring the same way, and write "Lemma 6.18 and Theorem 6.19" in the References.
