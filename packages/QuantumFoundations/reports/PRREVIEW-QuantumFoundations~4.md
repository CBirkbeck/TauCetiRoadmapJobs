## R02 (P3) — §0.2 has no negative-control Check for `¬FiniteDimensional ℂ B`

Location: `README.md` lines 113–117 (the three Checks of §0.2) and `Suggested.lean` line 75, `(hB : ¬ FiniteDimensional ℂ B)`.

Claim: the Checks test the Hilbert case, `ℓ¹`, and the trivial-intersection hypothesis; nothing shows that the infinite-dimensionality hypothesis of `kakutaniMackey_complex` (Pankov line 319, "infinite-dimensional complex Banach space") cannot be dropped, although §0.3 shows the analogous sharpness for the real rank hypothesis.

Witness: on `ℂ²` take an involutive field automorphism `σ ≠ conj` of ℂ (`σ = τ∘conj∘τ⁻¹` for a discontinuous automorphism τ with `τ(ℝ) ≠ ℝ`); its fixed field `F` has `[ℂ:F] = 2`, so `F` is real closed, `ℂ = F(√−1)` and `σ(a+b√−1) = a−b√−1` for `a,b ∈ F`. Put `h(x,y) = σ(x₁)y₁ + σ(x₂)y₂` and `X⊥ = {y : h(x,y) = 0 ∀x ∈ X}`. Then `h(y,x) = σ(h(x,y))`, so `h` is reflexive; `X ↦ X⊥` is order-reversing and involutive (finite dimension, `h` nondegenerate); and `h(x,x) = Σ(a_i² + b_i²) ≠ 0` for `x ≠ 0` in `F`, so `X ∩ X⊥ = {0}`. No inner product induces it: by `projectiveCollineation_unique_scalar` (rank `2 ≥ 2`) the automorphism of any semilinear map inducing the point map `ℂx ↦ ((ℂx)⊥)⁰` is determined, and it is `σ ≠ conj`.

Repair: add this as a fourth Check of §0.2 ("in finite dimension at least two the complex statement fails: …"), so the hypothesis `¬FiniteDimensional ℂ B` has its witness as the rank hypotheses of §0.1 and §0.3 have theirs.
