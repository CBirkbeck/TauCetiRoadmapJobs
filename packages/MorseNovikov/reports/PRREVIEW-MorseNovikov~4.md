**The reading of `zeroDet` at the pin.** `tangentBundleCore` (`Mathlib/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Tangent.lean:87–93`) has `coordChange i j x := fderivWithin 𝕜 (j.1.extend I ∘ (i.1.extend I).symm) (range I) (i.1.extend I x)`, and the fibre-bundle chart at a point of the total space is the trivialization at its base point followed by the base chart. So in the chart of `TangentBundle I M` at `(p, v p)` the section `x ↦ (x, v x)` reads `x̂ ↦ (x̂, C(x)(v x))` with `C(x)` the coordinate change from the chart at `x` to the chart at `p`, which is the vector field written in chart-`p` coordinates. Its derivative at `p̂` has first component the identity and second component `d(C(·)v)(p̂) = C(p)(dv_p) + (dC)(v p) = dv_p` when `v p = 0`, since `C(p) = id`. Hence `zeroDet I v p = det dv_p` in the chart at `p` at every zero, chart-independent there because the linearisation at a zero transforms by conjugation; at a non-zero the value is chart-dependent junk that neither `hnd` nor the sum reads. `I.Boundaryless` makes `range I = univ`, so the `fderivWithin` is the full derivative. `mfderiv` is `Mathlib/Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/Defs.lean:336`; `ContinuousLinearMap.snd ℝ E E : E × E →L[ℝ] E` composes with it because `TangentSpace I.tangent _ = E × E` definitionally.
