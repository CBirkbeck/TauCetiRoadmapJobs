## R01 (P3) — `[P.IsReduced]` is not used by the ordinary Stembridge step

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/RootSystemsPartII/Suggested.lean` line 36 (`[P.IsReduced] [P.IsCrystallographic] (b : P.Base)`), and `README.md` §1.2 ("for a finite reduced crystallographic real root pairing").

Claim: the statement and the proof route of `stembridge_step` do not use reducedness. The route uses only the root-sum theorem for a negative pairing (`RootPairing.root_add_root_mem_of_pairingIn_neg`, whose section carries `[Finite ι] [CharZero R] [P.IsCrystallographic] [IsDomain R]` and no `IsReduced`, `Finite/Lemmas.lean` lines 43, 76, 91, 228), the nonpositivity of off-diagonal Cartan entries, and finiteness; Rapoport's proof (Rap00 line 195 through line 214) argues in the same way for the relative coroot system of an unramified group, which may be non-reduced.

Witness: `BC₁` with positive roots `α, 2α`, `λ = 0`, `μ = 2α`: the budget is `2α`, the maximal eligible root is `2α` itself, `λ + 2α = μ` has simple-coroot value `⟨2α, α∨⟩ = 4 ≥ 0` and remaining budget 0; the step exists and nothing in the argument used reducedness.

Repair: the author's to take or leave. Either drop `[P.IsReduced]` from `stembridge_step` and say in §1.2 that reducedness is not needed, or keep it as the standing convention of the roadmap (every later target does need it through the fold). No change to the truth of any statement.
