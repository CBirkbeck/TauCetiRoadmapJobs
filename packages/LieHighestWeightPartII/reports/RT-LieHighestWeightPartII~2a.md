   **Claim:** ReductiveGroups Layer 1 supplies this representation-generation theorem. The mathematical implication used here is correct, but the claimed supplier target is absent at the version this package may consume.

   **Witness:** `TauCetiRoadmap/ReductiveGroups/README.md:1301` supplies faithful embeddings and coefficient generation; line 1332 supplies reconstruction adapters.    The native faithful-comodule criterion generates the *coordinate algebra*, not all comodules under tensor operations. The near-match `TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/TensorGeneration.lean:173` is Burnside–Brauer for finite groups. ReductiveGroups is already on main, so an open revision cannot supply an unstated result. An earlier available owner is `TauCetiRoadmap/MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles/README.md`, §6.3, “Generation, functoriality and finite type”: its tensor-generation and closed-immersion criteria, together with §6.2's coefficient reconstruction, give the needed consequence. That owner precedes this package in `merge-order/ORDER.txt` (positions 92 and 241).

   **Exact repair text:** replace the parenthetical and complete the proof input as follows:

   > Apply MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles §§6.2–6.3 to the rigid tensor subcategory generated by a faithful comodule V. Its reconstructed coordinate Hopf algebra is the subalgebra generated by the coefficients of V and their antipode images; ReductiveGroups Layer 1 identifies this algebra with the whole coordinate algebra. The reconstruction and subquotient criteria therefore imply that every finite-dimensional representation is a subquotient of a finite direct sum of tensor words in V and V∨. Products and inverses of eigenvalues whose absolute values belong to q^(ℤ/2) still have absolute values in q^(ℤ/2), and this property passes to subquotients.

   Add this exact contract under the supplier contracts:

   > From MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles §§6.2–6.3: coefficient reconstruction, tensor-generation and the closed-immersion/subquotient criterion, applied to the rigid tensor subcategory generated by a faithful finite-dimensional comodule in §5.3.

   Add `MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles §§6.2–6.3` to §5.3's *Needs* and Layer 5's Dependencies. No new Lean declaration is needed.
