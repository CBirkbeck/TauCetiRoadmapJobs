## R01 (P2) — §2.12(a) cites NoncommutativeAndEquivariantIwasawa for the two-character Eisenstein series `E_n(φ₁, φ₂)`, which that roadmap does not state; the Wiles-vanishing target here is stated on those series

**Location.** README 367 ("the Hilbert Eisenstein series `E_n(φ₁, φ₂)` of parallel weight `n`, spanning a complement of the cusp forms, with the constant-term formula … (Skinner–Wiles (3.10)), are NoncommutativeAndEquivariantIwasawa §6.1 step 0(c) and §6.22") and, in the same clause, "Wiles' vanishing: a linear combination of the Eisenstein series `E_n(φ, φ^{−1}ψ)`, over unramified characters `φ` of p-power order, all of whose constant terms … vanish, is zero"; README 96 (the supplier contract, same attribution).

**Claim.** The supplier provides the family `E_n(φ₁, φ₂)` for pairs of characters with `φ₁φ₂ = ψ`, spanning a complement of the cusp forms, so that the vanishing target is stated on supplied objects.

**Witness.** NoncommutativeAndEquivariantIwasawa at the head of its pull request, §6.1 step 0(c) (its README 2369): the series `G_{k,ε}` with expansion `2^{−r}L_Σ(ε, 1 − k) + Σ_μ (Σ_{𝔞∋μ} ε(g_𝔞)N𝔞^{k−1}) q^μ`, one character only, i.e. SW99's `E_n(1, ε)`; §6.22 (2910–2933) adds restriction, `U_β` and unit-cusp comparisons of these; §F.5 (2288–2310) constructs the form spaces, lattices, Hecke operators and the one-character family `E(1,χ)` (also PadicFamilies §5.10, its 2889), and IntegralIwasawaTheory §9.3 (its 1175–1185) uses only `W_k(ψ, 1)`. No two-character series and no complement-spanning statement occur in any of the three suppliers, and `roadmaps grep` for `E_n(φ₁, φ₂)` over main and the open heads hits only this README (367). Computed: the ideal coefficient of `E_n(φ₁, φ₂)` at a prime `𝔮` (Dirichlet series `L(s, φ₁)L(s − n + 1, φ₂)`, SW99 p. 47, sw99 1511–1513) is `φ₁(𝔮) + φ₂(𝔮)N𝔮^{n−1}`, while that of every `G_{n,ε}` is `1 + ε(𝔮)N𝔮^{n−1}`; the two agree at every `𝔮` only when `φ₁ = 1`. The vanishing target of this roadmap is stated for `φ ≠ 1` (p-power order, unramified), so its statement uses forms no supplier defines. In general `E_n(φ₁, φ₂)` is the `φ₁`-twist of the supplied `E_n(1, φ₁^{−1}φ₂)` on ideal coefficients, `Σ_{𝔞𝔟=𝔪} φ₁(𝔟)φ₂(𝔞)N𝔞^{n−1} = φ₁(𝔪)Σ_{𝔞|𝔪}(φ₁^{−1}φ₂)(𝔞)N𝔞^{n−1}`, but neither NEI §F.5 nor PadicFamilies §5.10 lists a character twist among their operations, so this identification is itself a target.

**Repair.** In §2.12(a) and the supplier contract (README 96), attribute to NoncommutativeAndEquivariantIwasawa only what it states: the one-character series `E_n(1, ε)` (its `G_{n,ε}`) with its constant terms at every cusp and the `q`-expansion principle. State here, as the third Eisenstein target of this roadmap, the series `E_n(φ₁, φ₂)` for `φ₁` unramified of `p`-power order and `φ₁φ₂ = ψ`: a form of parallel weight `n`, level `𝔫p` and nebentypus `ψ` whose ideal coefficients are `Σ_{𝔞𝔟=𝔪} φ₁(𝔟)φ₂(𝔞)N𝔞^{n−1}`, obtained from the supplied `E_n(1, φ₁^{−1}φ₂)` by the `φ₁`-twist of ideal coefficients and standard constants (Shimura, *The special values of the zeta functions associated with Hilbert modular forms*, Duke Math. J. 45 (1978), the Eisenstein series of §3, as SW99 p. 47 cite it), with the locator for its constant terms. Either delete "spanning a complement of the cusp forms" from the attribution (the decomposition `f = F + G` is used only inside SW99's proof of Proposition 3.18, which ClassicalSerreModularity `I.1/nearly-ordinary-hecke-algebras` owns) or state it here with Shimura's locator. Add a Check: at a prime `𝔮` with `φ₁(𝔮) ≠ 1`, the coefficient `φ₁(𝔮) + φ₂(𝔮)N𝔮^{n−1}` differs from `1 + ε(𝔮)N𝔮^{n−1}` for every `ε`, so the two-character series is not one of the supplied ones. Then the Wiles-vanishing target is stated on objects this roadmap owns, and the supplier contracts claim nothing the suppliers lack.
