1. **F01** (P1) A p-torsion module cannot surject onto the integral lattice

   **Location:** `packages/HilbertModularVarietiesAndShimuraCurvesPartII/README.md:887`, §4.4: “W₂ surjects onto a lattice τ′°” and “M∞(W₂) = M∞(τ′°)”.

   **Claim:** The proof identifies a quotient of L/pL with a nonzero integral lattice, and identifies their patched values. Both assertions are false as written. Here τ′° is finite free over O and has nonzero cosocle σ_v; the displayed W₂ is killed by p.

   **Witness:** pW₂=0, whereas τ′°[p]=0, so Hom_O(W₂,τ′°)=0. BHHMS23 line 4872, Proposition 8.3.3, p. 97, has an overbar on the target in the PDF; the extraction loses it.

   **Exact repair:** Say that W₂ surjects onto τ′°/pτ′°, with kernel having no modular constituents. Replace the patched equality by M∞(W₂) ≅ M∞(τ′°/pτ′°) ≅ M∞(τ′°)/pM∞(τ′°). Apply §3.2 to this reduction. Keep the integral lattice τ′° in its existence statement. Check the same distinction at the subsequent use of Lemma 8.3.5. Add the negative control Hom_O(O/p,O)=0, while the identity O/p→O/p is surjective. This repair removes the contradiction on that instance and matches the visually inspected source PDF, pp. 97–98; this is not an error in the printed source.

3. **F03** (P2) Residual weight support does not impose the integral central character

   **Location:** `packages/HilbertModularVarietiesAndShimuraCurvesPartII/README.md:764`, §3.3: “let σ° be any K-stable W(𝔽)-lattice in σ”.

   **Claim:** The listed hypotheses on the locally algebraic σ_i do not put σ° in the domain of the fixed-character functor of §1.4. Having modular constituents modulo p fixes only the residual central character. The statement needs the exact character ψ⁻¹ on O_Fv×.

   **Exact repair:** Require each σ_i, and hence σ°, to have central character ψ⁻¹|I_Fv under the stated Artin identification; equivalently require σ° to belong to §1.4's coefficient category. Add §1.4 to Needs and add the determinant-twist negative control. The tame type before twisting has the required Teichmüller character; the twist preserves every residual constituent and the smooth part but fails the new hypothesis at 30. BHHMS23 line 4421 explicitly fixes the domain character; Corollary 8.2.4 is inside that domain, not a theorem on arbitrary lattices with the same residual character.
