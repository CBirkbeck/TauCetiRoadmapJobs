## F03 (P3) — applied

**Verification.** Mok2012 Theorem 1, line 478 to line 479, and the Main Theorem, line 548 to line 550, state that the boundary components are abelian varieties. §8.2 stated only complex tori.

I took option (b). The roadmap now states what Mok proves, and AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A5.3 (`appellHumbert`, open, merge order line 42, earlier than this roadmap's place) supplies the needed step: a complex torus is algebraizable if and only if it admits a positive Riemann form, and a positive H gives an ample bundle whose third and higher powers give a projective embedding. Its factor of automorphy is α(λ) exp(πH(z,λ) + πH(λ,λ)/2), with H linear in the first variable.

The multiplier of §8.1 is exactly this factor, with H_b(u,a) = −(4/τ)⟨a,u⟩ and α = χ:
- the exponents agree term by term;
- E_b = Im H_b = (4/τ) Im⟨a,a′⟩ is integral because the commutator T_{−4Im⟨a,a′⟩} lies in ⟨T_τ⟩;
- the §8.1 law χ(a+a′) = χ(a)χ(a′)e^{−4πi Im⟨a,a′⟩/τ} is (−1)^{E_b}χ(a)χ(a′).

So L_b⁻¹ = L(−H_b, χ̄) is positive.

**Change.** §8.2: after "with normal bundle L_b" it adds "each T_b is the analytification of an abelian variety on which the conormal bundle L_b⁻¹ is ample", with the Appell–Humbert derivation, Mok2012 line 548 to line 550, and ComplexTori Milestone 1 for T_b. Two new Checks; *Needs:*, Dependencies, Scope, supplier contract, plan 8.2 and `suppliers` updated. Summary unchanged, now backed.

## F05 (P3) — applied differently

**Verification.** ℂ* = ⟨T_1⟩\𝔖 for n = 1, and ⟨T_1⟩ is not a lattice, so the Check was outside §10.5's hypothesis. The adjacent nodal-cubic Check ("compactifies ℂ* by one point") has the same defect.

**Why differently.** The review proposed PSL(2,ℤ), which has X_Γ = ℂ with one end. That instance is valid, but it no longer tests that two ends receive two distinct points, and it cannot carry the nodal-cubic Check, which needs X_Γ ≅ ℂ*. I used the image of Γ₀(2) instead. Its values:
- index 3;
- cusp classes ∞ and 0, of widths 1 and 2;
- one elliptic point, (1+i)/2, of order 2, stabilised by [[1,−1],[2,−1]];
- no point of order 3, since trace ±1 forces a, d odd, a contradiction;
- genus 1 + 3/12 − 1/4 − 2/2 = 0.

So X_Γ ≅ ℂ* with two cusp ends, and both Checks lie inside the theorem's hypotheses.

**Change.** §10.5: "ℙ¹ is the only … compactification of ℂ*" → the same for X_Γ ≅ ℂ*, Γ the image of Γ₀(2), with the values above; "The nodal cubic compactifies ℂ*" → "For the same Γ, the nodal cubic compactifies X_Γ ≅ ℂ*", one branch in each cusp end.
