## F03 (P3) — applied

**Verification.** On main, AdelicAlgebraicGroups §4.3 defines `Neat.IsNeatAut`: an α in `GL (Fin n) ℂ` is neat when every finite-order element of the subgroup generated by its eigenvalues is 1 (`TauCetiRoadmap/AdelicAlgebraicGroups/Suggested.lean`, namespace `Neat`). `Neat.IsNeatLevel` requires this for ρ(g) for every g in every `levelArithmetic x U`. At its open head, ShimuraVarieties §P.4 adopts `Neat.IsNeatLevel` for its levels, so "K_f neat" in §11.3 means §4.3's notion.

I recomputed the review's witness. For n = 2, γ = m·a₂ acts by (u,v) ↦ (2iu, 4v). In y = (z₀ − z₂, z₁, i(z₀ + z₂)) the form is Im(ȳ₀y₂) − |y₁|², which diag(1/2, i, 2) preserves. The eigenvalues of Ad(γ) are 1 (three times), ±2i, ±i/2, 4 and 1/4. None of them is a nontrivial root of unity, but 2i·(i/2) = −1 lies in the group they generate. So the two notions separate. The implication §4.3-neat ⇒ neat here holds, because a nontrivial root of unity is a nontrivial torsion element.

The §11.3 implication holds by AdelicAlgebraicGroups §4.3's eigenvalue theorem. Apart from the 1 on the scalars, the eigenvalues of Ad(ι pr_H γ̃) are those of the adjoint action of γ̃ on the ideal 𝔥 ⊗ ℂ, which is an algebraic representation of 𝐆^ad over ℂ.

**Change.**
- Conventions: a sentence relating the two notions, saying that §4.3's is strictly stronger and that only the condition here is used.
- §6.4: a new Check with γ, including the computed eigenvalues.
- §11.3: "neat when K_f is neat" → "neat in the sense of the Conventions when K_f is neat in the sense of AdelicAlgebraicGroups §4.3 (`Neat.IsNeatLevel`)", plus a two-line proof.
