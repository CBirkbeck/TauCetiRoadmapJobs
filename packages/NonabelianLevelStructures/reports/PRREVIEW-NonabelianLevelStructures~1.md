## Source issues

- **Error** in CHEN2024 at Remark 2.2.4, p. 324: the source says the divisor is reduced exactly when the base is; it should be required that the divisor surject onto the base (an unramified cover over a non-reduced base has empty divisor); affects nothing.
- **Error** in CHEN2024 at proof of Proposition 3.2.2(b), p. 353: the source says the maps forgetting distinct marked points are proper; it should be that they are smooth and surjective with geometrically connected fibres, which is what the connectedness step needs; affects the proof.
- **Error** in CHEN2024 at Definition 4.1.2 and Lemma 4.4.4(g), pp. 361 and 370: the source takes the quotient map of a glued cover to be flat; it should be finite only, since at a ramified balanced node the fibre has length 3 over generic rank 2; affects a stated result.
- **Misprint** in CHEN2024 at Proposition 4.4.3(d), p. 369: the source says the loop lies in the fundamental group based at t∞; it should be based at t₀; affects nothing.
- **Error** in CHEN2024 at §4.11, p. 388: the source says d′ = 2 for the dihedral group of order 2k, k odd; it should be d′ = 1, the centralizer of a generating rotation being the rotation subgroup; affects nothing.
- **Gap** in CHEN2024 at Lemma 4.12.2, proof, p. 390: the source says a power of one cyclic generator meets the other cyclic group trivially when j > 0; it should be argued otherwise, since the bound on the intersection does not give this; affects the proof.
- **Misprint** in CHEN2024 at Proposition 5.2.16(d), proof, p. 402: the source says absolute irreducibility is equivalent to commutator trace equal to 2; it should be different from 2; affects nothing.
- **Gap** in CHEN2024 at Proposition 5.2.16(d), proof, p. 402: the source says both generators of a dihedral-type image are projective involutions; it should also treat a rotation with a reflection, where the reflection and the product are involutions; affects the proof.
- **Misprint** in CHEN2024 at Remark 5.2.21, p. 404: the source gives the matrix of γ∞ with upper-right entry −1; it should be +1, in the convention of the two other matrices; affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in CHEN2024 at Lemma 5.3.1, proof, case (3), p. 408: the source says the degenerate nonsplit slice is empty; it should be the single point (a, 0, 0); affects nothing.
- **Misprint** in CHEN2024 at Proposition 5.6.1(a), p. 418: the source gives the number of points over j = 0 as (D − 3)/3; it should be (D + 2)/3, one fixed point and cycles of length 3; affects nothing.
- **Error** in CHEN2024 at Theorem 5.6.4, p. 419 (and Theorem 1.2.10, p. 310): the source says finitely many elliptic curves over K carry such a structure; it should be finitely many j-invariants, quadratic twists sharing the fibre; affects a stated result.
- **Error** in CHEN2024 at Proposition 6.4.1, case (E4), p. 428: the source lists S₅ among the exceptional projective images; it should be S₄; affects a stated result.
