   - In §0.3, after the sentence ending "with ramification indices the orbit lengths.", insert:
     "Prove also the Galois correspondence for the elliptic stack. For a geometric elliptic curve E
     over k with a positively oriented basis of π₁(E ∖ O), the fibre functor at E is an equivalence
     from representable finite étale covers of 𝓜(1)_k to finite sets with an action of
     Out⁺(F₂) ≅ SL₂(ℤ); connected covers correspond to transitive sets, hence to finite-index
     subgroups up to conjugacy; the automorphism group of a point of the cover of a subgroup Γ is the

     component of M((ℤ/3)²)_k. Its fibre is an orbit of 24 of the 48 surjections ℤ² → (ℤ/3)², a
