1. **F01** (P2) The maximal-ideal blow-up Check needs positive dimension

   **Location:** `packages/DirectSummandsAndBigCohenMacaulay/README.md:1413`: “the blow-up of a regular local ring of dimension d at its maximal ideal, RΓ(X₀, O) = A₀”.

   **Claim:** This describes the maximal-ideal blow-up as an example of the theorem without restricting d. Regular local rings include fields.

   **Witness:** Take A₀=k, d=0 and m=0. Its Rees algebra has degree-zero part k and all positive parts zero. Its Proj is empty, so RΓ=0, whereas A₀=k≠0.

   Thus this blow-up is not surjective and the map k→0 cannot have a left inverse. The theorem at line 1402 correctly assumes surjectivity; the example must explicitly respect it. The same boundary is recorded in `TauCetiRoadmap/DeformationAndDerivedPatchingAlgebraPartII/README.md:1560` and its zero-ideal Check at line 1570.

   **Exact repair:** Restrict this Check to d≥1 and add the negative control: for a field the blow-up at its zero maximal ideal is empty, so the proper-surjective hypothesis fails. Retest d=1: the maximal ideal is generated by a nonzerodivisor, its blow-up is the identity and RΓ=A₀. Retest d=2 with the existing k⟦X,Y⟧ example: H⁰=A₀ and Hⁱ=0 for i>0. Leave the headline theorem unchanged.
