## Source issues

- **Error** in ANDRE18B at §0.1 statement (4), printed p. 72: the monomial equivalence "`(x₁⋯x_d)ᵐ ∈ (x₁ⁿ,…,x_dⁿ)` iff `m ≥ n`" is asserted for every local ring; it should assume `d ≥ 1` (for `d = 0` the empty product `1` is never in the zero ideal); affects a stated result.
