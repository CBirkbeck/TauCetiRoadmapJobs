## Corrections and unsupported conclusions

The accepted conservative correction is retained: for m=(k−1)q+r the stratum cutoff is 2dn−4qδ+4(r+1), giving +4(k−1), not +4. For k=3,d=4,n=24,m=5 the values are 192 versus the printed 188. The exact ceiling threshold includes the last odd-length stratum. The strict irreducibility condition is tδ>k−1; neither equality tδ=k−1 nor the printed sharp equality tδ=1 reaches total degree zero. The READMEs' numerical worked examples are recomputed below.

The prime radical in Lean asserts irreducibility of the mapping scheme without adding reducedness. All coefficients generate the ideal, including the leading coefficient before incidence is imposed. The exact-degree assertion for test algebras now explicitly excludes the zero algebra: its sole tuple has all coefficients zero, although its empty spectrum still gives the unique functorial point. The last odd-length full stage uses its ambient dimension L≤2⌈L/2⌉, rather than applying the Padé-chart dimension theorem outside its range. The excess-dimension bound moves kd to the dimension side; this avoids a junk natural subtraction when N<k. The top a-degree of the residue phase substitutes the lower-coefficient variables into f₀; it is not all of f₀ evaluated on the tuple containing the fixed leading term.

The README cannot support an unconditional sharp minor bound, equality-endpoint irreducibility, scalar −Id on a nonsplit primitive extension, an unconditional compact-open double-loop equivalence, a global stronger topology inferred from a local collar formula, a trace on a singular scheme inferred from smooth purity, or unconditional first-page degeneration. These are separated into precise proof problems, supplier contracts or conditional consequences, as the accepted design requires.
