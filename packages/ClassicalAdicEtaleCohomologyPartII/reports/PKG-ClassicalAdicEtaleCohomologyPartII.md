The generated skeleton and worklist were saved in job scratch before prose expansion.

## Corrections and supplier proof inputs

The old mnemonic H0/H3/H5/P8, DD.0 and AdicSpacesPartII F0/R2/R3 citations were resolved to actual supplier sections. Formal/coherent geometry is AdicSpacesPartIII; generic cotangent and derived completion are DerivedModuleAlgebra; affinoid finite-stage descent includes PerfectoidSpaces §P5.13.4. They cover valuation models, finite branched covers, proper generic smoothness, valuation coherent duality, valuation properness, enhanced proper hyperdescent, general nearby-cycle coherence, integral toric field extension, and quasi-excellent scheme direct images. These are declarations of required supply, not claims that the missing supplier statements are already available.
