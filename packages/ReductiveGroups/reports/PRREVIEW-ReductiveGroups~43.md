## R02 (P3) — the hypothesis of `exists_conjugate_eq_of_isMaximalTorus` is described as a false universal statement

Re-raised: R01 of the previous review; the passage is unchanged.

Location: `README.md:575–578` ("two maximal tori are conjugate provided every diagonalizable closed subgroup conjugates into a distinguished maximal torus `D` (the hypotheses `hcontainI`, `hcontainJ`)") and `README.md:1774–1776` ("provided every diagonalizable closed subgroup conjugates into a distinguished one").

Claim: the native theorem assumes that every diagonalizable closed subgroup lies in a conjugate of `D`.

Witness: `TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Torus/Conjugation.lean:149–177`, re-read today: `hcontainI` and `hcontainJ` quantify over nothing; each says that if the subgroup cut out by `I` (respectively `J`) is diagonalizable then `D.conjugate g ≤ I` for some `g`. The universal reading is false: in `PGL₂` over `k̄`, characteristic not 2, the Klein four-subgroup generated by the images of `diag(1, −1)` and `[[0,1],[1,0]]` is a diagonalizable closed subgroup in no torus, since a maximal torus is `𝔾_m` with one element of order 2.

Repair: in both places say "provided each of the two given tori, if diagonalizable, lies in a conjugate of a fixed maximal torus `D`", and in Layer 7 say the route (every torus in a Borel, Borel conjugacy, conjugacy of maximal tori of a connected solvable group) discharges this for maximal tori. Tested: both maximal tori of `PGL₂` are conjugate to the diagonal one, so the corrected hypothesis holds there.
