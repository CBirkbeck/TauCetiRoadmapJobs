## Draft pull request body

Build the theory of quasi-coherent sheaves on prestacks in the functor-of-points style of Lurie and Gaitsgory–Rozenblyum. A prestack is a functor from test rings to spaces, and its ∞-category of quasi-coherent sheaves `QCoh(X)` is the limit of the module ∞-categories `Mod_A` over its affine points. The roadmap does this once for three ∞-categories of test rings: ordinary commutative rings, animated rings, and connective commutative algebras of `D(ℤ)`. It proves that the classical and animated theories are restrictions of the last. It builds the coherent module-category functor and the module theory of connective algebras: the t-structure, flatness, perfect and almost perfect modules. It proves flat hyperdescent for module categories over every connective test ring, with no finiteness or descendability hypothesis. From that it derives invariance of `QCoh` under fpqc hypersheafification, Čech descent, pushforward along affine morphisms with base change and the projection formula, and `QCoh(h_X) ≃ Dqc(X)` for every Mathlib scheme. It then treats the t-structure, perfect = dualizable, quotients by group prestacks and discrete groups, the hearts and `D⁺` of classical quotient stacks, and compact generation: qcqs schemes, perfect stacks and their permanence, Ben-Zvi–Francis–Nadler's perfectness of `[X/G]` in characteristic zero, and the failure of compactness of `O_{BΓ}` in characteristic p. The roadmap imports and extends the local theories that other roadmaps state: the classical `QCohPrestack` of PrismaticCohomologyPartII §4.0, the animated faithfully flat descent and derived parameter stacks of LanglandsParameterStacks §§1.7–1.8, and the lattice quotients of HabiroCohomologyFoundations §§1.27–1.28. Each extended statement says where it agrees with the import, so the derived-geometry roadmaps downstream have a single owner of `QCoh`. `Suggested.lean` states the one headline statable at the pins. In `DerivedCategory (Rep k Γ)`, for a finite group whose order is divisible by the characteristic of the field, the trivial representation is not compact: its additive corepresented functor does not preserve coproducts.

## Fixes a mathematician would flag (from the final read)

1. **8.2, the definition of a perfect prestack.** It said "`QCoh(X)` is presentable and equivalent to `Ind(Perf(X))`". But `Perf(X)`, a limit over the (u+1)-small `Pts(X)`, is not small a priori, and the supplied Ind-completion (EnhancedDerivedSheaves E5:presentability.1) is that of a small ∞-category. The definition now requires `Perf(X)` essentially small and the extension `Ind(Perf(X)) → QCoh(X)` to be an equivalence; presentability follows. BZFN Def. 3.2 and Prop. 3.9 are unchanged in content: under compact generation, compact = dualizable makes `Perf(X)` the essentially small category of compact objects.
2. **3.4(b) and 6.2(a): "sheafification" → "hypersheafification".** The hypothesis put "the sheafification `L X`" in a subcategory of hypercomplete sheaves, which silently required the sheafification to be hypercomplete. The proofs use only `QCoh(X) ≃ QCoh(L X)` for the hypersheafification (3.3(a)), so the hypothesis now names the fpqc hypersheafification; 3.4(b)'s route now records that step. This is a strict generalization, matching what the routes prove and what 8.1 and 7.4 use.
