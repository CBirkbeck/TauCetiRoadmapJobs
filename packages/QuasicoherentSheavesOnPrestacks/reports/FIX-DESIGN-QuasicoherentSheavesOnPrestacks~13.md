## F02 (P3) — applied

Old → new: the first API bullet of 3.1 said that ι and θ carry Čech nerves to Čech nerves. It now also says that they carry flat hypercovers to flat hypercovers, with the argument by induction on latching objects. A new 3.1 Check shows the claim failing without flatness. In 3.2, "Through θ and θι" now cites this. The plan's `stability` entry and the 3.2 statement mirror it, and the plan gains the test `latching_objects`.

**Verified.** The finding is right.

- **The gap.** 3.2 states hyperdescent in `CRing` "through θι", and 3.2's API says `Mod` is a hypercomplete sheaf "in each of the three contexts". That needs ι to carry `CRing`-flat hypercovers to `AnRing`-flat hypercovers. The bullet covered only Čech nerves. ι does not preserve pushouts (1.1), and `L_n` is a colimit over a punctured cube.
- **The argument.** `L_n(A_•)` is the colimit of the `A_m` over the proper subsets of `[n]`, with ∅ giving A.
  - Adjoin the subsets one at a time in order of size. When S is adjoined, it is maximal and all its proper subsets are already present. So the colimit grows by the pushout along `L_S → A_S`, which is the latching map `L_m → A_m`, `m = |S| − 1 < n`, and is faithfully flat.
  - By induction, the source and the running colimit are discrete and the same in both categories. A pushout of discrete rings along a flat map is the same in `CRing` and `AnRing` (1.1). So the latching objects and maps agree, and ι preserves faithful flatness (1.3).
  - θ preserves colimits and `π_*` (1.1), so the θ case is immediate.
