## F01 (P3) — applied

**What changed.** In §3.9, "the mixed Hodge structures on the pronilpotent fundamental Lie algebra … form a good variation" is replaced. The new text says that the quotients by the terms of the lower central series form good variations, compatible under the projections. For `n ≥ 2` the Lie algebra has infinite rank: it is their inverse limit, a pro-object. A weight-`m` word is read on the quotient by the `(m+2)`-nd term. The following sentence is now plural. A §3.9 API bullet and a new Check were also changed or added.

**Verification.**

- The finding is correct. §3.6 defines good variations through MixedHodgeModulesAndIrregularHodgeTheory §3.1. At that roadmap's open head, §3.1 asks for "a local system `L` of finite-dimensional `ℚ`-vector spaces".
- For `n = 2` the Lie algebra is free on two generators. Witt's formula `(1/k)Σ_{d|k} μ(d)2^{k/d}` gives the graded ranks `2, 1, 2, 3, 6, 9`. The rank is infinite.
- The source itself calls the fundamental Lie algebra of the curve a pro-object of the category (G08 §10.4, p. 96, G08 line 4233). That locator is now cited at the claim.

**How the repair differs from the suggested wording.** The reviewer's three edits are applied, with these additions:

- The numbering of the lower central series is fixed: the whole algebra is the first term. Without this, "`(m+2)`-nd term" would be ambiguous. I used words instead of the symbol `Γ_k`, which would clash with the complex `Γ(F,4)`.
- One sentence explains why §3.6's condition on graded pieces holds for each quotient. Its `gr^W_{−2j}` is `ℚ(j)` tensored with the degree-`j` part of the free Lie algebra `𝔤_S` of §3.5. This local system is constant because pure braids move each puncture loop within its conjugacy class. Without that remark, the requirement "`gr^W_{−2n}` is a sum of constant `ℚ(n)`" was left unchecked.
- The case `n = 1` (Lie algebra `ℚ(1)`) is stated.
- "a framed good Hodge–Tate variation" becomes "a combination of framings of that quotient between `gr^W_{−2}` and `gr^W_{−2m−2}`, a class of framed … variations". The image of a word is a class in `L^HT_m`, not a single object.

**Pattern searched.** I searched the README and `Suggested.lean` for "pronilpotent", "fundamental Lie", "fundamental group", "good variation", "Hodge correlator variation" and "ind-/pro-object".

- **§3.9 API bullet (line 522): changed to match.** It now says that `Cor^Hod` of a weight-`m` word is a class of framings of the quotient by the `(m+2)`-nd term.
- **Places that needed no change:**
  - §3.8 (line 495) speaks of an *action* on the fundamental Lie algebra, i.e. on a pro-object. It makes no finite-rank claim.
  - The gap `punctured-line-path-torsors` (line 101) asks for the groupoid in `MT(F)`. That is the supplier's ind-object (PeriodsAndSpecialValues PS.9.10).
  - The §5.7 motivic lift (line 882) uses "framed subquotients", which have finite rank.
  - §3.13 (lines 563–574) and §3.9's comparison (line 515) speak of the variation of a word or a symbol. These are finite-rank framed objects.
  - `Suggested.lean` line 25 only names the carriers that are missing at the pins.

**New Check (§3.9, "Finite rank of the variations").** For `n = 2`:

- The full Lie algebra has graded ranks `2, 1, 2, 3, 6, …`, so it is not a variation of §3.6.
- The quotient by the third term has rank 3, with `gr^W_{−2} = ℚ(1)²` and `gr^W_{−4} = ℚ(2)`.
- The word `⟨X_{s₁},X_{s₂}⟩` is dual to the derivation `X_{s₁} ↦ [X_{s₁},X_{s₂}]`, `X_{s₂} ↦ [X_{s₂},X_{s₁}]`. This derivation kills `X_∞`, lands in degree two, and gives the Kummer variation of `s₁ − s₂`.

For `n = 1`, every positive special derivation is zero, which agrees with §3.4.
