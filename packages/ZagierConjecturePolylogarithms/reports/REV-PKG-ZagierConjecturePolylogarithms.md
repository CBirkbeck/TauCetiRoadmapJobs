1. **F01** (P2) Weight-two sign of the realization map contradicts the cut-cobracket convention

   **Location.** `packages/ZagierConjecturePolylogarithms/README.md` line 532 (§3.13, first Check): "Weight two: `S₂(y) ↦ −Cor_∞(0,1,y)`, whose cut cobracket must equal the image of `δ[y]₂ = (1−y)∧y`; this comparison fixes the minus sign, and the opposite sign gives `y∧(1−y)`." Also line 516 (§3.13 statement, `Sₘ(y) ↦ −Cor_∞(0,…,0,1,y)` at `m = 2`) and line 484 (§3.8, third Check).

   **Claim.** With the minus sign, `S₂(y) ↦ −Cor_∞(0,1,y)` respects cobrackets, and the plus sign would give `y∧(1−y)`.

   **Witness.** Compute under the roadmap's own conventions. §3.4 defines the cut cobracket as the sum of `⟨vᵢ,…,vⱼ⟩∧⟨vⱼ,…,v_{i−1}⟩` over cyclic cuts (the order of GR (66), GR line 1217); §3.8 gives `Cor_∞(b₀,b₁) = u(b₀ − b₁)` (GR (67), line 1250). For the cyclic word `(0,1,y)` the three cuts give `u(0−1)∧u(1−y) + u(1−y)∧u(y−0) + u(y−0)∧u(0−1) = (1−y)∧y`, since `u(−1) = 0`. So `δ Cor_∞(0,1,y) = (1−y)∧y = δ[y]₂`, and `δ(−Cor_∞(0,1,y)) = y∧(1−y) ≠ δ[y]₂`. The Check's two conclusions are exactly reversed. GR's own printed cobracket of a weight-two correlator (GR line 1255) gives the same value, so the sign in GR Definition 2.3 (line 1269) is an error of the source that the README copied (recorded under Source issues); GR's other formula for the same symbols, `{[a,b,c,d]}₂ = −Cor_a(b,c,d)` (line 1275), gives `{y}₂ = −Cor_∞(1,0,y)`, whose cut cobracket is `(1−y)∧y`, as required.

   **Repair.** In §3.13 state the weight-two map as `S₂(y) ↦ Cor_∞(0,1,y)` (equivalently `−Cor_∞(1,0,y)`), and rewrite the first Check: "the cut cobracket of `Cor_∞(0,1,y)` is `u(1−y)∧u(y) = (1−y)∧y`, since `u(−1) = 0`; the minus sign gives `y∧(1−y)`." Then fix the weights three and four of the same map against `δS₃(y) = [y]₂⊗u(y)` and `δS₄(y) = S₃(y)⊗u(y)` after pinning, in Conventions, how the weight-`(k,1)` part of `Λ²L` is identified with `L_k⊗L₁` (the roadmap nowhere says whether `a∧b` or `b∧a` with `a ∈ L_k` corresponds to `a⊗b`; the sign of the weight-three and weight-four symbols depends on it). The witness for weight three: the cuts of `(0,0,1,y)` reduce to `Cor_∞(0,1,y)∧u(y)` because `Cor_∞(0,0,1)` and `Cor_∞(y,0,0)` have zero cut cobracket and are classes over ℚ in weight two, hence zero; with `Cor_∞(0,1,y) = [y]₂` this is `[y]₂∧u(y)`, so the sign of `S₃(y) ↦ ±Cor_∞(0,0,1,y)` is fixed by the chosen identification, and the same for `S₄` and the double symbols (GR Proposition 2.5, line 1299). Adjust the §3.8 third Check accordingly: by (67), `Cor_∞(0,1,y) = −Li₂^M(1−y)`, and the Hodge value `−(1/2)(2πi)^{−2}L₂(z)` of §3.9 is that of `Cor^H(1,0,z)` (M20 line 895), the reversed cyclic order, which in weight two is the negative. No Lean statement changes: neither headline mentions the realization.

2. **F02** (P3) The §3.10 weight-one Check does not separate

   **Location.** `packages/ZagierConjecturePolylogarithms/README.md` line 501 (§3.10, third Check): "In weight one (`m = 1`) the correlator `log|z₀ − z₁|` is not continuous at `z₀ = z₁`, so the hypothesis `m > 1` is needed."
