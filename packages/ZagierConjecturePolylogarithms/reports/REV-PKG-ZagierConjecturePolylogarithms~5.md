1. **F01** (P2) The universal lift needs an extension and specialization construction

   **Location.** `packages/ZagierConjecturePolylogarithms/README.md:837`, :843–847, §5.7. The route concludes that the lift “satisfies (ii) and (iii) by construction” and says “The content of the target is the vanishing in (a) and (b)”.

   **Claim.** Solving the two generic-field obstruction problems supplies a natural lift on every generic configuration, compatible with every discrete valuation having infinite residue field, using the specialization of §§1.2 and 5.3.

   **Witness.** Put `v_j=(1,t_j,t_j²,t_j³)` with `t=(2,3,4,5,6,8/5,0,1)`. All 70 maximal minors are nonzero; the proposed coordinates are `(ρ₁,ρ₂,ρ₃,ρ₄)=(4,5/2,2,4)`. Thus `ρ₁−ρ₄=0`.

   This is an exact rational computation, using Vandermonde determinants. It lies in the advertised domain of `G₄`, but not in the distinct-point hexagon domain of §2.4 (`README.md:322`). The candidate's displayed formula has `ρ₁−ρ₄` in a denominator (CGR line 448 and line 450); its alternate hexagon description has coincident points (CGR line 465). The calculation does not disprove existence of an extended class. It disproves the implicit inference that genericity of the original configuration makes the proposed representative regular everywhere needed. Neither (a) nor (b) supplies such a representative or proves extension independent of representatives and deformations.

   There is a second domain check on the same specialization step. §1.2 (`README.md:134`) defines specialization over `F(t)` at a rational parameter, including special double-symbol collision rules. §5.3 (:766) gives the weight-lowering residue diagram for single-symbol complexes on smooth complex strata. Neither defines the specialization on the combinatorial `L₄` needed in (iii) for an arbitrary valued field. The Gauss 2-adic valuation of `ℚ(t)` has infinite residue field `𝔽₂(t)`. A construction made independently over prime fields does not commute with that reduction by field-embedding naturality: there is no embedding `ℚ → 𝔽₂(t)`.

   **Exact repair.** Make extension and specialization a third explicit mathematical obligation, with a source and target, before asserting (ii)–(iii). Construct the specialization of the required combinatorial classes on the valuation rings/local configuration spaces in question; prove preservation of the specialized relations, compatibility with the full cobracket, independence of representatives and deformation paths, and compatibility across characteristics. One may use an unramified subspace containing the lift's values rather than assert an unnecessary specialization of every element of `L₄`. Prove that the generic lift extends to every generic configuration and that these extensions commute with the valuations in (iii). Replace the assertion that only (a) and (b) remain by this full list, and add the resulting target to the Needs of §§5.7–5.8. Keep all-infinite-field scope.

   **Repair test.** Add the eight rational moment-curve points above as a negative-control Check: all 70 minors remain nonzero while the candidate has a collision, so an extension, not division by a totalized zero denominator, must give its value. Test the new specialization at the Gauss valuation `ℚ(t) → 𝔽₂(t)` as well as equal-characteristic parameter specialization. These tests discriminate the required extension from generic-field existence and embedding naturality alone.

2. **F02** (P2) Number-field realization does not supply the realization in complex families

   **Location.** `packages/ZagierConjecturePolylogarithms/README.md:722`, §5.1: “Compose ... with the Hodge realization of §3.13”; consumers are §5.4 (:782) and the period-uniqueness Check of §5.7 (:855). The cited realization begins “For a number field `F`” at :551.

   **Claim.** That composition gives the variation-valued periods on complex parameter spaces, their differential identity, and their currents on `k(Y)=ℂ(Y)` in the normal-crossing diagram.

   **Witness.** For `X=ℙ¹_ℂ∖{0,1,∞}`, the symbol `S₄(t)` has coefficient field `ℂ(t)`, not a number field. At `t=i`, its proposed ordinary period has angular derivative `−3iζ(3)/256`, not zero.

   The derivative is approximately `−0.0140866043339i`, agreeing with the repaired formula. The issue is its domain, not that formula. §3.8 constructs motives over number fields. §3.9 constructs numerical tree integrals for complex arguments, but defines its `Cor^Hod` comparison by realizing §3.8 under a complex embedding. Neither is a coalgebra morphism from the combinatorial presentation into good Hodge variations over this `X`. §3.7 supplies a period map on a variation once the variation is given. Pointwise number-field realizations do not supply it or justify differentiation in arbitrary complex parameters. The same missing input appears in §3.13's rigidity route and §5.7's claim that a primitive class over the generic complex configuration has constant period. G08 line 793–798 constructs Hodge correlators in families; it is an available route, not the number-field map that the README cites.

   **Exact repair.** Add an owned Hodge realization in families, separate from the motivic number-field realization. For smooth complex parameter varieties and the finite rational functions in an admissible configuration, construct the corresponding framed Hodge–Tate variations on their common admissible open, compatibly with restriction, pullback and the passage to germs. Prove the full cut/cobracket identities and annihilation of the specialized relations; include the collision compatibility used by the existing targets. Compare with §3.13 after specialization to algebraic configurations. G08 Theorem 1.12 and its family construction, together with the cited M20 relations and continuity, provide source inputs. Use this target in §§3.13, 5.1, 5.4 and 5.7, and adjust the standing convention at :31 so that it does not limit these analytic realizations to number fields. There is no need to demand mixed Tate motives over `ℂ(t)` from the number-field supplier contract.

   **Repair test.** The new map must accept `S₄(t)` on the stated punctured complex line and give `dR(S₄(t))=R([t]₃⊗t)`, with both angular evaluations equal to `−3iζ(3)/256` at `i`. Restriction to a smaller admissible open must give the same germ. The old number-field map cannot even take this input; the new construction addresses precisely that failure.
