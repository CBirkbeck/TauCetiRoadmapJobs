## Scope

**Cohomology comparisons, Part II: classical syntomic producers and relative open-curve coefficients.** Continue the existing comparison roadmap with the classical rigid/log-syntomic complexes, their period maps and semistable targets, and the filtered Frobenius-connection and relative cohomology interfaces needed by arithmetic consumers. Supply a general library, including all primes and all complete mixed-characteristic discrete valuation fields with perfect residue field covered by the sources. Finite extensions of Q_p are included without a good-reduction or unramified-field restriction on the whole roadmap. Individual theorems retain their actual log-smoothness, Cartier-type, compactification, weight and Frobenius-lift hypotheses.

The extension owns the classical syntomic assembly and comparison adapters beyond the base, and the relative Fil-F-MIC/Ext/evaluation/residue producer. Reuse existing rigid, crystalline, de Rham, étale, period-ring and derived constructions. Regulator maps out of K-theory, Milnor-symbol extensions and their relations, integration formulas, double/triple indices, heights and special values belong to consumers. The source's symbol-comparison theorems specify compatibility required of these interfaces; they do not move the symbol producer here. No computational algorithm is in scope.

## Final targets

The following are final interfaces, not implementation stages. Write statements in the author's own words. Source numbering below refers to the versions read.

1. **Rigid syntomic cohomology on its natural bases.** For a smooth separated finite-type scheme X over a complete mixed-characteristic DVR V with perfect residue field k, put K = Frac(V) and K_0 = Frac(W(k)). For nonnegative r, supply the functorial rigid syntomic complex assembled from the homotopy fibre of `1−φ/p^r` on `RΓ_rig(X_k/K_0)`, the filtered algebraic de Rham complex of X_K, and their maps to `RΓ_rig(X_k/K)`. Supply its long exact sequence, products and compatibility with the component maps. Compactifications and rigid embeddings are presentation data, with independence and functoriality; the de Rham-to-rigid map is not asserted to be an equivalence for arbitrary open X. Over K=K_0 the simplified mapping-fibre presentation is available as in Remark 8.7(3). Import any already-owned rigid/de Rham realization rather than reconstructing it.

   Locators: BesserI Definitions 4.1 and 4.13, Propositions 4.7 and 4.14, §5, Definition 6.1, Propositions 6.2–6.3 and Definition 6.5 (BesserI line 328, BesserI line 405, BesserI line 491, BesserI line 500, BesserI line 648, BesserI line 712, BesserI line 719, BesserI line 727, BesserI line 752); Remark 8.7(3), manuscript p. 28 (BesserI line 1073). These manuscript page numbers belong to the operator-held text, not an asserted conversion to journal pagination.

2. **Divided and undivided log-syntomic complexes.** In the log-syntomic setting over W(k), for n≥1 and r≥0 retain two complexes: `S_n(r)=Fib(J_n^{<r>} →[1−φ_r] O_cr,n)` and `S'_n(r)=Fib(J_n^{[r]} →[p^r−φ] O_cr,n)`. Here `J_n^{<r>}` is formed using a higher mod-p level on which Frobenius lands in p^r, so divided Frobenius is defined before reduction; it is not arbitrary division in a mod-p^n ring. Include independence of that auxiliary level, crystalline realization, étale-local log-PD presentations and gluing, products, and transition maps in n. The maps `ω:S'_n(r)→S_n(r)` and `τ:S_n(r)→S'_n(r)` have composites p^r; preserve their p^r error bounds. Products commute with ω; τ is generally not multiplicative. For log-smooth arithmetic pairs, include completed and rational complexes and the rational filtered log-crystalline/de Rham fibre description.

   Locators: EN §2.1 and §2.1.1–2.1.2, pp. 4–6 (EN line 158, EN line 165, EN line 193, EN line 198, EN line 243); NN §3.2, equation (32), pp. 25–26 (NN line 1299, NN line 1323). NN and CN call the undivided complex `S_n`; this brief uses EN's prime to prevent accidental identification.

3. **Period maps with separate exact and bounded conclusions.** Let V be as above, i:X_0→X the special fibre, and j:X_tr→X the open locus of trivial log structure. For fine saturated X log-smooth over V with its canonical log structure, construct the divided period map and the exact comparison `S_n(r) ≃ τ_{≤r} i*Rj_* Z/p^n(r)` for `0≤r≤p−2`. For semistable X, including the horizontal-boundary and ramified base-change charts in CN §1.1.1, construct the undivided period map to `i*Rj_* Z/p^n(r)'` in every weight. On cohomology sheaves in degrees 0≤q≤r its kernel and cokernel are killed by p raised to an exponent depending on e,p,r, independently of n and X, as stated in CN Theorem 1.1(ii). Keep separately its roots-of-unity refinement `p^(Nr+c_p)`, including the stated dimension dependence at p=2. This is an error bound on a map to nearby cycles, not an untruncated global integral comparison.

   Use the chosen source's actual lattice and adapter: NN/EN use `Z_p(r)'=(p^a a!)⁻¹ Z_p(r)` with `a=floor(r/(p−1))`; CN §1.1.1 uses its displayed p-power normalization. Record the inclusions/rescalings when moving between them. Ordinary and modified Tate lattices are not definitionally the same. If the generic-fibre comparison is exported on X, use the glued syntomic-étale complexes E_n and E'_n and their triangles, rather than replacing nearby cycles by global cohomology. EN Theorem 2.5 gives the truncated exact/bounded conclusions for these complexes.

   Locators: EN Theorem 2.2, §2.2.1, pp. 7–8 (EN line 299, EN line 318); EN §2.2.2 and Theorem 2.5, pp. 8–9 (EN line 336, EN line 360); CN §1.1.1, Theorem 1.1, p. 2, and §5.1.2/Theorem 5.4, p. 54 (CN line 54, CN line 60, CN line 70, CN line 78, CN line 2587); NN §4.1, p. 44 (NN line 2324). EN Theorem 2.3 reports a sharper-looking bound than CN v4; retain the verified CN v4 bound unless the design verifies the normalization/version change. CN's introductory discussion also mentions a larger small-weight range; use the precise divided theorem just cited, and do not transfer it to the undivided complex.

4. **Hyodo–Kato and semistable-complex targets; rational descent.** For a proper fine log-smooth X over the canonical log DVR V, with Cartier-type special fibre, supply or import its finite-dimensional Hyodo–Kato cohomology D^q over K_0, bijective semilinear φ and monodromy N with `Nφ=pφN`. A uniformizer π yields `ι_π:D^q⊗K_0 K ≃ H^q_log-dR(X_K/K)`; replacing π by πu changes it by composition with `exp(log(u)N)`. The induced de Rham monodromy is independent of π. For an arithmetic pair log-smooth over V and of Cartier type and r≥0, the rational syntomic complex is canonically equivalent to the total homotopy fibre of the Hyodo–Kato square with top map `(1−φ/p^r,ι_π)` into `RΓ_HK ⊕ RΓ_dR/F^r`, vertical maps N and (N,0), and bottom map `1−φ/p^(r−1)`. Keep the twist in the bottom map and all comparison data.

   For every K-variety, the rational h-descent extension has functorial product-compatible arithmetic and geometric period maps giving equivalences after `τ_{≤r}` for every r≥0. Its semistable presentation and period maps agree with the classical producer where both apply. Import an existing base-owned h-descent or semistable comparison theorem by its accepted section rather than planning it again; the new obligation is the classical interface and its compatibility. Do not infer an integral isomorphism from rationalization or from the small-weight theorem.

   Locators: HK §§3.2–3.6, pp. 243–246 (HK line 698, HK line 715, HK line 740, HK line 770), Theorem 5.1, pp. 262–263 (HK line 1321, HK line 1335); NN Proposition 3.8, pp. 26–27 (NN line 1341, NN line 1384), Theorem A(1),(4), Proposition 4.6 and Corollary 4.11 (NN line 85, NN line 93, NN line 2400, NN line 2778). HK's proper theorem is not asserted for every nonproper log-smooth object; use NN's arithmetic-pair/descent formulation for that extension.

5. **Relative filtered Frobenius connections and extension evaluation.** Let V be a complete mixed-characteristic DVR with perfect residue field, equipped with the Frobenius lift F_V required by AM §2; let S=Spec(B) be smooth affine over V and σ a compatible p-Frobenius on B†. The general Fil-F-MIC(S,σ) object consists of coherent algebraic de Rham and overconvergent rigid modules identified after extension from B_K to B†_K, an integrable connection, a finite descending filtration with locally free graded pieces and Griffiths transversality, and invertible horizontal semilinear Frobenius. Supply its tensor/Tate operations, pullback, change-of-σ equivalence, exact structure tested on every filtration step, and Yoneda Ext groups. It is an exact category; do not assume it is abelian to reuse an abelian Ext API.

   For every such coefficient object H and degree i≥0, supply `Ext^i(O_S,H)→H^i(S(H))`, where S(H) is the mapping fibre of `1−Φ` from the filtered connection de Rham complex to the overconvergent connection complex. Compose with the map to classical syntomic cohomology for Tate coefficients under AM's stated hypotheses. If F^0 H_dR=0, the degree-one map is injective, and an extension with its unique filtered lift e is represented by `(∇e,(1−Φ)e)`. For `H=H^n(U/S)(n+1)` with the vanishing in AM §2.7, export the two projections δ and R_σ of equation (2.22), including the dependence of R_σ on σ. These are coefficient interfaces; symbol-to-Ext constructions remain downstream.

   Locators: AM §2 standing assumptions, Definition 2.1, Remark 2.2 and Definition 2.3, pp. 3–5 (AM line 117, AM line 131, AM line 155, AM line 181); exactness and pullback, p. 5 (AM line 186, AM line 206); Definition 2.4, equations (2.7)–(2.9), Lemma 2.5, pp. 6–7 (AM line 249, AM line 266, AM line 275, AM line 283); §2.7, equation (2.22), p. 19 (AM line 795, AM line 808). A Frobenius lift on every ramified V is not claimed.

6. **Relative rigid/de Rham realization and the open-curve residue sequence.** For smooth quasi-projective u:U→S with S smooth affine over an AM base V, supply its relative de Rham and rigid cohomology, Gauss–Manin connection, Hodge filtration, Frobenius and comparison map c. Promote this datum to Fil-F-MIC only when the relative rigid sheaves are coherent in every degree and c is bijective (AM Assumption 2.20). Prove these conditions in both the projective smooth case and the full good-compactification setting: smooth projective compactifications S̄ and X̄ over V, relative simple normal-crossings boundary T=S̄−S, projective extension f:X̄→S̄, horizontal divisor D_h relative normal-crossings over V, vertical divisor D_v=f⁻¹(T), total D_h∪D_v relative normal-crossings over V, horizontal boundary over S relative normal-crossings over S, and the compactified log morphism log-smooth and integral with base of Zariski type. Export the associated coherent overconvergent sheaf acyclicity on the smooth affine analytification and its comparison with relative continuous-differential cohomology. No unrestricted coherence theorem for arbitrary open families is promised.

   If u is a smooth affine relative curve with connected fibres, with smooth projective compactification X/S and finite étale boundary D, and the preceding coherence/comparison conclusions hold, export the exact sequence in Fil-F-MIC
   `0 → H¹(X/S) → H¹(U/S) → H⁰(D/S)(−1) → H²(X/S) → 0`.
   Include its filtered, horizontal and Frobenius-compatible residue map and the induced maps on extensions. D need not split into rational sections. Keep the residue target's Tate twist; downstream tame-symbol/residue compatibility must use this interface.

   Locators: AM Definition 2.12 and Remark 2.13, pp. 11–12 (AM line 451, AM line 497); Settings 2.14/2.16 and Propositions 2.15/2.17, pp. 12–14 (AM line 510, AM line 530, AM line 567); Proposition 2.18 and Lemma 2.19, p. 14 (AM line 587, AM line 609), with the affine embedding used in the proof (AM line 612); Assumption 2.20 and Remark 2.21(3), p. 15 (AM line 625, AM line 639). The affine hypothesis must be explicit when using the acyclicity claim; the unqualified wording of Lemma 2.19 cannot justify it on a proper curve.

7. **Frobenius-point specialization and comparison to absolute syntomic evaluation.** Over W(k), a smooth affine scheme with a compatible system of p-Frobenius lifts has a unique Frobenius-compatible W(k)-point above each k-point. Pullback of the filtered extension and its S(H) class to such a point must agree with specialization of the connection/Frobenius representative, hence with evaluation of R_σ when defined. Retain Frobenius-compatibility of the point; this is not evaluation at an arbitrary analytic point. After extension to algebraically closed k, vanishing at a lift of every k-point detects a section of a finite locally free module over the completed generic base. For a smooth projective Y/W(k) with relative normal-crossings D and U=Y−D, identify rational log-syntomic and rigid syntomic cohomology with products and period maps compatible. This supplies the absolute target of relative evaluation.

   The consuming AM symbol-comparison application has further hypotheses: projective log-smooth f:(Y,M)→(Q,L) over W(k), smooth affine Q with relative simple normal-crossings T, reduced relative simple normal-crossings D on Y, affine S=Q−T and U=Y−(D∪f⁻¹T), both total and relative horizontal normal-crossings conditions, a compatible system of base log-Frobenius lifts, coherence in every degree, `0≤n<p−1`, and vanishing of both `H^n(ω^{≥n+1}_{Y_m/Q_m})` and `H^{n+1}(ω^{≥n+1}_{Y_m/Q_m})` for every m>0. Preserve the factor `(−1)^n` in that comparison. For the curve specialization in AM §4.1, retain p>2, connected fibres, finite étale horizontal boundary and vertical multiplicities prime to p, together with its coherence and Frobenius hypotheses. These are hypotheses of that application, not restrictions on targets 1–6 and not permission to discard p=2 from the general producer.

   Locators: AM Theorem 3.7 and its setting, pp. 27–28 (AM line 1185, AM line 1200), Lemmas 3.8–3.10, pp. 28–30 (AM line 1229, AM line 1289, AM line 1302), specialization in its proof, p. 31 (AM line 1352), and §4.1/Lemma 4.2/Theorem 4.5, pp. 32–36 (AM line 1432, AM line 1483, AM line 1625). ## Risks

- **Absent ownership contracts:** the base and named crystalline/period/rigid suppliers are missing from the supplied tree; main-path lookup did not recover the base. Import general infrastructure from its owner; identify genuinely missing general foundations before choosing headline carriers. - **Noninterchangeable comparisons:** integral lattices, sites, truncations, divided Frobenius and normalizations must remain visible. Do not assume the filtered exact category is abelian or replace R_σ by a Frobenius-independent invariant.
