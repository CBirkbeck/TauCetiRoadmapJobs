## Corrections and unsupported implications

- The source contains a few conflicting conventions and misprints already adjudicated by the accepted design. The package retains the corrected main statements, rather than stale acceptance strings: the local counit uses h = diag(1,−π^ν), the dual function model uses Dτ, full Hom differs from finite-support Hom, the normalization module includes λ = 0, and the torus-to-group induction direction is preserved.
- The accepted wording called the map Iunit⊛V → V an “inverse unitor.” Its actual direction is the unitor. Both the factorization criterion and Petersson unit formula now have that direction explicitly.
- The initial normalization-injection witness omitted the cancellation of the modulus by the weight. At q = 3, a = π, d = λ = y = 1 and f(π,1) = 1, the weighted paths are (1/3)·3 = 1 and 1; unweighted extension gives 1/3 and 1. The README uses these values.
- The half-character conjugation in Weyl lifting is now oriented explicitly: h = |a/d|⁻¹ᐟ²f. For normalized-symmetric values f(π,1) = q⁻¹, f(1,π) = 1, the two h-values both equal q⁻¹ᐟ². The opposite half-character does not give ordinary swap invariance.
- A standard-vector calculation on F² is only a test of matrix transpose. It is not a smooth complex representation of GL₂(F). The README makes this distinction where it uses the elementary matrices as witnesses.
- The determinant polarization and its perfect Gram pairing hold over every commutative ring, including the zero ring, characteristic two and square-zero thickenings. That foundation is stated at this natural algebraic generality. The metaplectic/exotic layers still have the source's characteristic-different-from-two hypothesis.
- The spin lattice's uniqueness is for the stated decomposable fractional compact-open O_A lattice, with exponent −ord(ω)/2; it is not a claim about arbitrary modules in A.
- The cuspidal complement uses positive Hermitian Petersson orthogonality. The theta trace pairing remains bilinear with inverse-transpose. Their roles and scalar conventions are distinguished.

The README does not assert that a source has supplied a global spherical counit formula or the spin product-transport proof. These are full mathematical targets in §§9.2 and 9.4. G3 and G4 remain proof obligations. The regular-bimodule coevaluation and both triangle identities remain targets of §5.3; the generic categorical tensor is supplied by the exact extension to EnhancedDerivedSheaves. The local trace formula alone is not treated as a proof of categorical self-duality. General compact-data global zeta convergence is registered with its supplier, rather than inferred from cuspidal convergence. These limitations do not narrow the targets.
