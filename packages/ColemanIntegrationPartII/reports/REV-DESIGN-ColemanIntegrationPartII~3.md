1. **F01** (P1) The logarithm supplier does not cover arbitrary abelian varieties over ℂ_p

   **Location.** `packages/ColemanIntegrationPartII/design/README.md:438`: “For an abelian variety A over a finite p-adic field or ℂ_p”. The same target says to use the supplied abelian logarithm; line 446 supplies only AnabelianGeometryAndNonabelianChabauty §5.8. See also `packages/ColemanIntegrationPartII/design/plan.json:1168`.

   **Claim.** The invariant-form step and subtraction of the invariant differential error in §4.0a work for every A/ℂ_p using this supplier.

   **Witness.** At supplier head 59d989dd138cd28b4a4643ab233c04ad6d62aa34, `TauCetiRoadmap/AnabelianGeometryAndNonabelianChabauty/README.md:6833` assumes A/K, K/ℚ_p finite. Its line 6837 extends this A's logarithm to A(ℂ_p). An elliptic curve with j transcendental over ℚ_p has no such finite-field model.

   For a concrete family, take E_t: y²=x(x−1)(x−t) with t∈ℂ_p transcendental over ℚ_p. Its j-invariant is a nonconstant rational function of t, hence transcendental. The supplier's `abelianLog.cp` enlarges the points of a finite-field-defined variety; it does not enlarge the class of varieties. Thus the cited section does not provide the prerequisite at the advertised generality. This is an ownership/supplier-scope defect, not a claim that the general logarithm is mathematically false.

   **Exact repair.** Keep the general ℂ_p target. Before the rigidified primitive construction, name and own the additional logarithm theorem for arbitrary A/ℂ_p, its local analyticity, identity differential, additivity, uniqueness and homomorphism compatibility. Prove its agreement with the imported logarithm when A descends to finite K. Colmez's open-subgroup torsion theorem and logarithm construction provide the source route: colmez1998 line 3079, Theorem II.1.9, printed p. 76; line 3147, Lemma II.1.13, printed p. 78. Alternatively declare a precise gap with the earlier logarithm supplier. Update the plan and supplier contract together; add the transcendental-j example as a scope Check. Do not duplicate the already supplied finite-K theorem.

2. **F02** (P2) Logarithmic-span base change needs an algebraicity restriction

   **Location.** `packages/ColemanIntegrationPartII/design/README.md:461`: “In an algebraically closed overfield … the geometric logarithmic subspace is spanned by these scalar-extended generators.” The identical API is `packages/ColemanIntegrationPartII/design/plan.json:1289`, `descendedLogForms_split`.

   **Claim.** Extending scalars on logarithmic forms built using finite separable constant extensions of k spans all geometric logarithmic forms over an arbitrary algebraically closed overfield E.

   **Witness.** Take k=ℚ, E=ℂ, X=ℙ¹ and α transcendental over ℚ. Every scalar-extended traced generator has poles only at algebraic points and infinity. The geometric logarithmic form dt/(t−α) has residue 1 at α.

   A finite E-linear combination of the generators remains regular at α, so it cannot equal dlog(t−α). The trace-as-sum-of-embeddings identity is correct; the subsequent spanning assertion is the overclaim. A geometric expression over a transcendental extension need not have a finite algebraic field of definition, contrary to an unrestricted reading of the proof at line 463.

   **Exact repair.** State the full spanning equality for an algebraic closure k̄/k. For an arbitrary algebraically closed E/k retain the trace identity, and identify the E-span only with logarithmic forms generated by rational functions defined over finite algebraic extensions of k inside E. For descent of k-defined forms, explicitly choose the algebraic decomposition over k̄ before embedding in E or ℂ_p. Keep the independent geometric construction over E for genuinely E-defined data. Add dt/(t−α) as the negative-control Check, and propagate these qualifications to the plan's API and proof sketch. The existing finite-extension trace construction and nonsplit quadratic Check need not be discarded.
