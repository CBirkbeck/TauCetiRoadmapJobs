# Design report: ColemanIntegrationPartII

The artifacts are `packages/ColemanIntegrationPartII/design/README.md`, `packages/ColemanIntegrationPartII/design/plan.json` and `packages/ColemanIntegrationPartII/sources.toml`. The parent is ColemanIntegration. There are seven layers, 31 owned targets, 11 definition targets, 99 discriminating Checks, five certified mathematical sources and eight explicit contract gaps. Every target has its source locator, hypotheses, proof sketch, prerequisites, named API and Checks. The README contains the same mathematics in layer order. No content checkout, merge order, target file, issue or pull request was edited.

All five filed final-target families are addressed. The arbitrary-reduction all-prime natural single-integration theorem is retained in full as a supplier contract, without a duplicate construction target. Layers 1, 3 and 4 give harmonic realization and annular interpolation. Layers 2–4 give double indices and the corrected global pairing. Layers 5–6 give local triples, global wide-open triples and the actual Coleman one-form pairing. Layer 7 gives branch transport, coefficient traces, nonsplit residue traces, degree-zero divisors without a rational basepoint and model/extension descent. Regulator constructions, motivic relations, heights, special values and effective algorithms are not part of this producer.

This is a detailed design with unresolved supplier bindings, not certification that all prerequisites have been supplied. ColemanIntegration is absent from the main and package trees. The main-branch GitHub lookup of its README also returned 404. Its §7.7 and §§7.10–7.11 remain requested locators, not verified section contents. Consequently overlap with the absent base cannot be conclusively excluded; the contracts identify exactly what must be imported and the continuation owns the additional mathematics specified here. The comparison Part II has a triage target, but no design and no position in the supplied order. It is not used as a supplier. These limitations cannot honestly be converted into invented sections or opaque assumptions asserting all the desired conclusions.

A complete pinned file-tree search was made for both libraries (10,680 Mathlib tree entries and 13,144 Tau Ceti entries, neither truncated). It found no Coleman/Vologodsky integration, double/triple index or multigraph harmonic-cochain producer. Object searches additionally located the simple-graph Laplacian and formal Laurent derivative, which are imported. ## Backward chains and missing contracts

The formal graph lane terminates at native Graph, simple-graph Laplacian and finite linear algebra. The local formal index lane terminates at native LaurentSeries and polynomial algebra, plus the specified residue laws. The analytic versions terminate at the rigid geometry supplier and the missing base annular/Coleman contracts. The corrected cohomological double pairing and Frobenius vanishing terminate at the explicit unbound curve-comparison contract. Harmonic identification uses the earlier corrected pairing, the imported single integral and precisely stated reciprocity/Jacobian bridges. Finally trace and divisor descent terminate at pinned field trace and the imported finite-extension/Galois naturality. No proof consumes a regulator or height theorem.

The source dependency is acyclic: Besser §§3–4 → harmonic identification of Besser–Zerbes → Besser §5. The pairing formula in Theorem 4.10 is proved by expanding opposite ends, without using the later Theorem 5.1 mentioned in the source proof. The skew-monodromy identity similarly follows from N²=0, χN=N and cancellation of graph products, without using Corollary 5.3 as a premise.

The eight gaps are:

- `base-single`: Provide the natural universal single-integration theorem for all finite p-adic fields, with its actual carriers and finite-extension/Galois compatibilities. Supplier: ColemanIntegration; section: §7.7 (requested, text unavailable). Direct targets: 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 7.1, 7.2, 7.3, 7.4.
- `base-annuli`: Provide analytic annular Laurent expansions, residue and substitution laws, logarithm branches, and their compatibility with formal Laurent differential germs. Supplier: ColemanIntegration; section: Analytic residue and annular integration section (exact locator unavailable). Direct targets: 2.1, 4.1.
- `base-coleman`: Provide connected Coleman wide opens, their finite end sets, the actual Coleman function/form algebra, uniqueness of primitives up to a scalar and integration of Coleman one-forms. Supplier: ColemanIntegration; section: Coleman wide opens and Coleman-function algebra section (exact locator unavailable). Direct targets: 3.1, 3.2, 6.1, 6.3.
- `base-reciprocity`: Provide Coleman and natural-single-integral double-index reciprocity together with global residue cancellation and local Coleman restriction before harmonic normalization. Supplier: ColemanIntegration; section: Single/double integration reciprocity section (exact locator unavailable). Direct targets: 3.1, 4.2, 6.2.
- `curve-comparison`: Bind an allowed earlier producer and exact section for the full curve de Rham/rigid/semistable comparison, including ordered cup products, trace and Frobenius normalizations. Supplier: none; section: Curve Čech–de Rham, cup, graph projection and Frobenius/monodromy contract (no producing section bound). Direct targets: 3.1, 3.2, 3.4, 3.5, 6.3.
- `coleman-form-relations`: Provide the actual Coleman one-form product module and the symbol/connection relation theorem used by Besser–de Jeu Lemma 9.1. Supplier: ColemanIntegration; section: Coleman one-form symbol/connection section (exact locator unavailable). Direct targets: 6.4.
- `base-jacobian`: Provide the abelian-logarithm/invariant-differential comparison and compact p-adic Jacobian point topology required by the curve integration comparison. Supplier: ColemanIntegration; section: Abelian logarithm and curve restriction section (exact locator unavailable). Direct targets: 4.2, 7.2.
- `tate-differential`: Provide the pullback of the normalized Tate invariant differential to dz/z and its compatibility with annular charts of a regular Tate model. Supplier: none; section: Analytic differential comparison extending EllipticCurves Layer 4 (producing extension unbound). Direct targets: 7.7.

The `none` supplier on an unbound gap is intentional. CohomologyComparisonsPartII cannot be equated with CohomologyComparisons and cannot be imported solely because it has a triage target. Once a permitted earlier producer and exact section are provided, its `gap:` prerequisite can become a `roadmap:` prerequisite. The design makes no claim that the unresolved analytic base sections already exist. No missing contract is encoded as a theorem hypothesis containing that theorem's conclusion.

## Adversarial mathematics pass

Every owned target is covered below, including its named API. The result distinguishes a checked algebraic identity or source-supported statement from a proof that still needs a recorded supplier. All formal targets use fields of characteristic zero; the zero ring and characteristic-p coefficients are excluded by those explicit hypotheses. All geometric integration targets use finite extensions of ℚ_p; equal-characteristic fields and noncomplete arbitrary DVR fraction fields are not silently included. Coefficient extension is finite and separable, so inseparable trace pathologies are outside its hypothesis. The semistable **realization** has split/separated hypotheses, while the exported natural integral and descent theorem do not. No Lean signature with an implicit standing variable is claimed to have been elaborated.
