   **Location:** packages/AutomorphicCongruences/README.md:327, “in particular … ≥ ord_P(J) when N′ = 0”; also its consequence (iii).

   **Witness:** Take K=Q(i), p=5, A₀=R₀=Z₅, I=J=(5), n=1, χ=1, ρ=ε, χ′=ε², ν=ε²χ_K, σ=χ′⊕ρ⊕χ. The canonical orbit is A·e₃, so N=N′=0, while ord₅(J)=1.

   The datum satisfies the stated hypotheses: the three residual inertia characters are 1, ω, ω²; F₅ has four nonzero elements; the anti-diagonal symmetric polarization has ν(c)=−1. Take V⁺=0 and M⁺=Frac(A)e₃. Descent is the identity, and Σ={2,5,∞}, including the ramification of Q(i). The middle Selmer group is the μ₅∞ group unramified outside 2 and locally trivial at both primes over 5. Its class-group contribution is zero (class number 1). The S-unit contribution is generated by 1+i, and (1+i)⁴=−4=1−5 has 5-adic logarithm of valuation 1 at either prime over 5. Its image is therefore primitive in the rank-one local principal-unit Z₅-module, so the induced map on Q₅/Z₅ is injective and the global Selmer group is zero. Its characteristic valuation is 0, contradicting the claimed 0≥1. SU line 1961 explicitly permits a nonspanning orbit; SU lines 2031 and 2054–2055 require ℒ⊗Frac(A)=M before deriving Fitt_R(N)⊆I.

   **Exact repair:** Replace the “in particular” clause in (ii) by: “If N′=0 and ℒ⊗_A Frac(A)=M, then ord_P Ch ≥ ord_P(J), by SU Lemma 4.3.10(iii).” Replace (iii) by: “If ord_P Ch_F^Σ(ψν)=0 and the characteristic-zero alternative of SU Lemma 4.3.11 holds, then ord_P Ch_M^Σ(ρ₀⊗χ⁻¹)≥ord_P(J).
