## R01 (P2) — gap:katz-cm-padic-l asks PadicFamilies for a construction an earlier roadmap already supplies, and bundles five unrelated results

**Location.** `TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicCongruences/README.md` lines 111 and 2318 (supplier contract "What PadicFamilies must provide (gap:katz-cm-padic-l): … Katz's two-variable p-adic L-function on Γ_K and its anticyclotomic restriction …"); same slug in `design/plan.json`.

**Claim.** The gap declares that no roadmap earlier in the merge order provides Katz's two-variable p-adic L-function of an imaginary quadratic field, and that PadicFamilies Layer 4 must build it.

**Witness.** AutomorphicPadicLFunctionsPartII (merge order line 303, before this roadmap at line 352) §8.5 "Critical Hecke-value interpolation over totally imaginary fields" states: for a p-ordinary lifted type Σ of L and a tame ideal f prime to p, a bounded measure μ_f on Gal(L(p^∞f)/L) interpolating every critical algebraic χ with the Katz Euler factors ∏_(v∈Σ_p)(1−χ(v⁻¹)/Nv)∏_(v∈Σ̄_p)(1−χ(v)) and periods Ω_p^α(Ω_p^∨)^β; its Checks treat ℚ(i) at p = 5 (d = 1) and exclude only inert p for imaginary quadratic K. That is Katz's measure for K with p split, pushed to Γ_K. NoncommutativeAndEquivariantIwasawa (merge order line 26) §4.3, "Construction (elliptic units and the two-variable 𝔭-adic measure)", also constructs "the bounded Katz measure ν_𝔣 ∈ A_D(𝔣)" with its exact interpolation for the class-number-one fields at p ≥ 5. Neither roadmap is cited anywhere in this README (`grep -c "AutomorphicPadicLFunctionsPartII\|NoncommutativeAndEquivariantIwasawa"` gives 0).

A second defect of the same contract: its `needs` sentence bundles five independent theorems from five sources (Katz's function; Hida2010 Theorem I on μ = 0; Hsieh2012 Theorems A–B; Finis2006 Theorem 1.1; Hid91's Rankin–Selberg function with its congruence denominator), each with its own hypotheses and consumers (§§0.9, 1.5, 1.7, 2.3, 2.4, 2.8, 2.11, 2.12, 2.22, 5.5, 6.5, 7.4). A supplier cannot discharge it in part, and the intake cannot track which clause is open.

**Repair.** Under *Exact supplier contracts* add a subsection `From TauCetiRoadmap.AutomorphicPadicLFunctionsPartII` importing §8.5 for the Katz measure (and cite NoncommutativeAndEquivariantIwasawa §4.3 as the class-number-one instance where §0.9 or §6.5 use it). Restate the remaining needs as separate gaps, each with one supplier section and one slug, for example: `gap:katz-ht93-normalization` on AutomorphicPadicLFunctionsPartII §8.5 (the comparison of μ_f's normalization with HT93 Theorem 7.1, (7.3a,b) and (8.8b), including the class-number factor and the anticyclotomic restriction), `gap:hida-anticyclotomic-mu` (Hida2010 Theorem I), `gap:hecke-l-values-mod-p` (Hsieh2012 Theorems A–B and Finis2006 Theorem 1.1), and `gap:hida-rankin-selberg-cm` on PadicFamilies Layer 4 (Hid91 Lemma 5.3(vi); FW Corollary 7.21). Replace every `(gap:katz-cm-padic-l)` in the *Needs* lines and Dependencies by the slug actually used there, add the new slugs to `plan.json`'s `gaps`, and keep the scope-table row for PadicFamilies consistent.
