## R01 (P2) — 5.5 claims "generates ⇒ basic" for every small family `D`; it fails when `D` has no equaliser shape

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/LocallyPresentableCategories/README.md` lines 915–917 ("Equalising a pair of arrows is a localisation (5.3). Prove, for a small family `D` and `R` with `w`-small hom-sets: `i` is basic iff `Generates D i`"); the same claim in the Summary, lines 36–39 ("and exactly when `R` is reached from the envelope of `I` by a small transfinite sequence of localisations and adjunctions of arrows (5.5)"), the Scope bullet at lines 59–61, and the Research context at line 989.

Claim: for every small family `D`, a functor `i : I → R` into a category with `D`-limits generates `R` under `D`-limits iff `R` is reached from the envelope `Ĩ` of the discrete category on `I` by localisations at small sets of arrows (5.3) and adjunctions of small sets of arrows, with 2-colimits at limit ordinals.

Witness: take `α` empty (no shapes; categories with `D`-limits are all categories, `D`-functors all functors, 5.3's localisation is the ordinary localisation, the adjunction of arrows the free extension), `I = PUnit`, `R` the one-object category of the group `ℤ/2` and `i` its object. `Generates D i` holds: `limitsClosure` with no shapes is the isomorphism closure (`LimitsClosure.lean` lines 35–39, constructors `of_mem`, `of_isoClosure`, `of_limitPresentation`), and `R` has one object. `i` is not basic: `Ĩ ≌ PUnit`, and neither step adds objects, so every object of every `Rβ` is isomorphic to the point. Define `M : Rβ ⥤ Type` by induction: the point goes to `Fin 3`, every adjoined arrow to a 3-cycle `σ` (conjugated by the chosen isomorphisms between objects); every arrow of every `Rβ` is then sent to a bijection, so `M` extends through every localisation, through every adjunction, and through the 2-colimits. If `E : Bℤ/2 ≌ Rγ`, then `M ≅ (M ∘ E) ∘ E'` for a quasi-inverse `E'`, so `M(h)` for an adjoined arrow `h` is conjugate to `(M ∘ E)(u)` with `u ∈ ℤ/2`, an endomap of order at most 2, while `σ` has order 3. Some arrow is adjoined, since localisations and 2-colimits of categories equivalent to `PUnit` stay equivalent to `PUnit`. The route's step "then equalise every pair of parallel arrows that `Φβ` identifies" (lines 925–926) uses the equaliser inclusion as the inverted arrow, which exists only when the equaliser shape is among the `D a` (5.3, third Check, says so: "if `WalkingParallelPair` is among the `D a`").

Repair: state the two directions separately. "Basic ⇒ generates" for every small `D` (the route at lines 917–922 is sound). "Generates ⇒ basic" under the hypothesis that some `D a` is equivalent to `WalkingParallelPair` (so that equalising a pair of arrows is the localisation at the equaliser inclusion, 5.3 third Check); with that hypothesis the route at lines 922–928 goes through. Add the negative-control Check: "`α` empty, `R = Bℤ/2`, `i` the point: `Generates D i` holds and `i` is not basic, by the model `Fin 3` with a 3-cycle on every adjoined arrow." Rewrite the Summary sentence at lines 37–39 and the Scope bullet at lines 59–61 accordingly ("and, when `D` contains the equaliser shape, exactly when …"), and the Research-context sentence at line 989. The Lean file states only `generates_iff_essSurj`, which is unaffected, and its docstring (lines 71–73) already says the basic-functor equivalence is not stated.

## R02 (P3) — The last Check of 5.5 justifies its conclusion with Kelly's small-sketch theorems outside their hypotheses

Location: README.md lines 945–958 ("Smallness of `D` is needed. … the target of a basic functor for all small colimits is the reflective orthogonality class of a small sketch in a presheaf category, closed there under `λ`-filtered colimits for some regular `λ` (Kelly, §6.2, Theorem 6.5, p. 117, §6.3, Theorem 6.11, p. 119, and §5.12, Theorem 5.56, p. 108, along the sequence), so each of its objects is presentable").

Claim: along a basic sequence for `D` all small shapes, each stage is (dually) the algebras of a small sketch, by Kelly 6.5, 6.11 and 5.56.

Witness: kelly-enriched lines 5027–5030: the regulus of Theorem 6.11 is small "because `A` is small", and 6.5 and 5.56 are stated for a small sketch and a dense functor from a small `A`. For `D` all small shapes the envelope of the point is the closure of the corepresentable under all small limits in `(Type w)ᵒᵖ`, which is all of `(Type w)ᵒᵖ` (every set is a coproduct of points); the sketch presenting a localisation or an arrow adjunction of a stage has that stage, not `I`, as its underlying category, and it is not small. The first part of the Check (the equaliser `X ≅ eq(Bool^{Set X} ⇉ Bool^{Set(Set X) ⊕ Set X})`, `Fin 3 ∉ I₁` by closure under `x + y + z`) is correct: I recomputed it (a limit of copies of `Bool` is closed under the pointwise ternary sum because `¬` and the constants commute with it; a three-element set closed under it would give `x + y + z ∈ {x, y, z}`, forcing two of them equal). The conclusion "`i` is not basic" is also true, but by a different argument: each `Rβᵒᵖ` is locally presentable, because `Type w` is and the 2-category of locally presentable categories and left adjoints is closed under the pushouts and chain colimits that the three kinds of step compute (the dual of the closure of accessible categories and accessible functors under the 2-limits of Makkai–Paré, Chapter 5, already cited in the Research context), while `Rγᵒᵖ ≌ (Type w)ᵒᵖ` is not (1.1, second Check).

Repair: replace the parenthesis and the clause "closed there under `λ`-filtered colimits for some regular `λ` … so each of its objects is presentable" by the locally-presentable argument above with its citation (Makkai–Paré, Chapter 5; or Bird, *Limits in 2-categories of locally presented categories*, 1984, added to the References), or delete the Check (it is outside the layer's convention by its own account; the R01 Check above already shows that the hypotheses of 5.5 matter).
