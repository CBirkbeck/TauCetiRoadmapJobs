## R02 (P2) — The definition of a sound doctrine in 5.4 has the wrong variance

Location: README.md lines 656–659: "`D` is sound when `C` is `D`-filtered as soon as, for each `a` and each `S : D a ⥤ C`, the category of cocones of `S` is connected".

Claim: soundness is defined through diagrams of shape `D a` in `C`.

Witness: the cited source defines it through diagrams of the opposite shape: lack-rosicky-2011 lines 733–736 (cache `50216f8b…`, §4.4): `C` is `D`-filtered when `C`-colimits commute in `Set` with `D`-limits; it follows that for any `D ∈ 𝔻` and any diagram `S : Dᵒᵖ → C` the category of cocones is connected; `D` is sound when the converse holds. The variance is forced, not a convention: for `S : E ⥤ C` put `F(e, c) = C(S e, c)`, a functor `Eᵒᵖ × C → Set`; then `lim_{Eᵒᵖ} colim_C F = 1` while `colim_C lim_{Eᵒᵖ} F = π₀(Cocones(S))`, so commuting with `Eᵒᵖ`-limits is what gives connected cocone categories for `E`-diagrams. With the README's definition, for a doctrine not closed under opposites (for instance the single pullback shape `• → • ← •`, whose opposite is the span) the class of sound doctrines, and hence the hypothesis of the two theorems stated in 5.4 (Lack–Rosický Theorem 4.18 and the local presentability at `κ`), is a different class from the source's, and the Checks (finite categories, finite discrete categories, the empty family) are all self-dual so they do not detect it.

Repair: write "each `S : (D a)ᵒᵖ ⥤ C`" at line 658, and add a Check that separates the two readings, for example: for `D` the single shape `WalkingCospan` (pullbacks), `D`-filteredness of `C` is tested on spans `(D a)ᵒᵖ ⥤ C`, not on cospans; a definition quantifying over cospans would accept categories whose colimits do not commute with pullbacks. While there, say "the number of morphisms of the `D a`" at line 665, since the Conventions measure size by morphisms.
