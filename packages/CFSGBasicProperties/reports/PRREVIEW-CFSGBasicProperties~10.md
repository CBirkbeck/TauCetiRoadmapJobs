## C02 — The Check of §2.4 calls `{α₁, α₂, α₁ + α₂}` an orbit; the orbits are `{α₁, α₂}` and `{α₁ + α₂}`

In type `A₂` the diagram–field map exchanges `α₁` and `α₂` and fixes `α₁ + α₂`, so the set named in line 470 is a union of two orbits. A reader applying the body of §2.4 literally ("for each orbit, the `F`-fixed points of the group generated by its root subgroups") gets two groups for `²A₂(q)`: order `q³` from `{α₁, α₂}`, whose root subgroups already generate all of `U`, and order `q` from `{α₁ + α₂}`, which is the centre of the first. The value `q³ = 27` in the Check is right.

The smallest change: write "the orbit `{α₁, α₂}`, whose root subgroups generate the group with roots `α₁, α₂, α₁ + α₂`". If the classes of Steinberg (positive roots with proportional restrictions to the fixed space) are meant in the body as well, say so there, so that the fixed root does not give a second twisted root group.

## C03 — The Example of F0–F1 reads as identifying the candidate with `PSL₂(𝔽_q)` for every prime power `q`

Lines 249–250 say that for `A₁(q)` "the candidate is `PSL₂(𝔽_q)`, finite for every prime power `q`". Read with "for every prime power" governing the whole clause, this contradicts the Check of §3.2 (lines 528–531): at `q = 2` the candidate is trivial and `PSL₂(𝔽₂)` has order 6, and at `q = 3` the candidate is the Klein four-group and `PSL₂(𝔽₃) ≅ A₄`. The identification holds exactly on the valid range `q ≥ 4`, where `SL₂(𝔽_q)` is perfect.

The smallest change: "the candidate is `PSL₂(𝔽_q)` for every valid `q`, that is `q ≥ 4`".

## C04 — The route of §9.3 omits the simplicity of `Co1`, which the cited Lemma 5.5 uses

§9.3 derives the list `1, ⟨z⟩, Q, G₀` of normal subgroups of `G₀` "from its extraspecial subgroup and the faithful irreducible action of `Co1` on `Λ/2Λ`" (lines 1293–1294), with *Needs:* §§8.4, 9.1–9.2. Those two inputs control the normal subgroups inside `Q`; a normal subgroup not contained in `Q` is handled through its image in `G₀/Q ≅ Co1`, and the source's proof names the simplicity of `Co1` as its third input (hohnseysen line 1054). That theorem is §8.3 here.

The smallest change: add "and the simplicity of `Co1` (§8.3)" to the sentence and §8.3 to the *Needs:* line.
