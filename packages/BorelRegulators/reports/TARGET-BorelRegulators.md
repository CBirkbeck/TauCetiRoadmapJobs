## Verdict and supply direction

The whole target is not a duplicate: its new covolume, primitive determinant and quotient-volume theorems remain unplanned in the inspected owners. It is also not permission to rebuild the existing regulator maps. The brief therefore calls for a distinct early general supplier with the existing map block transferred intact from PS.3.2a, and with stable/rank results imported from ArithmeticKTheory. The new subject is the archimedean arithmetic-group period/volume theorem, rather than the next integral K-group table or another stage of the later motivic fundamental-line program, so no Part II base is assigned.

The supplied merge order puts ArithmeticKTheory before Polylogarithms, but PeriodsAndSpecialValues and EndoscopicTransferAndUnitaryTraceComparison later. A wholesale dependency on either later roadmap would obstruct the intended supply to Polylogarithms. The brief identifies exact existing blocks to relocate: the regulator-specific PS.3.2a construction/comparison and the classical norm-one input of §6.2. The latter must bring its actual prerequisites with it; moving only a theorem name is insufficient. Their remaining applications stay downstream. No merge-order or other-package edit was made in this triage.

BorelRegulators is unused as an actual roadmap in main, completed work, open roadmap heads or indexed board packages. The existing `packages/BorelRegulators/sources.toml` is this intake's source scaffold. Consumer references to the proposed name do not constitute a roadmap. The name is therefore retained.

## Mathematical and source verification

Every new source was fetched through the sources service into its cache. Read the line-numbered extractions and checked formula-bearing scan pages visually, especially printed pp.618–619 and 631–632 of Borel 1977. The brief's statements are in our own words, with exact locators; no source passage or extraction was placed in the repository.

- borel1974 line 934, Theorem 7.5, p.259, and borel1974 line 1377, Proposition 12.2, p.271: stable comparison/ranks support the existing supplier. The initial provisional page locator was corrected after reading.
- borel1977 line 213 and borel1977 line 227, Proposition 2.4, pp.618–619: quotient volume is a nonzero positive rational multiple of the product of ζ_F(j), using the norm-one Tamagawa input and all finite-place factors. The brief retains Borel's gauge convention rather than silently using another archimedean Haar measure.
- borel1977 line 571, Theorem 6.2, pp.631–632, and borel1977 line 590, Remark 6.3, p.632: determinant formula with exponent d_n−Nn and its totally real even-weight consequence. Theorem 6.2, not the initially provisional §5.2 locator, is the relevant theorem. The weight conversion is m=n−1.
- burgos2002 line 3256, Theorem 9.12, p.80; burgos2002 line 3607, Remark 9.20, p.84; burgos2002 line 3681, Proposition 9.22, p.85: distinguish the original homotopy lattice from the renormalized embedding-coordinate lattice. This gives the leading-value covolume and its precise power-of-π conversion. The author copy's locators are used, avoiding the inconsistent pp.72–74 citation in PS.3.2a.
- burgos2002 line 3973, Examples 10.4–10.5, p.91, and burgos2002 line 4139, Theorem 10.9, pp.95–97: real Deligne target and factor-two Borel/Beilinson comparison.

The lattice is the integral K-group image; its rational span is not discrete. Weight one is the existing Dirichlet theorem on units, rather than a false finite-rank claim about Fˣ. Higher weights are unrestricted n≥2, over every number field. The rank-zero special-value theorem is retained explicitly: a zero-dimensional regulator isomorphism alone cannot prove it. Checks include ℚ in weights 2, 3 and 4, both parities for imaginary quadratic fields and arbitrary totally real even weights.
