# Design report: BorelRegulators

The deliverables specify 27 targets in seven layers, with exact statements, proof routes, supplier contracts, native-library contacts, APIs and discriminating examples. They cover all four final endpoint families in packages/BorelRegulators/target.md. Twelve explicit contract or acceptance gaps remain. The design is reviewable; it is not presented as a package ready for acceptance elaboration.

The main mathematical correction is to endpoint 3 of the triaged target. On printed pp.631–632, Borel’s actual compact-dual cohomological determinant has exponent −Nn, whereas the renormalized Tate-divided regulator determinant has exponent dₙ−Nn. In weight 3 over ℚ the two classes are ζ(3)/π³ and ζ(3)/π². They cannot be identified: their ratio has a factor π. Sections 5.1–5.4 keep the comparison and normalization conversion separate. Burgos Remark 9.20 gives R_Bo/R′_Bo=4π in that example: (2π)¹·2¹=4π. The empty determinant keeps the same exponent in either convention and yields §7.1 directly from the period argument.

The gauge audit also fixes an essential detail: Borel’s m∞ includes Δ_F^(−h/2), rather than the unscaled infinite product. This is visible in borel1977 line 153, §1.6 equation (1), printed p.617, and agrees with AdelicAlgebraicGroups §2.4. For ℚ(i), h=3 the factors 4^(−3/2) and 2³ cancel; removing the discriminant scalar changes the volume theorem.
