## R01 (P2) — The nonarchimedean half of 2.6 *top-form-measure* is already owned by LogicAndDefinabilityInNumberTheory §§2.7 and 2.12, which comes earlier in the merge order

As written, two roadmaps would build the same measure on the p-adic points of a smooth variety, and the same Fubini formula for a smooth morphism. They also use opposite orders for the relative form, so an implementer cannot tell which statement to formalise. LogicAndDefinabilityInNumberTheory is a new roadmap in an open pull request, placed before this one in the merge order. Its §2.7 (README lines 387–412) builds, for a nonarchimedean local field `F` and an `F`-analytic manifold, the measure of an `r`-gauge form with chart density `|f|^{1/r}`. It proves that analytic isomorphisms pull the measure back to the measure of the pulled-back form, and it treats the `F`-points of the smooth locus of an algebraic `X`. Its §2.12 (lines 486–504) proves, for a submersion and gauge forms, the existence of the relative form `θ` with `f^*ω_Y ⊗ θ = ω_X` and the identity `∫_X = ∫_Y ∫_{X_y}`, with `θ` algebraic in the smooth algebraic case. Its Scope (line 1841) claims both as the extension of AdelicAlgebraicGroups §2.4. At `r = 1` and `k` nonarchimedean of characteristic zero, these are the chart construction, functoriality and fibre formula of *top-form-measure* (README lines 34–45). This roadmap's Scope (line 471) claims the same object and does not name LogicAndDefinabilityInNumberTheory. The two also order the relative form differently: for `a = xy` with `ω = dx ∧ dy` and `ω′ = da`, §2.12 puts the base first and gets `θ = −dx/x`, while 2.6 puts the relative form first (`ω = θ ∧ π^*ω′`) and gets `θ = dx/x`. The measures agree.

The smallest repair has three parts. For nonarchimedean `k`, cite LogicAndDefinabilityInNumberTheory §§2.7 and 2.12 in *top-form-measure* and in (ii) of *homogeneous-gauge-measure*. Keep the archimedean case (`ℝ`, `ℂ`), the comparison with AdelicAlgebraicGroups §2.4 and the restriction to open subvarieties as this roadmap's own. Add the supplier to the Scope and to the supplier contracts, with one sentence stating that 2.6 orders the relative form oppositely to §2.12. The order-of-factors Check in (iv) already pins this roadmap's convention.

## R02 (P2) — The route of 4.1 credits ReductiveGroups Layer 7 with a theorem that roadmap does not state: that the subgroup generated by a maximal torus and the root subgroups of a closed symmetric set of roots is reductive with that root system

This is the step that makes `C_G(s)°` reductive, which is headline 4.1 (a) and stated in `Suggested.lean`. An implementer who follows the citation finds nothing, so the step has no owner. The route (README lines 428–430) and the supplier contract (line 492) both assign the step to ReductiveGroups Layer 7. Layer 7 on main has four targets: Borel subgroups and maximal tori, parabolics and Levi decompositions, the root datum with its Weyl group, and the Bruhat decomposition. Layer 7C adds the rank-one root maps and products of root groups over positive roots. A search of ReductiveGroups, and of every roadmap on main and in the open pull requests, for closed symmetric sets of roots or subsystem subgroups finds no such statement.

Either state the step as an item of 4.1 with its own locator, or derive reductivity directly from Layer 7C's rank-one maps. In either case, correct line 492 to list only what Layers 3, 7 and 9E state.

## C02 — The module docstring and the Summary disagree on whether the root-system clause belongs to headline 4.1

The Summary's headline (4) (README line 5) includes "reductive with root system `{α : α(s) = 1}`". The docstring of `Suggested.lean` (lines 65–66) says the root system "is the structure that the proof of the headline provides, not headline statements". The reason it is not statable is missing: at the pin, Tau Ceti has no roots of a reductive group relative to a maximal torus, only the coordinate datum `TauCeti.SplitTorus.coordinateRootDatum`. Either name that clause in the docstring as not statable, with that missing carrier, or drop it from the Summary's headline.

## C03 — The family index describes this roadmap as covering Siegel sets, which it does not

In `TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannHypothesisForDirichletLFunctions/README.md`, lines 49–51 read "Tamagawa measures, Siegel sets and orbit measures on adelic groups, and centralisers of semisimple elements". Siegel sets appear nowhere in this README; they are AdelicAlgebraicGroups §3.4. A more accurate line would be: "top-form and orbit measures on homogeneous spaces, Tamagawa numbers of `G_m`, of groups with a unipotent radical and of quotients by central induced tori, and centralisers of semisimple elements". Every pull request of the family carries the index, so this needs to change in each of them.
