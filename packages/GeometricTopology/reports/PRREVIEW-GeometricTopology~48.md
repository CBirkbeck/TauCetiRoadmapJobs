**Repair:** add one target to Artin.P, after the sentence defining the word-realization map: "Prove the reading lemma: if the projection of a smooth geometric braid to the first disc coordinate and the height is generic with crossings at distinct heights, the braid is isotopic to the stack, read upward, of the elementary braids `σ_i^(±1)` of its crossings, with the sign given by the front/back convention above; on each slab between consecutive crossing heights the braid has the same height-pattern as the straight braid, and on a slab with one crossing the same pattern as `σ_i^(±1)`, so the straight-line homotopy is an isotopy (Artin, the Lemma on printed p. 103).
