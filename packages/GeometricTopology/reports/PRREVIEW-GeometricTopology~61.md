**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/GeometricTopology/README.md:3801–3803` ("The untwisted Whitehead double of the trefoil has `Δ = 1` and is not the unknot (its genus is `1` and it is not slice by Layer 6), so `Δ = 1` does not detect the unknot").

Instance: the Levine–Tristram signatures, Arf invariant and determinant of §§6.2, 4.7 all vanish for `Δ = 1`, so Layer 6 has no obstruction to apply.

**Repair:** replace the parenthesis by "(its genus is `1`, Layer 4.11; it is topologically slice by Layer 6.4, and the untwisted double is not smoothly slice only by a Floer-theoretic fact outside this roadmap)". Retest: the Check then agrees with §6.4's Check and §4.11's Check, and the sentence's conclusion ("`Δ = 1` does not detect the unknot") is unchanged.
