## R02 (P2) — PL embeddings in codimension one are not known to be locally flat

**Location:** `README.md:620–623` (2.2): "this holds for all PL embeddings in codimension at most one and at least three (Rourke–Sanderson, Chapter 3)". **Claim:** every PL embedding of an `(m−1)`-manifold in an `m`-manifold is locally flat. **Witness:** local flatness of a PL embedding at a point says its link pair is the standard sphere pair. For the cone `c(Σ) ⊆ c(S⁴) = D⁵` on a PL `3`-sphere `Σ ⊆ S⁴`, local flatness at the apex says `(S⁴, Σ)` is the standard pair, that is, `Σ` bounds PL balls on both sides: the four-dimensional PL Schoenflies problem, open. Zeeman's unknotting theorem gives the codimension-`≥ 3` case; the codimension-one case is a theorem only in ambient dimension at most `3` (Alexander, Moise) and, for *locally flat* spheres, in dimension at least `5`, which is circular here. No chapter of Rourke–Sanderson claims the general codimension-one statement. **Repair:** "in codimension at least three (Zeeman's unknotting theorem, Rourke–Sanderson, Chapter 7) and in codimension zero; in codimension one it holds in ambient dimension at most three and is the PL Schoenflies problem in general, which is not a target". Keep the trefoil cone and the unknotted cone as the two Checks.
