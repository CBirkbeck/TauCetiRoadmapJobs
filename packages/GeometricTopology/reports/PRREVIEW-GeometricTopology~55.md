## R02 (P2) — Norm-minimizing surfaces need not avoid sphere components

**Location:** `README.md:2204` (10.3, third API bullet): "`x(a)` is attained by an incompressible surface with no sphere components (Thurston, Section 2)." **Claim:** for every closed oriented `3`-manifold `M` and every `a ∈ H₂(M; ℤ)`. **Witness:** `M = S² × S¹`, `a = [S² × pt] ≠ 0`, `x(a) = 0` (the sphere, as the section's own first Check says). A representative of `a` with `χ₋ = 0` and no sphere component is a union of tori; a torus `T ⊆ S² × S¹` is never incompressible in the sense consumed in 8.3 (`TauCeti.IsIncompressible`, injective on `π₁`), since `ℤ² → π₁(S² × S¹) = ℤ` is never injective. So no surface of the stated kind attains `x(a)`. The hypothesis that fails is irreducibility (the essential sphere); the bullet is true for irreducible `M`, and for general `M` with "no sphere component bounding a ball". **Repair:** "for irreducible `M`, `x(a)` is attained by an incompressible surface with no sphere components; in general by an incompressible surface none of whose sphere components bounds a ball (Thurston, Section 2)", and add `S² × S¹` with `a = [S² × pt]`, attained only by spheres, as a Check. Tested: on `Σ₂ × S¹` (irreducible) the bullet holds with `Σ₂ × pt`; on `S² × S¹` the amended bullet holds with the sphere.
