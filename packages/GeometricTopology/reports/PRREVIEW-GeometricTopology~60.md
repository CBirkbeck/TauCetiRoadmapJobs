## R01 (P2) — A Check of 7.3 asserts a filling of the Whitehead link exterior is `S³` that is not

Location: `README.md:2148–2150`, "`H₁` of the `5, 5/2` filling: the linking matrix of the Whitehead link is zero and the surgery presentation gives `H₁ = ℤ/5 ⊕ ℤ/5`, of order `25`; the `1, 1` filling gives `S³`, so the slopes matter."

Claim: the Dehn filling of the Whitehead link exterior with coefficients `1` on the unknotted component and `1` on the Whitehead curve, read in the preferred framings, is `S³`.

Witness: blow down the `+1`-framed unknotted component. The Whitehead curve meets its spanning disc twice with opposite signs, so it acquires one full twist and keeps framing `1 − lk² = 1`; a clasp with one full twist is a twist knot with `±2` half-twists, of determinant `|2n + 1| ∈ {3, 5}` for `n = ±2`, the trefoil or the figure-eight, never the unknot. The `1, 1` filling is therefore `±1`-surgery on a nontrivial knot in `S³`: its `H₁` is `0`, and by this roadmap's own Gordon–Luecke statement (`gordon_luecke_theorem`, 5.4: no filling of a nontrivial knot exterior along a non-meridian slope is `S³`) and Property P it is not `S³`; it is the Poincaré sphere or `Σ(2, 3, 7)` or its mirror. The sentence as written refutes a theorem stated in the same roadmap.

Repair: replace "the `1, 1` filling gives `S³`, so the slopes matter" by "the `1, 1` filling has `H₁ = 0` (surgery matrix the identity) and is `±1`-surgery on a twist knot, obtained by blowing down the unknotted component, so it is a homology sphere that is not `S³` (5.4); the slopes matter". Tested: `H₁` of the `(1, 1)` filling from the diagonal matrix is `0`, and the blown-down knot has determinant `3` or `5`, so the replacement is consistent with 5.4.
