### The changed passages

**Layer 8.5, KM 7.5.2 as restated (README 3621–3637).** The statement was checked by proving it along the route given, step by step:

- Residue field of `A^H`. In residue characteristic `p` the multiplicative representatives of a perfect residue field are the elements with `pᵐ`-th roots for all `m`, so an automorphism trivial on the residue field fixes them; in residue characteristic `0`, `|H|⁻¹ ∑ h(a)` is invariant and has the residue of `a`. Both need "identity on the residue field": on `A = W(𝔽_{p²})`, `n = 1`, `H` generated by Frobenius, `A^H = ℤ_p` and `N(p) = p²` is not a parameter, so the hypothesis cannot be dropped.
- Generation. With `I = (x₁, …, x_{n−1}, N(xₙ))`, `A/IA = D/(xₙ^{|H|})` for the discrete valuation ring `D = A/(x₁, …, x_{n−1})`, with `k`-basis `1, …, xₙ^{|H|−1}`; successive approximation gives coefficients converging in `A`, and their limits lie in `A^H` because each `h` is continuous and `A^H` is closed.
- Independence. `Frac(A)^H = Frac(A^H)` and `[Frac A : Frac A^H] = |H|` by faithfulness, so `|H|` generators of a module of rank `|H|` over a domain are a basis.
- Parameters. `A/IA` has length `|H|` and `A` is free of rank `|H|` with the same residue field, so `A^H/I` has length `1`: `I` is the maximal ideal of `A^H`, generated by `n = dim A^H` elements.
- Variant 7.5.3 by iteration. After dividing by `H_n` the ring `A^{H_n}` has parameters `x₁, …, x_{n−1}, N_n(x_n)`. `H_{n−1}` fixes `N_n(x_n)` (it fixes `x_n` and commutes with `H_n`), fixes `x_j` for `j ≠ n−1`, and acts faithfully on `A^{H_n}`: an element acting trivially there acts on `A` as some element of `H_n`, and faithfulness of the product makes it `1`. The hypotheses of 7.5.2 hold at each step.

**The three Checks added (3650–3659).** Recomputed: `y ↦ y + x` in characteristic `2` has `N(y) = y(y + x) = y² + xy`, `y + x ≡ x ≢ 0` modulo `y`, and `A = A^H ⊕ A^H y` over `A^H = k[[x, y² + xy]]`. For `k = 𝔽_p(t)`, `t ↦ t + y` extends to `k[[y]]` (every nonzero polynomial in `t + y` has nonzero residue, hence is a unit), `σ^p(t) = t + py = t`, `∏_i (t + iy) = t^p − t y^{p−1}` is invariant, `y ∈ A^H` is a uniformiser of `A^H`, so `e = 1`, `f = p`, residue field `𝔽_p(t^p)`, and `N(y) = y^p` is not a parameter. The hypotheses of the lemma other than perfectness hold (the fixing condition is empty for `n = 1`), so the Check is a true negative control.
