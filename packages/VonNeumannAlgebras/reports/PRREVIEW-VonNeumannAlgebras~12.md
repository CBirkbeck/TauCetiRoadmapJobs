## R02 (P1) — V1.2 states a false "iff": a subspace whose intersection with the unit ball is weakly closed need not be weakly closed

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/VonNeumannAlgebras/README.md:311–313` ("State the hypotheses on subspaces explicitly: a subspace is weakly closed iff strongly closed iff its intersection with the unit ball is."). The sentence is new in this head (change set line 419).

**Claim:** The last "iff" is false in the direction from the ball to the subspace. Take `H = ℓ²(ℕ)` and the roadmap's own functional `φ(x) = Σ n⁻² ⟪e_n, xe_n⟫` of V1.1 (`:264–266`), and `V = ker φ`. (a) `V ∩ ball` is weakly closed: the unit ball is weakly compact (`:313–314`, houdayer-invitation line 284), weak and ultraweak coincide on it (`:261–262`, peterson-notes line 986), `φ` is ultraweakly continuous, so `ball ∩ ker φ` is relatively closed in the ball for the ultraweak, hence for the weak, topology, and the ball is weakly closed in `B(H)`. (b) `V` is not weakly closed: a linear functional is continuous iff its kernel is closed, and `φ` is not weakly continuous (V1.1 `:265`, jones-vna line 947–951, and the README's own Check `:283–285`). (c) `V` is not strongly closed either, since a strongly closed convex set is weakly closed (`:306–308`). So `V ∩ ball` is weakly and strongly closed while `V` is neither. The true statement is the ultraweak one: a subspace is ultraweakly closed iff ultrastrongly closed iff its intersection with the unit ball is ultraweakly (equivalently weakly) closed, which is the Krein–Šmulian theorem for the dual pair `(S₁(H), B(H))` and needs V4.Core's duality and BanachSpaceTheory B2, both of which V1.2's `*Needs:*` line (`:325–327`) does not cite.

**Witness:** `φ(p) > 0` while every finite matrix-coefficient functional vanishes on `p` (`:283–285`); the kernel-closed criterion for continuity of a linear functional.

**Repair:** Replace the clause by "a subspace is weakly closed iff strongly closed; a subspace is ultraweakly closed iff ultrastrongly closed iff its intersection with the unit ball is ultraweakly closed, by the Krein–Šmulian theorem (BanachSpaceTheory B2) applied to the predual duality of V4.Core, so this last criterion is proved in V4.Core", and add `ker φ` as a negative-control Check in V1.2: `ker φ ∩ ball` is weakly closed, `ker φ` is not. Control: the instance `V = ker φ` satisfies every clause of the corrected sentence.
