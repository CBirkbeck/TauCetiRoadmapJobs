## R03 (P3) — The total-variation bound in V2.BorelCalculus does not follow from polarization alone

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/VonNeumannAlgebras/README.md:537–539` “by polarization the complex measures μ_(ξ,η) with … total variation at most ‖ξ‖‖η‖”.

**Claim:** Polarization defines μ_(ξ,η) as (1/4)Σ_k conj(i^k) μ_(ξ+i^kη) and bounds its variation by ‖ξ‖²+‖η‖², which after rescaling ξ↦tξ, η↦η/t gives 2‖ξ‖‖η‖, not ‖ξ‖‖η‖. The sharp bound is true, but it comes from the Riesz representation of the functional f↦⟨ξ,π(f)η⟩ on `C(K)`, whose norm is at most ‖ξ‖‖η‖ and equals the total variation of its regular representing measure.

**Witness:** K a point, H=ℂ, π=id, ξ=η=1: μ_(ξ,η)=δ with variation 1; the four polarization terms have masses |1+i^k|²=4,2,0,2, so the polarization bound is 8/4=2.

**Repair:** Write “by polarization the complex measures μ_(ξ,η) … ; their total variation is at most ‖ξ‖‖η‖ because μ_(ξ,η) represents the functional f↦⟨ξ,π(f)η⟩ on `C(K)`, of norm at most ‖ξ‖‖η‖”, or weaken the bound to 2‖ξ‖‖η‖, which is all the boundedness of the sesquilinear form needs. On the witness both readings give variation 1 ≤ 1 and 1 ≤ 2.
