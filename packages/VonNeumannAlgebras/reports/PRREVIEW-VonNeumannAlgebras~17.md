## R02 (P3) — The inclusion-matrix entry is undefined when `p_j q_i = 0`

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/VonNeumannAlgebras/README.md:806–808`: "`λ_{ij}` is the integer `k` for which `e(p_j q_i M q_i p_j)e` is a type `I_k` factor when `e` is a minimal projection of the subfactor `p_j q_i N` of the factor `p_j q_i M q_i p_j`".

**Claim:** The defining clause presupposes `p_j q_i ≠ 0`. When `p_j q_i = 0` the corner `p_j q_i M q_i p_j` is the zero algebra, which by the Conventions (`:79`, "A factor has nonzero identity") is not a factor, `p_j q_i N` has no minimal projection, and no `k` is determined; the entry formula (`:812`, the rank of `p_j e_i`) gives `0` there, but it is stated as a theorem to be proved about `λ_{ij}`, not as part of its definition. The three inclusion Checks (`:825–829`) all have every entry positive, so none discriminates. The general realisation of "every admissible inclusion matrix" (`:813–814`, Jones Exercise 4.4.5) needs zero entries. Jones's own definition has the same gap (recorded under Source issues).

**Witness:** `N = ℂ ⊕ ℂ ⊆ M = ℂ ⊕ ℂ` by the identity: `q₁ = p₁ = (1, 0)`, `q₂ = p₂ = (0, 1)`, `p₁ q₂ = 0`, so `p₁ q₂ M q₂ p₁ = 0` is not a factor and `:807` assigns no `λ₂₁`; the rank formula gives `rank(p₁ e₂) = rank 0 = 0`.

**Repair:** In the definition add "and `λ_{ij} = 0` when `p_j q_i = 0`", and add the Check "`ℂ ⊕ ℂ ⊆ ℂ ⊕ ℂ` by the identity: `Λ` is the identity matrix, the dimension formula reads `1 = 1·1 + 0·1`, and a definition that only assigned entries to nonzero corners would leave two entries blank". Tested on the instance: with the convention `Λ = [[1, 0], [0, 1]]`, `r₁ = 1 = λ₁₁ k₁ + λ₂₁ k₂ = 1·1 + 0·1`, composition with itself gives `Λ² = Λ`, and `Λ` has no zero row or column, as `:822` requires.
