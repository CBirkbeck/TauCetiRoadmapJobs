# DESIGN-MotivicEtaleKTheory report

The design covers all six endpoints of packages/MotivicEtaleKTheory/target.md in seven layers and 25 targets. It includes 80 discriminating Checks. There are six definition targets; constructions also carry at least three Checks. The deliverables are design/README.md, design/plan.json, sources.toml and this report in packages/MotivicEtaleKTheory. No target was consolidated into this job from another package.

The plan builds on shared simplicial higher cycles, actual perfect K-groups, Adams operations, ordinary intersection and motive comparisons. It does not re-plan the motivic-to-K tower, finite-coefficient étale descent, ordinary Chow theory, degree-zero Chern theory, native mixed Hodge linear algebra, current carriers or arithmetic regulator computations. The original projective case is an example of the smooth quasi-projective statement. Arbitrary fields, characteristic 2, purely inseparable transfers, disconnected schemes, nonreduced support and singular closed complements retain their stated scopes.
