## R01 (P3) — "`𝒜(X)` and `A(X)` are unchanged as sets" under `e ↦ λe` is false for `λ ∉ ℚ`

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/LefschetzStructures/README.md` lines 469–472, the sentence "Nothing here depends on `e`: under `e ↦ λe`, … `𝒜(X)` and `A(X)` are unchanged as sets".

Claim: rescaling the Tate marking leaves the algebraic ring and the algebraic classes unchanged as subsets of `End H(X)` and `H(X)`. Witness: MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles §2.3 (line 374 at its head `57b8fe4`) rescales by `λ ∈ Fˣ` with `F` the coefficient field (this roadmap's `K`), and gives `cl^r ↦ λ^{−r} cl^r`, the fact the sentence itself quotes. On `ℙ¹` with `K = ℚ_ℓ` and `λ` an `ℓ`-adic unit outside `ℚ`, `A¹(ℙ¹) = ℚ·cl¹(pt)` becomes `ℚ·λ⁻¹cl¹(pt)`, a different `ℚ`-line of `H²(ℙ¹) = K`; likewise the degree-`r` part of `𝒜(X)` becomes `λ^{−r}` times itself (a correspondence of degree `r` acts through `cl^{d+r}` and `pr₂,*`, which scale by `λ^{−(d+r)}` and `λ^{d}`), so `𝒜(ℙ¹)` loses the operator `L` and gains `λ⁻¹L`. Both sets change whenever `λ ∉ ℚ`; the sentence holds only for `λ ∈ ℚˣ`, the case of MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles' own `ℙ¹` Check (`e ↦ 2e`).

What is true, and what the sentence's conclusion needs: the automorphism `θ` of `H(X)` acting on `H^i` by `λ^{−⌊i/2⌋}` carries the structures for `e` to those for `λe`: `θLθ⁻¹ = λ⁻¹L`, `θΛθ⁻¹ = λΛ`, `θ𝒜(X)θ⁻¹` is the new `𝒜(X)` (its degree-`r` part multiplied by `λ^{−r}`), `θ(A(X))` the new `A(X)`, the grade projectors commute with `θ`, and on classes of even degree `⟨θx, θy⟩_{λe} = λ^d·λ^{−d}⟨x, y⟩_e = ⟨x, y⟩_e`. So `LambdaAlgebraic`, `ProjectorsAlgebraic`, `PositiveOnAlgebraicPrimitive` and `PairingNondegenerateOnAlgebraic` each hold for one marking iff for the other; the positivity computation `λ^{d − (d−2i) − 2i} = 1` of the sentence is correct as written.

Repair: replace "`𝒜(X)` and `A(X)` are unchanged as sets" by "the degree-`r` parts of `𝒜(X)` and `A(X)` are multiplied by `λ^{−r}`, so the automorphism of `H(X)` acting by `λ^{−⌊i/2⌋}` on `H^i` carries the structures for `e` to those for `λe`, preserving the pairing on algebraic classes and each of 1.1's four properties". Tested on the `ℙ¹` instance above: `θ = 1` on `H⁰`, `λ⁻¹` on `H²`, `θ(ℚ·cl¹(pt)) = ℚ·λ⁻¹cl¹(pt)`, `θ(L)(1) = θ(cl¹(pt)) = λ⁻¹cl¹(pt) = (λ⁻¹L)(1)`.

## R02 (P3) — The `ℙ¹ × ℙ¹` Checks of 2.3 write the primitive part as a `ℚ`-line

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/LefschetzStructures/README.md` lines 478–481: "On `ℙ¹ × ℙ¹` with `H² = Ka ⊕ Kb` … `P² = ℚ(a − b)` and `A¹ ∩ P² = P²`" and "With `ξ = 2a + b` instead, `P² = ℚ(2a − b)`".

Claim: the primitive part `P²` of the Lefschetz structure on `H(ℙ¹ × ℙ¹)` is `ℚ(a − b)`, and it equals its intersection with the algebraic classes. Witness: `P²` is a `K`-subspace of `H² = Ka ⊕ Kb` (0.1 and the Conventions: `P^i = H^i ∩ ker L^{n−i+1}`), and `L(xa + yb) = (x + y)ab` has kernel `K(a − b)`, not `ℚ(a − b)`; `A¹ = ℚa ⊕ ℚb` (MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles §7.3, `A_H^j(X)` the rational image "even when the coefficient field `F` is larger than `ℚ`"), so `A¹ ∩ P² = ℚ(a − b) ≠ P²` whenever `K ≠ ℚ`, the `ℓ`-adic case. The Check of 2.4 on the same object says it correctly ("`a − b` spans `A¹ ∩ P²`"). The computation `L(xa + yb) = (x + y)ab` and, for `ξ = 2a + b`, `L(xa + yb) = (x + 2y)ab` with kernel spanned by `2a − b`, are right.

Repair: write "`P² = K(a − b)` and `A¹ ∩ P² = ℚ(a − b)`", and "`P² = K(2a − b)`" in the next bullet (the clause "the primitive part depends on `ξ`, the algebraic classes do not" stands). Tested: with `K = ℚ_ℓ`, `x = √2·(a − b)` for a `√2 ∈ ℚ_ℓ` (any `ℓ ≡ ±1 mod 8`) lies in `K(a − b)` and not in `ℚa ⊕ ℚb`.
