## R01 (P3) — "(for `λ ∉ ℚ` they change as sets)" is false in degree `0` and whenever `λ^r ∈ ℚ`

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/LefschetzStructures/README.md` lines 483–484, "the degree-`r` parts of `𝒜(X)` and `A(X)` are multiplied by `λ^{−r}` (for `λ ∉ ℚ` they change as sets)". Carried from the previous two reports (their R01); the passage is unchanged and nothing on the pull request answers it.

Claim: for every `λ ∈ Kˣ` outside `ℚ`, each degree-`r` part of `𝒜(X)` and of `A(X)` is a different set for the marking `λe` than for `e`. Witness: in degree `0` the factor is `λ⁰ = 1`, so `A⁰(X) = ℚ·1` and the degree-`0` operators never change; with `K = ℚ₇` and `λ = √2 ∈ ℚ₇` (`3² ≡ 2 mod 7`, Hensel), `λ ∉ ℚ` but `λ^{−2} = 1/2 ∈ ℚ`, so on `ℙ²` the degree-`2` part `A²(ℙ²) = ℚξ²` is multiplied by `1/2` and is the same `ℚ`-line. The multiplication statement before the parenthesis is right (`cl^{d+r} ↦ λ^{−(d+r)}cl^{d+r}`, `Tr_X ↦ λ^d Tr_X`, MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles §2.3 at `b8e693e`, line 376), and a part can change: `A¹(ℙ¹) = ℚ·cl¹(pt)` becomes `ℚ·λ⁻¹cl¹(pt)` for `λ ∉ ℚ`.

Repair: replace the parenthesis by "(for `λ^r ∉ ℚ` they can change as sets: on `ℙ¹`, `A¹ = ℚ·cl¹(pt)` becomes `ℚ·λ⁻¹cl¹(pt)`)". Tested on the three instances above: `r = 0`, and `λ = √2` with `r = 2`, have `λ^r ∈ ℚ` and are no longer claimed to change; `ℙ¹`, `r = 1`, `λ = √2` has `λ ∉ ℚ` and `ℚ·λ⁻¹cl¹(pt) ≠ ℚ·cl¹(pt)` because `cl¹(pt) ≠ 0` and `λ⁻¹ ∉ ℚ`.

## R02 (P3) — "`𝒜(X)` meets the hypotheses of `lambdaAlgebraic_of_charpoly`" claims one hypothesis too many

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/LefschetzStructures/README.md` lines 389–390, "for `X` over a finite field, with `ℓ`-adic cohomology and `F = ℚ`, `𝒜(X)` meets the hypotheses of `lambdaAlgebraic_of_charpoly`".

Claim, read as written: every hypothesis of `lambdaAlgebraic_of_charpoly` holds for `𝒜(X)`, hence its conclusion `Λ ∈ 𝒜(X)`, hence by 2.4 `B_H(X)` for every smooth projective `X` over a finite field. Witness: `Suggested.lean` lines 229–234 give the theorem four hypotheses, `hπ` (projectors algebraic), `hD` (characteristic polynomials on each `H^i` over `F`), and `u` with `hu`, `hu_bij` (for each `i ≤ n` an algebraic bijection `H^{2n−i} → H^i`). The paragraph's own second and third sentences establish only `hD` (Katz–Messing Theorem 2, 2)) and `hπ` (Theorem 2, 1)), and its last sentence makes the bijection hypothesis the condition of an "iff": that condition is not known for every `X` over a finite field (MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles §7.4 at `b8e693e`, line 1353, lists `B` for curves, surfaces, abelian varieties, flag varieties and their products and hyperplane sections only), so the first sentence overstates what the next three prove.

Repair: write "`𝒜(X)` meets the projector and characteristic-polynomial hypotheses of `lambdaAlgebraic_of_charpoly`" (or "all hypotheses of `lambdaAlgebraic_of_charpoly` except the algebraic bijections `u_i`"). Tested: with that wording, the sentence claims exactly `hπ` and `hD`, which the next two sentences prove from Katz–Messing, and the final "iff" is then the theorem with `u` as its remaining hypothesis in one direction and `Λ^{d−i} ∈ 𝒜(X)` (2.4 and 0.3) in the other.

## R03 (P3) — Katz–Messing Theorem 2 is stated for an absolutely irreducible `X` and for integral cycles; the paragraph drops both

Location: `TauCetiRoadmap/LefschetzStructures/README.md` lines 389–393, "for `X` over a finite field … a cycle on `X × X` whose characteristic polynomial on `H^i(X)` has rational coefficients (Katz–Messing 1974, Theorem 2, 2)) … the grade projectors are algebraic (ibid., Theorem 2, 1))".

Claim: Theorem 2 of Katz–Messing applies to the `X` of Layer 2 and to the degree-`0` part of an element of `𝒜(X)`. Witness: Katz's author-page copy of the paper (cache `9283138c`, lines 173–189, printed pp. 76–77) states Theorem 2 for `X` projective, smooth and absolutely irreducible over `𝔽_q`, part 1 for the Künneth components (rational combinations of graphs of Frobenius iterates) and part 2 for an integrally algebraic cycle of codimension `n`, whose characteristic polynomial on each `H^i` lies in `ℤ[T]`. Layer 2's varieties are MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles §2.2's, whose Conventions (at `b8e693e`, line 22) include finite disjoint unions, and the degree-`0` part of an element of `𝒜(X)` is a rational cycle (`Corr^0(X, X) = CH^d(X × X)_ℚ`, line 24 there). The conclusion survives both relaxations, but by steps the paragraph does not state: a rational cycle `Z = Z′/m` has `charpoly_Z(T) = m^{−N} charpoly_{Z′}(mT) ∈ ℚ[T]`, and a disconnected `X` is handled component by component (the component projectors are the classes of the components, and the trace argument runs on the sum).

Repair: state the theorem's hypotheses: "for a geometrically irreducible `X` over a finite field … the degree-`0` part, a rational cycle on `X × X`, a rational multiple of an integral cycle whose characteristic polynomial on `H^i(X)` has integer coefficients (Katz–Messing 1974, Theorem 2, 2)), so its own has rational coefficients". Tested on `X = ℙ¹ ⊔ ℙ¹` over `𝔽_q`: the paragraph as written cites Theorem 2 for it, which the source does not cover; with "geometrically irreducible" the claim is within the source, and the components are each covered.
