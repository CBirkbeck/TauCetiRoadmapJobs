## R02 (P3) — locators: spectral gap, fullness, the ICM section, the s-malleable definition, co-amenability

Location: `README.md` lines 371–374 ("on the orthogonal complement of its invariant vectors define spectral gap … (Anantharaman–Popa, Definition 15.1.1, p. 257); prove equivalence with failure of weak containment of the trivial representation there (Corollary 15.1.4)"); lines 383–384 ("fullness, spectral gap, absence of property Γ and triviality of bounded central sequences are equivalent (Theorem 15.3.2)"); lines 726–727 ("(Popa, Strong rigidity I, §1; Popa, ICM 2006, §5)"); lines 234–235 (co-amenability bullet, no locator).

Claims and witnesses:

- Anantharaman–Popa Definition 15.1.1 (anantharaman-popa-ii1 lines 11321–11338) defines spectral gap on the whole space (no weak containment of the trivial representation in `π` itself, `max_{g∈F} ‖π(g)ξ−ξ‖ ≥ c‖ξ‖` for every `ξ ∈ H`); under it the trivial representation has no spectral gap. The README's definition on `invariants^⊥` is the variant of Remark 15.1.2 (lines 11339–11344), which the book explicitly sets aside. The README's second Check (line 398, "the trivial representation on `ℂ` has spectral gap vacuously … a definition on the whole space would fail for it") therefore contradicts the definition it cites. The three "obviously equivalent conditions" of 15.1.1 are the weak-containment equivalence the README asks for; Corollary 15.1.4 (line 11378) is the spectral form `Sp(h) ⊂ [−1, 1−δ]` for the averaging operator `h = |F|⁻¹ Σ_{g∈F} π(g)`.
- Theorem 15.3.2 (lines 11715–11718) states only full ⇔ spectral gap for a separable II₁ factor. The equivalences with property Γ and with bounded central sequences are Theorems 15.2.3 and 15.2.4 (lines 11529, 11562).
- In the ICM survey, §5 is "Techniques for intertwining subalgebras" (popa-icm2006 line 590); malleable and s-malleable deformations are §6, "Deformation and rigidity in II₁ factors" (line 678), with s-malleability at lines 805–806 and the free-product case in 6.4 (line 815).
- The README's s-malleable deformation (lines 723–727) keeps only the automorphism group, the involution and `βα_tβ = α_{−t}`. Popa I §1.4 (popa-strong-rigidity-i lines 414–417, 424–426) and the Chapter 19 definition (anantharaman-popa-ii1 lines 13209–13215) also require the density/freeness condition `sp^w M α₁(M) = M̃` with `τ(x α₁(y)) = 0` for trace-zero `x, y`. The README's first Check (line 736, the trivial deformation is s-malleable) holds only for the lax form; the sources' form excludes it. The lax form is legitimate (the ICM survey, lines 785–789, uses it), but the kept variant should say in one clause how it differs.
- The bullet "`L(Γ)` is amenable relative to `L(Λ)` iff `Γ` has a `Γ`-invariant mean on `ℓ∞(Γ/Λ)`" carries no locator (it is Monod–Popa's co-amenability theorem; Anantharaman–Popa's Chapter 13 does not state it).

Repair: cite "Remark 15.1.2" for the complement definition and "Definition 15.1.1" for the weak-containment equivalence, keeping Corollary 15.1.4 for the averaging-operator form; add "Theorems 15.2.3–15.2.4" beside 15.3.2; replace "ICM 2006, §5" by "§6 (6.3–6.4)"; add to 8.1 one clause "without the density condition `sp^w M α₁(M) = M̃` of Popa I (1.4.3), which is imposed separately in 8.3 and 10.2"; give the co-amenability bullet its locator (N. Monod and S. Popa, *On co-amenability for groups and von Neumann algebras*, C. R. Math. Acad. Sci. Soc. R. Can. 25 (2003), Proposition 6, or the primary source the author prefers) and add it to the References.
