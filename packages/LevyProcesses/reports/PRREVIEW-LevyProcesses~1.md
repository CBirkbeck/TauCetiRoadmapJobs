## C01 — Two citations attach to statements more general than the source's, without saying so

A reader who opens the cited places finds a narrower theorem than the one stated and cannot tell which hypotheses the roadmap's own route removes. In 1.2 (line 107) Papapantoleon's Theorem 4.9 is cited for a Lévy process in law, but the source states it for its Definition 2.1: a càdlàg process adapted to a filtration satisfying the usual conditions (Papapantoleon lines 205–213 and 509–519), and with the truncation `1{|x| < 1}`. In 5.1 (line 515) Bretagnolle–Ouwehand's Theorem 1.4 is cited for a process with right-continuous paths and an arbitrary filtration with independent future increments; the source proves it for the càdlàg regularised version and its fixed filtration, on `{T < ∞}`, by optional sampling (BretagnolleOuwehand lines 59–61 and 81–95).

Both statements here are true as written and carry complete routes, so nothing changes in them. The repair is a clause at each citation, as 5.2 already does for Sato's Theorem 31.5: for example "(Papapantoleon, Theorem 4.9, for càdlàg adapted processes; …)" and "(Bretagnolle–Ouwehand, Theorem 1.4, for the càdlàg version and its own filtration; …)".

## N01 — The Examples of Layer 3 do not name the map that carries `ν` to `ℝ≥0`

"with `ν'` the image of `ν` on `ℝ≥0`" (line 432): `TauCeti.bernsteinLevyKhintchineExponent` takes a `Measure ℝ≥0`, so the comparison needs a named map; would "the image of `ν` under `Real.toNNReal`" do?

## N02 — The negative control of 5.1 describes `F_τ` slightly differently from Mathlib

At line 561 the event `A` is placed in `F_τ` "since it lies in the σ-algebra generated by all the `F_t`". Mathlib's `IsStoppingTime.measurableSpace` asks for measurability in the ambient σ-algebra together with `A ∩ {τ ≤ t} ∈ F_t`; the conclusion is unchanged (the event is ambient-measurable), and "since it is measurable" would match the definition.
