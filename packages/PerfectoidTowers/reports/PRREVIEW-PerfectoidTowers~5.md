## R04 (P3) — The plus-ring clause of P8.5 is cited to a source that states only the rational clause

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/PerfectoidTowers/README.md:745–748`: "`A^{G+}⟨T/s⟩ → (A⁺⟨T/s⟩)^G` are isomorphisms … hence `O_Y = (q_*O_X)^G` and `O_Y⁺ = (q_*O_X⁺)^G` ([CGJ] Proposition 2.1.3, p. 5)".

**Claim:** CGJ Proposition 2.1.3(2) proves that `A^G⟨T/s⟩ → A⟨T/s⟩^G` is an isomorphism; it says nothing about the two rings of integral elements.

**Witness:** CGJ lines 205–243: the statement names only the coequalizer property and the proof shows "the natural map A^G⟨T/s⟩ → A⟨T/s⟩^G is an isomorphism". The plus-ring equality is true but comes from elsewhere: both candidate plus rings of `B = A^G⟨T/s⟩` have the same adic spectrum (the image of `q⁻¹(U)` under P8.2's quotient map is `U`), and a ring of integral elements of a Tate ring is the set of functions bounded by one on its spectrum, so they coincide.

**Repair:** Keep the clause and attribute it to P8.2 (*invariant-spectrum-homeomorphism*) together with Huber's characterisation of a ring of integral elements by its adic spectrum, citing CGJ only for the rational isomorphism; or add that two-line argument as the proof sketch of the plus clause. On `A = K × K` with the swap and `U` the whole spectrum both sides are the diagonal `K⁺`.
