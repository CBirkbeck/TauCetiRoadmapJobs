## Source issues

- **Gap** in HJ at Theorem 5.3, proof, printed p. 28 (and Theorem 5.8, proof, p. 31): the proof takes the preimage of a G-clean neighbourhood of π(x) in the maximal Hausdorff quotient to be G-clean at x; it should assume that π is injective on the orbit of x (automatic for taut |X|, footnote 7), since otherwise the stabiliser of π(x) is larger than that of x; affects the proof.
- **Gap** in HJ at Theorem 5.8, proof, printed p. 31: the proof cites Hansen's Theorem 1.4 for the affinoid perfectoid quotient Spa(A^{H_x}, A^{+H_x}); it should cite a result without the hypotheses of that theorem (|G| invertible, or a K-linear action over a perfectoid field), namely KL16 Theorem 3.3.26 or CGJ Proposition 2.1.3; affects the proof.
- **Gap** in HJ at Lemma 5.10, proof, printed p. 32: the proof sets T to be the perfectoidisation of S⁺ with 1/ϖ adjoined and calls it a perfectoid Tate ring; it should take the ϖ-adic completion first, since in characteristic p the perfectoidisation is the uncompleted perfection; affects the proof.
- **Gap** in BHW at Lemma 3.7, proof, printed p. 10: the proof reduces the sheaf equality on Y ×_L U to the product opens V × U with V affinoid in Y; it should argue on all opens, since product opens are not a basis of the fibre product (the Gauss point of the diagonal of the bidisc has no product neighbourhood inside |T − S| ≤ |π|); affects the proof.
- **Error** in CHJ at Lemma 6.19(2), printed p. 53: the source says the restriction map on the mixed completed tensor from X(1/f) to X(f,1/f) has dense image because it is the map on bounded sequences and (1) holds; it should exclude infinite pseudobases, where the bounded family T^i on the unit circle of the disc of radius |ϖ|^{-1} has no bounded approximants from {1 ≤ |T| ≤ |ϖ|^{-1}}; affects the proof (the global-generation part of Theorem 6.20).
