## R05 (P3) — two Check descriptions are false although their values are right

Location and claim:

- `README.md` line 327, "`Y=S¹×T²⊔S¹×T²` with `Σᵢ={pt}×T²`; cutting and regluing crosswise gives again two copies of `S¹×T²`". Cutting each copy along its torus gives two copies of `T²×[0,1]`; regluing the `+` side of the first to the `−` side of the second and the `+` side of the second to the `−` side of the first (the excision gluing, km0807 lines 1938–1944) joins the two intervals into one circle: the excised manifold `Ỹ` is one copy of `S¹×T²` (circle of twice the length) containing the two parallel tori `Σ̃₁`, `Σ̃₂`. This is why the source reads the left side as a tensor product when `Y` has two components (km0807 lines 1948–1949) and gets a single group on the right. The displayed values `C⊗C=C` and `C` are correct (Proposition 7.4; the two parallel tori are homologous, so the simultaneous eigenspace is the one of a single torus).
- `README.md` line 482, "on `S¹×S²` the SO(3) bundle with `w₂` dual to `S²`": `w₂∈H²(S¹×S²;Z/2)≅Z/2` is Poincaré dual to the circle factor and pairs oddly with `[S²]`; this is the bundle IF8 line 317 writes as `(S¹×S²,m₀)` with `m₀` the core circle, and IF0 line 30 uses the same "dual to a circle factor" phrasing for `T³`. The conclusion (no flat connection, since a flat bundle is pulled back from `S¹` and has `w₂=0`) is right.

Witness: as quoted; for the first item the two-interval count `[0,1]∪[0,1]` glued end to end is one circle.

Repair: "cutting both along their tori and regluing crosswise gives one copy of `S¹×T²` containing the two parallel tori `Σ̃₁⊔Σ̃₂`; both sides are `C⊗C=C` and `C`"; and "the SO(3) bundle with `w₂` dual to the circle factor, odd on `S²`".
