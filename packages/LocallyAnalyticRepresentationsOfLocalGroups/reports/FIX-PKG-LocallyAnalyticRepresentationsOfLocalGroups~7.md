## REV-PKG-LocallyAnalyticRepresentationsOfLocalGroups~6/F02 (P3) — applied

The fourth Check of §2.5 changed:
- **Old:** "the trivial character B = ℚ_p and M = c_0(ℕ, E)".
- **New:** "the trivial character B = E, a one-dimensional E-Banach representation, and M = c_0(ℕ, ℚ_p), since E ⊗̂_(ℚ_p) c_0(ℕ, ℚ_p) = c_0(ℕ, E) for E finite over ℚ_p (B = ℚ_p is not an E-vector space once E ≠ ℚ_p)".

The finding is right: §2.1 defines a Banach representation as an E-Banach space, so when E ≠ ℚ_p, ℚ_p is not an object of Ban^adm_E(G).

One correction to the review's own witness: B = E is one-dimensional over E (two-dimensional over ℚ_p when E = ℚ_p(√p)), not "2-dimensional" as an E-representation. This does not affect the repair.

I searched for the same pattern (a ℚ_p-space standing as an E-representation) with `B = ℚ_p`, `V = ℚ_p`, `(ℕ, ℚ_p)`, `C(G_0, ℚ_p)` and `⊗̂_(ℚ_p)`. The only other uses of the tensor clause, in 2.3, 3.8 and 3.9, tensor an E-representation with the ℚ_p-Banach space C^an(G_n, ℚ_p). That is the correct variance, so I found no other instance.
