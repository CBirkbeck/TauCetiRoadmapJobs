3. **F03** (P3) A Check of 0.5 uses the regular representation of a non-compact group, which is not a locally analytic representation

   **Location:** `README.md:263`: "For V = C^an(G, E) with the left regular action, μ ∗ f is the convolution of μ with f, and δ_g ∗ f = f(g⁻¹ ·)."

   **Witness:** G = ℚ_p, f = 1 on p^(−m) + p^mℤ_p for m ≥ 1 and 0 elsewhere. If the orbit map were a power series on p^Nℤ_p, evaluation at p^(−m), m > N, would make 1_(p^mℤ_p) a power series on p^Nℤ_p. EM17 line 3463 assumes G compact.

   **Repair:** Replace the Check by: "For compact G and V = C^an(G, E) with the left regular action, μ ∗ f is the convolution of μ with f, and δ_g ∗ f = f(g⁻¹ ·).
