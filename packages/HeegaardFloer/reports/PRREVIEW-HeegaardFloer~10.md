## R02 (P3) — F2.4 cites Floer–Hofer–Salamon §5 "for strips"

**Location:** `README.md:2689–2692`, "for strips, Floer–Hofer–Salamon, *Transversality in elliptic Morse theory for the symplectic action*, … §5, by locator"; References 4917–4919, "transversality for strips (F2.4)".

**Claim:** Floer–Hofer–Salamon prove transversality for Hamiltonian Floer trajectories `u : ℝ × S¹ → M` with `∂_s u + J(∂_t u − X_H) = 0` (periodic orbits as ends, no boundary condition); strips `ℝ × [0,1]` with Lagrangian or totally real boundary are not in that paper.

**Witness:** the paper is uncertified in the manifest and was not read; the setting is confirmed from a lecture note on an author page that follows FHS95 verbatim: its moduli space is "`u : ℝ × S¹ → M`, `∂_s u + J(u)(∂_t u − X_{G_t}(u)) = 0`" with periodic-orbit asymptotics, and its tools are the Carleman similarity principle, unique continuation and injective points, as in the paper's own section list. The strip case with boundary in a Lagrangian is Floer's and Oh's.

**Repair:** at line 2690 replace "for strips, Floer–Hofer–Salamon …, §5, by locator" by "for Hamiltonian cylinders, Floer–Hofer–Salamon, *Transversality in elliptic Morse theory for the symplectic action*, Duke Math. J. 80 (1995), 251–292, §5, by locator, whose unique-continuation and injective-point route is adapted here to strips with totally real boundary (Y.-G. Oh, *Fredholm theory of holomorphic discs under the perturbation of boundary conditions*, Math. Z. 222 (1996), 505–520, by locator)", and change the References line 4919 to "transversality for Hamiltonian cylinders, adapted to strips (F2.4)". Both sources stay by locator; the manifest keeps `fhs1995` as uncertified.
