1. **F01** (P1) §6.2 rests on the `ℓ`-adic unipotent monodromy at `0`, which nothing supplies

   **Location.** `packages/KloostermanSheavesAndBesselIsocrystals/README.md:1591–1595` (§6.2): "a semisimple group
   generated by a procyclic image cannot contain a nontrivial principal unipotent element as required";
   `*Needs:* §§4.2, 6.1, 1.8; §RD.2.51; DeligneWeightsAndPurity §8.19`. Also L1722–1724 (§6.6) and L2544–2545.

   **Claim.** The contradiction is reached on the `ℓ`-adic companion: tame at both ends, so finite geometric
   monodromy, against a principal unipotent element.

   **Witness.** The only unipotent element this roadmap constructs is `exp(tN)` in the `p`-adic `G_geo` (§4.2). The
   source closes the argument with the `ℓ`-adic one: XZ line 2408 cites HNY Theorem 1 for it, i.e. HNY line 860–862
   (Theorem 1(2), p. 261), proved in HNY §4.3 (lines 1214–1247) from Gaitsgory's central sheaves. §1.6 states
   parts (1) and (3) of that theorem only; no layer, no supplier section and no gap states part (2), and
   `roadmaps grep -i 'central sheaves'` finds no owner. Three further inputs of the same route have owners but are
   not in the `*Needs:*` line: the `p`-adic Grothendieck–Ogg–Shafarevich formula (§RD.6.55), the `ℓ`-adic
   Euler–Poincaré formula and the tame fundamental group of `𝔾_m`. §6.3, §6.4, §6.6 and Layers 7 and 11 all use
   §6.2. The consumer GlobalShtukasAndFunctionFieldLanglandsPartII §4.0 cites "§KB.1, hny Theorem 1(2)" (= §1.6
   here) for the same fact, and §6.6 says the parameter is "attached to the companion" (S03).

   **Repair.** Replace the route of §6.2 (from "Route:" to "as required.") by:

   > Route: suppose wild inertia at `∞` acted trivially on every `Be†_W`. Then `Irr_∞(Be†_W) = 0`, and
   > `Irr_0(Be†_W) = 0` by §4.2, so `χ_c(𝔾_m, Be†_W) = 0` (§RD.6.55). The `L`-functions of `Be†_W` and `Kl_W^{ét,ℓ}`
   > agree (§1.8), and the degree of each as a rational function is minus its compactly supported Euler
   > characteristic (§RD.6.25 and the `ℓ`-adic trace formula), so `χ_c(𝔾_m, Kl_W^{ét,ℓ}) = 0`, and
   > `curveEulerPoincareSwan` (EtaleDualityAndPerverseSheaves §5.2) gives `Sw_0 + Sw_∞ = 0`: the `ℓ`-adic companion
   > is tame at both ends. The tame geometric fundamental group of `𝔾_m` is procyclic
   > (InverseGaloisAndArithmeticFundamentalGroups §IG.1.2), so the `ℓ`-adic geometric monodromy group is
   > commutative; its neutral component is semisimple (DeligneWeightsAndPurity §8.19), hence trivial, and the
   > group is finite. The arithmetic monodromy group modulo the geometric one is commutative, being topologically
   > generated by Frobenius, so for a faithful `W` the neutral component of the arithmetic monodromy group of the
   > semisimplification of `Kl_W^{ét,ℓ}` is a torus. By gap:companion-monodromy the neutral component of the
   > arithmetic monodromy group of the semisimplification of `Be†_W` is then a torus. But `Be†_W` is geometrically
   > semisimple, so its semisimplification has the same geometric restriction, on which `exp(tN)` (§4.2) is a
   > nontrivial connected unipotent subgroup: a contradiction. The local monodromy at `0` of the `ℓ`-adic sheaf
   > (HNY Theorem 1(2), p. 261) is not used and is not a target of this roadmap.

   `*Needs:*` of §6.2 becomes: §§4.2, 6.1, 1.8; PadicDifferentialEquationsAndRigidCohomology §§RD.2.51, RD.6.25,
   RD.6.55; gap:companion-monodromy; DeligneWeightsAndPurity §8.19; EtaleDualityAndPerverseSheaves §5.2;
   InverseGaloisAndArithmeticFundamentalGroups §IG.1.2. Add the two new suppliers to the scope list, to the
   supplier contracts and to the Layer 6 Dependencies (both precede this roadmap: merge order 108 and 55), and
   gap:companion-monodromy to the Layer 6 Dependencies. In §1.6 add: "Part (2) of HNY Theorem 1 (tameness and
   principal unipotent monodromy of `Kl_H^{ét,ℓ}` at `0`) is not a target; its `p`-adic counterpart is §4.2." Under
   *Downstream consumers* add the same sentence, so that the consumer stops citing §1.6 for it. Tested: for a
   torus `N = 0` and no contradiction arises (the existing third Check of §6.2); for `H = SL₂` the `p`-adic group
   contains `exp(tE₁₂)`, which no torus contains. The gap is a supplier contract of
