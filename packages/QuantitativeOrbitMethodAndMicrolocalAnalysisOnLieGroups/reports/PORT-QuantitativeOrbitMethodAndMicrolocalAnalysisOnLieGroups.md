## Draft pull request body

This roadmap builds Nelson–Venkatesh's microlocal calculus on unitary representations of Lie groups, uniform in the representation, and carries it to the smoothly weighted asymptotic formula for Gan–Gross–Prasad periods (Acta Math. 226 (2021), Theorem 30.1). Symbols on the imaginary dual of the Lie algebra are quantized through the exponential chart and measured on the Sobolev scale of the Nelson operator; the uniform star-product calculus and the order-zero bound (Layers 0–1), localization at infinitesimal characters (Layer 2) and Rossmann's character formula (Layer 3) give Kirillov-type trace asymptotics with uniform trace-ideal bounds; uniform Plancherel bounds and local relative characters (Layer 4), the stable invariant theory and volume forms of orthogonal and unitary GGP pairs (Layer 5) and the stable relative-character expansion (Layer 6) compare restriction to the subgroup with integration over stable Hamiltonian fibres; positive inverse branching (Layer 7), semiclassical limit states equidistributed by Ratner's theorem (Layer 8) and a microlocally truncated period Parseval identity (Layer 9) give the weighted period asymptotic for anisotropic pairs over a number field, with the binary orthogonal self-twist excluded. The calculus of Layers 0–3 is general-purpose for any later microlocal application to representations, and Layer 5 is the GGP invariant theory any relative-trace comparison of stable orbits consumes. `Suggested.lean` states the uniform order-zero bound for the quantization map and, on the standard matrix models over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, the disjoint-spectrum stability criterion, the torsor theorem for stable fibres and surjectivity of the joint invariant map, for both the orthogonal and the unitary pair; the other headline theorems (the rescaled operator calculus, Rossmann's formula and the orbit trace expansion, the Plancherel extensions, volume compatibility, the stable relative-character expansion, inverse branching, limit states and the period asymptotic) are named in the module docstring with the carrier each lacks at the pins (Sobolev scales of a unitary representation, tempered duals with Plancherel measure, Harish-Chandra characters, KKS measures, fibral volume forms, automorphic quotients).
