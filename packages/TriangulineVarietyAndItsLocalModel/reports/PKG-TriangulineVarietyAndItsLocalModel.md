## Mathematical corrections

In addition to the design's verified type, sign, endpoint and dimension repairs, the package corrects the following points.

1. The regular positive-lattice completion point includes α⁻¹ applied to the Hodge flag.
2. Exact period realization preserves extension sequences and functoriality; it need not reflect nonsplitting. The overstrong Check was replaced.
3. The local multiplicity problem asks for integer cycles and equations for every full parameter. Its API's effectivity requirement and generic-parameter-only wording contradicted the statement and were corrected. Effectivity belongs to the crystalline theorem.
4. A repeated-root fibre of the ordered-root cover has one underlying point but scheme length two. Its Check now records both facts.
5. The NT WD criterion only uses k_{τ,2} when n≥2; at n=1 there is no adjacent inequality.
6. Large-gap semistability requires algebraic restriction on the full unit group, not merely integral derivatives. A ramified finite-order line has weight zero but need not be semistable; the exact unit-character equation excludes it.
7. The rank-two companion example had its noncritical interpretation reversed. With increasing BHS h=(0,2), the Hodge line is Fil⁰. A noncritical first Frobenius line is different, giving relative position s=w₀ and only w=s. The split weight-zero first line equals Fil⁰, giving relative position 1 and permitting both permutations. This agrees with BHS19 Remark 4.1.6, lines 2991–2993, and Theorem 4.2.3, line 3040; Ding25 Lemma 4.1, lines 2651–2658, independently rules out nondominant companions for all-refinement noncritical objects.
8. Consequently the accepted review's E17 crystalline counterexample cannot establish necessity of dominance. Its gaps −2 and slope −3 satisfy the bare inequalities numerically, but its claimed noncritical w_x=1 uses the wrong permutation convention. The anti-dominant companion would require w₀≤1 and is absent. The accepted target's dominant hypothesis is retained; the package makes no claim that this failed example proves the larger source statement false.
9. The illustrative flat singular fibre is E[t]→E[x,y], t↦xy, with fibre E[x,y]/(xy). A quotient map E[x,y]→E[x,y]/(xy) would not supply a flat example.

All twelve dimension triples n=1,2,3,4 and d=1,2,3 were recomputed with exact integer arithmetic. They satisfy ambient=n²+dn², trianguline=n²+dn(n+1)/2, weight fibre=ambient−dn(n+1)/2, full-character fibre=weight fibre−n, original zero kernel=1−n+dn(n−1)/2, modified zero kernel=1−n+n(n−1)/2 and modification kernel=(d−1)n(n−1)/2. The first/last sum formula was evaluated with the whole intersection dimension subtracted. Only n≥2 instances are used for the crystabelline self-Ext assertions.
