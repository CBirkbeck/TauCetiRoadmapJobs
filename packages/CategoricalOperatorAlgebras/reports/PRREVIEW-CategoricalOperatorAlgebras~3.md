Repair, with values. (i) Replace the transpose Check by an instance where the conjugate is visible: in the W*-category of normal `ℂ²`-modules, pairs `(c₁,c₂)` of Hilbert spaces, let `F(c₁,c₂)=(0,c₁)` (the bimodule with `X_(12)=ℂ` and the other blocks zero). Then `⟨F(c),d⟩=HS(c₁,d₂)` and the transpose `F^t(d₁,d₂)=(d₂,0)` gives `⟨c,F^t(d)⟩=HS(c₁,d₂)`, while the wrong candidate `F^t=F` gives `⟨c,F(d)⟩=HS(c₂,d₁)`, a different space (dimension `dim c₂·dim d₁` instead of `dim c₁·dim d₂`).
