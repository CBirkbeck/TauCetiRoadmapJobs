**Witness:** For K=ℚ(i), use the centered unit square. The points (1/4,2) and (−1/4,2) lie strictly above every hemisphere and inside the square. The PSL₂ element diag(i,−i) identifies them.

**Repair:** Distinguish the translation Ford region from its quotient by the cusp rotations.
