1. **F01** (P2) A self-pairing does not supply algebra laws

   **Location.** `packages/SpectralSequencesAndConvergencePartII/design/README.md:1272`: “A double complex concentrated in column `0` with its pairing is a differential graded algebra”. Also `packages/SpectralSequencesAndConvergencePartII/design/plan.json:1275`, `one_column`.

   **Claim.** The pairing defined at README 1247–1252 makes a one-column complex a differential graded algebra. That definition specifies a bilinear chain map; it imposes neither associativity nor a unit, and does not even require all three complexes to coincide.

   **Witness.** Put `K⁰⁰=ℤe⊕ℤf`, all other terms zero, and `e²=f`, `fe=e`, `ef=f²=0`. Both Leibniz equations are `0=0`, but `(ee)e=e` and `e(ee)=0`.

   **Exact repair.** Replace the Check in both files by: “Place a differential graded algebra A in column 0 and use its associative unital multiplication as the self-pairing. Then `′E₁=H(A)` with its induced algebra product.” Keep the general definition of a pairing unchanged. Add the rank-two multiplication above as the negative control distinguishing a pairing from a differential graded algebra.

   **Repair test.** The rank-two multiplication is excluded because its associator at `(e,e,e)` is `e≠0`. For `A=ℤ[ε]/(ε²)`, `|ε|=1`, `d=0`, both associations of `(1,ε,1)` are `ε`, the unit acts identically, and `′E₁=A=H(A)` with the stated product. This repairs the missing hypotheses without narrowing the general pairing theory.

2. **F02** (P3) The socle witness labels the wrong variable

   **Location.** `packages/SpectralSequencesAndConvergencePartII/design/README.md:1024`: “because `X ⋆ −` is not right exact: `R → X` induces the zero map `soc(R) = k·xy → soc(X)`”. Also `packages/SpectralSequencesAndConvergencePartII/design/plan.json:930`.

   **Claim.** The displayed socle map is the image of `R → X` under `X ⋆ −`.

   **Witness.** For the stated ring and modules, `R ⋆ k=soc(R)` has dimension 1, whereas `X ⋆ R≅X` has dimension 3. The displayed map is obtained by applying `− ⋆ k`.

   **Exact repair.** Replace the final explanation in both files by: “Balance holds; the identification with ⋆ fails because `− ⋆ k` is not right exact: applying it to `R ↠ X` gives the zero map `soc(R)=k·xy → soc(X)=kx⊕ky`, which is not surjective.”

   **Repair test.** The quotient kills `xy`, so this map has rank 0 with two-dimensional target. Thus it fails to preserve the epimorphism, as required. The two derived degree-zero values remain `k`, while `X ⋆ k=k²`. The claim that `X ⋆ −` also fails right exactness is true, but this particular displayed map witnesses the other partial functor.
