## R01 (P2) — 5.5 claims that every rank-3 chirotope is an arrangement deletion

**Location:** `TauCetiRoadmap/PseudolineArrangementsAndOrientedMatroids/README.md` line 1054–1055: "Hence every rank-3 chirotope (with 1.7) is the deletion of the line at infinity from the chirotope of a monotone arrangement, up to isomorphism."

**Claim:** every rank-3 chirotope is isomorphic to `t ↦ A.chiFun (some ∘ t)` for some monotone arrangement `A`.

**Witness:** `ofVectorsFun` of `e₁, e₂, e₃, 0` has a loop.
- A rank-3 deletion of `A.chiFun` has no loop. A pseudoline in no basis would make every triple through it concurrent, so all lines would pass through one point and the rank would be at most 2.
- Relabelling and reorientation preserve loops.
- The same argument applies to the parallel pair in `e₁, 2e₁, e₂, e₃`.

**Repair:** replace the sentence with: "Hence every simple rank-3 chirotope is, up to isomorphism, the deletion of the line at infinity from the chirotope of a monotone arrangement; by 1.7 every rank-3 chirotope is obtained from one of these by adding loops and parallel or antiparallel copies."

**Tested** on `e₁, e₂, e₃, 0`:
- its simplification is `e₁, e₂, e₃`;
- 5.5's first Check (`w = (1, 1, 1)`) represents it by three lines in general position;
- 1.7 adds the loop back.
