## Verified sound

**The repaired sentence (README line 61–64), clause by clause, at the pins.**

- **"Tau Ceti's longest element … defined for crystallographic root pairings."** `TauCeti.longestElement` is at `TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/LongestElement.lean` line 197. It is defined under `[Finite ι] [IsDomain R] [P.IsCrystallographic]` (line 128), `[P.IsReduced]` (line 159) and `[P.IsRootSystem]` (line 192).
- **"… and dominant chambers."** `RootPairing.dominantChamber` (`Chamber.lean` line 141) itself carries only `[Preorder R]` and `b : P.Base`. However, Mathlib's `RootPairing.Base` (`Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Base.lean` line 68–76) places every root in ± the additive monoid generated by the simple roots.
  - Hence `s_j α_i = α_i − ⟨α_i, α_j^∨⟩ α_j` forces integral simple pairings, by linear independence, and with them all pairings are integral.
  - `H₃` has no base: computed, `s₁ α₂ = α₂ + 2cos(π/5) α₁ = α₂ + 1.618… α₁`, and the two simple roots are conjugate (odd entry 5), so no rescaling removes the factor.
  - So the clause is accurate in substance.
- **"Which RootSystems consumes."** RootSystems on main (3b3f7ef), README line 191–201, consumes `TauCeti.dominantChamber`, `TauCeti.openDominantChamber` and `TauCeti.longestElement` with exactly those hypotheses.
- **"The longest permutation enters 4.1–4.2 as Mathlib's `Fin.revPerm`."** `Fin.revPerm` is at `Mathlib/Data/Fin/Rev.lean` line 40. It occurs in 4.1 (line 751, 762), 4.2 (line 770, 773, 779, 781), Convention 8 (line 129) and `Suggested.lean` line 402. Line 1313 uses `CoxeterMatrix.reindex Fin.revPerm` as a reversal of the index set, and the sentence does not claim otherwise.
