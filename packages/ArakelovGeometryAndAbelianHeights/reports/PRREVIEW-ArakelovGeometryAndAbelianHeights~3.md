## R03 (P3) — §1.10 constructs the group of one-cycles that the pinned Chow module provides, and line 67 restricts the pinned Chow group to schemes over a field

Witness: `Chow.cyclesOfDimension X k` (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Basic.lean:86`) is defined for every scheme `X` as the cycles supported on points whose specialization height is `k`, that is, whose closure has Krull dimension `k`; `Chow.ChowGroup X k` (:295) needs only `[IsLocallyNoetherian X]`; the module docstring says no quasi-compactness or separation is imposed and treats "locally of finite type over a field" only as the case where a rationally trivial sum is automatically a `k`-cycle. Values: for `X = Spec O_K` the generic point has height 1, so the fundamental cycle lies in `Chow.cyclesOfDimension X 1`, as the first Check of §1.10 requires; a closed point has height 0 and is excluded; a fibre `𝔸¹_{𝔽_𝔭}` of `𝔸¹_{O_K}` is a 1-cycle of the pinned group and is excluded here only by the properness condition, which is the one thing §1.10 adds.
