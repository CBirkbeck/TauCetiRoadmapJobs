## R06 (P3) — A Check of §5.7.9 computes nothing

## R09 (P3) — The route of the first API bullet of §4.1.1 does not prove it

**Location** `README.md:386`: “the identity of an initial object is initial in `Fun(Δ[1], C)`, its pushouts are the identities, two-out-of-three gives the equivalences”.
**Claim** Pushouts of `id_∅` are identities, and two-out-of-three then yields every equivalence.
**Witness** A square with top `id_∅` and bottom `g : X → Y` is a pushout exactly when g is an equivalence (HTT line 22431). From identities alone, two-out-of-three on (f, f⁻¹, id) knows one edge of three.
**Repair** Replace by: “the identity of an initial object is initial in `Fun(Δ[1], C)`; every equivalence is a pushout of it, and a morphism homotopic to f is a pushout of f, so the pushout condition gives both; retracts are colimits of idempotents”.
