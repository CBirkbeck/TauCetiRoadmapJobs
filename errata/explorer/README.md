# The explorer's errata register (to 2026-10-21)

`source-issues.json.gz` is the explorer's `data/source-issues.json` as it stood when the explorer was
stopped on 2026-10-21: 15,335 mistakes found in published sources by its workers while extracting
papers and planning roadmaps (9,550 misprints, 3,304 errors, 2,481 gaps; 13,582 confirmed by a
review, 1,525 awaiting review, 228 rejected). The board's own register continues in `errata/REGISTER.md`
and `errata/source-issues.json`, fed by the `## Source issues` sections of review and red-team comments.
